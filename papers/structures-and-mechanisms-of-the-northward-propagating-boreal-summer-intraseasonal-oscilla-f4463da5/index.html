Abstract

摘要

通过分析 ECHAM4 模式模拟和 NCEP–NCAR 再分析，揭示了北传的北半球夏季季节内振荡（BSISO）的时空结构。

The spatial and temporal structures of the northward-propagating boreal summer intraseasonal oscillation (BSISO) are revealed based on the analysis of both the ECHAM4 model simulation and the NCEP–NCAR reanalysis.

模型模拟的 BSISO 结构和演化特征与 NCEP-NCAR 再分析得出的结果有许多相似之处。

The BSISO structure and evolution characteristics simulated by the model bear many similarities to those derived from the NCEP–NCAR reanalysis.

最显著的特征是涡度和比湿场中相对于 BSISO 对流的显著的经向不对称性。

The most notable features are the remarkable meridional asymmetries, relative to the BSISO convection, in the vorticity and specific humidity fields.

具有等效正压结构的正涡度扰动出现在对流中心以北几纬度处。

A positive vorticity perturbation with an equivalent barotropic structure appears a few latitude degrees north of the convection center.

最大比湿也显示对流层低层有明显的北移。

The maximum specific humidity also shows a clear northward shift in the lower troposphere.

提出了两种内部大气动力学机制来理解BSISO向北传播的原因。

Two internal atmospheric dynamics mechanisms are proposed to understand the cause of the northward propagation of the BSISO.

第一种是垂直切变机制。

The first is the vertical shear mechanism.

与该机制相关的关键过程是在存在平均流垂直切变的情况下，由于自由大气斜压模式和正压模式之间的耦合而产生正压涡度。

The key process associated with this mechanism is the generation of barotropic vorticity due to the coupling between the free-atmosphere baroclinic and barotropic modes in the presence of the vertical shear of the mean flow.

自由大气中诱发的正压涡度进一步引起行星边界层（PBL）中的水汽汇聚，导致对流加热北移。

The induced barotropic vorticity in the free atmosphere further causes a moisture convergence in the planetary boundary layer (PBL), leading to the northward shift of the convective heating.

第二个机制是水汽-对流反馈机制。

The second mechanism is the moisture–convection feedback mechanism.

两个过程导致低层水汽北移。

Two processes contribute to the northward shift of the low-level moisture.

一是PBL中偏南风的水汽平流。

One is the moisture advection by the mean southerly in the PBL.

另一个是由于平均经向比湿梯度而导致的 BSISO 风的水汽平流。

Another is the moisture advection by the BSISO wind due to the mean meridional specific humidity gradient.

比湿不对称导致对流加热北移。

The asymmetric specific humidity contributes to the northward shift of the convective heating.

构建了一个理论框架来研究向北传播的BSISO模式的不稳定性以及包括海气相互作用在内的各种机制的相对作用。

A theoretical framework is constructed to investigate the instability of the northward-propagating BSISO mode and the relative roles of various mechanisms including air–sea interactions.

特征值分析表明BSISO北传是季风区夏季平均气流的不稳定模态。

An eigenvalue analysis indicates that the northward propagation of the BSISO is an unstable mode of the summer mean flow in the monsoon region.

典型波长为2500公里。

It has a typical wavelength of 2500 km.

虽然东风切变主要对北纬 5°以北的向北传播做出贡献，但水汽反馈和海气相互作用也有显著贡献，特别是在赤道附近和以南地区。

While the easterly shear contributes to the northward propagation primarily north of 5°N, the moisture feedback and the air–sea interaction also contribute significantly, particularly in the region near and south of the equator.

内部大气动力学对于导致 BSISO 在热带印度洋上空向北传播至关重要。

The internal atmospheric dynamics are essential in causing the northward propagation of the BSISO over the tropical Indian Ocean.

1. Introduction

引言

季节内振荡 (ISO) 是热带大气中最重要的信号之一。

The intraseasonal oscillation (ISO) is one of the most significant signals in the tropical atmosphere.

Madden 和 Julian（1971）的开创性研究首先在坎顿岛的纬向风场中发现了周期为 40—50 天的季节内振荡信号。

The pioneering work by Madden and Julian (1971) first detected the ISO signal, with a period of 40–50 days, in the zonal wind field over Canton Island.

后来他们进一步根据压力场和风场记录了ISO的空间结构（Madden和Julian 1972），发现这种振荡是全球尺度的，主要特征是赤道向东传播，具有纬向波数1结构。

Later they further documented the spatial structure of the ISO based on pressure and wind fields (Madden and Julian 1972) and found that this oscillation is of global scale and is characterized primarily by equatorial eastward propagation with a zonal wavenumber-1 structure.

虽然强东传的季节内振荡（Madden–Julian 模态）主要见于北半球冬季，北半球夏季的季节内振荡则主要表现为印度洋区域的北传，以及西太平洋离赤道区域的西北传。

While the strong eastward propagating ISO (the Madden–Julian mode) is primarily observed in boreal winter, the ISO in boreal summer is dominated by northward propagation in the Indian sector and northwestward propagation off the equator in the western Pacific.

Yasunari（1979，1980）是最早记录南亚季风区季节内振荡相关云和对流由赤道带明显北移至约 30°N 的研究者之一。

Yasunari (1979, 1980) was among the first to document the marked northward movement of clouds and convection associated with the ISO from the equatorial zone to around 30°N over the South Asian monsoon region.

这种模态的变化与印度季风主要的活跃期和中断期循环有关。

The fluctuation of this mode is associated with major ‘‘active’’ and ‘‘break’’ cycles of the Indian monsoon.

Yasunari注意到北移是由赤道印度洋上空向东传播的云扰动引发的。

Yasunari noticed that the northward movement was initiated by eastward-propagating cloud disturbances developing over the equatorial Indian Ocean.

其他研究利用不同气象变量进一步记录了这种周期为 30—50 天的北传模态（如 Sikka and Gadgil 1980；Krishnamurti and Subrahmanyam 1982；Murakami et al. 1984；Lorenc 1984；Cadet 1986；Lau and Chan 1986；Wang and Rui 1990）。

This northward propagating mode with the 30–50-day period was further documented by other studies using various meteorological variables (e.g., Sikka and Gadgil 1980; Krishnamurti and Subrahmanyam 1982; Murakami et al. 1984; Lorenc 1984; Cadet 1986; Lau and Chan 1986; Wang and Rui 1990).

Sikka 和 Gadgil (1980) 指出，北半球夏季，印度经度 (70°–90°E) 上空有两个最大云区 (MCZ)，一个位于北纬 15°，另一个位于赤道附近。

Sikka and Gadgil (1980) pointed out that during boreal summer there are two maximum cloud zones (MCZ) over the Indian longitudes (70°–90°E), one being along 15°N and the other near the equator.

两个对流区表现出跷跷板特征，即赤道MCZ存在而另一个不存在，反之亦然。

The two convection zones exhibit a seesaw characteristic, that is, the equatorial MCZ is present while the other one is absent, or vice versa.

最有趣的是，季风MCZ的活跃/中断周期与赤道MCZ的北移密切相关。

Most interestingly, the active/break cycle of the monsoon MCZ is closely connected to the northward propagation of the equatorial MCZ.

Krishnamurti 和 Subrahmanyam（1982）指出，在印度次大陆区域，赤道附近生成的一系列槽脊稳定地向北传播，并在喜马拉雅山南麓附近消散。

Krishnamurti and Subrahmanyam (1982) noted a steady meridional propagation of a train of troughs and ridges that form near the equator and dissipate near the foot of Himalayas over the Indian subcontinent sector.

该模态的经向长度尺度约为3000公里，经向相速约为每天0.75°纬度。

The meridional length scale of this mode is about 3000 km, and its meridional phase speed is about 0.75° latitude per day.

Wang 和 Rui（1990）分析了 1975—1985 年的 10 年候平均 OLR 数据，将季节内振荡事件分为东传（65%）、独立北传（20%）和西传（15%）三类。

By analyzing 10-yr (1975–85) pentad mean OLR data, Wang and Rui (1990) classified the ISO events into three categories: eastward propagation (65%), independent northward propagation (20%), and westward propagation (15%).

受限于赤道附近的东传模态在北半球冬季比夏季更活跃。

The eastward-propagation mode, which is trapped near the equator, is more active in boreal winter than in summer.

与东传无关的独立北传发生在北半球夏季 5—10 月的印度和西太平洋季风区。

The independent northward propagation, which is not associated with the eastward propagation, is found over Indian and western Pacific monsoon regions in boreal summer from May to October.

对于向东传播模式，确定了一种特殊场景，其中向北传播与沿赤道向东传播相关。

For the eastward propagating mode, a special scenario is identified in which the northward propagation is associated with the eastward propagation along the equator.

北半球夏季印度洋和西太平洋地区的全部北传个例中，约半数为独立北传，其余约半数似乎与赤道沿线东传的对流相联系。

Among the total northward-propagating cases over the Indian and western Pacific regions during the boreal summer, about half of them are independent northward propagation cases, and the other half seem to be connected with eastward propagating convection along the equator.

上述观测研究表明，北移是亚洲季风区北半球夏季ISO（BSISO）最显著的特征之一。

The aforementioned observational studies indicate that the northward propagation is one of the most striking features of the boreal summer ISO (BSISO) in the Asian monsoon region.

一个悬而未决的问题是，是什么原因导致季风区BSISO对流向北传播。

An open question is what causes the northward propagation of the BSISO convection in the monsoon region.

Webster (1983) 假设进入大气行星边界层 (PBL) 的陆地表面热通量可能在对流之前破坏大气稳定，导致对流区向北移动。

Webster (1983) hypothesized that the land surface heat flux into the atmospheric planetary boundary layer (PBL) may play a role in destabilizing the atmosphere ahead of the convection, leading to a northward shift of the convective zone.

然而，观测表明最强的向北传播信号出现在印度洋北部（沿孟加拉湾经度），而不是陆地上空。

However, observations show that the strongest northward propagation signal appears over the northern Indian Ocean (along Bay of Bengal longitudes), not over the land.

Goswami 和 Shukla（1984）提出，对流—热力松弛反馈可能是赤道与季风最大云区之间产生驻波式振荡的关键。

Goswami and Shukla (1984) proposed that the convection–thermal relaxation feedback might be a key for generating a standing oscillation between the equatorial and monsoon MCZs.

在他们的假设中，对流活动会导致静态稳定性的增加，从而抑制对流本身；同时，动力和辐射弛豫降低了湿静态稳定性，并使大气进入新的对流不稳定状态。

In their hypothesis, convective activity results in an increase of static stability, which depresses convection itself; meanwhile, dynamical and radiative relaxation decreases moist static stability and brings the atmosphere to a new convectively unstable state.

然而，他们没有解决与 ISO 向北传播相关的机制。

However, they did not address the mechanism associated with the northward propagation of the ISO.

基于中间大气模型的模拟，Wang和Xie（1997）提出了罗斯贝波发射假设如下。

Based on simulations from an intermediate atmospheric model, Wang and Xie (1997) proposed a Rossby wave emanation hypothesis as follows.

当对流东移至赤道中太平洋时，平均海温和比湿降低，使赤道对流辐射出罗斯贝波，并形成一道“对流锋”。

When the convection moves eastward to the central equatorial Pacific, the reduction of the mean SST and specific humidity leads to the emanation of Rossby waves from the equatorial convection, which forms a “convection front.”

这道对流锋从赤道到 20°N 向西北倾斜，呈 V 形；随着整个波包东移，表现出表观的北移。

This convection front tilts northwestward from the equator to 20°N (a V shape), resulting in an apparent northward movement as the entire wave packet migrates eastward.

Lawrence 和 Webster（2002）的波数—频率分析图显示，相当一部分北传与东传分量相联系，从而支持了这一罗斯贝波辐射假说。

This Rossby wave emanation hypothesis was supported by Lawrence and Webster (2002), who showed that a large portion of the northward propagation is connected to the eastward moving component in their wave-number–frequency analysis maps.

然而，罗斯贝波发射假说并不能解释独立的向北传播的ISO。

However, this Rossby wave emanation hypothesis does not explain the independent northward propagating ISOs.

大气和海洋之间的相互作用是导致向北传播的另一个可能机制。

The interaction between the atmosphere and ocean is another possible mechanism contributing to the northward propagation.

这是因为表面潜热通量的减少（由于总风速的降低）可能会增加对流以北的海面温度（SST），导致 PBL 中的水汽汇聚（Kemball-Cook 和 Wang 2001）。

This is because reduced surface latent heat fluxes (due to a decrease in the total wind speed) may increase sea surface temperature (SST) to the north of the convection, leading to a moisture convergence in the PBL (Kemball-Cook and Wang 2001).

因此对流以北的大气不稳定，对流向北移动。

Thus the atmosphere north of the convection is destabilized, and the convection moves northward.

虽然观测数据证实了海表温度与对流相位关系，但尚不清楚海气相互作用是否在向北传播中发挥主动作用，或者海表温度只是对大气强迫的被动响应。

While observational data confirm the SST–convection phase relationship, it is not clear whether the air–sea interaction plays an active role in the northward propagation or the SST is simply a passive response to the atmospheric forcing.

这项研究的目标是揭示导致 BSISO 向北传播的基本大气动力学过程。

The goal of this study is to reveal essential atmospheric dynamical processes that give rise to the northward propagation of the BSISO.

具体要解决的科学问题如下。

The specific scientific questions to be addressed are as follows.

北传ISO模式下各气象变量之间的经向相位关系如何？

What is the meridional phase relationship among various meteorological variables for the northward propagating ISO mode?

这种经向相位关系由什么造成？

What causes the meridional phase relationship?

为什么南亚和西太平洋季风区有利于向北传播？

Why is the northward propagation favorable over the South Asian and western Pacific monsoon regions?

内部大气动力学和海气相互作用对于北移传播的相对作用是什么？

What is the relative role of the internal atmospheric dynamics and air–sea interaction in contributing to the northward propagation?

为了实现这一目标，我们依靠大气环流模型（AGCM）模拟（其中我们通过指定气候月平均海表温度排除海气相互作用的影响）和观测的分析。

To achieve this goal, we rely on the analysis from both atmospheric general circulation model (AGCM) simulations (in which we exclude the effect of air–sea interactions by specifying climatological monthly mean SST) and observations.

在第 2 节中，给出了模型和观测数据的简要描述。

In section 2, a brief description of the model and the observational data is given.

在第3节中，北移ISO模态的时空结构是基于AGCM模拟和观测构建的。

In section 3, the temporal and spatial structures of the northward moving ISO mode are constructed based on both the AGCM simulations and the observations.

对 BSISO 结构的分析导致发现了两种新机制，这将在第 4 节中讨论。

The analysis of the BSISO structures leads to the discovery of two new mechanisms, which are discussed in section 4.

在第5节中，我们进一步提出了一个简单的理论框架，以探索向北传播的BSISO模式的不稳定性本质，并了解包括海气相互作用在内的各种机制在促进向北传播中的相对作用。

In section 5, we further advance a simple theoretical framework to explore the instability nature of the northward propagating BSISO mode and to understand the relative role of various mechanisms including the air–sea interaction in contributing to the northward propagation.

最后，最后一节给出了总结。

Finally, a summary is given in the last section.

2. Model and data

模式与数据

本研究中使用的模型是 ECHAM AGCM 4.0 版（Roeckner 等人，1996），分辨率为 T30 和 19 个垂直级别。

The model used in this study is the ECHAM AGCM version 4.0 (Roeckner et al. 1996) at a resolution of T30 and 19 vertical levels.

本 AGCM 中使用的对流方案是由 Nordeng (1994) 修改的穿透、浅层和中层对流的质量通量方案 (Tiedtke 1989)，以便将云底质量通量与穿透对流的对流不稳定性联系起来。

The convection scheme used in this AGCM is the mass flux scheme for penetrative, shallow, and midlevel convection (Tiedtke 1989) modified by Nordeng (1994) so that the cloud-base mass flux is linked to convective instability for the penetrative convection.

该模型积分15年，下边界条件由气候月平均SST给出。

This model is integrated for 15 years, with the lower boundary condition given by the climatological monthly mean SST.

输出日平均场用于分析。

Daily averaged fields are written out for analysis.

为了检查模型模拟的可靠性，我们分析了国家环境预测中心-国家大气研究中心（NCEP-NCAR）再分析数据集进行比较。

To check the reliability of the model simulation, we analyze the National Centers for Environmental Prediction–National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCEP–NCAR) reanalysis dataset for comparison.

同样，采用1980年至2001年期间的日平均数据，再分析数据的水平分辨率为2.5°纬度×2.5°经度，垂直方向有12个压力级别。

Again, daily averaged data are used for a period from 1980 to 2001, and the horizontal resolution of the reanalysis data is 2.5° latitude × 2.5° longitude and vertically there are 12 pressure levels.

三维变量包括纬向风、经向风、气压坐标垂直速度以及比湿。

The 3D variables include the zonal and meridional wind components, vertical p velocity, and specific humidity.

采用美国国家海洋和大气管理局的日平均向外长波辐射（OLR）数据作为对流的代理指标。

The daily averaged outgoing longwave radiation (OLR) data from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration is employed as the proxy for convection.

为了将模型降水与观测数据进行比较，使用了气候预测中心 (CPC) 降水数据合并分析 (CMAP；Xie 和 Arkin 1997)。

To compare the model precipitation with the observed, the Climate Prediction Center (CPC) Merged Analysis of Precipitation data (CMAP; Xie and Arkin 1997) are used.

为分离季节内振荡信号，采用带通滤波。

To isolate the ISO signal, a bandpass filtering method is applied.

对于日数据，每年提取4到24之间的谐波，对应15-90天的周期。

For daily data, harmonics between 4 and 24 are extracted each year, which corresponds to the period of 15–90 days.

对模式输出和 NCEP–NCAR 再分析中的每个气象场均应用这种时间滤波。

This time filtering is applied to each meteorological field for both the model output and the NCEP–NCAR reanalysis.

3. BSISO structures as revealed from the AGCM and reanalysis data

BSISO 的结构

在揭示 ISO 结构之前，我们首先检查北半球夏季平均季风模拟中的模型性能。

Before revealing the ISO structure, we first examine the model performance in the mean monsoon simulation in boreal summer.

图1为观测和模拟的气候夏季平均降水量和风场。

Figure 1 shows the observed and simulated climatological summer mean precipitation and wind fields.

模式捕捉到了夏季降水的总体分布，包括菲律宾和南海附近以及赤道印度洋的降水中心。

The model captures the gross pattern of the summer rainfall such as the rainfall centers around Philippine and the South China Sea and over the equatorial Indian Ocean.

然而，该模型低估了孟加拉湾的降雨量，但高估了赤道印度洋的降雨量。

The model, however, underestimates the rainfall amount over the Bay of Bengal but overestimates it over the equatorial Indian Ocean.

南亚季风区北半球夏季最显著的环流特征是亚洲大陆上空的巨大反气旋环流和对流层上层盛行东风，以及对流层下层跨赤道索马里急流和西风带。

The most notable circulation features in boreal summer over the south Asian monsoon region are the gigantic anticyclonic circulation over the Asian continent and prevailing easterlies in the upper troposphere and the cross-equatorial Somali jet and westerlies in the lower troposphere.

ECHAM AGCM 很好地再现了这些特征。

These features are well reproduced by the ECHAM AGCM.

正如稍后将讨论的，这种明显的平均流垂直切变对于 BSISO 模式的放大和向北传播起着重要作用。

As will be discussed later, this pronounced vertical shear of the mean flow plays an important role for the amplification and northward propagation of the BSISO mode.

BSISO 模式的经向相位传播可以通过 15 年积分期间沿 70°–95°E 的降雨量的 Hovmöller 图很容易地识别（图 2）。

The meridional phase propagation of the BSISO mode may be readily identified by a Hovmöller diagram of the rainfall along 70°–95°E during a 15-yr integration period (Fig. 2).

请注意，向北传播在每年的北半球夏季最为明显。

Note that the northward propagation is most pronounced during the boreal summer each year.

传播也表现出很强的年际变化。

The propagation also exhibits strong interannual variability.

某些年份出现较多或较强的向北传播事件，而另一些年份出现较少或较弱的向北传播事件。

More or stronger northward propagation events appear in some years while less or weaker events appear in other years.

由于模式指定了气候态月平均海温，这种年际变化源自大气内部动力过程。

Interannual variability results from internal atmospheric dynamics as the climatological monthly mean SST are specified.

北半球冬季，降水趋于向南传播。

During the boreal winter, the rainfall tends to move southward.

向北和向南传播都是从赤道地区开始的。

Both the northward and southward propagations start from the equatorial region.

为揭示 BSISO 的经向和垂直结构，我们选取 23 个强北传个例进行合成分析。

To reveal the meridional and vertical structure of the BSISO, we take a composite approach by selecting 23 strong northward propagation cases.

构建合成时规定，第 0 天季节内振荡对流沿 70°—95°E 恰好到达 5°N。

6. Summary and discussion

总结与讨论

本研究基于ECHAM模拟和NCEP-NCAR再分析，揭示了向北传播的BSISO模式的时空结构。

In this study, the spatial and temporal structure of the northward propagating BSISO mode is revealed based on the analysis from the ECHAM simulation and the NCEP–NCAR reanalysis.

AGCM 输出与 NCEP-NCAR 再分析的比较表明，ECHAM AGCM 模拟的 BSISO 演化特征与再分析数据得出的演化特征非常相似。

The comparison of the AGCM output with the NCEP–NCAR reanalysis shows that the evolution features of the BSISO simulated by the ECHAM AGCM bears close similarities with those derived from the reanalysis data.

我们的综合分析表明，与BSISO模式相关的对流在西赤道印度洋发起后，沿着赤道向东传播。

Our composite analysis reveals that after being initiated in the western equatorial Indian Ocean, the convection associated with the BSISO mode propagates eastward along the equator.

赤道东印度洋上空90°E左右，对流开始北移至北印度洋。

Over the eastern equatorial Indian Ocean around 90°E, the convection begins to move northward to the northern Indian Ocean.

大气环流模式模拟和 NCEP–NCAR 再分析都显示，5°N 至 20°N 的北传相速约为 1 m s⁻¹，而赤道附近及其以南较慢。

Both the AGCM simulation and the NCEP–NCAR reanalysis show a northward phase speed of about 1 m s⁻¹ between 5°N and 20°N and a slower phase speed near and south of the equator.

合成分析主要捕捉独立的向北传播事件，印度洋向北传播与赤道西太平洋向东传播之间没有明显的联系。

The composite analysis mainly captures the independent northward propagation events, as there is no obvious connection between the northward propagation in the Indian Ocean and the eastward propagation in the equatorial western Pacific.

对向北传播的BSISO模态结构的诊断表明，涡度场和水汽场相对于对流中心存在显著的经向不对称性。

The diagnosis of the northward propagating BSISO mode structure unveils that there are significant meridional asymmetries, with respect to the convection center, in the vorticity and moisture fields.

具有等效正压结构的正涡度中心位于对流中心以北几度处。

A positive vorticity center with an equivalent barotropic structure is located a few degrees north of the convection center.

扰动比湿的极大值位于对流层低层，其相位也倾向于在对流以北约几个纬度。

The perturbation specific humidity, which has a maximum center in the lower troposphere, also tends to lead the convection by a couple of degrees.

提出了一个简单的理论模型来理解向北传播的起源。

A simple theoretical model is advanced to understand the origin of the northward propagation.

基于BSISO结构，我们提出了两种内部大气动力学机制：

Based on the BSISO structure, we propose two internal atmospheric dynamics mechanisms:

1）垂直切变机制。

1) The vertical shear mechanism.

季风区夏季平均气流具有较强的东风切变特征。

The summer mean flow in the monsoon region is characterized by a strong easterly shear.

这种垂直切变将自由大气中的斜压模式和正压模式耦合在一起，导致对流北部产生正压涡度和发散。

This vertical shear couples the baroclinic and barotropic modes in the free atmosphere and leads to the generation of barotropic vorticity and divergence to the north of the convection.

后者进一步使边界层水汽辐合北移，进而使季节内振荡对流北移。

The latter further leads to the northward shift of the moisture convergence in the PBL and thus the ISO convection.

不过，这种机制仅在北半球远离赤道的地方有效。

However, this mechanism is effective only away from the equator in the Northern Hemisphere.

2) 水汽-对流反馈机制。

2) The moisture–convection feedback mechanism.

有两个过程导致对流层低层比湿北移。

Two processes contribute to the northward shift of the specific humidity in the lower troposphere.

它们是：

They are

1) PBL 中平均偏南风的水汽平流和

1) the moisture advection by the mean southerly in the PBL and

2）扰动风对平均比湿梯度的平流。

2) the advection of the mean specific humidity gradient by the perturbation wind.

水汽分布不对称进一步导致对流加热北移，导致BSISO模态北移。

The asymmetric moisture distribution further leads to the northward shift of the convective heating and as a result the BSISO mode moves northward.

特征值分析表明，向北传播的BSISO模态是季风区夏季平均气流的不稳定模态。

Our eigenvalue analysis indicates that the northward propagating BSISO mode is an unstable mode of the summer mean flow in the monsoon region.

这解释了该地区频繁出现BSISO对流向北传播的情况。

This explains the frequent appearance of the northward propagation of the BSISO convection in the region.

分析结果还指出了各个过程对于北传贡献的相对重要性。

The analysis result also points out the relative importance of various processes in contributing to the northward propagation.

虽然垂直切变在北半球远离赤道的地区发挥着重要作用，但水汽-对流反馈和海气相互作用也发挥着重要作用，特别是在赤道附近和以南地区。

While the vertical shear plays an important role away from the equator in the Northern Hemisphere, the moisture–convection feedback and air–sea interaction also contribute significantly, particularly in the region near and south of the equator.

总体而言，鉴于模型和观测中BSISO的相似结构和传播特征，我们得出结论，内部大气动力学对于热带印度洋上空向北传播至关重要。

Overall, given the similar structure and propagation characteristics of the BSISO in the model and the observation, we conclude that it is the internal atmospheric dynamics that are essential to give rise to the northward propagation over the tropical Indian Ocean.

与观测中的 BSISO 结构相比，模式的一个差别在于低层辐合的相位。

A discrepancy of the model compared to the observed BSISO structure is the phase of the low-level convergence.

根据NCEP-NCAR再分析数据，低层辐合似乎领先对流2-3°，低层垂直运动轴向北倾斜。

With the NCEP–NCAR reanalysis data, the low-level convergence appears to lead the convection by 2–3° with the vertical motion axis tilting to the north at the lower level.

这种向北的移动很可能是由于 PBL 顶部的正涡度强迫导致的埃克曼抽吸引起的。

It is likely that this northward shift is caused by the Ekman pumping due to the positive vorticity forcing at top of the PBL.

但模式中没有这一北移，说明 ECHAM 的边界层动力过程仍需改进。

However, this shift does not appear in the model, pointing out the need for improvement of the PBL dynamics in the ECHAM.

我们的观测和AGCM分析表明，强烈的向北传播的BSISO并不依赖于赤道东移分量。

Our observational and AGCM analyses indicate that strong northward propagating BSISOs do not depend on the equatorial eastward moving component.

因此，这里提出的机制与 Wang 和 Xie（1997）以及 Lawrence 和 Webster（2002）提出的 Rossby 波发射机制有根本的不同。

Thus, the mechanisms proposed here are fundamentally different from the Rossby wave emanation mechanism proposed by Wang and Xie (1997) and Lawrence and Webster (2002).

两者都可能反映了真实现象的两个极端。

Both may reflect two extremes of the real phenomenon.

当前研究发现的强向北传播情况可能主要反映了独立向北传播情景，而较弱向北传播情况则与赤道东移ISO相关的罗斯贝波发射情景有关。

It is likely that the strong northward propagation cases picked up by the current study primarily reflect the independent northward propagation scenario, while the weaker northward propagation cases are linked to the Rossby wave emanation scenario in association with equatorial eastward moving ISOs.

所提出的机制可能也与北半球冬季南半球的向南传播有关（见图2）。

The proposed mechanisms might be also relevant to the southward propagation in the Southern Hemisphere in boreal winter (see Fig. 2).

我们将在未来的工作中进一步研究这些场景。

We will further examine these scenarios in future work.
