摘要

Abstract

准确预估青藏高原（TP）未来增暖的特征，对区域生态系统稳定和社会经济发展，以及理解青藏高原在调节亚洲季风和更大尺度气候格局中的作用，都至关重要。

Accurate projections of future warming characteristics in the Tibetan Plateau (TP) are essential for regional ecosystem stability and socioeconomic development and for understanding its role in modulating the Asian Monsoon and broader-scale climate patterns.

本研究将基于观测的物理约束——土壤湿度异常与海表温度异常对青藏高原夏季近地面气温（SAT）的协同影响——与近地面气温时空变化的评估相结合，对 CMIP6 模式进行评估并筛选出最优模式。

This study evaluated and selected optimal CMIP6 models by combining an observation-based physical constraint—the synergistic influence of soil moisture anomalies and sea surface temperature anomalies on summer TP surface air temperature (SAT)—with an assessment of SAT spatiotemporal variations.

通过比较四种多模式集合方法，发现随机森林（RF）方法能够最有效地减小历史模拟中的时间和空间偏差，从而为青藏高原夏季近地面气温的未来预估提供更可靠的基础。

Four multi-model ensemble methods were compared, and the random forest (RF) method was found to most effectively reduce temporal and spatial biases in historical simulations, thus providing a more reliable basis for future projections of summer SAT over the TP.

基于10个最优模式的随机森林预估显示，在 SSP1-2.6 情景下，青藏高原在未来近期（2015–2044年）呈显著增暖趋势（0.38°C/10a），但在未来中远期（2045–2100年）增暖趋于停滞（0.03°C/10）。

Based on 10 optimal models, the RF-based projections show that SSP1-2.6 scenario, the TP exhibits significant warming trend (0.38°C/10a) in early-term future (2015–2044) but stagnates (0.03°C/10) during the mid- and long-term future (2045–2100).

在 SSP2-4.5 情景下，青藏高原近地面气温在2015–2070年期间呈显著增暖趋势（0.33°C/10a），随后减缓至0.14°C/10a。

Under the SSP2-4.5 scenario, the TP SAT shows a significant warming trend (0.33°C/10a) during 2015–2070, then slows to 0.14°C/10a.

在 SSP5-8.5 情景下，青藏高原近地面气温在整个2015–2100年期间持续快速增暖（0.60°C/10a）。

Under the SSP5-8.5 scenario, the TP SAT maintains rapid warming (0.60°C/10a) throughout 2015–2100.

与未经优化筛选的18个模式的传统多模式集合平均（MME，即 MME-18）相比，基于随机森林的预估表明未来增暖更强，尤其是在青藏高原西部。

The RF-based projections indicate stronger future warming (particularly in western TP) compared to the conventional multi-model ensemble mean (MME) of non-optimized 18 models (MME-18).

这些结果凸显了未进行适当模式优化时低估青藏高原增暖的风险，表明这一脆弱区域值得特别关注。

These results highlight the risk of underestimating TP warming without proper model optimization, warranting particular attention on this vulnerable region.

1. 引言

1. Introduction

作为对全球气候变化高度敏感的区域，青藏高原（TP）正经历加速增暖1。

As a region highly sensitive to global climate change, the Tibetan Plateau (TP) is experiencing accelerated warming1.

1979–2020年期间，青藏高原年平均气温以每十年0.34°C的速率升高，约为全球增暖速率的两倍2。

During the period 1979–2020, the mean annual temperature over the TP increased at 0.34°C per decade, approximately twice the global warming rate2.

快速增暖对当地生态系统、水资源、农业和基础设施产生显著影响。

The rapid warming significantly impacts local ecosystem, water resources, agriculture, and infrastructure.

此外，青藏高原气候异常在调节亚洲季风和更大尺度气候格局方面发挥关键作用2–10。

Additionally, the TP climate anomalies play a crucial role in modulating the Asian Monsoon and broader-scale climate patterns2–10.

因此，研究青藏高原未来气候变化，对于当地和更大范围的气候预估与适应都至关重要。

Therefore, studying future TP climate change is critical for both the local and broader climate projection and adaptation.

气候模式已被广泛用于模拟历史气候变率和预估气候变率11–15。

Climate models have been widely applied in simulating historical and projected climate variability11–15.

基于耦合模式比较计划（CMIP）的模拟，已有大量研究评估并预估了青藏高原的气候变化16–20。

Based on Coupled Model Intercomparison Project (CMIP) simulations, numerous studies have evaluated and projected climate change over the TP16–20.

多数研究结果表明，青藏高原的增暖趋势将随排放增加而增强19,20。

Most findings indicated that the warming trend of the TP will intensify with increasing emissions19,20.

然而，由于复杂地形放大了模式误差，青藏高原的模拟及未来预估仍存在显著不确定性21,22。

However, significant uncertainties exist in TP simulations and future projections due to amplified model errors caused by complex terrain 21,22.

早期 CMIP 模拟在青藏高原存在冷偏差16,23,24。

Earlier CMIP simulations exhibited cold biases over the TP16,23,24.

尽管 CMIP6 已取得显著进展，能够更真实地再现近地面气温（SAT）的空间分布25，但许多模式在青藏高原仍存在冷偏差26–29。

Although CMIP6 has shown remarkable progress and can more realistically capture the spatial distribution of surface air temperatures (SATs)25, many models still exhibit cold biases in the TP 26–29.

因此，评估 CMIP6 模式的表现，对于可靠预估青藏高原气候至关重要。

Therefore, evaluating CMIP6 model performance is essential for reliable TP climate projections.

目前，青藏高原近地面气温的预估主要依赖多模式集合平均（MME），其对增暖幅度的估计存在相当大的不确定性16,17,19,20,30。

Current projections of SAT over the TP predominantly rely on multi-model ensemble means (MME), which show considerable uncertainties in estimated warming magnitudes16,17,19,20,30.

观测约束方法（例如多模式加权、基于归因的约束和涌现约束）能够有效降低预估不确定性31。

Observational constraint methods (e.g., multi-model weighting, attribution-based constraint, emergent constraint) can effectively reduce projection uncertainty31.

然而，很少有研究采用此类观测约束方法来改进青藏高原的气候预估1,32,33。

However, few studies have employed such observational constraint methods to improve climate projections for the TP 1,32,33.

Zhou 和 Zhang1表明，采用历史归因结果作为约束后，预估的青藏高原增暖幅度显著高于 CMIP5 多模式集合平均的估计，揭示出后者系统性低估了青藏高原气温对人为强迫的响应。

Zhou and Zhang1 demonstrated that using historical attribution results as constraints leads to the projected TP warming magnitude significantly exceeding the CMIP5 MME estimates, revealing that the latter systematically underestimated the TP’s temperature response to anthropogenic forcing.

理想的模式应准确再现观测到的物理关系，这有助于降低预估不确定性34,35。

An ideal model should accurately reproduce observed physical relationships, which can help reduce projection uncertainties 34,35.

这些关系有助于降低气候模式预测和预估中的不确定性36。

These relationships can help reduce uncertainties in predictions and projections of climate models36.

例如，利用观测到的降水—气温关系识别能够正确模拟陆气反馈过程的模式，可有效降低极端气温预估中的不确定性37,38。

For instance, using the observed precipitation-temperature relationship to identify models that correctly simulate land-atmosphere feedback processes, the uncertainties in extreme temperature projections can be effectively reduced 37,38.

热带地区的气温—湿度关系可作为评估模式表现的观测约束39。

The tropical temperature-humidity relationship can serve as an observational constraint for evaluating model performance39.

在模式筛选中，将观测到的关系用作基于物理机制的约束，可有效降低预估不确定性。

Using observed relationships as physics-based constraints for model selection can effectively reduce projection uncertainty.

近期研究发现，东欧—西西伯利亚的土壤湿度异常与黄海—日本海的海表温度异常（SSTA）共同影响青藏高原东部的夏季近地面气温40，从而提供了一种潜在的基于物理机制的约束。

Recently, the combined influence of East European-West Siberian soil moisture anomalies and Yellow-Japan Sea SSTAs was found to regulate summer SATs over the eastern TP40, offering a potential physics-based constraint.

这也引出了关键问题：CMIP6 模式能否准确再现土壤湿度异常与海表温度异常对整个青藏高原夏季近地面气温的共同调节作用？

This also raises critical questions: Can CMIP6 models accurately reproduce the combined modulation of the soil moisture anomalies and SSTAs on summer SATs across the entire TP?

这些基于物理机制的观测约束能否提高青藏高原近地面气温预估的准确性？

Can these physics-based observational constraints improve the accuracy of TP SAT projections?

为提高青藏高原夏季近地面气温预估的可靠性，这些问题值得进一步研究。

These questions warrant investigation to improve the reliability of summer SAT projections over the TP.

2. 数据与方法

2. Data and Methods

2.1 数据

2.1 Data

本研究使用从国家气象信息中心获得的 CN05.1 月平均近地面气温数据集（空间分辨率为0.25°×0.25°），评估 CMIP6 模式的表现41。

This study used the CN05.1 monthly SAT dataset (0.25°×0.25°spatial resolution) obtained from the National Meteorological Information Center to assess the performance of CMIP6 models41.

该数据集已被广泛应用于气候变化研究和模式评估42–45。

This dataset has been extensively applied in climate change studies and model evaluations42–45.

此外，青藏高原区域（73°–105°E、26°–40°N，海拔超过2,000 m）的区域平均夏季近地面气温，与青藏高原89个观测站的夏季近地面气温平均值吻合良好；在1961–2014年期间，两者的相关系数为0.95，证实了该数据集的可靠性。

Moreover, the regionally averaged summer SAT over the TP region (73°–105°E, 26°–40°N, above 2,000 m elevation) shows good agreement with the summer TP SAT average from 89 observational stations in the TP, with a correlation coefficient of 0.95 for the period 1961–2014, confirming its reliability.

本研究还使用了美国国家环境预报中心/美国国家大气研究中心（NCEP/NCAR）提供的位势高度数据（分辨率为2.5°×2.5°）、ERA-land 月尺度土壤湿度数据（0–7 cm土层；分辨率为0.1°×0.1°）46，以及美国国家海洋和大气管理局（NOAA）提供的扩展重建海表温度数据集（第5版；分辨率为2.0°×2.0°）47。

This study also used geopotential heights at 2.5°×2.5° resolution from the National Centers for Environmental Prediction/National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCEP/NCAR), the ERA-land monthly soil moisture (0–7 cm layer; 0.1°×0.1° resolution)46, and the extended reconstructed SST dataset (Version 5; 2.0°×2.0° resolution) obtained from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) 47.

所有数据均提取自1961至2014年。

All data were extracted from 1961 to 2014.

本研究采用了18个具备地形数据的 CMIP6 模式的模拟数据（模式清单见图1a）。

This study employed model-simulated data from 18 CMIP6 models with topographic data (see the list in Fig. 1a).

利用大气温度递减率，对近地面气温数据进行地形订正，使其匹配 CN05.1 网格的地形45。

The SAT data were topographically adjusted to match the CN05.1 grid topography using atmospheric lapse rates45.

我们使用了历史模拟（1961–2014年），以及三种共享社会经济路径（SSP）情景（SSP1-2.6、SSP2-4.5和SSP5-8.5）下的未来预估（2015–2100年）48,49。

We used historical simulations (1961–2014) and future projections (2015–2100) under three Shared Socioeconomic Pathway (SSP) scenarios (SSP1-2.6, SSP2-4.5, and SSP5-8.5) 48,49.

本研究使用的所有 CMIP6 模式输出均来自第一次集合运行（r1i1p1f1），以确保初始条件、物理参数化和边界条件的一致性。

All CMIP6 model outputs used in this study represent the first ensemble run (r1i1p1f1) to ensure consistent initial conditions, physical parameterizations, and boundary conditions.

将1995–2014年指定为基准期，用于未来气候变化的比较分析。

The period 1995–2014 was designated as the baseline period for comparative analysis of future climate change.

除直接评估青藏高原夏季近地面气温外，我们还将土壤湿度异常和海表温度异常的共同作用作为观测约束，评估模式的表现。

Besides direct evaluation of summer TP SATs, we used the combined effect of the soil moisture anomalies and SSTAs as observational constraints to evaluate the performance of models.

因此，我们使用了多种模式输出变量（位势高度、海表温度和表层土壤湿度）。

As such, we used various model output variables (geopotential heights, SSTs, and surface soil moistures).

所有模式变量均通过双线性插值，使其与观测数据集的分辨率相匹配。

All model variables were bilinearly interpolated to match observational dataset resolutions.

2.2. 方法

2.2. Methods

使用 Taylor 图和 Taylor 技巧评分（TSS），定量评估模拟与观测之间的一致性28,50,51。

The Taylor diagram and Taylor skill score (TSS) were used to quantitatively assess the consistency between simulations and observations28,50,51.

年际变率技巧评分（IVS）用于评估模式再现青藏高原夏季近地面气温年际变率的能力52,53。

The interannual variability skill score (IVS) is used to evaluate the ability of models to reproduce the interannual variability of the summer TP SAT 52,53.

我们评估了四种多模式集合方法：传统等权重多模式集合平均（MME）、基于独立性和性能的气候模式加权（ClimWIP）51,54–57、线性回归（LR）51和随机森林（RF）58。

We evaluated four multi-model ensemble approaches: the conventional equal-weight MME, Climate model Weighting by Independence and Performance (ClimWIP)51,54–57, Linear Regression (LR)51, and Random Forest (RF) 58.

为减轻过拟合并评估泛化能力，我们以1961–1994年为训练期、1995–2014年为验证期训练和验证集合模型，并利用均方根误差（RMSE）和决定系数（R2评分）评估其稳健性与表现。

To mitigate overfitting and evaluate generalizability, we train the ensemble models on the period 1961–1994 and validate them on the period 1995–2014, with their robustness and performance assessed using the root mean square error (RMSE) and coefficient of determination (R2 score).

有关上述评估方法和多模式集合方法的详细信息，请参阅补充材料。

For detailed information on the above evaluation and multi-model ensemble methods, please refer to the supplementary material.

3. CMIP6 模式评估

3. Evaluation of CMIP6 models

3.1 青藏高原夏季近地面气温的模拟表现

3.1 Simulation performance for summer SATs over the TP

Taylor 图评估（图1a）表明，全部18个模式均能较合理地模拟青藏高原夏季近地面气温的气候态空间分布。

Taylor diagram evaluation (Fig. 1a) shows that all 18 models reasonably simulate the climatological spatial distribution of summer SATs over the TP.

大多数模式模拟的近地面气温分布与观测分布高度一致，相关系数超过0.80。

The simulated SAT distributions in most models show high consistency with the observed distribution, with correlation coefficients exceeding 0.80.

NorESM2-LM、NorESM2-MM、CESM2-WACCM、ACCESS-CM2和TaiESM1在模拟近地面气温空间分布方面表现出色（相关系数>0.95，且TSS>0.89）。

NorESM2-LM, NorESM2-MM, CESM2-WACCM, ACCESS-CM2, and TaiESM1 demonstrated superior performance in simulating the spatial distribution of SATs (correlation coefficients > 0.95 and TSS > 0.89).

相比之下，CanESM5和CanESM5-1的空间模拟技巧相对较差，其空间相关系数较低，TSS评分低于0.40。

In contrast, CanESM5 and CanESM5-1 exhibited relatively poor spatial simulation skills, with lower spatial correlation coefficients and TSS scores below 0.40.

IVS评估（图1b）表明，大多数模式在再现年际变率方面具有较高技巧（IVS<0.20）。

The IVS evaluation (Fig. 1b) shows that most models demonstrated high skills in reproducing the interannual variability (IVS < 0.20).

然而，CanESM5和CanESM5-1的表现明显较弱（IVS>6.0）（图1b），这与它们较差的空间模拟技巧（图1a）一致。

However, CanESM5 and CanESM5-1 exhibited considerably weaker performance (IVS > 6.0) (Fig. 1b), consistent with their poor spatial simulation skills (Fig. 1a).

依据与观测到的青藏高原近地面气温空间型的高度相似性（TSS≥0.8），以及对其年际变化的较强模拟能力（IVS≤0.2），我们筛选出12个最优模式：ACCESS-CM2、ACCESS-ESM1-5、AWI-CM-1-1-MR、BCC-CSM2-MR、CESM2-WACCM、CMCC-CM2-SR5、CMCC-ESM2、MPI-ESM1-2-HR、MRI-ESM2-0、NorESM2-LM、NorESM2-MM和TaiESM1。

Based on high spatial similarity with the observed pattern of TP SAT (TSS ≥ 0.8) and strong simulation capability for the interannual variation of TP SAT (IVS ≤ 0.2), we identified 12 optimal models: ACCESS-CM2, ACCESS-ESM1-5, AWI-CM-1-1-MR, BCC-CSM2-MR, CESM2-WACCM, CMCC-CM2-SR5, CMCC-ESM2, MPI-ESM1-2-HR, MRI-ESM2-0, NorESM2-LM, NorESM2-MM, and TaiESM1.

3.2 利用基于物理机制的观测约束进行评估

3.2 Evaluation by using physics-based observational constraints

东欧平原—西西伯利亚平原的土壤湿度异常与黄海—日本海的海表温度异常，共同调节青藏高原东部夏季近地面气温的多尺度变化40。

The soil moisture anomalies in the East European Plain-West Siberian Plain and SSTAs in the Yellow Sea-Japan Sea jointly modulate multiscale variations of summer SATs over the eastern TP40.

具体而言，东欧平原—西西伯利亚平原的土壤湿度异常，通过局地陆气相互作用调节其上方对流层上层的位势高度，激发向下游传播的 Rossby 波列。

Specifically, soil moisture anomalies in the East European Plain-West Siberian Plain modulate the overlying upper-tropospheric geopotential heights via local land-air interactions, triggering a downstream Rossby wave train.

该波列产生一个从青藏高原东部延伸至黄海和日本海的高压异常，反映出南亚高压增强并向东伸展。

This wave train generates a high-pressure anomaly extending from the eastern Tibetan Plateau to the Yellow and Japan Seas, which reflects an intensified and eastward-extended South Asian High.

海表温度异常通过局地热力强迫加强这一高压异常，最终导致青藏高原东部夏季近地面气温异常。

The SSTAs reinforce this high-pressure anomaly through local thermal forcing and ultimately cause summer SAT anomalies over the eastern TP.

简而言之，这些土壤湿度异常和海表温度异常的共同作用，导致青藏高原东部夏季近地面气温异常40。

In short, the combined effect of these soil moisture and SST anomalies leads to summer SAT anomalies over the eastern TP40.

由于本研究关注整个青藏高原夏季近地面气温的评估与预估，我们计算了1961–2014年青藏高原区域平均近地面气温时间序列，并据此对200 hPa位势高度（图2a）、土壤湿度（图2b）和海表温度异常（图2c）进行回归分析。

Since this study focuses on the evaluation and projection of summer SATs over the entire TP, we calculated the TP regionally averaged SAT time series for the period 1961–2014 and used it to perform regression analyses on 200 hPa geopotential heights (Fig. 2a), soil moistures (Fig. 2b), and SSTAs (Fig. 2c).

A similar west-cooling-east-warming pattern appears under SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5 (Fig. 9m–q), except during the long-term future under SSP5-8.5 (Fig. 9r).

与MME-18相比，基于RF的预估显示出更强的增暖，尤其是在青藏高原西部，从而使整个青藏高原的增暖在空间上更加均匀（图9d–i）。

Compared to the MME-18, the RF-based projections demonstrate stronger warming, particularly in western TP, resulting in more spatially homogeneous warming throughout the entire TP (Fig. 9d–i).

这些发现表明，基于RF的预估主要减小了青藏高原西部的冷偏差。

These findings indicate that the RF-based projections primarily reduce the cold bias in western TP.

我们进一步考察了青藏高原西部（85°E以西）的增暖幅度，发现与MME-18相比，基于RF的预估显示出明显更强的增暖（图10）。

We further investigate the warming magnitude in the western TP (west of 85° E) and find that the RF-based projections show a much more dramatic warming compared to the MME-18 (Fig. 10).

在SSP1-2.6情景下，基于RF的预估中，青藏高原西部近地面气温相对于1995–2014年基准期持续呈正异常，2045–2100年的平均增暖达到1.1°C（图10a）。

Under SSP1-2.6, the western TP SAT in the RF-based projections shows persistent positive anomalies relative to the 1995–2014 baseline, with mean warming reaching 1.1°C during 2045–2100 (Fig. 10a).

相比之下，基于MME-18的预估几乎始终低于这一基准（图10a中的蓝线）。

In contrast, the MME-18-based projections are almost always lower than this baseline (blue line in Fig. 10a).

在SSP2-4.5情景下，基于RF的预估也显示持续增暖（2071–2100年平均为1.66°C）（图10b）。

Under SSP2-4.5, the RF-based projections also show persistent warming (mean 1.66°C during 2071–2100) (Fig. 10b).

基于MME-18的预估直到21世纪40年代末仍低于基准，2071–2100年的平均增暖仅为较小的0.94°C（图10b）。

The MME-18-based projections remain below the baseline until the late 2040s, showing only modest mean warming of 0.94°C in 2071–2100 (Fig. 10b).

在SSP2-8.5情景下，基于MME-18的预估直到21世纪40年代初才超过观测基准（图10c中的蓝线），而基于RF的预估呈现更快的增暖趋势（图10c）。

Under SSP2-8.5, the MME-18-based projections do not exceed the observed baseline until the early 2040s (blue line in Fig. 10c), while the RF-based projections exhibit a more rapid warming trend (Fig. 10c).

鉴于青藏高原西部已经经历了显著增暖，在全球增暖背景下该地区未来出现降温似乎不太可能。

Given that western TP has already experienced significant warming, future cooling under global warming seems unlikely.

因此，基于RF的预估更为可靠。

Therefore, the RF-based projections are more reliable.

这些结果提示，青藏高原西部未来的增暖可能比基于MME-18的预估更为严重。

These results suggest that future warming in the western TP may be more severe than the MME-18-based projections.

5. 结论与讨论

5. Conclusions and discussion

准确预估青藏高原未来的增暖，对于当地生态和社会经济的可持续性至关重要。

Accurately projecting future TP warming is crucial for local ecological and socioeconomic sustainability.

本研究将对青藏高原近地面气温变化的直接评估，与由土壤湿度异常和海表温度异常共同影响青藏高原夏季近地面气温而建立的基于物理机制的观测约束相结合，评估了CMIP6模式。

This study evaluated CMIP6 models by combining direct assessment of TP SAT variations with physics-based observational constraints derived from the combined influence of soil moisture anomalies and SSTAs on summer TP SAT.

我们确定了最优模式和集合方法，以分析不同情景下的未来变化。

Optimal models and ensemble methods were identified to analyze future changes under different scenarios.

主要结论如下：

The main conclusions are as follows:

（1）通过将基于物理机制的观测约束与统计技巧评估（Taylor和IVS评分）相结合的相互验证，我们确定了10个最优模式。

(1) Using mutual verification combining physics-based observational constraints and statistical skill assessment (Taylor and IVS scores), we identified 10 optimal models.

在四种模式集合方法（MME、ClimWIP、LR和RF）中，我们发现RF方法有效减小了冷偏差，并准确模拟了青藏高原夏季近地面气温的气候态空间分布及多时间尺度（年代际和趋势）变化。

Among four model ensemble methods (MME, ClimWIP, LR, and RF), we found that the RF method effectively reduced the cold bias and accurately simulated the climatological spatial distribution and multi-scale (decadal and trend) temporal variations of summer TP SATs.

（2）基于最优模式的RF预估表明，青藏高原夏季将显著增暖，其增暖幅度和演变过程强烈依赖于排放情景。

(2) The RF-based projections with the optimal models show that significant summer TP warming, with its magnitude and evolution strongly dependent on the emission scenario.

在SSP1-2.6情景下，青藏高原在初期显著增暖（2015–2044年为0.38°C/十年），此后未呈现明确趋势。

Under SSP1-2.6, the TP exhibits significant early-term warming (0.38°C/decade during 2015–2044), with no clear trend thereafter.

在SSP2-4.5情景下，2015–2070年青藏高原近地面气温呈显著增暖趋势（0.33°C/10a），此后增暖趋势放缓（0.14°C/10a）。

Under SSP2-4.5, the TP SAT shows a significant warming trend (0.33°C/10a) during 2015–2070, and a slower warming trend (0.14°C/10a) afterwards.

在SSP5-8.5情景下，2015–2100年整个时期青藏高原近地面气温均呈显著增暖趋势（0.60°C/10a）。

Under SSP5-8.5, the TP SAT shows a significant warming trend (0.60°C/10a) throughout 2015–2100.

未经优化的、基于MME-18的预估系统性地低估了青藏高原的增暖，尤其是青藏高原西部的增暖。

The non-optimized MME-18-based projections systematically underestimated the TP warming, especially over western TP.

受约束的预估提示，未来的增暖，尤其是脆弱的青藏高原西部的增暖，可能比通常预估的更为严重。

The constrained projections suggest future warming, particularly in the vulnerable western TP, may be more severe than commonly projected.

Zhou和Zhang1采用最优指纹法，将青藏高原历史增暖归因于人为活动，尤其是温室气体强迫。

Using optimal fingerprinting, Zhou and Zhang1 attributed historical warming over the TP to anthropogenic activities, particularly greenhouse gas forcing.

将归因结果作为观测约束，预估的青藏高原增暖可能超过CMIP5多模式集合的预估1。

Using the attribution results as observational constraints, the predicted TP warming may exceed the CMIP5 multi-model ensemble1.

尽管本研究的约束条件与Zhou和Zhang1的研究不同，但结论一致，进一步支持了青藏高原未来可能出现更明显增暖的判断。

Although the constraint conditions in this study differ from Zhou and Zhang1, the conclusions are consistent, further supporting the likelihood of more pronounced future TP warming.

青藏高原持续且显著的增暖可能会提高当地土壤温度，加速冰川融化18,59，改变多年冻土60,61，增强蒸发，降低土壤湿度，并加剧草地退化和荒漠化62–64。

Sustained and significant warming in the TP would likely increase local soil temperatures, accelerate glacier melt18,59, alter permafrost60,61, enhance evaporation, reduce soil moisture, and exacerbate grassland degradation and desertification62–64.

青藏高原西部高海拔地区的夏季积雪覆盖，可影响中国东部的夏季降水异常65,66。

Summer snow cover in the high-altitude western TP can influence summer precipitation anomalies over eastern China65,66.

在更强的增暖下，积雪覆盖减少或消失可能显著改变中国东部的气候异常，这需要特别关注。

Under stronger warming, reduced or vanished snow cover could significantly alter climate anomalies in eastern China, which demands particular attention.

此外，欧洲土壤湿度异常对青藏高原夏季近地面气温起着关键调节作用。

Additionally, European soil moisture anomalies play a critical regulating role in summer TP SAT.

如果这一调节机制及其背后的物理约束仍保持完整，未来的极端增暖可能得到一定程度的缓解。

If the regulatory mechanism and its underlying physical constraint remain intact, future extreme warming could be somewhat mitigated.

这表明，应对气候变化离不开跨国和跨区域合作。

This suggests that multinational and multi-regional cooperation is essential to address climate change.

为增强气候韧性，应将区域气候影响和反馈效应纳入全球气候治理议程。

To enhance climate resilience, regional climate impacts and feedback effects should be integrated into global climate governance agendas.

方法

Methods

1. Taylor图与Taylor技巧评分（TSS）：

1. Taylor diagram and Taylor skill score (TSS):

Taylor图将相关系数（CC）、均方根误差（RMSE）和空间标准差比值（RSD）整合到极坐标图中，被认为是评估模式模拟性能的有效工具。

By integrating the correlation coefficient (CC), root mean square error (RMSE), and ratio of spatial standard deviation (RSD) into a polar coordinate plot, the Taylor diagram is considered an effective tool for evaluating the performance of model simulations.

TSS的公式如下：

The formula for the TSS is as follows:

TSS = 4(1 + R_m)^2 / [((σ_0/σ_m) + (σ_m/σ_0))^2(1 + R_0)^2](1),

TSS = 4(1 + R_m)^2 / [((σ_0/σ_m) + (σ_m/σ_0))^2(1 + R_0)^2](1),

其中，R_m为相关系数，R_0为最大相关系数（设为常数1），σ₀和σₘ分别为观测和模拟的标准差。

where R_m is the correlation coefficient, R_0 is the maximum correlation coefficient (set as a constant 1), and σ₀ and σₘ are the standard deviations of the observations and simulations, respectively.

TSS值越接近1，表明模拟与观测之间的一致性越好（Zhu et al 2020, Li et al 2021）。

A TSS value closer to 1 indicates better consistency between the simulations and observations (Zhu et al 2020, Li et al 2021).

2. 年际变率技巧评分（IVS）：

2. Interannual variability skill score (IVS):

IVS值按以下公式计算（Scherrer, 2011）：

The IVS value is calculated according to the following equation (Scherrer, 2011):

IVS = (STD_m / STD_o − STD_o / STD_m)^2

IVS = (STD_m / STD_o − STD_o / STD_m)^2

其中，STD_o和STD_m分别表示观测数据和历史模拟数据的标准差。

where STD_o and STD_m denote the standard deviations of the observed and historically simulated data, respectively.

IVS值越低，表明模拟性能越好。

A lower IVS value indicates better simulation performance.

IVS已被有效应用于评估对青藏高原近地面气温年际变率的模拟（Peng et al 2022）。

The IVS has been effectively applied in evaluating the simulation of interannual variability of TP SAT (Peng et al 2022).

3. 四种多模式集合方法：

3. Four multi-model ensemble approaches:

（1）常规多模式集合平均（MME），对所有参与模式赋予相等权重（通常称为“模式民主”）。

(1) The conventional MME, which assigns equal weighting to all participating models (commonly called “model democracy”).

（2）基于独立性和性能的气候模式加权（ClimWIP），通过考虑每个模式的技巧（性能权重）和模式间独立性（独立性权重），为各模式赋予不同权重（Chen et al 2011, Knutti et al 2017, Sanderson et al 2015, 2017, Li et al 2021）。

(2) The climate model weighting by independence and performance (ClimWIP), which assigns different weights to each model by considering its skill (performance weight) and inter-model independence (independence weight) (Chen et al 2011, Knutti et al 2017, Sanderson et al 2015, 2017, Li et al 2021).

本研究利用青藏高原夏季近地面气温的平均气候态和趋势计算加权参数。

In this study, the weighting parameters were calculated using the mean climatology and trend of summer SAT over the TP.

（3）线性回归（LR），一种使误差平方和最小化的线性算法，特别适用于涉及多个模式或集合成员的回归问题（Li et al 2021）。

(3) The linear regression (LR), a linear algorithm that minimizes the sum of squared errors and is particularly suitable for regression problems involving multiple models or ensemble members (Li et al 2021).

（4）随机森林（RF），一种bagging算法，以决策树为估计器构建树的集合，用于解决分类或回归问题（Liaw and Wiener, 2002）。

(4) The random forest (RF), a bagging algorithm that uses decision trees as estimators to construct an ensemble of trees for solving classification or regression issues (Liaw and Wiener, 2002).
