abs-p01-s001 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

青藏高原（TP）气象站的估算表明，自20世纪80年代以来，地表感热通量一直在持续减小；模拟研究则表明，这种变化可能与激发罗斯贝波列而导致的东亚季风减弱有关。

Estimations from meteorological stations over the Tibetan Plateau (TP) indicate that since the 1980s the surface-sensible heat flux has been decreasing continuously, and modeling studies suggest that such changes are likely linked to the weakening of the East Asian Monsoon through exciting Rossby wave trains.

abs-p01-s002 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

然而，整个青藏高原地表感热和潜热通量的空间与时间变化仍然未知。

However, the spatial and temporal variations in the surface-sensible and latent heat fluxes over the entire TP remain unknown.

abs-p01-s003 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

本研究通过综合地面测量、再分析产品和遥感产品等多种资料源，刻画1984—2007年青藏高原0.5°网格地表感热和潜热通量的空间与季节变化特征。

This study aims to characterize the spatial and seasonal variability of the surface-sensible and latent heat fluxes at 0.5° over the TP from 1984 to 2007 by synthesizing multiple data sources including ground measurements, reanalysis products, and remote-sensing products.

abs-p01-s004 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

交叉验证得到的月尺度融合感热和潜热通量均方根误差（RMSE）分别为14.3 Wm⁻²和10.3 Wm⁻²。

The root mean square errors (RMSEs) from cross validation are 14.3 Wm−2 and 10.3 Wm−2 for the monthly fused sensible and latent heat fluxes, respectively.

abs-p01-s005 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

融合感热和潜热通量异常与气象站估算结果一致，同时讨论了融合资料的不确定性。

The fused sensible and latent heat-flux anomalies are consistent with those estimated from meteorological stations, and the uncertainties of the fused data are also discussed.

abs-p01-s006 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

融合感热、潜热通量与相关地表异常（如平均温度、温度范围、积雪覆盖和归一化差异植被指数（NDVI））以及大气异常（如云量和水汽）之间的联系具有季节依赖性，这表明青藏高原陆地—生物圈—大气相互作用对气候变化的反馈可能并不均一。

The associations among the fused sensible and latent heat fluxes and the related surface anomalies such as mean temperature, temperature range, snow cover, and normalized difference vegetation index (NDVI) in addition to atmospheric anomalies such as cloud cover and water vapor show seasonal dependence, suggest that the land–biosphere–atmosphere interactions over the TP could display nonuniform feedbacks to the climate changes.

abs-p01-s007 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

未来研究若能借助模拟结果的证据，厘清青藏高原地表感热和潜热通量异常的驱动因素与响应，将会很有意义。

It would be interesting to disentangle the drivers and responses of the surface-sensible and latent heat-flux anomalies over the TP in future research from evidences of modeling results.

1 Introduction

main-p01-s002 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

陆地表面能量平衡（SEB）是陆地表面过程的重要物理特征。

The land surface energy balance (SEB) is a critical physical characteristic of the land surface processes.

main-p01-s003 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

它表述了陆地表面将净辐射（NR）分配为潜热通量（LE）、感热通量（H）和地表土壤热通量（G）的能力（式1）；这一分配控制着地球表面的水文、地球化学和生态过程（Liang et al., 2010）：

It states the ability of the land surface to partition the net radiation (NR) into latent heat flux (LE), sensible heat flux (H), and ground heat flux (G) (Eq. 1), which governs the hydrological, biogeochemical, and ecological processes at the Earth’s surface (Liang et al., 2010):

main-p01-eq001 · PDF p.1 · equation

NR = LE + H + G。（1）

NR = LE + H + G. (1)

main-p01-s004 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

相较于已经利用地表观测、遥感和再分析资料进行评估的全球陆地SEB，由于地面观测站点数量有限且缺乏综合验证，区域尺度陆地SEB的时空特征分析受到的关注较少（Kiehl and Trenberth, 1997；Trenberth et al., 2009；Jung et al., 2011；Stephens et al., 2012；Stevens and Schwartz, 2012；Wild, 2012）。

In contrast to the global land SEB that has been accessed by using surface observations, remote-sensing, and reanalysis data sets, less attention has been paid to analyzing the spatiotemporal characteristics of the land SEB at the regional scale due to the limit number of ground observation sites and the lack of comprehensive validation (Kiehl and Trenberth, 1997; Trenberth et al., 2009; Jung et al., 2011; Stephens et al., 2012; Stevens and Schwartz, 2012; Wild, 2012).

main-p01-s005 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

尽管长期地面观测SEB的资料有限，但已有研究利用地面测量和遥感观测对蒸散发的年代际变化进行了经验量化（Wang and Liang, 2008；Jung et al., 2010；Yao et al., 2012）。

Although limited long-term ground observations measure the SEB, the decadal changes of evapotranspiration have been quantified empirically by using ground measurements and remote-sensing observations (Wang and Liang, 2008; Jung et al., 2010; Yao et al., 2012).

main-p01-s006 · PDF p.1 · paragraph

然而，在区域尺度应用全球资料之前开展综合验证与比较十分重要，因为这类资料的可靠性在很大程度上取决于输入资料的特征，例如卫星产品的均一性和站点分布（Mueller et al., 2013）。

However, it is important to conduct comprehensive validation and comparison before applying global data sets at the regional scale, because the reliability of such data sets is largely dependent on the characteristics of inputs such as the homogeneity of satellite products and the distribution of stations (Mueller et al., 2013).

main-p02-s001 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

青藏高原SEB受到广泛关注，因为它在物理上联系着东亚季风，而东亚季风支撑着约25%的人口（Wang and Liang, 2008；Immerzeel et al., 2010）。

The SEB over the Tibetan Plateau (TP) is of great research interest because of its physical links to the East Asian monsoon, which sustains approximately 25 % of the world’s population (Wang and Liang, 2008; Immerzeel et al., 2010).

main-p02-s002 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

数值试验和地质观测支持这样一种理论：亚洲季风的形成与青藏高原隆升有关（Kutzbach et al., 1993；An et al., 2001）。

Numerical experiments and geologic observations have supported the theory such that the formation of the Asian monsoon is related to the uplift of the TP (Kutzbach et al., 1993; An et al., 2001).

main-p02-s003 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

除青藏高原通过分割急流而产生的机械强迫外（Manabe and Terpstra, 1974），青藏高原热力强迫与亚洲季风爆发及强度相联系的物理过程，也已根据急流移动、南亚高压形成、青藏高原气象观测的日变化以及数值试验进行推断（Ye and Gao, 1979；Yanai and Li, 1994；Ye and Wu, 1998；G. Wu et al., 2012）。

In addition to the mechanical forcing of the TP through partitioning of the jet stream (Manabe and Terpstra, 1974), physical processes by which the thermal forcing of the TP is linked to the onset and strength of the Asian monsoon have been inferred from the shift of the jet stream, the formation of the south Asian high, the diurnal variation of meteorological observations of the TP, and numerical experiments (Ye and Gao, 1979; Yanai and Li, 1994; Ye and Wu, 1998; G. Wu et al., 2012).

main-p02-s004 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

在利用全球环流模式（GCM）研究青藏高原抬升加热效应时，有研究提出了青藏高原“感热驱动的空气泵”概念（G. Wu et al., 2007）。

The concept of “sensible-heat driven air pump” over the TP has been proposed in research of the elevated heating effect over the TP by using the global circulation model (GCM) (G. Wu et al., 2007).

main-p02-s005 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

该概念认为，夏季（冬季）的地表加热（冷却）会使空气柱辐合（辐散）并上升（下沉），从而调节局地环流和北半球环流。

This concept suggests that the surface heating (cooling) in summer (winter) causes air columns to converge (diverge) and to ascend (descend), regulating both local and Northern Hemisphere circulation.

main-p02-s006 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

此外，这些过程分别在夏季高层大气形成南亚高压、在冬季使大陆区域气压升高，可能分别促成中国北旱南涝的格局和该国寒冷的大陆性气候（Duan et al., 2013）。

Furthermore, such processes create the south Asian high in the upper atmosphere in summer and the increasingly high pressure over continental areas in winter, likely contributing to the pattern of droughts in the north and floods in the south in China in addition to the country’s cold continental climate, respectively (Duan et al., 2013).

main-p02-s007 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

青藏高原SEB对于研究陆地—生物圈—大气相互作用、分析陆地生态系统和水文系统的变化，以及评估气候变化的影响和反馈都至关重要。

The SEB over the TP is essential for the study of land–biosphere–atmosphere interactions, analysis of the changes in terrestrial ecosystems and hydrological systems, and assessment of the impacts of and feedbacks to the climate changes.

main-p02-s008 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

已有研究考察了青藏高原不同土地覆盖类型站点SEB的日、季节和年变化，包括草地（Y. Ma et al., 2003；Tanaka et al., 2003；Liu et al., 2009；Bian et al., 2012）、草甸（Gu et al., 2005；Yao et al., 2008, 2011）以及冰川和高山区域（Zou et al., 2009；Yang et al., 2011c；Chen et al., 2012；Zhang et al., 2013）。

Previous research has investigated the diurnal, seasonal and annual variation of the SEB at stations over various land cover types in the TP, including grassland (Y. Ma et al., 2003; Tanaka et al., 2003; Liu et al., 2009; Bian et al., 2012), meadow (Gu et al., 2005; Yao et al., 2008, 2011), and glacial and alpine areas (Zou et al., 2009; Yang et al., 2011c; Chen et al., 2012; Zhang et al., 2013).

main-p02-s009 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

研究者已发展出多种先进方法：从改进常规气象观测参数化方案中反演SEB（Yang et al., 2002, 2003, 2008；Chen et al., 2010, 2013b；Guo et al., 2011b；Lee et al., 2012），从卫星观测中反演SEB（Y. Ma et al., 2006, 2012；W. Ma et al., 2009, 2011；Jiménez et al., 2009；Zhang et al., 2010），以及整合这两类资料源（Wang and Liang, 2008；Jung et al., 2009；Yao et al., 2012）。

Advanced methods have been developed to retrieve SEB from improved parameterization of routine meteorological observations (Yang et al., 2002, 2003, 2008; Chen et al., 2010, 2013b; Guo et al., 2011b; Lee et al., 2012) satellite observations (Y. Ma et al., 2006, 2012; W. Ma et al., 2009, 2011; Jiménez et al., 2009; Zhang et al., 2010), and the integration of both data sources (Wang and Liang, 2008; Jung et al., 2009; Yao et al., 2012).

main-p02-s010 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

然而，这些方法在刻画青藏高原SEB时仍存在较大不确定性，导致由再分析或传统整体空气动力学方法推断的感热通量年代际变化出现失真和不一致（Yang et al., 2011b；Zhu et al., 2012）。

The uncertainties of those approaches in characterizing the SEB over the TP, however, remain large, resulting in misrepresentation and inconsistency of the inferred decadal changes of the sensible heat flux from reanalysis or the conventional bulk aerodynamic method (Yang et al., 2011b; Zhu et al., 2012).

main-p02-s011 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

具体而言，由于气象站分布不均衡，且遥感产品受晴空条件要求限制而时间覆盖稀疏，基于观测的结果不足以确定整个青藏高原SEB的区域格局（Y. Ma et al., 2006, 2011）；而模拟结果的可靠性不仅受到输入误差在模式反演中传播的限制（Wang and Dickinson, 2012），还受到复杂地形和高度异质区域参数化不确定性的限制（Chen et al., 2013a）。

Specifically, observation-based results are inadequate for determining the regional pattern of the SEB over the entire TP due to the unbalanced distribution of meteorological stations and the sparse temporal coverage of the remote-sensing products under the requirement of clear-sky conditions (Y. Ma et al., 2006, 2011), whereas the reliability of modeling results is largely limited not only by the propagation of input error through the model retrieval (Wang and Dickinson, 2012), but also by the uncertainty of parameterization under complex terrain and highly heterogeneous areas (Chen et al., 2013a).

main-p02-s012 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

相反，GCM已被广泛用于评估青藏高原变暖（Wang et al., 2008, 2011）、积雪强迫（Qian et al., 2011；Turner and Slingo, 2011）和感热通量减弱（Liu et al., 2012；G. Wu et al., 2012；Duan et al., 2013）对东亚降水变化的影响。

Instead, GCMs have been applied extensively to access the effects of warming (Wang et al., 2008, 2011), snow forcing (Qian et al., 2011; Turner and Slingo, 2011), and weakened sensible heat flux (Liu et al., 2012; G. Wu et al., 2012; Duan et al., 2013) over the TP on the precipitation changes in East Asia.

main-p02-s013 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

然而，与地面观测得到的变化进行比较时，区域尺度青藏高原SEB年代际变化的量化，以及SEB变化与观测地表和大气状况之间关系的评估，仍存在空缺。

Nevertheless, gaps remain in quantification of the decadal changes of the SEB over the TP at the regional scale in comparison with changes derived from ground observations and in assessment of the relationship between the changes of the SEB and the observed surface and atmospheric conditions.

main-p02-s014 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

本研究的目标是整合近二十年的地面观测、遥感和再分析资料，分析青藏高原地表感热与潜热通量的时空特征。

The objective of this study is to analyze the spatiotemporal characteristics of the surface-sensible and latent heat fluxes over the TP by integrating ground observations, remote-sensing, and reanalysis data sets over the recent two decades.

main-p02-s015 · PDF p.2 · paragraph

以下各节将介绍包括预处理、资料融合及时空刻画在内的资料源和方法；描述验证结果；讨论融合资料的不确定性；考察空间和季节变化；并总结由结果得到的主要发现及其含义。

The following sections introduce various data sources and methodologies including preprocessing, data fusion, and spatiotemporal characterization; describe the validation results; discuss the uncertainties of the fused data; investigate the spatial and seasonal variability; and conclude with major findings and implications derived from the results.

Two data sets based on meteorological station data over the TP reported by (Yang et al., 2009) and (Yang et al., 2011a), hereafter referred to as Yang09 and Yang11, respectively, were chosen for comparison purposes.

2.2 Remote-sensing data sets

main-p04-s002 · PDF p.4 · paragraph

基于遥感的蒸散发估算最初用于评估泛北极盆地和阿拉斯加蒸散发的年代际变化（Zhang et al., 2009），随后应用于全球陆地表面（Zhang et al., 2010）。

Remote-sensing-based estimations of evapotranspiration have been developed to access decadal changes of evapotranspiration over the pan-Arctic basin and Alaska (Zhang et al., 2009), which were then applied to the global land surface (Zhang et al., 2010).

main-p04-s003 · PDF p.4 · paragraph

Zhang10采用修正的Penman–Monteith方法估算植被区蒸散发，并使用由34个FLUXNET站点参数化的生物群区特定冠层导度模式。

Zhang10 estimates evapotranspiration over vegetated areas by a modified Penman–Monteith approach with a biome-specific canopy conductance model parameterized from 34 FLUXNET sites.

main-p04-s004 · PDF p.4 · paragraph

主要输入包括：NASA全球植被变化监测与制图研究（GIMMS）提供的先进甚高分辨率辐射计（AVHRR）NDVI（Tucker et al., 2005）；国家环境预报中心（NCEP）/国家大气研究中心（NCAR）第1版再分析（NRA-1）提供的气温和水汽压（Kalnay et al., 1996）；以及GEWEX地表辐射收支（GEWEX SRB 3.0）提供的短波辐射（Pinker and Ewing, 1985；Pinker and Laszlo, 1992）。

The major inputs are NDVI from the Advanced Very High Resolution Radiometer (AVHRR) provided by NASA Global Inventory Modeling and Mapping Studies (GIMMS) (Tucker et al., 2005), air temperature and water vapor pressure from the National Center of Environmental Prediction (NCEP)/National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR) reanalysis version 1 (NRA-1) (Kalnay et al., 1996), and shortwave radiation from GEWEX Surface Radiation Budget (GEWEX SRB 3.0) (Pinker and Ewing, 1985; Pinker and Laszlo, 1992).

main-p04-s005 · PDF p.4 · paragraph

包括HBM在内的48个FLUXNET站点独立验证得到的精度为R²=0.80–0.84。

The independent validation over 48 FLUXNET sites including HBM satisfied accuracy at R2 = 0.80–0.84.

main-p04-s006 · PDF p.4 · paragraph

近期对Zhang10的评估表明，在青藏高原黄河上游和长江流域，它优于其他再分析和遥感资料集（Xue et al., 2013）。

A recent evaluation of Zhang10 suggests that it outperforms other reanalysis and remote-sensing data sets over the upper Yellow River and Yangtze River basins in the TP (Xue et al., 2013).

main-p04-s007 · PDF p.4 · paragraph

近期，研究者利用GLEAM建立了一套全球陆地蒸散发资料集（Miralles et al., 2011a, b, 2014）。

Recently, a global land evapotranspiration data set has been developed by using GLEAM (Miralles et al., 2011a, b, 2014).

main-p04-s008 · PDF p.4 · paragraph

GLEAM采用Priestley–Taylor方法，根据观测地表净辐射和气温估算潜在蒸发。

GLEAM applied a Priestley–Taylor approach to estimate the potential evaporation from observed surface net radiation and air temperature.

main-p04-s009 · PDF p.4 · paragraph

实际蒸散发由潜在蒸发乘以蒸发胁迫因子得到；该因子由观测植被光学厚度（用于近似植被含水量）和估算的根区土壤湿度推导。

The actual evapotranspiration is estimated from potential evaporation multiplied by the evaporative stress factor, which is derived from the observed vegetation optical depths (to approximate vegetation water content) and the estimated root zone soil moisture.

main-p04-s010 · PDF p.4 · paragraph

The difference between the GLEAM evapotranspiration and the FLUXNET observations is within ±20 % at most sites.

Considering the temporal coverage and the improved accuracy of the daily evapotranspiration, the data set from the third experiment (e3) from the Miralles et al. (2014) is used in this study, which uses net radiation from GEWEX SRB 3.0, air temperature from the International Satellite Cloud Climatology Project (ISCCP) (Rossow et al., 2004) merged with ERA-Interim and the Atmospheric Infrared Sounder (AIRS) (Ferguson et al., 2010), and precipitation from Climate Prediction Center unified gauge-based analysis (Chen et al., 2008) as major inputs.

2.3 再分析资料集

2.3 Reanalysis data sets

main-p05-s003 · PDF p.5 · paragraph

NCEP CFSR是第一个采用陆地—大气—海洋—海冰耦合系统的全球再分析产品（Saha et al., 2010）。

The NCEP CFSR is the first global reanalysis product to apply a coupled land–atmosphere–ocean–sea ice system (Saha et al., 2010).

main-p05-s004 · PDF p.5 · paragraph

陆面分析由Noah陆面模式产生，该模式除受大气同化输出驱动外，还受观测降水和雪深驱动（Ek et al., 2003）。

The land surface analysis is produced from the Noah land surface model, which is forced by the output from the atmospheric assimilation in addition to observed precipitation and snow depth (Ek et al., 2003).

main-p05-s005 · PDF p.5 · paragraph

该模式具有更高的空间分辨率（T382，约0.31°），并改进了月尺度陆地表面能量和水分闭合，因此优于此前的NCEP再分析资料集（Meng et al., 2012）。

This model outperforms the previous NCEP reanalysis data sets with higher spatial resolution (T382, approximately 0.31°) and improved land surface energy and water closures at the monthly scale (Meng et al., 2012).

main-p05-s006 · PDF p.5 · paragraph

MERRA是由NASA全球模拟与同化办公室（GMAO）支持的近期再分析资料集，时间覆盖始于1979年；它包含与GOES-5大气GCM耦合的流域水文模式（Koster and Suarez, 1996；Koster et al., 2000）和多层积雪模式（Stieglitz et al., 2001）。

MERRA, a recent reanalysis data set supported by NASA’s Global Modeling and Assimilation Office (GMAO) covering temporal period since 1979, incorporates a catchment hydrological model (Koster and Suarez, 1996; Koster et al., 2000) and a multi-layer snow model (Stieglitz et al., 2001) that are coupled to the GOES-5 atmospheric GCM.

main-p05-s007 · PDF p.5 · paragraph

全球评估表明，采用MERRA得到的水循环有所改善（Rienecker et al., 2011）。

Global assessments suggest an improvement in the hydrological cycles with MERRA (Rienecker et al., 2011).

main-p05-s008 · PDF p.5 · paragraph

ERA-Interim是第一个采用欧洲中期天气预报中心（ECMWF）提供的四维变分资料同化方案（4D-Var）的再分析产品（Dee et al., 2011）；该方案同化2 m气温和湿度，以校正土壤湿度和温度分析（Douville et al., 2000）。

ERA-Interim is the first reanalysis product to apply the four-dimensional variational data assimilation scheme (4D-Var) provided by the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) (Dee et al., 2011), which assimilates 2 m air temperature and humidity to correct soil moisture and temperature analyses (Douville et al., 2000).

main-p05-s009 · PDF p.5 · paragraph

ERA-Interim采用ECMWF陆面交换网格方案（TESSEL）（Viterbo and Beljaars, 1995；Viterbo and Betts, 1999）和积雪方案（Douville et al., 1995）模拟陆地表面过程。

The Tiled ECMWF Scheme for Surface Exchanges over Land (TESSEL) (Viterbo and Beljaars, 1995; Viterbo and Betts, 1999) and the snow scheme (Douville et al., 1995) are used in the ERA-Interim for modeling land surface processes.

main-p05-s010 · PDF p.5 · paragraph

JRA-25通过应用日本气象厅（JMA）数值同化和预报系统，旨在为亚洲区域提供高质量资料（Onogi et al., 2007）。

JRA-25 aims to provide high-quality data for the Asian region by applying the Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) numerical assimilation and forecast system (Onogi et al., 2007).

main-p05-s011 · PDF p.5 · paragraph

JRA-25采用简单生物圈方案，并使用观测积雪资料（Sellers et al., 1986；Sato et al., 1989）。

A simple biosphere scheme is used in JRA-25 with observed snow data (Sellers et al., 1986; Sato et al., 1989).

main-p06-s003 · PDF p.6 · paragraph

Monthly mean SEB components over the TP were extracted from ground measurements, remote-sensing, and reanalysis data sets.

随后，根据缺测时间少于一半的逐日逐小时平均值计算日平均值。

Then, daily mean value was calculated from daily hourly mean values with less than half missing.

在日尺度上利用观测的Bowen比对地表能量平衡进行闭合（Twine et al., 2000）。

The surface energy balance was closed by using the observed Bowen ratio on the daily scale (Twine et al., 2000).

原位资料月平均值由一个月内覆盖超过15天的日平均值计算得到。

The monthly mean value of in situ data was calculated from the daily mean covering more than 15 days in a month.

同样从原始再分析资料集提取SEB分量的日平均值，随后插值到0.5°分辨率：MERRA、Interim和JRA-25采用最近网格资料，CFSR采用新投影网格所覆盖网格的平均值。

main-p06-s009 · PDF p.6 · paragraph

月平均值由日平均值计算得到。

The monthly mean value was calculated from the daily mean.

The sensible heat flux of Zhang10 and GLEAM-LE is estimated from the difference between the net radiation from GEWEX SRB 3.0 and the latent heat flux, assuming the ground heat flux is relatively small and negligible (Jiménez et al., 2011).

青藏高原站点SEB的残差可能由测量误差、采样不匹配、土壤能量储存、平流以及非均一性引起的大尺度涡动造成（Li et al., 2005；Foken et al., 2006；Foken, 2008）。

各站点仪器安装高度不同、代表性足迹差异很大，因此SEB闭合需要大气稳定性和地表均一性。

Measurements are mounted at different heights with substantially different footprints at each station, which requires stability and surface homogeneity to close the SEB.

main-p06-s015 · PDF p.6 · paragraph

具体而言，既有研究分析和本研究夏季的高残差误差表明，红外湿度计的系统误差是闭合问题的关键，尤其是在降雨日；这与比湿测量波动减弱导致较低潜热通量有关（Tanaka et al., 2003；Yang et al., 2004）。

Specifically, analysis from previous studies and high-residue error in summer from the current study suggest that the systematic error from the infrared hygrometer is critical to the closure problem, particularly during rainfall days, which is associated with lower latent heat flux from weakened measured fluctuation of specific humidity (Tanaka et al., 2003; Yang et al., 2004).

此外，冻融季与闭合比（式2中的CR）的变化有关；在完全冻结阶段，闭合比达到负值（Guo et al., 2011a）。

In addition, the freezing and thawing season is related to variation in closure ratio (CR in Eq. 2), which reaches a negative value in the completely frozen stage (Guo et al., 2011a).

尽管对于青藏高原涡动协方差测量能量平衡及不平衡的成因和修正尚未达成共识或形成规程，但通过计算日平均值，在日尺度闭合SEB可以限制日变化不平衡的影响，例如冻结和融化过程以及植物光合作用能量储存造成的影响（Tanaka et al., 2003；Yang et al., 2004；Yang and Koike, 2008；Guo et al., 2011a）。

Although no agreements or protocols has been reached for the causes and corrections of energy balance and imbalance from eddy-covariance measurement over the TP, closure of the SEB on the daily-scale limits the effect of imbalance from diurnal variation, such as that through the freezing and melting processes and the energy storage from photosynthesis of plants, by calculating the daily average (Tanaka et al., 2003; Yang et al., 2004; Yang and Koike, 2008; Guo et al., 2011a).

Extensive eddy-covariance measurements in various climatic regions and land cover types over the TP are required in future research to quantify conditions under which the energy budget is unbalanced.

The root mean square error (RMSE) from cross validation (RMSE_CV) was computed as the average RMSE after applying this process multiple times (omitting one site for validation each time).

In the case of high-level data fusion for the SEB, such as feature-level image fusion according to Pohl and Van Genderen (1998), the MLR fused method constrains the error of the fused SEB through minimization of the residue error.

Advanced statistical methods with the potential for revealing unseen (i.e., nonlinear) processes by linear approximations were selected for comparison with the MLR results, including supported vector regression (SVM) (Vapnik, 1999) and random forest (RF) regression (Breiman, 2001).

4.3 融合地表能量通量的不确定性

4.3 Uncertainties of the fused surface energy fluxes

main-p11-s002 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

估算潜热和感热通量所用输入和方法具有不同不确定性，使站点、再分析和遥感资料集SEB的质量评估变得复杂。

The varied uncertainties from inputs and methods by which the latent and sensible heat fluxes are estimated complicate the quality assessment of the SEB from stations, reanalysis, and remote-sensing data sets.

main-p11-s003 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

具体而言，融合SEB的输入误差是指地面测量、再分析或遥感资料集月值带来的误差。

Specifically, the input error of the fused SEB is referred to as errors from the monthly value for ground-measured, reanalysis, or remote-sensing data sets.

main-p11-s004 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

既有研究表明，就温度、降水、湿度、气压、风速等常规测量地表变量以及地表辐射收支而言，没有任何单一再分析资料集优于其他资料集；这说明用于估算SEB的陆面模式对地表变量日循环（如地—气温度、风速和空气动力学粗糙度）的表示与参数化存在差异（Wang and Zeng, 2012；Zhu et al., 2012）。

Previous studies suggest that no single reanalysis data set is superior to others in terms of routinely measured surface variables such as temperature, precipitation, humidity, pressure, and wind speed and the surface radiation budget, which suggests divergent representation and parameterization of the diurnal cycles of surface variables such as ground–air temperature, wind speed, and aerodynamic roughness in the land surface models used to estimate the SEB (Wang and Zeng, 2012; Zhu et al., 2012).

main-p11-s005 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

Zhang10的不确定性可能来自作为输入的AVHRR GIMMS NDVI误差，以及作为训练样本的涡动协方差测量能量不平衡（Zhang et al., 2010）。

The uncertainty from Zhang10 could be inherited through errors from the AVHRR GIMMS NDVI as inputs and the energy imbalance of eddy-covariance measurements as training samples (Zhang et al., 2010).

main-p11-s006 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

另一个主要输入误差来源是净辐射和向下短波辐照度，它们在很大程度上决定可分配为热通量的可用能量（Liang et al., 2014）。

Additional dominant input error is sourced from net radiation and downward shortwave irradiance, which largely determine the available energy to be partitioned into heat fluxes (Liang et al., 2014).

main-p11-s007 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

验证结果还表明，再分析或遥感潜热通量的精度显著高于感热通量；这可能是因为青藏高原潜热通量估算不仅受能量平衡约束，也受水分平衡约束（Mueller et al., 2013）。

The validation result also proves that the accuracy of the latent heat flux is significantly higher than the sensible heat flux from reanalysis or remote sensing, which is possibly attributed to the estimation of the latent heat flux is constrained from not only the energy but also water balance over the TP (Mueller et al., 2013).

main-p11-s008 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

融合SEB的不确定性还受到计算过程中的误差传播影响，包括涡动协方差反演、阈值筛选、平均、插值和资料融合。

The uncertainty of the fused SEB is also affected by error propagation through calculations, including eddy-covariance retrieval, threshold filtering, averaging, interpolation, and data fusion.

main-p11-s009 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

值得注意的是，涡动协方差反演和MLR资料融合同时包含乘法及加/减法，这表明融合SEB的不确定性可能在这些计算中被放大。

It is worth noting that eddy-covariance retrieval and MLR data fusion employ both multiplication and addition/subtraction, suggesting that uncertainty of the fused SEB could be amplified from those calculations.

main-p11-s010 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

然而，资料融合方法适用于本区域应用，因为整合地面测量、再分析和遥感产品能够约束不确定性，其RMSE_CV低于使用各个校准输入资料集的结果（表5）。

Nevertheless, the data fused approach is an applicable method for this regional application because of constraining the uncertainty by integrating ground measurement, reanalysis, and remote-sensing products, which lowered the RMSE_CV than that by using each calibrated input data set (Table 5).

main-p11-s011 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

此外，在使用不同空间分辨率的多个资料集进行综合整合时，重采样尺度引入的融合资料误差是一个新出现的问题。

In addition, the error of the fused data introduced by the resample scale is an emergent issue in the case of integrating synthesis that uses multiple data sets at various spatial resolutions.

main-p11-s012 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

通过将所提出的资料融合方法应用于0.1°、0.3°、0.5°、1.0°、1.5°、2.0°和2.5°七种网格尺度，评估重采样尺度的影响，以比较融合、再分析和遥感资料集对重采样尺度的敏感性（图7）。

在不同尺度之间，地表土壤热通量和感热通量的RMSE_CV变化小于潜热通量。

The RMSE_CV of the ground heat flux and the sensible heat flux is less variable than that of the latent heat flux among the various scales.

main-p11-s014 · PDF p.11 · paragraph

05-conclusions

main-p13-s006 · PDF p.13 · heading

4.6 对陆地—生物圈—大气相互作用和气候变化的含义

4.6 Implications for land–biosphere–atmosphere interactions and climate change

main-p13-s007 · PDF p.13 · paragraph

融合感热和潜热通量激发了人们进一步理解青藏高原陆地—生物圈—大气相互作用变化的兴趣。

The fused sensible and latent heat fluxes prompt interest in further understanding of the changes in land–biosphere–atmosphere interactions over the TP.

main-p13-s008 · PDF p.13 · paragraph

在陆面模式中应用遥感观测，使SEB能够在空间连续尺度上量化并延伸到二十多年的卫星时代；若仅使用足迹尺度的地面观测，这是无法实现的。

Applying remote-sensing observations in land surface modeling enables the quantification of the SEB at the spatial continuous scale to be extended to the satellite era over two decades, which is otherwise impossible when using ground observations at the footprint scale alone.

main-p13-s009 · PDF p.13 · paragraph

地表感热和/或潜热通量与云量、水汽、温度和温度范围之间的相关所显示的季节依赖性表明，区域陆地—生物圈—大气相互作用可能与夏季风和冬季风的进退有关。

The seasonal dependence, as indicated from the correlation among surface-sensible and/or latent heat flux and cloud cover, water vapor, temperature, and temperature range, suggests that regional land–biosphere–atmosphere interactions may be associated with the advance and retreat of the summer and winter monsoon.

main-p13-s010 · PDF p.13 · paragraph

关于陆地—生物圈—大气相互作用如何减弱或放大青藏高原地表潜热和感热通量异常的机制，受到的关注很少；因此，未来研究应使用能够再现过去气候变化和青藏高原地表热通量变化的模式应对这一挑战。

The mechanisms by which land–biosphere–atmosphere interactions mitigate or amplify the anomalies of the surface latent and sensible heat flux over the TP have received little attention; therefore, future investigation should address this challenge by using models capable of reproducing past climate changes and variation of the surface heat fluxes over the TP.

5 结论

5 Conclusions

main-p13-s012 · PDF p.13 · paragraph

本研究利用0.5°融合月尺度潜热和感热通量，展示了1984—2007年青藏高原地表感热和潜热通量的空间与季节变化；所用资料包括AsiaFlux、ChinaFLUX、GAME/Tibet和CAMP/Tibet地面测量资料集，Zhang10和GLEAM-LE遥感资料集，以及CFSR、MERRA、ERA-Interim和JRA-25再分析资料集。

The spatial and seasonal variability of the surface-sensible and latent heat fluxes over the TP from 1984 to 2007 was presented by using the fused monthly latent and sensible heat fluxes at 0.5° from ground-measured data sets including AsiaFlux, ChinaFLUX, GAME/Tibet, and CAMP/Tibet; remote-sensing data sets from Zhang10 and GLEAM-LE; and reanalysis data sets including CFSR, MERRA, ERA-Interim, and JRA-25.

main-p13-s013 · PDF p.13 · paragraph

首先在月尺度上将再分析和遥感资料集的地表感热和潜热通量与地面测量结果进行验证，随后采用多元线性回归方法进行综合。

The surface-sensible and latent heat fluxes from reanalysis and remote-sensing data sets were first validated against those from ground measurement at the monthly scale and were then synthesized by using the multiple linear regression approach.

main-p13-s014 · PDF p.13 · paragraph

在日尺度上闭合能量平衡，以减少青藏高原站点日循环演变带来的不确定性。

The energy balance was closed at the daily scale to reduce uncertainties evolving in the diurnal cycles over the TP stations.

main-p13-s015 · PDF p.13 · paragraph

从多个方面讨论了融合地表感热和潜热通量的不确定性；与插值尺度和融合方法选择造成的不确定性相比，输入误差和仪器误差传播带来的不确定性可能是主导因素。

The uncertainties of the fused surface-sensible and latent heat fluxes were discussed from various aspects in which uncertainties propagating from input errors and instrumental errors were likely the dominant factors when comparing with that from the interpolation scale and the selection of the fusion method.

main-p13-s016 · PDF p.13 · paragraph

留一站点交叉验证结果表明，融合月尺度潜热和感热通量的RMSE_CV最低，分别为10.3 Wm⁻²和14.3 Wm⁻²，优于使用各个校准资料集的结果。

Results from the leave-one-site-out cross validation suggest that the fused monthly latent and sensible heat fluxes had the lowest RMSE_CVs, at 10.3 Wm−2 and 14.3 Wm−2, respectively, against those using each calibrated data set.

main-p13-s017 · PDF p.13 · paragraph

此外，仅使用CMA站气象观测资料的数据集（Yang09）所显示的月循环，也被融合感热通量一致地捕捉到。

In addition, the fused sensible heat flux consistently captured the monthly cycle to the data sets by using only meteorological observations at CMA stations (Yang09).

main-p14-s001 · PDF p.14 · paragraph

在区域尺度上，潜热和感热通量显示出不同的季节演变和分布格局，这可能与青藏高原的纬度梯度、海拔差异和季风气候有关。

At the regional scale, the latent and sensible heat fluxes displayed different seasonal evolution and distribution patterns, which are likely associated with latitudinal gradient, elevation difference, and monsoon climate over the TP.

main-p14-s002 · PDF p.14 · paragraph

潜热通量最高值区位于青藏高原东部和西南山地南坡，这些区域受夏季风影响较大；而感热通量在中部—北部山地较高，这些地区春夏季较为干燥。

The highest value area of latent heat flux was located in the east TP and the south face of the southwest ranges, which are largely affected by the summer monsoon, whereas the sensible heat flux was high the middle-northern ranges, which are dry in spring and summer.

main-p14-s003 · PDF p.14 · paragraph

由于没有校准站点的区域不确定性可能很大，所得到的感热和潜热通量空间格局仍需在青藏高原西部进一步验证。

The derived spatial pattern of the sensible and latent heat flux requires further validation over the western TP since uncertainties could be very large over area without any calibration sites.

main-p14-s004 · PDF p.14 · paragraph

通过Pearson相关分析解释了青藏高原平均潜热和感热通量年际异常，分析因素包括云量、水汽、NDVI、积雪覆盖、温度、最高温度和温度范围。

The interannual anomalies of the latent and sensible heat fluxes averaged over the TP were explained through Pearson correlation analysis including such factors as cloud cover, water vapor, NDVI, snow cover, temperature, maximum temperature, and temperature range.

main-p14-s005 · PDF p.14 · paragraph

除春季外，所有季节潜热通量与水汽之间均存在强相关；而感热通量在春季和夏季存在较强相关，但方向相反。

A strong correlation between latent heat flux and water vapor existed in all seasons except spring, whereas a strong high correlation to sensible heat flux existed in spring and summer but in opposite directions.

main-p14-s006 · PDF p.14 · paragraph

夏季与感热和潜热通量显著相关的变量数量最多，而且相较于其他季节，大多数相关的幅度也最大。

During summer, the number of significant correlated variables with the sensible and latent heat fluxes was the largest, and the magnitude of most correlations was also the largest for all seasons.

main-p14-s007 · PDF p.14 · paragraph

这些结果表明，地表和大气变量的协同变化可能通过影响地表辐射收支、植被（土壤）的蒸腾（蒸发）或温度梯度，与青藏高原年代际变化相关。

These results imply that the coherent changes in the surface and atmospheric variables could relate to decadal changes over the TP by influencing the surface radiation budget, transpiration (evaporation) of vegetation (soil), or temperature gradient.

main-p14-s008 · PDF p.14 · paragraph

这些结果证实，陆地—生物圈—大气相互作用可能调节地表感热和潜热通量的时间变化，尽管这种耦合的强度具有季节依赖性。

Such results confirm that land–biosphere–atmosphere interactions may regulate the temporal variation of the surface-sensible and latent heat fluxes, although the magnitude of such couplings has seasonal dependence.

main-p14-s009 · PDF p.14 · paragraph

由于约四分之一的世界人口受到东亚季风影响，而东亚季风由青藏高原上的感热和潜热通量驱动，未来若利用数值模拟量化地表感热和潜热通量变化的驱动因素及响应，将会很有意义。

Because approximately one-fourth of the world’s population is affected by the East Asian monsoon, which is fueled by sensible and latent heat fluxes over the TP, it would be interesting to quantify the drivers and responses of the changes in surface-sensible and latent heat fluxes in future research by using numerical modeling.
