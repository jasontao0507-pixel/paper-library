摘要

Abstract

海洋热浪可以通过大气遥相关在不同海盆之间相互影响，但目前对这种海盆间联系的认识仍然不足。

Marine heatwaves can interact across ocean basins through atmospheric teleconnections, but such inter-basin links remain poorly understood.

本研究表明，加勒比海北半球春季和夏季的海洋热浪，与此前冬季印度洋的海洋热浪存在显著联系。

Here we show that boreal spring and summer marine heatwaves in the Caribbean Sea are significantly connected to marine heatwaves in the Indian Ocean during the preceding winter.

利用观测资料和气候模式模拟，我们发现，北半球冬季印度洋海洋热浪会触发大气对流，产生向东传播的印度洋—太平洋—大西洋罗斯贝波列。

Using observational data and climate model simulations, we identify that boreal winter Indian Ocean marine heatwaves trigger atmospheric convection, generating an eastward-propagating Indo-Pacific-Atlantic Rossby wave train.

该波列改变了区域哈德莱环流，使加勒比海上空的大气变得温暖、湿润且稳定，减少海洋的潜热损失，驱动海表增暖并促进海洋热浪增强。

This wave train alters the regional Hadley circulation and renders the overlying atmosphere warm, moist, and stable over the Caribbean Sea, reducing latent heat loss from the ocean, driving sea surface warming and promoting the intensification of marine heatwaves.

这些发现揭示了一条稳健的遥相关路径，使一个海盆的气候信号能够增强另一个海盆的海洋热浪。

These findings reveal a robust teleconnection pathway that allows climate signals from one basin to amplify marine heatwaves in another.

引言

Introduction

海洋热浪（MHWs）是海水异常增暖的极端事件。

Marine heatwaves (MHWs) are extreme events of anomalous seawater warming.

这类事件不定期发生，可持续数天至数月[1,2]。

These events occur sporadically and can persist from days to months[1,2].

大量研究已经考察了不同海域的海洋热浪。

Numerous studies have investigated MHWs across diverse marine regions.

某些强度特别高的海洋热浪受到了广泛关注，研究者已对其成因、发展过程和影响评估开展了全面研究[3,4,5,6,7]。

Certain exceptionally intense MHWs have garnered significant interest and have undergone comprehensive investigation on their origins, development, and impact assessment[3,4,5,6,7].

Oliver等[8]的历史回顾指出，全球海洋热浪的增暖趋势呈明显加速，强度和持续时间都随时间增加。

Oliver, et al.[8] provided a historical overview indicating that global MHWs have demonstrated a marked acceleration in warming trends, with an increase in both intensity and duration over time.

在全球变暖背景下，预计这一趋势仍将持续，可能使未来海洋热浪的发生频率和严重程度进一步上升[9,10]。

Given the context of global warming, this trend is expected to continue, potentially resulting in an increased occurrence and severity of future MHWs[9,10].

海洋热浪严重损害生物资源、改变生态系统结构，并对水产养殖和海洋旅游等行业产生不利影响、造成经济损失，从而威胁海洋生态系统[11,12,13,14,15,16]。

MHWs imperil marine ecosystems by causing significant damage to biological resources, altering ecosystem structure, and negatively impacting industries, such as aquaculture and marine tourism, leading to economic losses[11,12,13,14,15,16].

此外，海洋热浪与飓风或海洋酸化等其他极端事件共同作用时，还会加剧灾难性后果[17,18,19]。

Moreover, MHWs in synergy with other extreme events like hurricanes or ocean acidification can amplify the severity of catastrophic outcomes[17,18,19].

要应对并减轻海洋热浪的影响，亟需深入理解海洋热浪。

To address and mitigate the impacts of MHWs, an urgent need exists for a thorough understanding of MHWs.

近期研究显著推进了我们对海洋热浪成因和演变的认识。

Recent studies have significantly advanced our knowledge of the origins and evolution of MHWs.

一般而言，海洋热浪可由大气运动引起的异常海气热通量触发，也可由异常洋流造成的异常海洋热平流触发[3,20,21]。

Generally, MHWs can be triggered by anomalous air-sea heat fluxes due to atmospheric motion or by anomalous oceanic heat advection caused by anomalous currents[3,20,21].

这些驱动因素可能直接源于受影响区域[21,22,23]，也可能通过复杂的海洋和大气过程，受到远方因素的间接影响[4,24,25]。

These drivers may originate directly from the affected region[21,22,23] or be indirectly influenced by distant factors through complex oceanic and atmospheric processes[4,24,25].

在某些区域，已得到较充分研究的海洋热浪成因包括气候模态、海洋或大气行星波过程，以及高压阻塞引起的环流变化等[24,26,27,28,29]。

In certain regions, well-researched factors contributing to MHWs include climate modes, oceanic or atmospheric planetary wave processes, and circulation changes due to high-pressure blocking, etc.[24,26,27,28,29].

历史资料表明，特定区域的海洋热浪呈现出不同的变率特征；一些研究将其与气候内部变率联系起来，认为这些特征受到背景环流和海表温度（SST）变化的调节[26,27,30,31,32]。

Historical data reveal that MHWs in specific regions exhibit distinct variability patterns, which some studies link to internal climate variability, regulated by background circulation and sea surface temperature (SST) changes[26,27,30,31,32].

这些信息有助于预测海洋热浪变率。

This information aids in predicting MHW variability.

基于海洋热浪驱动因素的研究，一些研究已经利用与海洋热浪有关的海洋和大气指数，或利用数值模式，成功预测了海洋热浪[33,34]。

Based on research into MHW drivers, some studies have successfully predicted MHWs using oceanic and atmospheric indices associated with MHWs or numerical models[33,34].

这些指数也可以由海洋热浪本身构建。

These indices can also be derived from MHWs themselves.

早期研究已经记录了海洋热浪与远端大气、海洋现象之间的遥相关联系[35,36,37,38]。

Teleconnections linking MHWs to remote atmospheric and oceanic phenomena have been documented in earlier studies[35,36,37,38].

尽管一些极端事件之间通过大气罗斯贝波形成的遥相关关系已被识别[39]，但对海洋热浪中此类关系的探索仍处于早期阶段。

While teleconnection relationships among some extreme events have been identified through atmospheric Rossby waves[39], the exploration of such relationships in MHWs is still in its early stages.

与其他天气和气候系统相似，不同区域的海洋热浪之间可能存在潜在共性和相互作用。

There may be underlying commonalities and interactions among MHWs in various areas, similar to other weather and climate systems.

海洋热浪作为大气下边界的一种强迫，可以影响大气运动、传播自身信号，进而影响其他区域的海洋热浪，使它们之间形成遥相关。

As a forcing at the atmospheric bottom boundary, MHWs can influence atmospheric motion, propagate their signals, and thereby affect MHWs in other regions, leading to teleconnections among them.

在北大西洋，加勒比海（CS）区域（50°–85°W，8°–24°N）的海洋热浪强度低于西边界流区域。

In the North Atlantic Ocean, the Caribbean Sea (CS) region (50°–85°W, 8°–24°N) experiences MHWs of lower intensity compared to those in western boundary current regions.

然而，加勒比海此类事件的破坏性十分显著，值得关注。

Nonetheless, the destructiveness of these events in the CS is highly significant and warrants attention.

加勒比海拥有大量珊瑚礁和高度生物多样性，对海洋热浪极为敏感，因此海洋热浪在这里的影响尤为突出[11,40]。

Home to a vast number of coral reefs and a high level of biodiversity, CS is acutely sensitive to MHWs, contributing to their remarkable impact[11,40].

此外，加勒比海地区分布着许多发展中国家，渔业等传统涉海产业对当地经济至关重要[41]。

Additionally, many developing countries are located in the CS region, where traditional marine-related industries such as fishing are crucial to the local economy[41].

尽管高强度海洋热浪区域已经受到广泛研究，加勒比海海洋热浪仍有待进一步考察。

Despite the extensive research on high-intensity MHW regions, the study of MHWs in the CS remains an area for further investigation.

加勒比海位于北半球西风带与热带哈德莱环流的交汇处，这一独特地理位置使其尤其容易受到经西风波导从其他地区传播而来的大气波动影响。

The CS’s unique geographical position, at the intersection of the northern hemisphere westerly belt and the tropical Hadley circulation, makes it particularly susceptible to the transmission of atmospheric waves from other regions via the westerly waveguide.

这些大气波动能够影响高层大气动力过程，继而可能影响加勒比海海洋热浪。

These atmospheric waves can influence upper-layer atmospheric dynamics, which in turn may affect MHWs in CS.

印度洋（IO）海洋热浪因其对区域气候、生态系统和渔业的深刻影响而日益受到关注[27,36]。

MHWs in the Indian Ocean (IO) have garnered increasing attention due to their profound impacts on regional climate, ecosystems, and fisheries[27,36].

这些极端增暖事件通常由大尺度气候强迫与局地海气相互作用共同驱动。

These extreme warming events are often driven by a combination of large-scale climatic forcings and local air-sea interactions.

印度洋偶极子（IOD）和厄尔尼诺—南方涛动（ENSO）的正位相被认为是重要前兆，它们有利于形成海盆尺度的增暖型态，并可能进一步增强为印度洋海洋热浪[3,27,32]。

Positive phases of the Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD) and El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO) are recognized as key precursors, favoring basin-scale warming patterns that can intensify into MHWs in Indian Ocean[3,27,32].

与此同时，海洋热平流和混合层动力过程等局地海洋过程，也在海洋热浪的发生和持续中发挥关键作用[31,42]。

Meanwhile, local oceanic processes—such as ocean heat advection and mixed-layer dynamics—also play crucial roles in their onset and persistence[31,42].

另外，有研究表明，印度洋变率可以通过大气遥相关影响大西洋气候[43,44]。

Separately, studies have shown that Indian Ocean variability can influence Atlantic climate via atmospheric teleconnections[43,44].

这提出了一个关键问题：离散而强烈的印度洋海洋热浪，是否也能对大西洋产生远程影响？

This raises a key question: can discrete, intense IO MHWs also trigger remote impacts on the Atlantic?

强烈且局地化的海洋热浪在强迫远端大西洋极端事件方面的具体作用，目前仍不清楚。

The specific role of intense, localized MHW in forcing remote Atlantic extremes remains unclear.

本研究通过分析观测资料和气候模式模拟，建立了印度洋与加勒比海海洋热浪之间的海盆间遥相关联系。

In this work, we establish an inter-basin teleconnection between MHWs in the IO and those in the CS by analyzing observational data and climate model simulations.

我们提出一种大气桥机制：北半球冬季印度洋海洋热浪强迫产生向东传播的印度洋—太平洋—大西洋罗斯贝波列；气候模式模拟部分再现了这一波列。

We propose an atmospheric bridge mechanism, in which boreal winter IO MHWs force an eastward-propagating Indo-Pacific-Atlantic Rossby wave train, which we partially reproduce in climate model simulations.

该波列引起异常的区域哈德莱环流，并减少海洋潜热损失，从而驱动海表增暖、促进加勒比海海洋热浪增强；局地海气边界层条件使这种异常增暖能够持续到随后春季和夏季。

This wave train induces anomalous regional Hadley circulation and reduces latent heat loss from the ocean, thereby driving sea surface warming and promoting the intensification of CS MHWs; local air–sea boundary layer conditions allow this anomalous warming to persist into the following spring and summer.

结果

Results

北半球冬季印度洋海洋热浪（MHWs）与随后春季（MAM）和夏季（JJA）加勒比海海洋热浪的关系

Relationships between boreal winter Indian Ocean Marien heatwaves (MHWs) and the following spring (MAM) and summer (JJA) Caribbean Sea MHWs

观测资料表明，DJF期间的印度洋海洋热浪，常伴随着随后春季（MAM）和夏季（JJA）的加勒比海海洋热浪（见图S1、S2）。

Observational data indicate that IO MHWs during the DJF are often accompanied by subsequent MHWs in CS during the following spring (MAM) and summer (JJA) seasons (see Figs. S1, 2).

为评估这两个区域的海洋热浪事件，我们基于区域平均的逐月海洋热浪日数构建了海洋热浪指数（MHWI）：印度洋区域为50°–70°E、0°–10°S，加勒比海区域为50°–85°W、8°–24°N。

To assess MHW events in these two regions, we develop marine heatwave index (MHWI) based on the regionally averaged monthly MHW days (50°–70°E, 0°–10°S for IO and 50°–85°W, 8°–24°N for CS).

印度洋MHWI与加勒比海MHWI的超前—滞后相关分布（图1a）表明，在不同月份中，印度洋海洋热浪通常超前于加勒比海MHWI，且这一超前—滞后关系在北半球冬季（DJF）最为突出。

The distribution of lead-lag correlation between the IO MHWI and CS MHWI (Fig. 1a) indicates that IO MHW generally leads CS MHWI across various months, with the lead-lag relationship being most pronounced in boreal winter (DJF).

超前时间约为3–6个月，这表明DJF印度洋海洋热浪可能与随后春季（MAM）和夏季（JJA）的加勒比海海洋热浪相联系。

The lead time is approximately 3–6 months, suggesting that IO MHW in the DJF may be linked to CS MHW in the following spring (MAM) and summer (JJA).

随后，我们对前一北半球冬季的季节平均印度洋MHWI，与随后春季（图1b）和夏季（图1c）的季节平均加勒比海MHWI进行了交叉谱分析。

Subsequently, cross-spectral analysis was performed on the seasonally averaged IO MHWI from the previous boreal winter and the seasonally averaged CS MHWI from the following spring (Fig. 1b) and summer (Fig. 1c).

结果表明，高频部分存在显著的相干关系，相干性大于0.5，且几乎没有相位滞后。

The results show significant coherence greater than 0.5 in the high-frequency portion, with nearly no phase lag.

图1d给出了强印度洋海洋热浪事件（见表S1和图S1）前后印度洋MHWI与加勒比海MHWI的合成分析。

Figure 1d shows the composite analysis of IO MHWI and CS MHWI before and after strong IO MHW events (shown in Table S1 and Fig. S1).

印度洋MHWI在北半球冬季达到峰值后，加勒比海MHWI开始上升，并在春季和夏季达到峰值。

CS MHWI begins to increase after IO MHWI peaks in boreal winter, reaching its peak during the spring and summer seasons.

我们计算了DJF印度洋MHWI与加勒比海MHWI的超前—滞后相关（图1e）；结果显示，前一DJF印度洋海洋热浪与3–6个月后、即4月至7月发生的加勒比海海洋热浪之间存在显著关系（平均r = 0.56，p < 0.05）。

We computed the lead-lag correlation between DJF IO MHWI and the CS MHWI (Fig. 1e) and the results reveal a significant relationship between MHWs in IO during previous DJF and subsequent MHWs in the CS during April to July, occurring 3–6 months later (averaged r = 0.56, p < 0.05).

此外，与基于原始SST异常的相关相比，基于海洋热浪的相关明显更强，峰值也更尖锐（图S3）。

Moreover, the MHW-based correlation is notably stronger and exhibits a sharper peak than that based on raw SST anomalies (Fig. S3).

值得注意的是，两区域海洋热浪的海气耦合特征相反：印度洋海洋热浪倾向于影响上方大气，而加勒比海海洋热浪倾向于受到大气影响（图S4）。

Notably, the two regions exhibit contrasting MHW air–sea coupling characteristics: IO MHWs tend to influence the overlying atmosphere, whereas CS MHWs tend to be influenced by the atmosphere (Fig. S4).

基于对两海域海洋热浪的分析，我们推测DJF印度洋海洋热浪活动可通过物理机制影响同期及随后加勒比海海洋热浪。

Based on the analysis of MHWs in the two sea areas, we speculate that the activity of DJF IO MHW can influence the concurrent and subsequent CS MHW through physical mechanisms.

印度洋—太平洋—大西洋罗斯贝波列中大气桥的产生与传播特征

The generation and propagation characteristics of the atmospheric bridge in Indo-Pacific-Atlantic Rossby wave tran

由于印度洋与加勒比海位于不同海盆，印度洋海洋热浪不能直接影响遥远的加勒比海。

Given that IO and the CS reside in different ocean basins, MHWs in the IO cannot directly influence the distant CS.

因此，所观测到的遥相关不能归因于直接的海洋过程。

The observed teleconnection, consequently, cannot be ascribed to direct oceanic processes.

相反，这类跨海盆相互作用通常与大尺度大气活动有关[10]。

Instead, large-scale atmospheric activities are typically linked to such cross-ocean-basin interactions[10].

我们将其称为印度洋—太平洋—大西洋遥相关，它可能是一支向东传播的罗斯贝波列（IPAT）。

We term this the Indo-Pacific-Atlantic teleconnection, which may potentially be an eastward-propagating Rossby wave train (IPAT).

我们假设，DJF印度洋海洋热浪的信号和能量通过IPAT向东传输到北大西洋，随后经局地海气相互作用增强加勒比海海洋热浪活动。

We hypothesize that the signals and energy from DJF IO MHWs are transmitted eastward to the North Atlantic Ocean via the IPAT, subsequently enhancing MHW activities in CS through local air-sea interactions.

在强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间，热带西印度洋、特别是赤道附近及其以南地区，出现了超过0.5 °C的SST异常（SSTa）（见图S2a）。

During intense DJF IO MHWs, SST anomalies (SSTa) exceeding 0.5 °C were observed in the tropical western IO, particularly near and south of the equator (see Fig. S2a).

与此同时，海洋热浪日数异常的空间型态与SSTa的分布十分相似。

Concurrently, the spatial pattern of MHW day anomalies closely resembled the distribution of SSTa.

如图S2a所示，与气候态相比，强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间，热带西印度洋大部分地区的海洋热浪日数每月增加超过6天。

As depicted in Fig. S2a, compared to the climatological state, the MHW days in most regions of the tropical western IO increased by over 6 days per month during intense DJF IO MHWs.

强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间，赤道印度洋的暖SST异常形成了大气桥，改变其上方的大气环流。

Warm SSTa in the equatorial Indian Ocean during intense DJF IO MHWs forms an atmospheric bridge that alters the overlying atmospheric circulation.

观测表明，在强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间，印度洋部分区域上方的上升运动贯穿整个大气层，其中心位于赤道附近，与海洋热浪活跃中心相对应（图S5a）。

Observations reveal that during intense DJF IO MHWs, ascending motions permeate the entire atmosphere over certain regions of the IO, with a center situated near the equator, corresponding to the active MHW center (Fig. S5a).

同时，在强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间，赤道西印度洋和东非出现显著降水异常（图S5b、c）。

Meanwhile, during strong DJF IO MHWs, significant precipitation anomalies occur over the equatorial western Indian Ocean and East Africa (Fig. S5b, c).

这些异常可能与印度洋海洋热浪的暖SST有关；此前研究也曾得出暖印度洋SST增强东非降水的类似结论[45,46]。

These anomalies are likely associated with the warm SSTs of the IO MHWs, and similar conclusions regarding enhanced East African rainfall by warm Indian Ocean SSTs have been reported in previous studies[45,46].

赤道西印度洋和东非的降水异常及其伴随的潜热释放，可能对高层大气构成扰动，从而激发罗斯贝波活动。

The precipitation anomalies and associated latent heat release over the equatorial western Indian Ocean and East Africa may act as a disturbance to the upper-level atmosphere, thereby exciting Rossby wave activity.

这表明，DJF印度洋海洋热浪可以作为一种扰动，通过影响大气上升运动和降水，触发高层大气罗斯贝波列，从而启动IPAT大气桥过程。

This indicates that the DJF IO MHWs can function as a perturbation, triggering upper atmospheric Rossby wave trains by influencing atmospheric ascending motions and precipitation, thereby initiating the IPAT atmospheric bridge process.

为确定IPAT发生在动力学上是否可行，我们分析了印度洋上空同期的罗斯贝波活动。

To ascertain the dynamic feasibility of the IPAT occurrence, we conducted an analysis of concurrent Rossby wave activity over the IO.

我们通过计算200 hPa罗斯贝波源（RWS）的变化，量化罗斯贝波列活动强度的变化。

Variations in the intensity of Rossby wave train activity were quantified by calculating changes in the Rossby wave source (RWS) at 200 hPa.

图S6表明，DJF印度洋海洋热浪活跃区域附近的RWS显著增强，说明DJF印度洋海洋热浪能够增强热带西印度洋和东非上空同期的罗斯贝波活动。

Figure S6 reveals a significant enhancement of the RWS near the active region of the DJF IO MHWs, indicating that the DJF IO MHWs can lead to increased concurrent Rossby wave activity over tropical western IO and East Africa.

此外，我们基于水平非均匀基本流，计算了200 hPa等压面上纬向波数1–7的定常罗斯贝波轨迹（图2a），重点考察热带印度洋上空（0°–10°S，30°–70°E）的波源。

Additionally, stationary Rossby wave trajectories in 200 hPa isobaric surface were computed based on non-uniform horizontal basic flows for Rossby standing waves with zonal wave numbers ranging from 1 to 7 (Fig. 2a), focusing on the wave source above the tropical IO (0°–10°S, 30°–70°E).

然后，根据波射线追踪结果，我们计算了罗斯贝波射线通量（WRF），以更好地分析传播路径和强度（图2b）。

Then, based on the results of wave ray tracing, we calculated the Rossby wave ray flux (WRF) to better analyze the propagation paths and intensity (Fig. 2b).

波射线追踪与WRF的结果展示了罗斯贝波列的完整轨迹：波列在强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间产生于赤道印度洋上空，起初在背景东风作用下向西传播至东非。

The results of the wave ray tracing and WRF depict the full trajectory of the Rossby wave train: generated over the equatorial Indian Ocean during intense DJF IO MHWs, it initially propagates westward toward East Africa under the background easterlies.

随后，与认为偏南气流有利于能量向北传播的理论研究[47,48,49]一致，波列从东非转向北传播，抵达北非和中东。

Subsequently, consistent with theoretical works[47,48,49], which posit that a southerly flow enables northward energy propagation, the wave train turns northward from East Africa to northern Africa and the Middle East.

在背景经向气流引导下，这一路径最终使起源于热带的波列到达西风带，再由西风带引导向东传播至北大西洋。

Steered by the background meridional flow, this path ultimately allows the tropical-originated wave train to reach the westerly wind belt, which guides it eastward to the North Atlantic.

图2b给出了强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间200 hPa经向风场异常。

Figure 2b depicts the anomalies of meridional wind fields at the 200 hPa level during the intense DJF IO MHWs.

通过分析同期大气环流异常和WRF，可以识别出与IPAT有关的几个关键大气活动中心，从而阐明其在大气中的传播路径。

Through the analysis of concurrent atmospheric circulation anomalies and WRF, several key atmospheric activity centers associated with IPAT can be identified, thereby elucidating its propagation path in the atmosphere.

图2a的波射线轨迹和图2b的WRF矢量均与大气环流异常中心相吻合，这证实了IPAT在动力学上的合理性。

The alignment of both the wave ray trajectories in Fig. 2a and the WRF vectors in Fig. 2b with the centers of atmospheric circulation anomalies affirms the dynamical rationality of the IPAT.

此外，我们使用水行星配置的CAM6数值模式开展模拟试验，研究DJF印度洋海洋热浪与IPAT之间的远程相关。

Furthermore, a simulation experiment was conducted to investigate the remote correlation between the DJF IO MHWs and the IPAT, utilizing the CAM6 numerical model in aquaplanet mode.

如图2c所示，模拟总体上再现了与IPAT相关的大尺度波动活动特征。

As illustrated in Fig. 2c, it broadly reproduces the large-scale wave activity characteristics associated with the IPAT.

起源于印度洋上空的波射线先向西传播，直到进入西风带后转而向东传播，其轨迹与观测资料一致。

Wave rays originating over the IO propagated westward till entering the westerlies and then propagated eastward, exhibiting a trajectory consistent with observational data.

太平洋和大西洋的低纬度至中纬度也出现了大气波动活动中心；模拟在北大西洋上空产生了局地大气环流异常，但其具体空间型态和振幅与再分析资料有所不同。

Atmospheric wave activity centers also emerged in the low to middle latitudes of the Pacific and Atlantic Oceans, with the simulation producing local atmospheric circulation anomalies over the North Atlantic, though the detailed spatial pattern and amplitude differ from those in the reanalysis data.

这些差异可能源于水行星配置的局限：其中背景波导更加沿纬向分布，导致波列也更偏向纬向。

These discrepancies may be attributed to limitations in the aquaplanet setup, where the background wave guide is more zonal, leading to a more zonally oriented wave train.

不过，加勒比海上空仍出现了相似的经向型态，进而起到减弱哈德莱环流的作用。

However, a similar meridional pattern happens over the CS, which in turn acts to reduce the Hadley cell circulation.

总体而言，模式模拟大体再现了由DJF印度洋海洋热浪驱动的大气波列向东传播的特征，并呈现出与IPAT类似的特点。

Overall, the model simulation broadly reproduces the eastward propagation characteristics of the atmospheric wave train driven by DJF IO MHWs, exhibiting features analogous to those of the IPAT.

印度洋—太平洋—大西洋罗斯贝波列在加勒比海的海气相互作用，及其对春季（MAM）和夏季（JJA）加勒比海海洋热浪的影响

The air-sea interaction of the Indo-Pacific-Atlantic Rossby wave train in the Caribbean Sea and its impact on spring (MAM) and summer (JJA) Caribbean Sea MHWs

在DJF期间，IPAT通过罗斯贝波列将印度洋信号传输至太平洋和北大西洋。

During DJF, IPAT transmits signals from the IO to the Pacific and North Atlantic via Rossby wave trains.

为阐明北大西洋上空大气对这一信号的同期响应，我们研究了200 hPa高层大气的辐合与辐散，以及500 hPa中层大气的垂直运动（图3a）。

To elucidate the atmospheric response over the North Atlantic to this signal during the concurrent period, we investigate the convergence and divergence in the upper atmosphere at 200 hPa, as well as the vertical motion in the middle atmosphere at 500 hPa (Fig. 3a).

在强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间，可以观测到罗斯贝波活动引起的显著高层大气辐合与辐散，它们分别在中层大气诱发下沉和上升运动。

Significant convergence and divergence movements in the upper atmosphere, resulting from Rossby wave activity, are observed during the intense DJF IO MHWs, thereby inducing descending and ascending motions, respectively, in the middle atmosphere.

大气下沉和上升的活动中心分别位于加勒比海以南的赤道附近（10°S–10°N），以及其北侧的中低纬度地区（20°–30°N）。

The activity centers of atmospheric sinking and rising are respectively located near the equator to the south of the CS (10°S–10°N) and in the mid-to-low latitude regions to the north (20°–30°N).

为考察这种经向运动偶极型态对海洋的影响，我们分析了强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间整个大气柱的动力和热力特征，从动力与热力两个角度研究DJF IPAT信号对同期北大西洋大气的影响。

To investigate the impact of these movement patterns dipole in the meridional direction on the ocean, we examined the dynamical and thermal characteristics of the entire atmospheric column during intense DJF IO MHWs, thereby analyzing the influence of the DJF IPAT signals on the concurrent North Atlantic atmosphere from both dynamical and thermal perspectives.

从动力角度看，DJF大气辐合与辐散导致赤道附近下沉、中低纬度上升，有利于同期形成与背景环流相反的反哈德莱环流异常（图3b和图S8）。

From a dynamical perspective, the DJF atmospheric convergence and divergence result in downward motion near the equator and upward motion in the middle and low latitudes, favoring the formation of a concurrent anti-Hadley circulation anomaly that is opposite to the background circulation (Fig. 3b and Fig. S8).

因此，这一DJF异常减弱了大气垂直运动，并在加勒比海上空同时产生异常偏南风。

Consequently, this anomaly in DJF attenuates the vertical motion of the atmosphere and generates an anomalous southerly wind over the CS concurrently.

从热力角度看，强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间，北大西洋上空整个大气柱都发生了不同幅度的增暖（图3b）。

From a thermal perspective, the entire atmospheric column over the North Atlantic has undergone warming of varying magnitudes during intense DJF IO MHWs (Fig. 3b).

这一现象源于大气对波动活动的调整：整个大气层的位势高度升高，继而使大气厚度增加（见图S9a）。

This phenomenon is attributed to atmospheric adjustments in response to wave activity, leading to an increase in the geopotential height of the entire atmospheric layer, which in turn results in an enhancement of atmospheric thickness (see Fig. S9a).

Li等[50]表明，大气厚度增加有助于整个大气层增暖。

Li et al.[50] demonstrate that an increase in atmospheric thickness contributes to the warming of the entire atmospheric layer.

值得注意的是，高层大气增暖幅度超过低层，这通常意味着大气结构更加稳定、对流减弱。

Notably, the amplitude of warming in the higher atmospheric levels exceeds that in the lower levels, typically suggesting a more stable atmospheric structure and diminished convection.

Consistent with the methodological framework of Gupta et al.[32], we further examine the daily SST during the 3–6 months following DJF IO MHW events and found that mean SST is significantly higher than climatology (p < 0.01) while the variance shows no significant difference (p = 0.06; Fig. S11).

这表明，加勒比海海洋热浪活动增加主要由背景SST升高驱动，而非由逐日变率变化驱动；背景SST升高是上述大气强迫的直接结果。

This indicates that the increased CS MHW activity is primarily driven by the rise in background SST—a direct consequence of the atmospheric forcing identified above—rather than by changes in daily variability.

这种大气强迫表现为图3c中的大尺度环流异常，其中经向大气运动的偶极型态在低层大气中诱发了环流异常偶极子。

This atmospheric forcing is manifested in the large‑scale circulation anomalies shown in Fig. 3c, where the meridional atmospheric movement dipole has induced a dipole of circulation anomalies in the lower atmosphere.

500 hPa大气异常下沉（上升）区域，在同期低层大气925 hPa对应为异常反气旋（气旋）中心。

Regions of abnormal atmospheric descent (ascent) at 500 hPa transform into concurrent anomalous anticyclonic (cyclonic) centers in the lower atmosphere at 925 hPa.

加勒比海南部的异常反气旋和北部的异常气旋形成的流场，与异常哈德莱环流下部的异常偏南风一致，两者均可解释海表异常偏南风的成因。

The anomalous anticyclone in the southern CS and the anomalous cyclone in the north form a flow field that is consistent with the anomalous southerly winds in the lower part of the abnormal Hadley circulation, both of which can explain the cause of the anomalous southerly winds at the sea surface.

异常偏南风与背景风场方向相反，因而减弱了局地背景风场，进而降低海表风速。

The anomalous southerly winds, being opposite in direction to the background wind field, weaken the local background wind field and consequently reduce the sea surface wind speed.

与此同时，这也有利于DJF期间更温暖、更湿润的空气从赤道地区流入，从而减小海气界面的温度差和湿度差（见图S9b）。

Simultaneously, this also facilitates the influx of warmer and more humid air from equatorial regions in DJF, thereby decreasing the temperature and humidity differences at the air-sea interface (see Fig. S9b).

整个大气层增暖，包括海表附近气温升高，使海气界面的温差减小。

The warming of the entire atmospheric layer, including an increase in sea surface air temperature, reduces the temperature difference at the air-sea interface.

在多种因素共同作用下，强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间，加勒比海海水蒸发受到抑制，海气界面潜热损失异常减少，DJF加勒比海海洋热浪因而活跃起来。

Under the influence of various factors, the evaporation of seawater in the CS is suppressed during intense DJF IO MHWs, leading to an anomalous reduction in the latent heat loss at the air-sea interface and the activation of DJF CS MHW.

为评估DJF印度洋海洋热浪对随后春季（MAM）和夏季（JJA）加勒比海海洋热浪的影响，我们计算了DJF印度洋海洋热浪发生前后数个月的加勒比海区域平均（50°–85°W，8°–24°N）哈德莱环流指数（HCI）、海气界面温差（dT）、相对湿度和海气界面潜热通量（海洋增暖时为正）（图3d）。

To assessing the impact of the DJF IO MHW on the CS MHW during subsequent spring (MAM) and summer (JJA) seasons, we computed the Hadley Cell Index (HCI), air-sea interface temperature difference (dT), relative humidity, and latent heat flux (positive when warming the ocean) at the air-sea interface for the CS regional average (50°–85°W, 8°–24°N) over a few months before and after the occurrence of the DJF IO MHW (Fig. 3d).

HCI定义为850 hPa与200 hPa纬向风速分量之差。

The HCI is defined as the difference between the zonal wind speed component at 850 hPa and that at 200 hPa.

在强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间，异常哈德莱环流、低层大气湿度增加和dT减小在随后两个月仍相对显著；在DJF印度洋海洋热浪之后3–5个月内，这些量没有剧烈变化，而是稳定在气候态水平附近。

During the intense DJF IO MHW, the anomalous Hadley circulation, the increase in low-level atmospheric humidity, and the decrease in dT all remain relatively significant for the subsequent two months and exhibit no drastic changes within 3–5 months following the DJF IO MHW, but instead stabilize near the climatological norm.

换言之，DJF印度洋海洋热浪所触发的信号能够增强同期及随后两个月的加勒比海海洋热浪活动，这与同期加勒比海海洋热浪逐渐活跃相对应。

In other words, the signals triggered by the DJF IO MHW can enhance the activity of the CS MHW during the same period and the subsequent two months, which corresponds to the gradual activation of concurrent CS MHW.

而这种环流环境在接下来3–5个月并未衰减，反而仍处于活跃状态，有利于加勒比海海洋热浪活动在春季（MAM）和夏季（JJA）保持较强。

And this circulation environment does not decay over the next 3–5 months, instead remaining in an active state, which facilitates the activity of CS MHWs to remain strong during spring (MAM) and summer (JJA).

这一现象值得进一步研究，因为暖SST异常通常会引发表面热通量反馈，抑制暖SST异常，使其无法长期持续[51,54,55]。

This phenomenon warrants further investigation, as warm SSTa typically elicits surface heat flux feedback that damps the warm SST anomalies, preventing their prolonged persistence[51,54,55].

在表面热通量反馈中，海洋大气边界层（MABL）对SST异常的热力调整具有重要作用[56,57,58]。

The Marine Atmospheric Boundary Layer (MABL) plays a significant role in the thermodynamic adjustment of SSTa in the surface heat flux feedback[56,57,58].

MABL是紧邻海表的低层大气。

The MABL is a layer of the lower atmosphere close to the sea surface.

在这一层内，空气物理性质沿垂直方向相对均匀，垂直运动强烈。

In this layer, the physical properties of the air are relatively uniform in the vertical direction, and vertical motion is intense.

由于MABL内空气的热含量和水汽容纳能力有限，海表附近大气从海水吸收热量和水汽后，会迅速与海水达到热平衡，此后两者之间不再继续交换热量[54]。

Due to the limited heat content and moisture-holding capacity of the air within the MABL, the atmosphere near the sea surface rapidly reaches thermal equilibrium with the seawater after absorbing heat and water vapor from it, and no further heat exchange takes place between them[54].

此时，MABL内强烈的垂直运动能够通过混合和输送消除低层空气的温湿异常，打破海气界面热平衡，使海水可以继续向空气散失热量。

At this point, the intense vertical motion within the MABL can eliminate the temperature and humidity anomalies in the lower air through mixing and transport, disrupting the thermal equilibrium at the air-sea interface and allowing the seawater to continue to lose heat to the air.

这一过程受到MABL稳定度、厚度以及海表上方空气物理特征的影响[59,60,61,62,63]。

This process is influenced by the stability and thickness of the MABL, as well as the physical characteristics of the air over the sea surface[59,60,61,62,63].

然而，如前所述，DJF IPAT信号引起的大气差异增暖会显著影响同期MABL特征。

However, as discussed previously, the differential warming of the atmosphere induced by the DJF IPAT signal exerts a significant influence on the concurrent MABL characteristics.

IPAT引起的高层增暖比低层更强（图3b），有利于提高MABL稳定度。

The warming in the upper layer caused by the IPAT is more intense than that in the lower layer (Fig. 3b), which facilitates an increase in the stability of the MABL.

此外，DJF的异常反哈德莱环流使加勒比海区域低层大气的风切变增强，MABL变薄（图S9c）。

Additionally, the anomalous anti-Hadley cell in DJF has caused an enhancement of wind shear and a thinner MABL in the lower atmosphere over CS region (Fig. S9c).

这些因素使MABL更加稳定，从而在强DJF印度洋海洋热浪期间抑制空气向上的垂直运动[59,61,63,64]。

These factors result in a more stable MABL, which suppresses the upward vertical movement of air during intense DJF IO MHWs[59,61,63,64].

海表空气通过垂直混合消除下方海水留下的暖湿特征的能力减弱，阻碍了海洋向大气散失热量的过程。

The ability of the air at the sea surface to remove the warm and moist imprint from the underlying seawater through vertical mixing is weakened, impeding the process of heat loss from the sea to the atmosphere.

加勒比海表面的水汽异常从前一冬季（DJF）持续到夏季（JJA），印证了这一过程（图3d）。

The abnormal water vapor on the surface of the CS persists from the previous winter (DJF) to summer ( JJA), corroborating this process (Fig. 3d).

这一过程最终表现为：在春季（MAM）和夏季（JJA），潜热通量没有出现表面热通量反馈冷却SST时预期的显著异常，从而使海洋热浪活动无法恢复到气候态水平。

This process ultimately manifests as the latent heat flux not showing a significant anomaly during spring (MAM) and summer (JJA) as expected in the surface heat flux feedback process to cool the SST, preventing the MHW activity from returning to the climatological norm.

相反，它仅使潜热通量维持在气候态水平附近，延长了IPAT信号对加勒比海海洋热浪的影响，使其从前一冬季到夏季（JJA）一直保持活跃。

Instead, it merely maintains the latent heat flux near the climatological norm, prolonging the impact of the IPAT signal on the CS MHW and keeping it active from the previous winter to summer (JJA).

We also used the data from ERA5 to support the conclusions presented in Fig. 3a and Fig. 3b (see Fig. S7).

讨论

Discussion

本研究揭示了一条海盆间遥相关路径，将北半球冬季印度洋海洋热浪与增强的加勒比海春夏季海洋热浪联系起来（图4）。

This study reveals an inter‑basin teleconnection pathway linking boreal winter Indian Ocean MHWs to intensified spring–summer Caribbean Sea MHWs (Fig. 4).

我们提出一种大气桥机制，如图4a所概括：印度洋海洋热浪引起的深对流和降水激发了向东传播的印度洋—太平洋—大西洋罗斯贝波列（IPAT）。

We propose an atmospheric bridge mechanism, summarized in Fig. 4a, in which IO MHW‑induced deep convection and precipitation excites an eastward‑propagating Indo‑Pacific‑Atlantic Rossby wave train (IPAT).

如图4b所示，IPAT到达北大西洋后，会驱动异常的反哈德莱环流，并使上方大气柱增暖。

As shown in Fig. 4b, upon reaching the North Atlantic, the IPAT drives an anomalous anti‑Hadley circulation and warms the overlying atmospheric column.

这些变化减小了海气温度与湿度梯度、减弱海表风，继而抑制蒸发，导致海洋潜热损失不足。

These changes reduce the air–sea temperature and humidity gradients, weaken surface winds, and then suppress evaporation, leading to a deficit in latent heat loss from the ocean.

由此产生的海表增暖，在海洋大气边界层的稳定作用下持续到随后春季和夏季（图4c）。

The resulting surface warming persists into the following spring and summer through the stabilizing effect of the marine atmospheric boundary layer (Fig. 4c).

对1986–1987年DJF印度洋海洋热浪事件的详细个例研究证实了这一机制的稳健性（图S12–S16）。

The robustness of this mechanism is confirmed by a detailed case study of the 1986–1987 DJF IO MHW event (Figs. S12–S16).

所提出的机制为海洋热浪发生提供了季节尺度的预测因子，并展示了一个海盆的极端事件如何远程调控另一个海盆的极端事件。

This proposed mechanism offers a seasonal‑scale predictor for MHW occurrences and demonstrates how extreme events in one basin can remotely orchestrate extreme events in another.

Then, IO MHWs trigger a Rossby wave train propagating through the atmospheric bridge on the upper level, leading to a divergence anomaly on the upper level over the North Atlantic as well as the atmospheric thickness increase.

b 底层异常哈德莱环流导致水汽和热量流入，同时风速降低，最终使蒸发减弱、潜热损失减少、表层海水增暖，促使加勒比海海洋热浪在同期活跃起来。

IOD和ENSO在印度洋—加勒比海海洋热浪遥相关中可能起到的调节作用，值得认真讨论。

The potential modulating role of the IOD and the ENSO in the IO–CS MHW teleconnection warrants careful discussion.

已知IOD和ENSO都强烈影响印度洋海洋热浪变率[27,37]，同时也对大西洋SST产生远程影响[65,66]。

Both IOD and ENSO are known to strongly influence the variability of MHWs in the Indian Ocean[27,37], and they also exert remote effects on Atlantic SST[65,66].

因此，它们可能有助于建立有利于印度洋—加勒比海海洋热浪遥相关的大尺度背景状态。

It is therefore plausible that they contribute to establishing the large-scale background state that facilitates the IO–CS MHW teleconnection.

然而，本研究的多方面证据表明，所识别的滞后关系在一定程度上仍独立于这些主导气候模态。

However, in this study, several lines of evidence suggest that the identified lagged relationship remains, to a certain extent, independent of these dominant climate modes.

从统计上看，通过滤波去除海洋热浪指数中的IOD和ENSO信号后，印度洋MHWI与加勒比海MHWI之间仍存在显著关联，保留了原始方差的51%（图S17和表S2）。

Statistically, after removing the signals of IOD and ENSO from the MHW indices through filtering, a significant association persists between the IO and CS MHWI, retaining 51% of the original variance (Fig. S17 and Table S2).

相比之下，原始ENSO—加勒比海海洋热浪相关系数（ENSO超前加勒比海MHWI 5个月）为0.44（R² = 19.4%）。

In contrast, the original ENSO–CS MHW correlation (ENSO leads CS MHWI by 5 months) is 0.44 (R² = 19.4%).

从加勒比海MHWI中去除印度洋海洋热浪影响后，偏相关降至0.19（R² = 3.6%，p < 0.05），仅保留原始ENSO—加勒比海海洋热浪方差的18%（表S2和图S18）。

After removing the influence of IO MHWs from CS MWHI, the partial correlation drops to 0.19 (R² = 3.6%, p < 0.05), retaining only 18% of the original ENSO–CS MHW variance (Table S2 and Fig. S18).

原始IOD—加勒比海海洋热浪相关系数为0.35（R² = 12.3%）；控制印度洋MHWI后，相关系数降至0.07（R² = 0.5%，p = 0.41），仅保留原始方差的4%（表S2和图S19）。

The original IOD–CS MHW correlation is 0.35 (R² = 12.3%); after controlling for IO MHWI, it falls to 0.07 (R² = 0.5%, p = 0.41), retaining only 4% of the original variance (Table S2 and Fig. S19).

这些结果表明，ENSO和IOD与加勒比海海洋热浪的表观关系，在很大程度上由印度洋起中介作用；而印度洋—加勒比海海洋热浪遥相关保留了相当大一部分独立于这些气候模态的成分。

These results demonstrate that the apparent relationships of ENSO and IOD with CS MHWs are largely mediated through the Indian Ocean, while the IO–CS MHW teleconnection retains a substantial component independent of these climate modes.

从动力学上看，这种大气响应型态以从印度洋延伸到加勒比海的中纬度罗斯贝波列为特征，与通常受ENSO或IOD扰动的典型东西向翻转环流不同。

Dynamically, the atmospheric response pattern—characterized by a mid-latitude Rossby wave train extending from the Indian Ocean to the Caribbean Sea—differs from the typical east–west overturning circulations usually perturbed by ENSO or IOD.

印度洋海洋热浪事件期间，热带大西洋对流层低层没有出现稳健的海盆范围纬向风异常（图S20），这支持了上述判断；如果遥相关主要通过变化的沃克环流实现，那么理应出现这种异常。

This is supported by the absence of robust, basin-wide zonal wind anomalies in the tropical Atlantic lower troposphere during IO MHW events (Fig. S20), which would be expected if the teleconnection were primarily mediated by an altered Walker circulation.

此外，去除ENSO和IOD信号后，印度洋海洋热浪期间加勒比海上空异常的反哈德莱环流并未发生根本改变（图S21）。

Furthermore, after removing the ENSO and IOD signals, the anomalous anti-Hadley circulation over the Caribbean Sea during IO MHW shows no fundamental change (Fig. S21).

综上，尽管IOD和ENSO可能塑造背景条件，并有可能增强印度洋与加勒比海海洋热浪之间的联系，但与加勒比海海洋热浪之间这一特定的3–6个月滞后联系，似乎主要通过IPAT机制实现。

In summary, although IOD and ENSO may shape the background conditions and could potentially enhance the connection between IO MHWs and CS MHWs, the specific 3–6 month lagged linkage with CS MHWs appears to be primarily channeled through the IPAT mechanism.

本研究强调，即便印度洋区域性极端SST增暖事件部分嵌入更大尺度的气候变率之中，它们仍可能激活不同的大气路径，随后影响遥远加勒比海的海洋热浪。

This study highlights that extreme regional SST warming events in the Indian Ocean, even when partially embedded within larger-scale climate variability, may activate distinct atmospheric pathways that subsequently influence MHWs in the remote Caribbean Sea.

本研究识别出一条不同于既有文献所强调的经典机制的海盆间遥相关路径。

This study identifies a distinct pathway for inter-basin teleconnection that differs from the canonical mechanisms emphasized in prior literature.

此前涉及太平洋的海气联系研究，大多强调赤道沃克环流大尺度调整的核心作用[67,68]。

Previous investigations into ocean-atmosphere linkages involving the Pacific have largely highlighted the central role of large-scale adjustments in the equatorial Walker Circulation[67,68].

此外，正如Wang[65]所指出的，印度洋主动影响北大西洋气候的具体过程仍缺乏充分认识，现有研究主要关注赤道大西洋变率或海盆尺度模态。

Furthermore, as noted by Wang[65], the specific processes by which the Indian Ocean actively influences North Atlantic climate remain insufficiently understood, with existing research focusing primarily on equatorial Atlantic variability or basin-scale modes.

在这一背景下，我们的发现揭示了一种不同的中纬度罗斯贝波列机制。

Against this backdrop, our findings reveal a distinct mid-latitude Rossby wave train mechanism.

IPAT起源于印度洋海洋热浪相关的SST强迫，经北半球中纬度西风波导向东传播，直接影响副热带加勒比海；后者是北大西洋一个重要的热带外区域，此前未被识别为这类遥相关的主要影响对象。

The IPAT originates from SST forcing associated with the IO MHW and propagates eastward via the Northern Hemisphere mid-latitude westerly waveguide, directly impacting the subtropical Caribbean Sea—a key extratropical region of the North Atlantic not previously identified as a primary target of such a teleconnection.

与沿纬向的沃克环流调整不同，这一路径通过对加勒比海局地哈德莱环流的经向调节发挥作用，从而影响低层风和表面热通量。

Unlike the zonally oriented Walker Cell adjustments, this pathway operates through meridional modulation of the local Hadley circulation over the Caribbean Sea, thereby affecting low-level winds and surface heat fluxes.

因此，本研究不仅回应了早期研究指出的机制认识空缺，还刻画了一条此前被忽视的印度洋影响热带外地区的路径，为理解区域极端增暖如何远程调控另一海盆的气候极端事件提供了更细致的视角。

Thus, our work not only addresses the mechanistic gap highlighted in earlier studies but also delineates a previously overlooked extratropical route of Indian Ocean influence, offering a refined perspective on how regional extreme warming can remotely orchestrate climate extremes in another ocean basin.

已有研究指出，许多海洋和大气过程对海洋热浪具有重要影响[4,24,27,69,70]。

Existing studies have pointed out that numerous oceanic and atmospheric processes have important impacts on MHW[4,24,27,69,70].

基于这些研究，研究者提出了许多海洋热浪预测分析方法[4,33,34]。

Based on these studies, many predictive analysis methods for MHW have been proposed[4,33,34].

然而，这些研究缺少对海洋热浪彼此之间相互作用的考察，而这正是本研究的重点。

However, these studies lack research on the interaction between MHWs themselves, which is the focus of this study.

通过分析海洋热浪与大气的相互作用，并结合大气桥的连接作用，本研究将这种联系拓展至海洋热浪与海洋热浪之间，证明海洋热浪也可以像其他天气和气候系统一样相互影响并形成遥相关，使我们能够从更广阔的视角理解海洋热浪。

By analyzing the interaction between MHW and the atmosphere and combining the connecting role of the atmospheric bridge, this study extends the connection to that between one MHW and another, proving that MHWs can also interact with each other and form teleconnections like other weather and climate systems, enabling us to understand MHW from a broader perspective.

所观测到的海洋热浪超前—滞后关系可能也是其他海洋区域的共同特征，可为海洋热浪发生提供潜在的季节尺度预测因子，并对改进海洋热浪相关灾害的防灾减灾策略具有重要潜力。

The observed lead-lag relationship between MHWs may be a common feature in other oceanic regions, offering a potential seasonal-scale predictor for MHW occurrences and holding significant potential for enhancing the prevention and mitigation strategies against MHW-related disasters.

海洋热浪的定义

Definition of marine heatwaves

海洋热浪按照Hobday等[1]的方法识别，定义为一种离散、持续的异常暖水事件，其间逐日SST至少连续5天超过随季节变化的第90百分位阈值。

MHWs are identified following Hobday, et al.[1] where a MHW is defined as a discrete prolonged anomalously warm water event during which daily SST exceeds the seasonally varying 90th percentile threshold for at least five consecutive days.

本研究采用固定基准期的定义。

A fixed-baseline definition is adopted in this study.

我们使用OISSTv2.1数据集，在每个网格上研究1982–2024年的海洋热浪。

We explore the MHWs from 1982 to 2024 using the OISSTv2.1 dataset on each grid.

为消除全球变暖对海洋热浪的影响，我们从SST资料中去除了线性趋势，并以整个可用时段（1982–2024年）为基准期。

The linear trend has been removed from the SST data to eliminate the influence of global warming on MHWs, and the entire available period (1982–2024) is used as the baseline.

海洋热浪日数定义为指定时段内发生海洋热浪的总持续时间（单位：天）。

The number of MHW days is defined as the total duration during which MHW occurs within a specific time period (unit: days).

为评估一个区域的海洋热浪事件，我们构建了基于区域平均逐月海洋热浪日数的海洋热浪指数（MHWI）。

To assess MHW events in a region, a marine heatwave index (MHWI) has been developed which is based on the regionally averaged monthly MHW days.

印度洋MHWI定义为0°–10°S、50°–70°E范围内逐月海洋热浪日数的平均值。

The IO MHWI is defined as the average of the monthly MHW days within the range of 0°–10°S and 50°–70°E.

加勒比海海洋热浪指数采用与印度洋MHWI相似的方法定义，但区域为8°–24°N、50°–85°W。

The CS MHW Index is defined in a similar manner to the IO MHWI, but its region is specified as 8°–24°N and 50°–85°W.

两个区域海洋热浪日数的年循环和季节平均海洋热浪阈值，分别见图S22和图S23。

The annual cycles of MHW days and the seasonally averaged MHW thresholds for the two regions are shown in Fig. S22 and Fig. S23, respectively.

鉴于海洋热浪事件的极端性质，为更好地把握其特征，应更加关注显著强海洋热浪发生的时段。

Given the extreme nature of MHW events, to better capture their characteristics, more attention should be paid to the periods when significantly intense MHWs occur.

因此，本研究对若干次强印度洋海洋热浪进行合成分析，以揭示海洋热浪对海洋和大气的影响。

Thus, in this study, a composite analysis of several intense IO MHWs is carried out to reveal the impacts of MHWs on both the ocean and the atmosphere.

去除线性趋势和年循环后，将北半球冬季（12月、1月和2月，简称DJF）印度洋MHWI超过一个标准差的事件定义为强DJF印度洋海洋热浪。

After removing the linear trend and the annual cycle, events in which the IO MHWI in boreal winter (December, January and February, DJF for short) exceeds one standard deviation are defined as strong DJF IO MHWs.

Numerical simulation

交叉谱分析

To investigate the frequency-dependent relationship between IO MHWI and CS MHWI time series, we employed the Blackman-Tukey method of cross-spectral analysis[81].

对于两个离散时间序列x(t)和y(t)（其中t=1,2,…,n），我们估计了自谱（Pxx(f)和Pyy(f)）及交叉谱（Pxy(f)）。

For the two discrete time series, x(t) and y(t) (where t=1,2,…,n), the auto-spectra (Pxx(f) and Pyy(f)) and the cross-spectrum (Pxy(f)) were estimated.

为此，首先计算最大滞后为m的自协方差和互协方差函数，其中m取约n/5，以兼顾谱分辨率和估计结果的稳定性[82]。

This was achieved by first computing the auto-covariance and cross-covariance functions up to a maximum lag m, where m was chosen to be approximately n/5 to balance spectral resolution against the stability of the estimates[82].

随后对这些协方差函数应用滞后窗，再进行傅里叶变换以得到谱[81,82]。

A lag window is then applied to these covariance functions before performing the Fourier transform to obtain the spectra[81,82].

由所得交叉谱推导出平方相干谱和滞后谱。

通过 2 年高通滤波去除 ENSO 和 IOD 信号后，相关系数为 0.44（R² = 19.4%），仍保留原始方差的 51%。

When both ENSO and IOD signals are removed via a 2‑year high‑pass filter, the correlation is 0.44 (R² = 19.4%), again retaining 51% of the original variance.

作为比较，原始 ENSO—加勒比海相关系数（ENSO 超前加勒比海 5 个月）为 0.44（R² = 19.4%）。

For comparison, the original ENSO–CS correlation (ENSO leads CS by 5 months) is 0.44 (R² = 19.4%).

After removing the influence of IO, the partial correlation drops to 0.19, retaining only 18% of the original ENSO–CS variance.

类似地，原始 IOD—加勒比海相关系数为 0.35（R² = 12.3%）；控制印度洋影响后，该值降至 0.07（不显著），仅保留原始方差的 4%。

Similarly, the original IOD–CS correlation is 0.35 (R² = 12.3%), which reduces to 0.07 (non‑significant) after controlling for IO, retaining only 4% of the original variance.

这些结果表明，ENSO 和 IOD 与加勒比海海洋热浪之间的表观联系在很大程度上通过印度洋传递，而印度洋—加勒比海遥相关仍保留了相当一部分独立于这些气候模态的成分。

These results demonstrate that the apparent relationships of ENSO and IOD with CS MHWs are largely mediated through the Indian Ocean, while the IO–CS teleconnection retains a substantial component independent of these climate modes.
