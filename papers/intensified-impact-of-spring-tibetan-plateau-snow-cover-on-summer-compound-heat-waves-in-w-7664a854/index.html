摘要 Abstract

本研究揭示，1998年之后，春季青藏高原（TP）西部积雪（TPSC）对随后夏季西欧（WE）复合热浪（CHWs）的影响发生了显著的年代际变化。

This study revealed a significant interdecadal change in the impact of spring western Tibetan Plateau (TP) snow cover (TPSC) on subsequent summer compound heat waves (CHWs) in western Europe (WE) after 1998.

这一年代际变化归因于西欧高压—青藏高原西部低压（WEH–TPL）大气环流型的变化。

This interdecadal change is attributed to a change in a western Europe high–western TP low (WEH–TPL) atmospheric circulation pattern.

该环流型的形成既源于TPSC的内在变率，也源于1998年后大西洋多年代际振荡（AMO）的位相转变。

This pattern arises due to both the inherent variability of TPSC and the phase transition of the Atlantic multidecadal oscillation (AMO) after 1998.

1998年后，春季至夏季青藏高原西部TPSC的幅度增大且持续性增强，强化了雪—大气耦合效应，使青藏高原和西欧上空的上升运动与下沉运动分别加强，并增强了WEH–TPL型。

The increased magnitude and persistence of western TPSC from spring to summer after 1998 enhanced the snow–atmosphere coupling effect, intensifying ascent and descent motion over the TP and WE, respectively, and strengthening the WEH–TPL pattern.

此外，1998年后的AMO正位相有利于罗斯贝波持续而稳定地向下游传播，从而增强WEH–TPL型以及TPSC–CHWs关系。

In addition, the post-1998 positive AMO phase favors continuous and stable downstream Rossby wave propagation, enhancing the WEH–TPL pattern and the TPSC–CHWs relationship.

进一步分析表明，1998年前后TPSC与AMO的年代际变化促成了北大西洋—欧亚大陆中部区域“双急流”的出现。

Further analyses reveal that the interdecadal changes in the TPSC and the AMO around 1998 contribute to the presence of “double jets” over the North Atlantic–central Eurasian sectors.

与TPSC相关的异常大气环流和AMO位相转变分别促成了增强西风急流的南支和北支。

The TPSC-related anomalous atmospheric circulation and AMO phase shift contribute to the southern and northern branches of the intensified westerly jet, respectively.

这些条件为西欧夏季复合热浪的形成与持续创造了有利环境。

These conditions create a favorable environment for the formation and persistence of summer CHWs in WE.

采用线性斜压模式开展的数值模拟试验证实了这些发现。

Numerical modeling experiments with a linear baroclinic model confirm these findings.

我们的发现表明，在气候变化背景下，TPSC在西欧夏季复合热浪的形成中发挥关键作用，并可能成为复合热浪的一个有价值预测因子。

Our findings suggest that in the context of a changing climate, TPSC plays a pivotal role in the genesis of summer CHWs in WE and may serve as a valuable predictor for CHWs.

意义声明 Significance Statement

本研究发现，自1998年起，春季青藏高原西部积雪影响西欧夏季复合热浪的方式发生了显著变化。

This study discovered that starting from 1998 there was a significant change in how spring snow cover on the western Tibetan Plateau affects summer compound heat waves in western Europe.

1998年后，青藏高原西部的积雪范围扩大，从春季到夏季持续得更久，从而增强了积雪与大气之间的相互作用。

After 1998, the snow cover on the western Tibetan Plateau increased in size and lasted longer from spring to summer, intensifying the interaction between the snow and the atmosphere.

这分别导致青藏高原和西欧上空出现更强的上升气流和下沉气流。

This led to more rising and sinking air over the Tibetan Plateau and western Europe, respectively.

此外，1998年之后，大西洋多年代际振荡（AMO）的正位相有利于西欧与青藏高原之间的环流联系。

Also, after 1998 a positive phase of the Atlantic multidecadal oscillation (AMO) was favorable for the circulation connection between western Europe and the Tibetan Plateau.

进一步分析表明，1998年后的这些积雪和AMO变化在北大西洋和欧亚大陆中部造成了“双急流”，为西欧夏季热浪创造了更有利的条件。

Further analysis showed that these changes in snow cover and the AMO after 1998 caused “double jets” in the North Atlantic and central Eurasia that created better conditions for summer heat waves in western Europe.

数值模式用于证实这些发现。

Numerical models are used to confirm these findings.

我们的研究表明，在气候变化背景下，青藏高原西部积雪对西欧夏季热浪的发展起着至关重要的作用，并可成为这些热浪的实用预测因子。

Our research indicates that, in a changing climate, the snow cover on the western Tibetan Plateau plays a crucial role in the development of summer heat waves in western Europe and can be a useful predictor for these heat waves.

1. 引言 1. Introduction

多变量复合热浪（CHWs）同时涵盖温度、湿度等多个气象变量，相较于单因子高温事件，会造成更为深远的社会与生态影响（J. Li et al. 2018; G. Zhang et al. 2022; Ullah et al. 2022）。

Multivariate compound heat waves (CHWs), which encompass multiple meteorological variables concurrently, such as temperature and humidity, yield more profound societal and ecological impacts in comparison with single-factor heat events (J. Li et al. 2018; G. Zhang et al. 2022; Ullah et al. 2022).

IPCC特别报告强调，由人类活动引起的气候变化很可能已经提高了过去复合事件发生的概率，而且随着全球持续变暖，这一概率预计还会进一步上升（Seneviratne et al. 2021）。

The IPCC Special Report underscores that the probability of compound events has probably increased in the past due to human-induced climate change and is expected to further increase with ongoing global warming (Seneviratne et al. 2021).

西欧（WE）人口密度高、对气候变化高度脆弱，因而面临复合热浪对人类健康、经济发展和生物多样性构成的更大风险（Fischer and Schär 2010; Y. Zhang et al. 2022; K. Zhang et al. 2023）。

Western Europe (WE), with its high population density and high vulnerability to climate change, faces increased risks to human health, economic development, and biodiversity from compound heat waves (Fischer and Schär 2010; Y. Zhang et al. 2022; K. Zhang et al. 2023).

因此，研究导致西欧复合热浪变化的因素十分必要。

Therefore, it is imperative to examine the factors contributing to the variation of CHWs in WE.

许多既往研究已经阐明，平均地形高度达4 km的青藏高原（TP），可通过热力和动力机制影响局地及远程气候（Kuo and Qian 1981; Qiu 2008; Liu et al. 2020; Wu et al. 2023）。

Numerous prior studies have elucidated that the Tibetan Plateau (TP), with an average topography height of 4 km, can influence both local and remote climates through thermodynamical and dynamical mechanisms (Kuo and Qian 1981; Qiu 2008; Liu et al. 2020; Wu et al. 2023).

青藏高原积雪（TPSC）可通过积雪反照率效应和积雪水文效应调节热力状况，并通过雪—气相互作用使大气环流发生显著变化（W. Li et al. 2018; You et al. 2020; Wang et al. 2020; Lin and Wu 2011），例如影响东亚夏季风（Zhang et al. 2004; Xiao and Duan 2016; Lu et al. 2023）和南亚夏季风（Blanford 1884; Kripalani et al. 2003; Wu and Qian 2003）。

Snow cover over the TP (TPSC) can modulate thermal conditions through snow-albedo and snow-hydrological effects, leading to significant changes in the atmospheric circulation through snow–air interactions (W. Li et al. 2018; You et al. 2020; Wang et al. 2020; Lin and Wu 2011), such as the East Asian summer monsoon (Zhang et al. 2004; Xiao and Duan 2016; Lu et al. 2023) and South Asian summer monsoon (Blanford 1884; Kripalani et al. 2003; Wu and Qian 2003).

以往大多数研究集中探讨TPSC对局地和下游天气与气候的影响（W. Li et al. 2018; You et al. 2020; Liu et al. 2020; Lyu et al. 2018; Han et al. 2021）。

The majority of previous research focuses on investigating the impact of TPSC on local and downstream weather and climate (W. Li et al. 2018; You et al. 2020; Liu et al. 2020; Lyu et al. 2018; Han et al. 2021).

我们对TPSC如何影响上游气候的认识仍然有限。

Our comprehension of the impact of TPSC on upstream climate remains limited.

Lu et al.（2018）表明，夏季青藏高原加热增强会增强南亚高压，激发向上游传播的罗斯贝波响应，进而使北大西洋、南欧、北非和西亚的降水减少。

Lu et al. (2018) demonstrated that intensified TP heating during summer strengthens the South Asian high, initiating an upstream Rossby wave response that results in reduced precipitation over the North Atlantic, southern Europe, North Africa, and West Asia.

Anomalous TP heating also drives variations in monsoon rainfall patterns in Pakistan via a TP-induced Rossby wave response in the upper troposphere, coupled with diminished water vapor transport from the eastern Bay of Bengal to Pakistan (Wang et al. 2019).

夏季青藏高原加热增强会通过调节青藏高原西部与地中海上空的纬向环流以及横跨地中海—非洲区域的经向环流，促进萨赫勒中东部降水增加（Nan et al. 2019）。

The augmented TP heating during summer promotes increased precipitation over the central-eastern Sahel by regulating zonal circulation over the western TP and the Mediterranean Sea, as well as meridional circulation across the Mediterranean Sea–Africa region (Nan et al. 2019).

Zhang et al.（2020）揭示，欧洲热浪的年代际增加与欧亚大陆中高纬度积雪覆盖率的下降密切相关。

Zhang et al. (2020) revealed that the interdecadal increase in Europe heat waves is closely linked to the reductions in Eurasian snow cover fraction across mid- to high latitudes.

TPSC减少可能会在欧亚大陆上引发一种独特的遥相关型，通过增强陆地—大气反馈而加剧热浪（Wu et al. 2016）。

Reduced TPSC may induce a distinct teleconnection pattern across the Eurasian continent, enhancing the heat waves by intensifying land–atmosphere feedback (Wu et al. 2016).

然而，TPSC对其上游西欧随后夏季复合热浪可能产生的影响尚未得到识别。

However, the possible impact of TPSC on subsequent summer CHWs across the upstream WE have not been identified.

本研究的目的之一是探讨TPSC可能在西欧复合热浪中发挥的作用。

One of the purposes of the current study is to investigate the possible role of TPSC in the CHWs in WE.

既往研究已经表明，起源于北大西洋的大气罗斯贝波型可对欧亚大陆气候产生显著影响（Qian et al. 2019; Wu and Zhang 2010; Henderson et al. 2018; Liu et al. 2014; Chen et al. 2016; Wu et al. 2011）。

Previous research has demonstrated that atmospheric Rossby wave patterns originating from the North Atlantic can exert a substantial influence on the Eurasian climate (Qian et al. 2019; Wu and Zhang 2010; Henderson et al. 2018; Liu et al. 2014; Chen et al. 2016; Wu et al. 2011).

值得注意的是，大西洋海表温度（SST）异常可能通过诱发大气环流异常来改变青藏高原的热力状态。

Notably, Atlantic sea surface temperature (SST) anomalies may modify the thermal states on the TP by inducing anomalies in atmospheric circulation.

例如，Cui et al.（2015）揭示，春季青藏高原大气热源在很大程度上取决于副热带西风急流的强度，而后者与北大西洋三极型海温异常模态（NAT）密切相关。

For example, Cui et al. (2015) revealed that the spring atmospheric heat source over the TP depends largely on the strength of the subtropical westerly jet, which is closely related to the North Atlantic tripole SST anomaly pattern (NAT).

Chen et al.（2022）表明，NAT正位相可激发一列罗斯贝波，该波列经过斯堪的纳维亚和乌拉尔山脉后到达青藏高原，最终在青藏高原上空低层诱发正位涡。

Chen et al. (2022) demonstrated that positive NAT can trigger a Rossby wave train, traversing Scandinavia and the Ural Mountains before reaching the TP, ultimately inducing a low-level positive potential vorticity over the TP.

Yu et al.（2021）指出，3月的NAT状况可能影响青藏高原春季感热加热。

Yu et al. (2021) indicated that March NAT conditions could impact spring sensible heating over the TP.

上述研究主要关注大西洋海温异常对青藏高原状况的气候效应（P. Zhang et al. 2022），而深入探讨大西洋海温如何调节TPSC气候影响的研究相对较少。

The aforementioned research has focused on the climate effect of Atlantic SST anomalies on TP conditions (P. Zhang et al. 2022), while relatively few studies have delved into understanding how Atlantic SSTs modulate the climate impacts of TPSC.

Lu et al.（2019）发现，青藏高原加热对热带东大西洋和西非降水型的上游影响也受到大西洋海温异常的调节。

Lu et al. (2019) uncovered that the upstream influence of TP heating on the precipitation patterns over the tropical eastern Atlantic and West Africa is also modulated by the Atlantic SST anomalies.

2. 数据和方法 2. Data and method

a. 数据 a. Data

周积雪数据来自罗格斯大学全球积雪实验室（http://climate.rutgers.edu/snowcover），1979–2021年期间的水平分辨率为25 km。

The weekly snow cover is obtained from the Rutgers University Global Snow Laboratory (http://climate.rutgers.edu/snowcover), which has a horizontal resolution of 25 km for the period of 1979–2021.

随后，我们将积雪数据转换为水平分辨率为1° × 1°的月平均数据（Qian et al. 2020）。

Then, we convert the snow cover data to monthly mean data with a horizontal resolution of 1° × 1° (Qian et al. 2020).

该积雪数据集可靠且特别适用于青藏高原，其在青藏高原西部与ERA5再分析资料吻合良好（在线补充材料中的Fig. S1），这一点也已得到既往研究的证实（Dong et al. 2023a）。

This snow cover dataset is reliable and particularly suitable for the TP, showing good agreement with ERA5 reanalysis in the western part of the TP (Fig. S1 in the online supplemental material), which also has been confirmed by previous studies (Dong et al. 2023a).

逐日ERA5–UTCI是一个由ERA5再分析资料衍生而来的新数据集（https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/cdsapp#!/dataset/derived-utci-historical?tab=form）（Di Napoli et al. 2021）。

The daily ERA5–UTCI is a novel dataset derived from the ERA5 reanalysis (https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/cdsapp#!/dataset/derived-utci-historical?tab=form) (Di Napoli et al. 2021).

UTCI是评估热应激的重要指标，能够有效衡量人体舒适度（Di Napoli et al. 2021）。

The UTCI is a valuable metric for evaluating heat stress and can gauge human comfort levels effectively (Di Napoli et al. 2021).

它综合了温度、湿度、风和辐射等多个变量。

It integrates multiple variables such as temperature, humidity, wind, and radiation.

因此，与湿球黑球温度类似（Li et al. 2020; Chen et al. 2022），UTCI可用于定义多变量复合热浪（Zscheischler et al. 2020）。

Therefore, UTCI can be used to define multivariate CHWs (Zscheischler et al. 2020), similar to wet bulb globe temperature (Li et al. 2020; Chen et al. 2022).

月平均地表和大气变量取自ERA5再分析资料（https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/dataset/ecmwf-reanalysis-v5）（Hersbach et al. 2020），包括地表感热通量、地表潜热通量、地表净短波辐射通量月平均值、位势高度、垂直速度、温度以及经向风和纬向风；这些变量覆盖1979–2021年，水平分辨率为1° × 1°。

Monthly mean surface and atmospheric variables are obtained from ERA5 reanalysis data (https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/dataset/ecmwf-reanalysis-v5) (Hersbach et al. 2020), including surface sensible heat flux, surface latent heat flux, mean surface net shortwave radiation flux, geopotential height, vertical velocity, temperature, and meridional and zonal wind that are available from 1979 to 2021 with a horizontal resolution of 1° × 1°.

a. Enhanced snow–atmosphere coupling over the TP

先前研究表明，青藏高原上空的大气环流可通过雪反照率效应和水文效应受到地面积雪异常的调制（Cohen and Rind 1991; W. Li et al. 2018; You et al. 2020; Liu et al. 2020）。

Previous work revealed that the atmospheric circulations above the TP can be modulated by the surface snow cover anomalies via snow albedo and hydrological effect (Cohen and Rind 1991; W. Li et al. 2018; You et al. 2020; Liu et al. 2020).

高海拔和雪—大气耦合效应使青藏高原西部的积雪能够持续到夏季（Xu and Dirmeyer 2011; Liu et al. 2022）。

The high elevation and snow–atmosphere coupling effect enable snow cover on the western TP to persist into summer (Xu and Dirmeyer 2011; Liu et al. 2022).

因此，春季TPSC被认为是随后夏季北半球大气环流异常的一个潜在预测因子。

Consequently, spring TPSC is recognized as a potential predictor of atmospheric circulation anomalies in the Northern Hemisphere during the subsequent summer.

Previous studies demonstrated that the TP low pressure system may have the potential to induce WE high pressure via the vertical circulation or Rossby wave responses (Lu et al. 2018, 2019; Nan et al. 2019).

然而，该RWS在随后的夏季减弱（Fig. 13b）。

Nevertheless, it weakens in the following summer (Fig. 13b).

相比之下，在+AMO期间，异常RWS比−AMO期间更明显（Fig. 13c），且该异常RWS在随后的夏季仍然显著，并进一步向东延伸至青藏高原西部（Fig. 13d），这表明其为促进西欧与青藏高原之间的联系提供了更有利的背景。

In contrast, during +AMO, the anomalous RWS is more pronounced than that in −AMO (Fig. 13c) and the anomalous RWS is still significant in the subsequent summer and extends farther eastward to western TP (Fig. 13d), suggesting a more conducive background for fostering the connection between WE and the TP.

这一发现与既有研究一致；这些研究强调，+AMO会增强罗斯贝波的存在和强度，并促进扰动向中纬度传播（Sun et al. 2015; Hao et al. 2016; S. Li et al. 2018; Wu et al. 2019）。

This finding aligns with prior investigations, which highlight that a +AMO amplifies the presence and intensity of Rossby waves and facilitates the propagation of perturbations toward the midlatitudes (Sun et al. 2015; Hao et al. 2016; S. Li et al. 2018; Wu et al. 2019).

综上，上述结果表明，+AMO更有利于维持WEH–TPL型，并有利于西欧与青藏高原之间形成更强的联系。

In summary, the above result indicates that +AMO is more favorable for the maintenance of the WEH–TPL pattern and a stronger connection between the WE and the TP.

为更好地理解AMO在P1和P2时期对WEH–TPL型所起的不同作用，Figure 14给出了两个时期春季和夏季与AMO指数相关的波作用通量。

To better understand the different roles of AMO in the WEH–TPL pattern in P1 and P2, the wave activity fluxes associated with the AMO index during the spring and summer for the two periods are presented in Fig. 14.

值得注意的是，与AMO相关的大气环流异常和波作用通量在北大西洋及欧亚大陆西部区域表现出相似的空间结构。

Notably, the atmospheric circulation anomalies related to the AMO and the wave activity fluxes exhibit similar spatial structures over the North Atlantic and the western Eurasian continent sector.

然而，P2时期的异常幅度比P1时期更明显。

However, the magnitudes of the anomalies are more pronounced in P2 than in P1.

此外，显而易见的是，在P2时期，波作用通量向东传播得更远，并可到达青藏高原西部。

Additionally, it is evident that during P2, the flux activity propagates farther eastward and can reach the western TP.

P2时期的夏季，60°N附近盛行正的大气环流异常，并在该区域北侧伴随异常西风、南侧伴随异常东风。

In P2, positive atmospheric circulation anomalies prevail around 60°N in summer, accompanied by anomalous westerly and easterly winds to the north and south of this region.

这些大气变化通过加强极地急流并使中纬度西风急流减速，促进双急流的形成（Fig. 15）。

These atmospheric changes contribute to the formation of double jets by reinforcing the polar jet and decelerating the midlatitude westerly jet (Fig. 15).

反过来，AMO引起的急流变化可进一步促进西欧上空高压系统的维持和增强。

Conversely, AMO-related alterations in the jets can further contribute to the maintenance and amplification of the high pressure system over WE.

AMO诱导的极地环流变化中的显著北支与TPSC诱导的中纬度急流变化共同作用，为形成从北大西洋延伸至西欧的双急流创造了有利条件。

Together, the substantial northern branch of the AMO-induced changes in polar circulation and the TPSC-induced alterations in midlatitude jets create favorable conditions for the formation of double jets extending from the North Atlantic to WE.

这进而促成西欧上空的高压异常，为该地区夏季CHW的发生营造了有利环境。

This, in turn, contributes to the high pressure anomaly over WE, creating a conducive environment for the occurrence of summer CHWs over this region.

c. 使用LBM的数值试验c. Numerical experiment with LBM

为进一步考察大气对AMO和TPSC的响应，我们使用线性斜压模式（LBM）开展了两组理想化数值试验。

To further examine the atmospheric response to the AMO and TPSC, we conducted two idealized numerical experiments using LBM.

第一组试验设置了两个负强迫，其中一个位于北大西洋，另一个相对较弱，位于青藏高原西部（EXP1）（Fig. 16a）。

In the first experiment, two negative forcings are set with one situated over the North Atlantic and the other one, relatively weaker, set over the western TP (EXP1) (Fig. 16a).

EXP1试验模拟P1时期的状况，即负AMO和相对较弱的TPSC强迫。

The EXP1 experiment simulates the conditions in P1 that correspond to a negative AMO and relatively weak TPSC forcing.

第二组试验（EXP2）在北大西洋设置一个正强迫，并在青藏高原西部设置一个强负强迫（Fig. 16b）。

In the second experiment (EXP2), one positive forcing is set over the North Atlantic and a strong negative forcing is set over the western TP (Fig. 16b).

EXP2试验代表正AMO和增强的TPSC强迫共同作用的状况，与P2时期的状况一致。

The EXP2 experiment is representative of the conditions associated with a positive AMO and intensified TPSC forcing, aligning with the conditions in P2.

这些强迫的位置和形状依据观测结果进行设计（Figs. 5 and 12）。

The positions and the shapes of these forcings are designed in accordance with observations (Figs. 5 and 12).

以青藏高原为中心的热力强迫位于75°E、38°N，纬度方向的半短轴为12°，经度方向的半长轴为12°（Fig. 16a）。

The thermal forcing centered over the TP is positioned at 75°E, 38°N, with a semiminor axis of 12° latitude and a semimajor axis of 12° longitude (Fig. 16a).

试验中的北大西洋热力强迫以320°E、30°N为中心，纬度方向的半短轴为25°，经度方向的半长轴为25°。

The thermal forcing over the North Atlantic in the experiments is centered at 320°E, 30°N, with a semiminor axis of 25° latitude and a semimajor axis of 25° longitude.

值得注意的是，模式试验结果对所施加强迫的具体形状并不敏感。

Notably, the model experiment results are not sensitive to the specific shapes of the imposed forcings.

考虑到大气对给定热力强迫响应的稳定性，本文仅考察积分第11天至第15天的平均结果。

Considering the stability of the atmospheric response to the prescribed thermal forcings, only the averages of day 11–day 15 of the integrations are examined.

FIG. 16. （a）EXP1和（b）EXP2中理想化热力强迫的水平空间分布（填色；°C）及垂直廓线。

FIG. 16. The horizontal spatial distribution (shading; °C) and vertical profile of the idealized thermal forcings for (a) EXP1 and (b) EXP2.

图中还给出了（c）EXP1和（d）EXP2的模式大气响应，即积分第11–15天平均的35°–50°N经向平均位势高度异常（等值线；gpm）、温度异常（填色；°C）及相应风场（矢量；垂直速度和纬向风）。

Also shown are the model atmospheric response average of 11–15 days of the meridional mean (35°–50°N) anomalies of the geopotential height (contours; gpm), temperature (shading; °C) and corresponding winds (vectors; vertical velocity and zonal wind) for (c) EXP1 and (d) EXP2.

在EXP2中，主要的大气响应表现为从大西洋延伸至西欧的高压异常，并伴随青藏高原上空的低压异常，与WEH–TPL型具有诸多相似之处（Fig. 16d）。

In EXP2, the prevailing atmospheric response is characterized by high pressure anomalies extending from the Atlantic to western Europe, coupled with low pressure anomalies above the TP, which bear many similarities to the WEH–TPL pattern (Fig. 16d).

这一型态与增强的纬向垂直环流相对应，其特征为青藏高原上空上升运动和西欧上空下沉运动，与1998年后观测到的情况一致（Fig. 7）。

This pattern coincides with an enhanced zonal vertical circulation, featuring upward motion on the TP and downward motion over WE, consistent with that in the observations after 1998 (Fig. 7).

相反，在EXP1中，西欧受负高度异常控制，青藏高原上空的环流异常和垂直环流均很弱（Fig. 16c）。

Conversely, in EXP1, WE is dominated by negative height anomalies, and the circulation anomalies above the TP, as well as the vertical circulation, are quite weak (Fig. 16c).

因此，数值模式结果表明，正AMO与偏多的TPSC同时发生时，有利于通过纬向垂直环流形成WEH–TPL型，并导致TPSC–CHW关系增强。

Therefore, the numerical model results indicate that a positive AMO and an excessive TPSC, when they occur concurrently, favor the emergence of the WEH–TPL pattern via zonal vertical circulation, and lead to an enhanced TPSC–CHWs relationship.

模式结果证实了观测分析所揭示的发现。

The model results confirm the findings revealed in the observations.

5. 结论与讨论5. Conclusions and discussion

本研究揭示，1998年后春季TPSC对西欧夏季CHW的影响显著增强。

The present study revealed a significant strengthening of the impact of spring TPSC on summer CHWs in WE after 1998.

我们已证明，TPSC对CHW影响的增强归因于西欧高压—青藏高原西部低压（WEH–TPL）型的强化，而该型态是1998年后青藏高原西部TPSC和AMO均发生年代际变化的结果。

We have demonstrated that the increased TPSC impact on CHWs is attributed to the intensification of a western Europe high–western TP low (WEH–TPL) pattern, which emerges as a result of both the interdecadal changes in the western TPSC and the AMO after 1998.

春季TPSC对西欧夏季CHW影响发生变化的可能机制可概括于Figure 17的示意图中。

The possible mechanism of the changed spring TPSC impact on summer CHWs in WE can be summarized in the schematic diagram of Fig. 17.

1998年后，青藏高原西部TPSC异常的幅度增强，且从春季持续至随后夏季的时间延长，因而表现出更有效调制局地热力异常的能力。

Anomalous western TPSC exhibits enhanced magnitudes and prolonged persistence from the spring into the following summer, demonstrating a more effective ability to modulate local thermal anomalies after 1998.

青藏高原更偏西部的TPSC异常会在青藏高原西部及其周边地区引发显著的深厚对流层低压异常。

The presence of anomalous more western TPSC gives rise to a notable deep tropospheric low pressure anomaly in the western TP and its surrounding regions.

该低压系统东、西两侧相应的上升和下沉运动通过纬向垂直环流维持WEH–TPL型（Fig. 17）。

The corresponding ascent and descent motions on the eastern and western flanks of this low system serve to sustain a WEH–TPL pattern through zonal vertical circulations (Fig. 17).

此外，1998年后的AMO正位相在触发从大西洋向下游传播至欧亚大陆的高层波列方面效能增强，从而为促进西欧与青藏高原之间的联系建立了更有利的背景。

In addition, the positive phase of the AMO after 1998 exhibits enhanced efficacy in triggering an upper-level wave train that propagates downstream from the Atlantic to the Eurasian continent, which establishes a more conducive background for fostering the connection between WE and the TP.

此外，青藏高原西部TPSC和AMO的变化在引起极地急流和中纬度急流显著增强的同时，又削弱中纬度急流方面均发挥关键作用。

Furthermore, the changes in both western TPSC and the AMO play pivotal roles in inducing a significant augmentation of the polar jet and midlatitude jet, concurrently weakening the midlatitude jet.

这种共同影响有利于形成双急流型，进而导致西欧高压增强及CHW发生得更频繁。

This joint influence favors the formation of the double jets pattern, consequently leading to enhanced high pressure and more frequent CHWs in WE.

大气环流对+AMO和偏多TPSC的这种响应可在线性斜压模式试验中得到再现和证实。

Such a response of atmospheric circulations to the +AMO and excessive TPSC could be reproduced and confirmed in the LBM model experiment.

FIG. 17. 偏多TPSC与+AMO共同增强TPSC–CHW关系的示意图。

FIG. 17. Schematic that shows the joint effect of the excessive TPSC and the +AMO in enhancing the TPSC–CHWs relationship.

既有研究提供了TPSC与AMO之间存在强关联的证据（Shen et al. 2011）。

Prior research presented evidence for a strong association between TPSC and the AMO (Shen et al. 2011).

J. Li et al. (2021)提出，在大西洋多年代际变率的正位相期间，北大西洋涛动（NAO）持续处于负位相，并伴随准定常罗斯贝波列；该波列引起异常绝热下沉，进而导致青藏高原变暖。

J. Li et al. (2021) proposed that during the positive phase of Atlantic multidecadal variability, the North Atlantic Oscillation (NAO) exhibits a persistent negative phase and is accompanied by a quasi-stationary Rossby wave train, which induces anomalous adiabatic descent and results in warming over the TP.

然而，在大西洋多年代际变率的负位相期间，NAO与青藏高原温度变化之间的联系减弱。

However, the connection between the NAO and temperature variations over the TP weakens during the negative phase of Atlantic multidecadal variability.

我们的研究强调，春季TPSC与西欧夏季CHW之间的联系受到AMO和TPSC二者变化的影响。

Our study underscores that the linkage between spring TPSC and summer CHWs in WE is impacted by variations in both AMO and the TPSC.

北大西洋SST、AMO与TPSC之间的相互作用值得进一步研究，它们各自对西欧CHW的贡献亦然，这些问题应成为未来研究的重点。

The interplay between the North Atlantic SST, the AMO, and TPSC warrants further investigation, as does their respective contributions to CHWs in WE, which should be the focus of future studies.
