摘要

Abstract

海洋热浪（MHWs）是海表温度异常偏高的时段，可持续数周至数月，并延伸数千千米。

Marine heatwaves (MHWs) are periods of unusually high sea surface temperature that can persist for weeks to months and extend across thousands of kilometers.

在气候变化背景下，海洋热浪的频率和强度不断增加，威胁着海洋生态系统和渔业，但控制其发生与演变的物理过程仍缺乏充分认识。

Their increasing frequency and intensity under climate change threaten marine ecosystems and fisheries, yet the physical processes that govern their occurrence and evolution remain poorly understood.

本文通过分析卫星和再分析资料表明，大气河（ARs）——大气水汽高度集中的狭长通道，常被称为“空中河流”——在北太平洋和北大西洋海洋热浪的发展中发挥着此前被忽视的作用。

Here we analyze satellite and reanalysis data to show that atmospheric rivers (ARs)—long, narrow corridors of concentrated atmospheric moisture, often described as “rivers in the sky”—play a previously overlooked role in the development of MHWs in the North Pacific and North Atlantic.

在大气河覆盖下，云量增加通过减少太阳辐射使海洋冷却，而异常暖湿的空气则通过减少海洋向外释放的湍流热通量使海洋增暖。

Under an AR, increased cloud cover cools the ocean through reduced solar radiation, while anomalously warm, humid air warms the ocean through the reduction of turbulent heat fluxes from the ocean.

这两种方向相反、且在诸多机制中占主导地位的机制随背景气候状态而变化，使大气河对海洋热浪的影响呈现季节和区域差异。

These two opposing mechanisms, dominant among others, vary with background climate state, causing seasonally and regionally varying ARs’ impacts on MHWs.

这些发现强调了认识海洋—大气复合极端事件及其在气候变暖背景下变化的重要性。

These findings stress the importance of understanding ocean-atmosphere compound extremes and their changes under a warming climate.

引言

Introduction

全球海洋吸收了人为温室气体排放所产生多余热量的 90% 以上，因此海洋增暖成为正在发生的气候变化最稳健的指标之一[1]。

The world’s oceans have absorbed over 90% of the excess heat due to anthropogenic greenhouse gas emissions, making ocean warming one of the most robust indicators of the ongoing climate change[1].

在海洋持续增暖的背景下，海洋热浪（MHWs）——相互离散且持续较长时间的海温异常偏高事件[2,3,4]——已成为全球普遍存在的现象，其频率、持续时间和强度均迅速增加[3,5,6,7]。

Against the backdrop of persistent ocean warming, marine heatwaves (MHWs)—discrete and prolonged periods of anomalously high ocean temperatures[2,3,4], have emerged as a globally pervasive phenomenon with their frequency, duration, and intensity increasing rapidly[3,5,6,7].

虽然海洋热浪研究主要关注海表温度绝对值较高的夏季极端事件，但海洋热浪更根本的定义依据是相对于气候平均态的偏离，因此从定义上说可发生于任何季节[4]。

Although MHW research has largely emphasized summer extremes, when absolute sea surface temperatures are high, MHWs are more fundamentally defined by deviations from the climatological mean and technically can occur in any season[4].

许多海洋物种会适应当季通常的温度，这可能降低它们对相对于季节基准态的异常增暖的耐受能力[8,9,10,11]，因而即便中等程度的温度异常也可能产生重要的生物学后果。

Many marine species acclimate to prevailing seasonal temperatures, which can reduce their tolerance to anomalous warming relative to the seasonal baseline[8,9,10,11], and even moderate temperature anomalies may thus have important biological consequences.

因此，海洋热浪全年都可能造成广泛的生态与经济冲击，包括海洋生物大规模死亡、渔业遭受严重影响，以及沿海经济的重大损失[12,13,14]。

Therefore, MHWs can cause widespread ecological and economic disruptions year-round, including mass mortalities of marine organisms, severe impacts on fisheries, and major losses to coastal economies[12,13,14].

然而，尽管影响日益增大，控制海洋热浪发展的物理驱动因素仍不清楚[2,15,16]，限制了我们预测和减轻其影响的能力。

Yet despite growing impacts, the physical drivers governing the MHW development remain elusive[2,15,16], limiting our ability to predict and mitigate their impacts.

已有研究提出，厄尔尼诺—南方涛动（ENSO）、太平洋年代际涛动（PDO）和北大西洋涛动（NAO）等大尺度气候变率模态，可通过改变背景海洋和大气状态来调节海洋热浪的发生概率及特征[17,18,19,20,21,22]。

Large-scale climate variability modes—such as the El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO), the Pacific Decadal Oscillation (PDO), and the North Atlantic Oscillation (NAO)—have been suggested to modulate the likelihood and characteristics of MHWs by altering background oceanic and atmospheric states[17,18,19,20,21,22].

除低频气候模态外，研究还表明，热带气旋和大气阻塞等生命史短但强度大的天气系统，也可能有助于海洋热浪的发展与维持[23,24,25,26]。

Besides the low-frequency climate modes, short-lived but intense weather systems, such as tropical cyclones and atmospheric blocking, have also been shown to potentially contribute to the development and persistence of MHWs[23,24,25,26].

大气河（ARs）作为中纬度地区的重要天气系统，常被称为“空中河流”，其特征是水汽输送高度集中的狭长通道[27,28,29,30,31]；大气河抵达陆地时可引发极端降水和洪涝，尤其在撞上北美西部或欧洲等地的山地时[18,32,33,34]。

As a key weather system in the mid-latitudes, atmospheric rivers (ARs)—often described as “rivers in the sky”—are characterized by long, narrow corridors of concentrated moisture transport[27,28,29,30,31] and can cause extreme precipitation and flooding upon arrival at the continents, especially when impinging on mountains like in the western North America or Europe[18,32,33,34].

大气河在其生命史的大部分时间里都在海洋上发展和移动，并常伴随强近地面风和充沛的对流层水汽[34,35,36]。

An AR spends most of its lifetime over the oceans, developing and migrating, and is often accompanied by strong surface winds and abundant tropospheric water vapor[34,35,36].

在这些大气条件下，大气河可能通过辐射、热力和动力作用影响或加剧海温异常[35,37,38,39]。

Given these atmospheric conditions, ARs have the potential to influence or exacerbate ocean temperature anomalies through radiative, thermodynamical, and dynamical effects[35,37,38,39].

这使我们提出假设：大气河可在次月尺度上影响海洋热浪的发展。

It leads us to hypothesize that ARs can influence the development of MHWs on sub-monthly timescales.

据我们所知，尽管这种海洋热浪—大气河联系对于易受此类事件影响的生态系统、渔业和社会十分重要，此前却尚未得到系统研究。

To the best of our knowledge, this MHW-AR connection has not been systematically studied before, despite its importance for ecosystems, fisheries and societies vulnerable to such events.

为填补这一空白，本研究利用卫星观测与再分析数据集，考察大气河对海洋热浪的影响及其背后的物理机制。

Aiming to fill this gap, in this study we use satellite observations and reanalysis datasets to investigate the impacts of ARs on MHWs and the underlying physical mechanisms.

我们特别关注热带外北太平洋和北大西洋，它们是海洋热浪与大气河均活跃且影响显著的突出热点区域[20,32,40,41]（补充图 1），这直接提示了大气河与海洋热浪之间存在相互作用的可能性。

We focus particularly on the extratropical North Pacific and North Atlantic that stand out as hotspots where both MHWs and ARs are active and impactful[20,32,40,41] (Supplementary Fig. 1), which directly points to the possibility of interactions between ARs and MHWs.

正如下文所示，大气河对海洋热浪发展具有显著影响，且这种影响随季节和区域而变化，这是海洋热浪动力学中一个被忽视的方面。

As we show below, ARs have prominent, seasonally and regionally varying impacts on MHW development—an overlooked dimension of marine heatwave dynamics.

结果

Results

观测到的大气河对海洋热浪的影响

The observed impacts of ARs on MHWs

我们采用“方法”中所述的判据，识别了 1982–2023 年北太平洋和北大西洋的全部海洋热浪与大气河。

All the MHWs and ARs in the North Pacific and the North Atlantic during 1982–2023 are identified with the criteria described in Methods.

随后，对所有已识别海洋热浪峰值日的大气河频率异常取平均。

Then, the AR frequency anomalies on the MHW peak day are averaged across all the identified MHWs.

这一过程针对每个海洋格点，并分别针对北半球夏季（6–8 月）和冬季（12–2 月）进行，以揭示大气河频率异常随季节变化的空间型（图 1a、b）。

This procedure is done separately for each ocean grid point and for boreal summer (June-August) and winter (December-February) so that seasonally varying spatial patterns of AR frequency anomalies can be revealed (Fig. 1a, b).

大气河频率异常信号最强的区域位于热带外海洋（30°N–65°N），那里也是大气河最活跃的区域（补充图 1a）。

The strongest signals of AR frequency anomalies are found in the extratropical oceans (30°N–65°N), where ARs are mostly active (Supplementary Fig. 1a).

夏季，与海洋热浪相联系的大气河频率异常在北太平洋呈马蹄形空间型：中纬度海洋存在一个正异常中心，其外分别环绕着位于副热带和副极地海洋的两个负异常区（图 1a）。

In summer, the MHW-associated AR frequency anomalies exhibit a horseshoe pattern over the North Pacific that has a positive center in the mid-latitude oceans surrounded by two negative lobes in the subtropical and subpolar oceans, respectively (Fig. 1a).

这里，大气河频率为负异常，意味着某一地点的海洋热浪达到峰值时，通常会观测到异常偏低的大气河频率，反之亦然。

Here, a negative AR frequency anomaly indicates that an anomalously low AR frequency is typically observed when a MHW peaks at the given location and vice versa.

然而，冬季的大气河频率异常呈现完全不同的空间型，几乎整个海盆均为正异常，意味着与海洋热浪相联系的大气河频率普遍偏高（图 1b）。

However, in winter, AR frequency anomalies show a completely different pattern with almost basinwide positive anomalies, which implies a consistently higher AR frequency associated with MHWs (Fig. 1b).

北大西洋在这两个季节也呈现相似的大尺度结构，但总体较弱。

Over the North Atlantic, similar large-scale structures are found for both seasons but overall weaker.

下文的分析与讨论将同时考虑这两个海盆。

Our analysis and discussion below will consider both basins.

为检验观测到的大气河—海洋热浪关系的稳健性，我们进一步利用第六次耦合模式比较计划（CMIP6）中 15 个模式的历史模拟，考察海洋热浪峰值日的大气河频率合成异常（补充表 1）。

To verify the robustness of the observed AR-MHW relationship, we further examine the composite AR frequency anomalies during MHW peak days using the historical simulations from 15 models participating in the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 (CMIP6) (Supplementary Table 1).

多模式平均能够较好地再现观测到的大气河—海洋热浪关系，同时捕捉到夏季的马蹄形空间型和冬季的全海盆结构（补充图 2）。

The multi-model mean can reasonably reproduce the observed AR–MHW relationship, capturing both the summertime horseshoe-shaped pattern and the wintertime basin-wide structure (Supplementary Fig. 2).

这些特征在不同模式中具有稳健性，超过 70% 的模式（15 个中有 11 个）在大多数区域给出的符号与多模式平均一致。

These features are robust across models, with more than 70% of them (11 out of 15 models) showing the same sign as the multi-model mean over most regions.

为探索潜在的因果联系，我们进一步研究海洋热浪与大气河之间的超前—滞后关系。

To explore a potential causal link, we further investigate the lead-lag relations between MHWs and ARs.

我们在每个格点上合成海洋热浪峰值日前后各 15 天的逐日大气河频率异常，然后对所得合成结果计算纬向平均（图 1c、d）。

We compute the composites of daily AR frequency anomalies during the 15 days before and after MHW peak days at each grid point and then compute the zonal average of the resulting composites (Fig. 1c, d).

在两个季节中，大气河频率异常通常提前一周以上出现，并在海洋热浪达到峰值前约两天达到最大幅度。

For both seasons, AR frequency anomalies tend to emerge more than a week in advance and reach the strongest magnitude about two days preceding the MHW peaks.

总体而言，海洋热浪峰值之前，夏季的大气河较少（图 1c），冬季则较多（图 1d）。

MHW peaks are overall preceded by fewer ARs in summer (Fig. 1c), and more ARs in winter (Fig. 1d).

这些超前—滞后关系也存在于 CMIP6 历史模拟中（补充图 2c、d）。

These lead-lag relations are also found in CMIP6 historical simulations (Supplementary Fig. 2c, d).

这些结果表明，大气河会影响海洋热浪的发展，其作用可能是通过海洋热浪事件达到峰值之前的累积效应实现的。

These results suggest that ARs influence the MHW development, probably through a cumulative effect prior to the peak of MHW events.

我们考察了海洋热浪峰值前 5 天平均的大气河频率异常，结果确实呈现出比峰值当天异常更清晰、空间上更连贯的结构，整体显著性水平也更高，尤其是在北太平洋（补充图 3；另见补充图 4）。

We examined the AR frequency anomalies averaged during the 5 days preceding the MHW peak, and these results indeed show clearer and more spatially coherent spatial structures with an overall higher significance level than the AR frequency anomalies at the MHW peak, particularly over the North Pacific (Supplementary Fig. 3; also see Supplementary Fig. 4).

中纬度纬带所有海洋热浪的合成海表温度倾向，在海洋热浪峰值前约两天达到最大（图 1e、f），与大气河频率异常的超前时间一致（图 1c、d）。

The maximum SST tendency for the composite of all the MHWs in the midlatitude bands occurs about two days before the MHW peak (Fig. 1e, f), consistent with the lead time of AR frequency anomalies (Fig. 1c, d).

相比之下，海洋热浪之后的大气河频率异常相对较弱。

In contrast, the post-MHW AR frequency anomalies are relatively weak.

上述超前—滞后关系表明，先行出现的大气河可能在海洋热浪事件的发展中发挥了重要作用。

The lead-lag relations above suggest that preceding ARs may have played an important role in the development of MHW events.

为证实这一点，我们现在转而以大气河为参照，研究大气河活动较强日的海表温度倾向合成（见“方法”）。

To confirm that, we now change the reference frame to ARs and investigate the composite SST tendency on the days with high AR activity (Methods).

大气河引起的海表温度倾向，其空间结构与海洋热浪峰值日的大气河频率异常十分相似，表现为夏季马蹄形的冷却效应和冬季全海盆的增暖效应（图 2a、e）。

The spatial structures of AR-induced SST tendency closely resemble those of AR frequency anomalies on MHW peaks, displaying a horseshoe-like cooling effect in summer and a basinwide warming effect in winter (Fig. 2a, e).

值得注意的是，北大西洋冬季与大气河相关的海表温度变化相对较弱，这与该区域海洋热浪所伴随的大气河频率异常较弱相一致（图 1b 和图 2e）。

Notably, the relatively weak wintertime AR-associated SST change over the North Atlantic is consistent with the weak MHW-associated AR frequency anomaly in this region (Fig. 1b and Fig. 2e).

上述结果支持了我们关于大气河能够影响其下方海洋热浪特征的假设，并进一步表明，这种影响可能具有空间和季节依赖性。

The results above support our hypothesis that ARs can influence the underlying MHW properties and further suggest that such influences can be spatially and seasonally dependent.

大气河引起海表温度变化的机制

Mechanisms of AR-induced SST changes

大气河引起的海表温度变化具有鲜明的季节差异，夏季呈马蹄形空间型，冬季呈全海盆结构，这引出了一个问题：哪些物理过程控制了这些不同的空间结构？

The pronounced seasonal contrast in AR-induced SST changes, which manifests as a horseshoe pattern in summer and a basinwide structure in winter, raises the question of what physical processes govern these distinct spatial structures.

为回答这一问题，我们利用逐日全球海洋再分析数据集，对 1993–2023 年的大气河日开展海洋混合层热收支分析（见“方法”）。

To answer this question, we conduct an ocean mixed-layer heat budget analysis on the AR days over the period of 1993–2023 with a daily, global ocean reanalysis dataset (Methods).

受数据可用性限制，这一分析时段短于此前海洋热浪—大气河关系分析的时段（即 1982–2023 年），但两个时段的大气河日海表温度倾向合成空间型几乎没有变化（补充图 5），证实了这些结果的稳健性。

Limited by data availability, this analysis period is shorter than that for our previous analysis of MHW-AR relation (i.e., 1982-2023), but the composite SST tendency patterns on AR days remain nearly unchanged between the two periods (Supplementary Fig. 5), confirming the robustness of these results.

在各热收支项中，无论夏季还是冬季，海表净热通量（Qnet）都是与大气河相关的海表温度倾向的主导驱动项（图 2b、f）；其空间型与观测到的海表温度倾向（图 2a、e）相似，空间相关系数分别为 0.42 和 0.72（p < 0.01）。

Among the heat budget terms, net surface heat flux (Qnet) acts as the dominant driver of the AR-associated SST tendency in both summer and winter (Fig. 2b, f), resembling the observed SST tendency patterns (Fig. 2a, e) with a spatial correlation of 0.42 and 0.72 (p < 0.01), respectively.

海洋水平平流在两个季节中的贡献均有限（图 2c、g），其大尺度特征主要由与相应海表风变化相关的经向埃克曼平流塑造（补充图 6 和 7）。

Horizontal oceanic advection has only a limited contribution in both seasons (Fig. 2c, g), and its large-scale feature is mainly shaped by the meridional Ekman advection associated with consistent surface wind changes (Supplementary Figs. 6 and 7).

更具体地说，西风（东风）异常往往引起异常向南（向北）的埃克曼输送，从而产生冷却（增暖）效应。

More specifically, a westerly (easterly) wind anomaly tends to induce an anomalous southward (northward) Ekman transport and therefore a cooling (warming) effect.

Hsu 等[37]报道了北太平洋 10 月至次年 3 月与大气河相关的海洋热平流具有类似特征；两项研究结果之间的微小差异，主要来自季节的定义和所用海洋再分析数据集（补充图 8）。

Hsu et al.[37]. reported similar features of AR-associated oceanic heat advection in the North Pacific during October-March, and the small difference between our results comes mainly from the definition of seasons and the ocean reanalysis dataset used (Supplementary Fig. 8).

残差项主要包括未解析的过程，如垂直卷入、垂直混合、水平扩散及其他次网格尺度变率。

The residual term mainly includes unresolved processes such as vertical entrainment, vertical mixing, horizontal diffusion, and other subgrid-scale variability.

它也可能部分源于 1993–2018 年海表热通量与海洋场来自不同数据源这一事实（详细讨论见“方法”）。

It may also partly arise from the fact that the surface heat flux and ocean fields during 1993–2018 are derived from different data sources (see “Methods” for a detailed discussion).

残差项的贡献适中（图 2d、h），其空间结构总体上与 Qnet 项相似，但符号相反且幅度较弱，意味着海洋过程相对于大气强迫具有阻尼作用，这与 Hsu 等[37]的结果大体一致。

The residual term shows a modest contribution (Fig. 2d, h) and exhibits generally similar spatial structures as the Qnet term but with an opposite, weaker magnitude, which implies a damping effect of oceanic processes as opposed to atmospheric forcing, broadly consistent with Hsu et al.[37].

为进一步量化这种海洋阻尼作用，我们计算了 Qnet 引起的海表温度倾向与残差项相关海表温度倾向之间的全海盆空间相关系数。

To further quantify this oceanic damping effect, we computed basin-wide spatial correlations between the Qnet-induced SST tendency and the SST tendency associated with the residual term.

冬季，整个研究区域、北大西洋和北太平洋的相关系数分别为 −0.60、−0.58 和 −0.60。

In winter, the correlations are –0.60 for the full study region, –0.58 for the North Atlantic, and –0.60 for the North Pacific.

这些较强的负相关表明，残差项持续抵消着由 Qnet 强迫产生的海盆尺度海表温度倾向空间型。

These strong negative correlations indicate that the residual term consistently offsets the basin-scale SST tendency pattern forced by Qnet.

夏季的相关性较弱（分别为 −0.34、−0.33 和 −0.32），表明阻尼作用有所减弱，但仍然明显。

In summer, the correlations are weaker (–0.34, –0.33, and –0.32, respectively), indicating a reduced but still evident damping effect.

残差项还与夏季混合层深度（MLD）异常呈正相关（r = 0.45），说明海洋混合层较深（较浅）的区域，往往具有较弱（较强）的残差冷却作用。

The residual term is also positively correlated with summertime mixed-layer depth (MLD) anomalies (r = 0.45), suggesting that regions with a deeper (shallower) ocean mixed layer tend to experience weaker (stronger) residual cooling.

这种表现与垂直混合过程在物理上相一致：混合层较浅时，海表温度对混合引起的冷却更敏感，因而冷却作用更强。

Such behavior is physically consistent with vertical mixing processes: when the mixed layer is shallow, the SST is more sensitive to mixing-induced cooling, leading to an enhanced cooling.

综合来看，这些结果突出表明，海表净热通量在调控海表温度对大气河的响应中发挥主导作用。

Taken together, these results highlight the dominant role of net surface heat flux in regulating the SST response to ARs.

大气河影响海表净热通量的具体过程是什么？

What are the detailed processes underlying the ARs’ influence on net surface heat flux?

为什么这些影响依赖于季节？

Why are these impacts dependent on season?

为回答这些问题，我们对海表净热通量进行分解，并量化辐射和湍流热通量各项的贡献。

To answer these questions, we decompose the net surface heat flux and quantify the contribution from each term of radiative and turbulent heat fluxes.

在气候平均态下，净短波辐射通量向下进入海洋，而净长波辐射通量向上（补充图 9b、c、g、h）。

Climatologically, net shortwave radiative flux is directed downward into the ocean, while net longwave radiative flux is upward (Supplementary Fig. 9b, c, g, h).

在研究区的大多数区域，潜热通量与感热通量等湍流热通量主要向上（补充图 9d、e、i、j），表明热量净输送方向为从海洋到大气。

The turbulent heat fluxes including latent and sensible heat fluxes are predominantly upward over most of the study region (Supplementary Fig. 9d, e, i, j), indicating a net heat transfer from the ocean to the atmosphere.

大气河可通过这四个分量影响海洋热浪：它通过减少净向下短波辐射通量产生冷却效应，同时通过减少净向上长波辐射通量，以及减弱或局部反转海洋向大气的湍流潜热和感热通量产生增暖效应（图 2i；空间结构另见补充图 10）。

An AR can influence MHW through all four components: it has a cooling effect through a reduced net downward shortwave radiative flux, and warming effects through a reduced net upward longwave radiative flux and weakened, or locally reversed, turbulent latent and sensible heat fluxes from the ocean to the atmosphere (Fig. 2i; also see Supplementary Fig. 10 for spatial structures).

具体而言，净向下短波辐射通量的减少主要源于大气河活跃时云量增加（补充图 11），而净向上长波辐射通量的减少则是水汽和云的温室效应增强所致（补充图 12）。

Specifically, the reduced net downward shortwave radiative flux is due mainly to the increase of clouds when ARs are active (Supplementary Fig. 11), while the reduced net upward longwave radiative flux is caused by the enhanced greenhouse effects of water vapor and clouds (Supplementary Fig. 12).

与大气河相联系的海表风速增加往往通过湍流潜热和感热通量加强海洋热量损失，但大气河输送的暖湿空气通过增强向下湍流热通量所产生的增暖效应超过了这种冷却效应（图 2j、k；空间结构另见补充图 13）。

The increase of surface wind speed associated with ARs tends to enhance ocean heat loss through turbulent latent and sensible heat fluxes, but this cooling effect is outweighed by the warming effect of the warm, moist air transported by ARs through the enhanced downward turbulent heat fluxes (Fig. 2j, k; also see Supplementary Fig. 13 for spatial structures).

这些特征总体上与文献[38]展示的年平均大气河相关海表热通量异常一致；下文将分别进一步讨论冬季和夏季的不同特征。

These characteristics are generally consistent with the annual-mean, AR-related surface heat flux anomalies shown by ref. [38]., and below we further discuss the distinct features for winter and summer, separately.

大气河相关海表净热通量变化的季节差异，来自这些相互竞争的冷却与增暖效应之间的精细补偿，而每一种效应本身都可独立地随季节变化。

The seasonal contrast in AR-associated net surface heat flux change results from a delicate compensation among these competing cooling and warming effects that can each independently vary with season.

夏季背景入射太阳辐射较强时，与大气河有关的云所引起的短波辐射冷却效应尤其强（补充图 11）。

The cloud-induced shortwave radiative cooling effect associated with ARs is particularly strong in summer when the background incoming solar radiation is large (Supplementary Fig. 11).

大气河相关水汽与云的温室效应在冬季较强，因为冬季背景大气发射率相对较低，因而长波吸收带的重叠较少（补充图 12）。

The greenhouse effects of water vapor and clouds associated with ARs are stronger in winter as the background atmospheric emissivity is relatively small and so there is less overlap of longwave absorption band (Supplementary Fig. 12).

与大气河相关的暖湿空气对海表湍流热通量的影响在冬季更强（图 2j、k 和补充图 13），这是背景风较强以及海气湿度差和温度差增强共同造成的（补充图 14 和 15）。

The effects of AR-associated warm and moist air on surface turbulent heat fluxes are stronger in winter (Fig. 2j, k; and Supplementary Fig. 13), caused by both stronger background winds and enhanced air–sea humidity and temperature contrasts (Supplementary Figs. 14 and 15).

总之，在背景大气状态的调制下，大气河通过湍流和长波辐射通量产生的增暖效应在冬季更强，而其短波冷却效应在夏季更强。

In summary, modulated by the background atmospheric state, the AR-associated warming effects through turbulent and longwave radiative fluxes are stronger in winter, while the AR-associated shortwave cooling effect is stronger in summer.

北太平洋和北大西洋大气河的综合效应是：夏季总体上抑制海洋热浪，但中纬度纬带（约 40–50°N）除外；冬季则有利于海洋热浪发生（图 3）。

The collective effect of ARs in the North Pacific and the North Atlantic is to generally suppress MHWs in summer, except for the mid-latitude band (~40-50°N), but to favor MHWs in winter (Fig. 3).

To further elucidate the horseshoe-shaped summer pattern, we decomposed the Qnet anomalies over the positive and negative SST tendency regions shown in Fig. 2a.

如补充图 16 所示，夏季大气河事件相关的短波和长波辐射通量异常在这两个区域中相当。

As shown in Supplementary Fig. 16, during summer, the shortwave and longwave radiative flux anomalies associated with AR events are comparable between the two areas.

相比之下，中纬度海表温度倾向为正的纬带内，湍流热通量异常（包括感热和潜热分量）强得多，约为海表温度倾向为负区域的两倍；这又主要是由海气温差和湿度差异常增强造成的（补充图 15a、c）。

In contrast, turbulent heat flux anomalies (including both sensible and latent components) are much stronger in the midlatitude positive SST tendency band, ~twice those in the negative SST tendency region, which is in turn due mainly to the enhanced air-sea temperature and humidity contrast anomalies (Supplementary Fig. 15a, c).

湍流热通量异常所增强的增暖效应抵消了短波辐射通量异常相关的冷却效应，使中纬度纬带的海表获得净热量，因而形成夏季的马蹄形空间型。

The enhanced warming effect associated with the turbulent heat flux anomalies acts to offset the cooling effect associated with the shortwave radiative flux anomalies, resulting in a net surface heat gain in the midlatitude band and therefore a horseshoe-shaped pattern in summer.

讨论

Discussion

本研究通过观测分析，识别出了大气河影响北太平洋和北大西洋海洋热浪的季节依赖性指纹特征，概括见图 3。

In this study, we have identified ARs’ seasonally dependent fingerprints on the MHWs in the North Pacific and Atlantic through observational analysis, as summarized in Fig. 3.

当大气河存在时，其伴随的云往往通过减少向下短波辐射通量使海洋冷却，而暖湿空气则通过减少海洋向大气释放的湍流热通量（潜热和感热）以及增加向下长波辐射通量，使海洋增暖。

When ARs are present, the associated clouds tend to cool the ocean by reducing the downward shortwave radiative flux, while the warm and moist air tends to warm the ocean through both reducing turbulent heat fluxes (both latent and sensible) from the ocean to the atmosphere and increasing downward longwave radiative flux.

在背景大气状态调制下，这些通量分量精细地共同作用，产生具有季节依赖性的净效应。

Modulated by the background atmospheric state, these flux components delicately work together to yield a seasonally dependent net effect.

大气河频率异常超前海洋热浪峰值数天，且具有不同的空间特征：夏季为以负异常为主的马蹄形空间型，冬季为以正异常为主的全海盆空间型。

AR frequency anomalies precede MHW peaks by several days with distinct spatial signatures—a horseshoe-shaped pattern with mainly negative anomalies in summer and a basinwide pattern with mainly positive anomalies in winter.

除海表热通量外，大气河相关降水还可能通过淡水引起的层结以及垂直混合的变化，影响上层海洋的热力变率[42,43]。

In addition to surface heat fluxes, AR-associated precipitation may potentially influence upper-ocean thermal variability through freshwater-induced stratification and changes in vertical mixing[42,43].

然而，利用观测数据集分离大气河相关降水对海表温度变率的独立贡献，是一项挑战。

However, isolating the independent contribution of AR-related precipitation on SST variability from observational datasets is challenging.

量化这一效应需要有针对性的气候模式模拟，值得在未来开展研究。

Quantifying this effect will require targeted climate model simulations that warrant future investigations.

背景海洋增暖为更频繁、更强烈的海洋热浪创造了条件，而大气河可在天气尺度上调节其发生时间、强度和空间结构。

While background ocean warming sets the stage for more frequent and intense MHWs, ARs can modulate their timing, intensity, and spatial structure on synoptic scales.

认识到大气河是海洋热浪的一种关键强迫机制，有助于增进我们对极端海温变率的理解，并突出表明需要进一步研究其在海洋热浪动力学中的作用。

Recognizing ARs as a key forcing mechanism for MHWs advances our understanding of extreme ocean temperature variability and underscores the need for further investigation into their role in MHW dynamics.

除了这种单向影响，已有研究还提出，海洋热浪可能对大气河特征产生反馈。

Beyond this one-way influence, previous studies have suggested the possibility of feedbacks from MHWs to AR characteristics.

例如，一项近期聚焦东北太平洋海洋热浪的研究发现，相比于与海洋热浪无关的大气河，出现在海洋热浪峰值日之后的大气河往往更强，且位置偏向东北[44]。

For example, a recent study focusing on MHWs in the northeastern Pacific showed that ARs following MHW peak days tend to be stronger and shifted northeastward compared to ARs not associated with MHWs[44].

此外，基于个例的研究表明，异常偏暖的海表温度可能增强大气河路径沿线的水汽辐合[45]。

In addition, case-based studies indicated that anomalously warm SST may enhance moisture convergence along AR pathways[45].

这些结果共同提示，大气河与海洋热浪之间可能存在双向相互作用；不过，对这类反馈的量化超出了本研究范围，需要利用完全耦合的大气—海洋模式开展有针对性的研究。

Together, these results point to potential two-way interactions between ARs and MHWs; however, quantifying such feedbacks is beyond the scope of the present study and will require targeted investigation using fully coupled atmosphere–ocean models.

大气河与热带外天气尺度的大气变率紧密相连，包括温带气旋、风暴路径、大气阻塞系统和急流等[46,47,48]。

ARs are closely linked to synoptic atmospheric variability in the extra-tropics, including extratropical cyclones, storm tracks, atmospheric blocking systems, jet streams, etc[46,47,48].

我们的发现表明，大气河变率是一条重要但此前被忽视的大气作用途径，将热带外海洋中的海洋热浪事件与这些大尺度大气环流变率联系起来。

Our findings highlight AR variability as an important but previously overlooked atmospheric pathway that connects MHW events to these large-scale atmospheric circulation variability in the extratropical oceans.

例如，大气阻塞被定义为准静止高压系统，它通过降低风速和增加短波辐射，对海洋热浪产生强烈影响[40,49]。

For example, atmospheric blocking, defined as a quasi-stationary high-pressure system, exerts a strong influence on MHWs through reduced wind speed and increased shortwave radiation[40,49].

与此同时，这些持续性反气旋异常能够改变大尺度气流和水汽分布，进而调节大气河的传播和强度[48,50,51]。

At the same time, these persistent anticyclonic anomalies can alter large-scale flow and moisture distribution, therefore modulating the propagation and intensity of ARs[48,50,51].

大气河能否通过水汽辐合以及与海洋热浪的相互作用，反过来影响天气尺度的环流变率，目前还没有得到充分认识。

Whether ARs can feed back onto the synoptic circulation variability through moisture convergence and the interactions with MHWs is not fully understood.

我们的结果进一步表明，气候变暖背景下大气河特征的变化，可能影响热带外海洋热浪的发生和强度[52,53,54]。

Our results further suggest that the changes in AR characteristics under a warming climate may influence the occurrence and intensity of MHWs in the extra-tropics[52,53,54].

反过来，全球变暖背景下不断增强的海洋热浪，也可能反馈于大气环流，重塑大气河活动[45,55,56]。

Conversely, intensifying MHWs under global warming may in turn feed back onto the atmospheric circulation to reshape AR activity[45,55,56].

大气河与海洋热浪之间的相互作用，可能是未来变暖背景下海洋—大气复合极端事件形成的一条关键途径，对海洋生态系统和区域水文气候至关重要。

The interaction between ARs and MHWs may represent a key pathway for the emergence of compound ocean–atmosphere extremes under future warming, critical for marine ecosystems and regional hydroclimate.

未来需要利用观测和耦合气候模式评估海洋热浪与大气河之间的双向相互作用，并探索将这些相互作用纳入考虑后，改善海洋热浪短期预报和长期预估的潜力。

Future research is needed to assess the two-way mutual interactions between MHWs and ARs in observations and coupled climate models, and to explore the potential to improve the short-term forecast and long-term projection of MHWs by accounting for these interactions.

方法

Methods

数据集

Datasets

我们使用 NOAA 逐日最优插值海表温度数据集第 2.1 版（DOISST v2.1）分析海洋热浪[57]。

MHWs are analyzed using the NOAA Daily Optimum Interpolation Sea Surface Temperature dataset, version 2.1 (DOISST v2.1)[57].

原始逐日海表温度数据的分辨率为 0.25° × 0.25°；在计算海洋热浪特征之前，通过对每个 1° 网格内的数据进行空间平均，将其重网格化至规则的 1° × 1° 网格。

The original daily SST data, available at 0.25° × 0.25° resolution, are regridded to a regular 1° × 1° grid by spatial averaging within each 1° cell prior to the calculation of MHW characteristics.

逐日大气场来自欧洲中期天气预报中心（ECMWF）第五代全球再分析数据集（ERA5），水平分辨率为 1° × 1°[58]。

Daily atmospheric fields are obtained from the fifth major global reanalysis produced by European Center for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) v5 (ERA5) dataset, with a horizontal resolution of 1° × 1°[58].

所用变量包括地表净感热通量、潜热通量、短波和长波辐射通量（均包含全天空与晴空条件）、2 m 气温、比湿以及 10 m 水平风。

Variables used include net surface sensible heat flux, latent heat flux, shortwave and longwave radiative fluxes (both all-sky and clear-sky), 2-m air temperature, specific humidity, and 10-m horizontal winds.

分析时段为 1982–2023 年，异常相对于同一时段的气候平均值计算。

The analysis spans the period of 1982–2023, with anomalies computed relative to the climatological mean over the same period.

我们使用 CMIP6 历史模拟的逐日海表温度输出，识别所选 15 个模式中的海洋热浪峰值日（补充信息表 1）。

Daily SST outputs from the CMIP6 historical simulations are used to identify MHW peak days in 15 selected models (Supplementary Information Table 1).

使用每个模式的逐日比湿和等压面水平风，计算垂直积分水汽输送（IVT）。

Daily specific humidity and pressure-level horizontal winds from each model are used to calculate vertically integrated water vapor transport (IVT).

为确保模式之间的一致性，每个模式仅采用一个集合成员（r1i1p1f1）进行分析。

To ensure consistency among models, only one ensemble member (r1i1p1f1) from each model is included in the analysis.

模式分析涵盖 1980–2014 年。

The model analyses cover the period 1980–2014.

为研究与大气河相关的海表温度响应为何存在季节差异，我们结合 GLORYS12V1 全球海洋再分析[59]与 ERA5 海表热通量，开展海洋混合层热收支分析。

To investigate the mechanisms underlying the seasonal contrast in AR-associated SST responses, we perform an ocean mixed layer heat budget analysis using the GLORYS12V1 global ocean reanalysis[59] together with surface heat fluxes from ERA5.

GLORYS12V1 基于哥白尼海洋环境监测服务（CMEMS）的业务全球预报系统，采用 NEMO 海洋模式，其表面受到 ECMWF 再分析大气通量的强迫——2018 年之前使用 ERA-Interim，2019 年之后使用 ERA5。

GLORYS12V1 is based on the operational global forecasting system of the Copernicus Marine Environment Monitoring Service (CMEMS) and employs the NEMO ocean model, which is forced at the surface by atmospheric fluxes from ECMWF reanalysis—ERA-Interim before 2018 and ERA5 after 2019.

我们重新计算了图 2 中的海洋混合层热收支，但将时段限定为 2019–2023 年；在这一时段中不存在数据源不一致的问题（即 GLORYS12V1 由 ERA5 强迫）。

We recalculated the ocean mixed layer heat budget in Fig. 2 but for the period of 2019–2023, during which there was no inconsistency issue (i.e., GLORYS12V1 is forced by ERA5).

总体而言，所得结果（补充图 17）与整个时段的结果（图 2）相似，但由于时段较短、所用样本量也较小，噪声明显更大。

Overall, the results (Supplementary Fig. 17) are similar to those for the entire period (Fig. 2), but much noisier due to the shorter period and therefore the smaller sample size being used.

此外，我们考察了整个时段内由 Qnet 引起的海表温度倾向，并分别考察了 2018 年之前和 2019 年之后的情况。

Also, we examined the Qnet-induced SST tendency over the entire period, as well as separately before 2018 and after 2019.

这三个时段的空间型和季节特征总体上非常相似（补充图 18）。

The spatial patterns and seasonal characteristics are overall very similar for those three periods (Supplementary Fig. 18).

这些检验增强了我们对结果稳健性的信心，但并未完全消除解释残差项时固有的不确定性。

These tests provide us additional confidence in the robustness of our results, although they do not completely eliminate the inherent uncertainty in interpreting the residual term.

因此，本研究在整个分析时段内一致采用 ERA5 来获取大气河相关的海表热通量和大气变量，以确保统一的大气参照框架。

Therefore, ERA5 is used consistently in this study to derive AR-related surface heat fluxes and atmospheric variables across the full analysis period, ensuring a uniform atmospheric reference framework.

GLORYS12V1 提供水平分辨率为 1/12°、具有 50 个垂直层的逐日输出，覆盖自 1993 年开始的卫星测高时代。

GLORYS12V1 provides daily output at 1/12° horizontal resolution with 50 vertical levels and spans the satellite altimetry era beginning in 1993.

本研究所用变量包括混合层深度（MLD）、海洋位温和海流。

The variables used in this study include mixed-layer depth (MLD), ocean potential temperature, and ocean currents.

在 GLORYS12V1 中，混合层深度依据密度判据诊断：相对于 10 m 深度处的密度，当位密度增量相当于当地表层条件下温度降低 0.2 °C 所引起的变化时，其对应深度即定义为混合层深度。

In GLORYS12V1, the MLD is diagnosed using a density-based criterion and is defined as the depth at which the potential density increase relative to the density at 10 m corresponds to a temperature decrease of 0.2 °C under local surface conditions.

统计分析

Statistical analysis

为评估本研究所识别大气河—海洋热浪关系的稳健性，我们采用双侧 Student t 检验，对合成场进行统计显著性检验。

To evaluate the robustness of the AR–MHW relationship identified in this study, statistical significance tests were applied to the composite fields using a two-tailed Student’s t-test.

此外，为检验所识别的关系是否受到长期趋势影响，我们对各格点的数据进行线性去趋势处理后，重复了所有分析。

In addition, to examine whether the identified relationship is influenced by long-term trends, all analyses were repeated using linearly detrended datasets at each grid point.

主要结论保持不变，表明结果并非由长期变率驱动（补充图 19）。

The main conclusion remains unchanged, indicating that the results are not driven by long-term variability (Supplementary Fig. 19).

海洋热浪的定义

MHWs definition

海洋热浪被定义为离散、持续且异常偏暖的海洋事件，其判据为逐日海表温度至少连续 5 天超过随季节变化的第 90 百分位阈值[4]。

A MHW is defined as a discrete, prolonged, and anomalously warm ocean event, occurring when daily SST exceeds a seasonally varying 90th percentile threshold for at least five consecutive days[4].

对于每个日历日，首先使用所有年份中以该日为中心的 11 天窗口内的数据，估算逐日海表温度的第 90 百分位值，从而计算阈值。

The threshold is computed for each calendar day by first estimating the 90th percentile of daily SST using data within an 11-day window centered on that day across all years.

为确保时间上的连续性，随后使用 31 天滑动平均对得到的逐日阈值进行平滑。

To ensure temporal continuity, the resulting daily threshold is subsequently smoothed using a 31-day moving average.

间隔不足 3 天的事件被视为同一次海洋热浪。

Events separated by fewer than three days are treated as a single MHW.

每次事件的峰值日定义为该事件期间海表温度达到最大值的日期。

The peak day of each event is defined as the day on which SST reaches its maximum during the event.

基于 DOISST 的分析以 1982–2023 年为气候基准期，而基于 GLORYS 的分析则用 1993–2023 年计算阈值。

For DOISST-based analysis, the climatological period is 1982–2023, whereas for GLORYS-based analysis, the threshold is computed over the 1993–2023 period.

在此基础上，对每个格点所识别的全部海洋热浪峰值日的逐日大气河频率异常取平均，计算大气河频率异常合成。

Based on these peak days, composites of AR frequency anomalies are computed by averaging daily AR frequency anomalies across all identified MHW peak days in each grid.

合成分别针对北半球夏季和冬季进行，以评估与极端海洋增暖相联系的大气河活动的季节差异（图 1）。

Composites are constructed separately for boreal summer and winter, enabling assessment of seasonal contrasts in AR activity associated with extreme ocean warming (Fig. 1).

大气河的定义

ARs definition

此前已有多种大气河识别算法被提出（综述见文献[60]）。

Multiple AR detection algorithms have been proposed before (see a review in ref. [60]).

本研究使用基于 IVT 的识别算法所识别的大气河；IVT 利用 ERA5 每 6 小时的风场和比湿场，在 0.25° × 0.25° 全球网格上，从 1000 hPa 垂直积分至 300 hPa 计算得到[61]。

In this study, we use the ARs identified using a detection algorithm based on IVT, calculated from ERA5 6-h wind and specific humidity fields, vertically integrated from 1000 to 300 hPa on a 0.25° × 0.25° global grid[61].

若某格点的 IVT 超过其所在位置及月份特定的第 85 百分位阈值，则将其标记为大气河的一部分；该阈值使用以每个日历月为中心的 5 个月滑动窗口计算。

A grid cell is tagged as part of an AR if the IVT exceeds the location- and month-specific 85th percentile threshold that is computed with a five-month moving window centered on each calendar month.

一组被标记格点只有在总长度超过 2000 km 且长宽比至少为 2 时，才被认定为大气河。

To qualify as an AR, a group of tagged grid cells must have a total length exceeding 2000 km and a length-to-width ratio of at least 2.

为与分析框架保持一致，我们将大气河识别结果重网格化至 1° × 1° 空间分辨率。

For consistency with the analysis framework, the AR detection results are regridded to a 1° × 1° spatial resolution.

由于输入数据的时间间隔为 6 小时，每个格点每天最多可识别到四次大气河。

Given the use of 6-h input data, up to four AR detections may occur at each grid cell per day.

逐日大气河频率定义为：每个格点上识别出大气河条件的 6 小时时次，占这些 6 小时时次总数的比例。

Daily AR frequency is defined as the fraction of these 6-h time steps during which AR conditions are detected at each grid cell.

在给定格点上，如果一天中四个 6 小时时次里有超过两个时次识别出大气河条件，则将该日历日归类为“大气河日”，无论这些识别时次是否连续。

A calendar day is classified as an “AR day” at a given grid point if AR conditions are detected in more than two of the four 6-h time steps within that day, regardless of whether the detections are consecutive.

为评估海洋对大气河活动的响应，我们对大气河日的海表温度倾向进行合成；这里海表温度倾向定义为海表温度的逐日变化。

To assess the oceanic response to AR activity, we compute composites of SST tendency—defined as the day-to-day change in SST—on AR days.

这些合成分别针对北半球夏季和冬季进行。

These composites are constructed separately for boreal summer and winter.

为更好地评估大气河—海洋热浪关系对大气河识别算法的稳健性，我们还分析了两种独立算法所识别的大气河：AR-CONNECT[62] 是一种采用绝对 IVT 阈值、基于对象的追踪算法；Lora v2[63,64] 则是一种考虑背景气候变率的相对—绝对混合识别方法。

To better assess the robustness of the AR–MHW relationship to AR detection algorithms, we additionally analyzed the ARs detected by two independent AR detection algorithms: AR-CONNECT[62], which is an object-based tracking algorithm using absolute IVT thresholds, and Lora v2[63,64], a hybrid relative–absolute detection method that accounts for background climate variability.

不同算法所得的空间结构和季节差异总体上在定性上相似，但大气河异常幅度存在定量差别（补充图 20 和补充图 21）。

The spatial structures and seasonal contrasts are overall qualitatively similar among these algorithms, although quantitative differences in AR anomaly magnitude are present (Supplementary Fig. 20 and Supplementary Fig. 21).

混合层热收支

Mixed-layer heat budget

为理解大气河和海洋热浪事件期间导致海表温度变率的大气与海洋过程，我们开展了海洋混合层热收支分析。

To understand atmospheric and oceanic processes responsible for SST variability during AR and MHW events, we conduct an ocean mixed-layer heat budget analysis.

海表温度倾向表示为：

The ocean surface temperature tendency is expressed as:

∂T∂t=QnetρCph−V·∇T+ε（1）

∂T∂t=QnetρCph−V·∇T+ε (1)

其中，T 为海表温度，ρ 为海水密度，Cp 为海水比热容，ℎ 为海洋混合层深度。

where T is the SST, ρ is the seawater density, Cp is the specific heat capacity of seawater, and ℎ is the ocean mixed-layer depth.

Qnet 表示海表净热通量，−V·∇T 表示混合层内的温度水平平流。

Qnet denotes the net surface heat fluxes, and −V·∇T represents the horizontal advection of temperature within the mixed layer.

本研究所有混合层热收支项均通过诊断计算获得。

All mixed-layer heat budget terms are computed diagnostically in this study.

具体而言，逐日混合层深度、海洋位温和海流来自 GLORYS12V1 再分析，而海表净热通量来自 ERA5 再分析。

Specifically, daily mixed-layer depth, ocean potential temperature, and ocean currents are obtained from the GLORYS12V1 reanalysis, while the net surface heat flux is derived from the ERA5 reanalysis.

水平平流项利用混合层内的逐日水平海流和温度梯度计算。

The horizontal advection term is calculated using daily horizontal currents and temperature gradients within the mixed layer.

残差项 ε 由热收支不平衡量估算，代表混合层内未解析过程的综合效应，包括垂直卷入、垂直混合、水平扩散及其他次网格尺度过程。

The residual term ε is estimated as the imbalance of the heat budget and represents the combined effects of unresolved processes within the mixed layer, including vertical entrainment, vertical mixing, horizontal diffusion, and other subgrid-scale processes.

感热与潜热通量异常的线性分解

Linear decomposition of sensible and latent heat flux anomalies

潜热和感热湍流通量异常可利用整体空气动力学公式进行线性分解。

Latent and sensible turbulent heat flux anomalies can be linearly decomposed using the bulk aerodynamic formulations.

具体而言，潜热通量（LH）和感热通量（SH）异常近似为：

Specifically, the latent heat flux (LH) and sensible heat flux (SH) anomalies are approximated as:

LH′=ρLvCe[U(qs−qa)]′≈ρLvCeU′(q¯s−q¯a)+ρLvCeU¯(qs′−qa′)（2）

LH′=ρLvCe[U(qs−qa)]′≈ρLvCeU′(q¯s−q¯a)+ρLvCeU¯(qs′−qa′) (2)

SH′=ρcpCh[U(Ts−Ta)]′≈ρcpChU′(T¯s−T¯a)+ρcpChU¯(Ts′−Ta′)（3）

SH′=ρcpCh[U(Ts−Ta)]′≈ρcpChU′(T¯s−T¯a)+ρcpChU¯(Ts′−Ta′) (3)

其中，ρ 为空气密度，Lv 为汽化潜热，Cp 为空气定压比热容，Ce 和 Ch 分别为水汽和热量的整体交换系数。

where ρ is air density, Lv is the latent heat of vaporization, Cp is the specific heat capacity of air at constant pressure, and Ce, Ch are the bulk transfer coefficients for moisture and heat, respectively.

U 表示风速，qs 和 qa 分别为海表和上方空气的比湿，Ts 和 Ta 分别为海表温度和近海面气温。

U denotes wind speed, qs and qa are the specific humidity at the surface and in the overlying air, and Ts and Ta are the sea surace and near-surface air temperatures.

撇号 (′) 表示异常，上横线表示气候平均值。

Prime (′) indicates anomalies, and overbar denotes climatological means.

这种分解将风异常的贡献与海气比湿差（或温差）异常的贡献区分开来。

This decomposition separates the contributions from wind anomalies and air-sea specific humidity (or temperature) anomalies.
