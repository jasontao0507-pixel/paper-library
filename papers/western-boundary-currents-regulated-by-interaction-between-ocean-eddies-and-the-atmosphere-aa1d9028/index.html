摘要ABSTRACT

目前的气候模式系统性地低估了与强西边界流相关的海洋锋面强度，例如黑潮延伸体和湾流延伸体的锋面，并且难以模拟这些锋面在中纬度海洋西边界的位置[1]。

Current climate models systematically underestimate the strength of oceanic fronts associated with strong western boundary currents, such as the Kuroshio and Gulf Stream Extensions, and have difficulty simulating their positions at the mid-latitude ocean’s western boundaries[1].

即使提高网格分辨率以解析海洋中尺度涡旋——这类强劲环流的水平尺度约为100千米，与锋面和洋流存在强烈相互作用——上述偏差仍可能存在[2]；要改进气候模式，我们需要更好地理解控制这些海洋锋面系统的动力学。

Even with an enhanced grid resolution to resolve ocean mesoscale eddies—energetic circulations with horizontal scales of about a hundred kilometres that strongly interact with the fronts and currents—the bias problem can still persist[2]; to improve climate models we need a better understanding of the dynamics governing these oceanic frontal regimes.

然而，现行的西边界锋面理论建立在海洋内部动力学基础上，并未考虑这些海洋锋面区域中强烈的海气反馈。

Yet prevailing theories about the western boundary fronts are based on ocean internal dynamics without taking into consideration the intense air–sea feedbacks in these oceanic frontal regions.

本文聚焦于日本以东、作为黑潮直接延续的黑潮延伸体急流，表明海洋中尺度涡旋—大气（OME-A）反馈对于这些强劲洋流的动力学及其调控具有根本作用。

Here, by focusing on the Kuroshio Extension Jet east of Japan as the direct continuation of the Kuroshio, we show that feedback between ocean mesoscale eddies and the atmosphere (OME-A) is fundamental to the dynamics and control of these energetic currents.

在能够解析涡旋的耦合气候模式模拟中，抑制OME-A反馈会使黑潮延伸体急流减弱20%–40%。

Suppressing OME-A feedback in eddy-resolving coupled climate model simulations results in a 20–40 per cent weakening in the Kuroshio Extension Jet.

这是因为OME-A反馈主导涡动位能的损耗，使从黑潮延伸体急流中提取的涡动位能有70%以上被耗散。

This is because OME-A feedback dominates eddy potential energy destruction, which dissipates more than 70 per cent of the eddy potential energy extracted from the Kuroshio Extension Jet.

缺少OME-A反馈时，为平衡能量收支，涡动位能生成必然减少，从而导致平均洋流减弱。

The absence of OME-A feedback inevitably leads to a reduction in eddy potential energy production in order to balance the energy budget, which results in a weakened mean current.

这一发现对于改进气候模式对主要海洋锋面的表征具有重要意义；这些锋面是模拟和预测温带风暴及其他极端事件[3–6]、以及预估气候变化对这些事件影响时不可或缺的组成部分。

The finding has important implications for improving climate models’ representation of major oceanic fronts, which are essential components in the simulation and prediction of extratropical storms and other extreme events[3–6], as well as in the projection of the effect on these events of climate change.

正文ARTICLE TEXT

随着高分辨率卫星观测和气候模式模拟的获得，越来越多的证据表明，海洋中尺度涡旋和锋面能够对大气行星边界层产生深刻影响[7–11]。

With the availability of high-resolution satellite observations and climate model simulations, it has become increasingly evident that ocean mesoscale eddies and fronts can have a profound influence on the atmospheric planetary boundary layer[7–11].

在黑潮延伸体和湾流延伸体等显著海洋锋面所在的关键区域，研究表明，中尺度和锋面尺度上的强海气耦合，对于维持大气风暴轴的位置[3,4,12,13]，以及为中纬度气候变率和天气形势变化提供影响来源[5,6,14]，都可能至关重要。

In critical regions where prominent oceanic fronts reside, such as the Kuroshio and Gulf Stream Extensions, studies have demonstrated that the strong coupling between the atmosphere and ocean at meso- and frontal scales can be vital in anchoring atmospheric storm tracks[3,4,12,13] and providing a source of influence on mid-latitude climate variability and weather pattern changes[5,6,14].

因此，我们迫切需要理解维持海洋锋面系统的基本动力学，并提高气候模式模拟和预测这些重要海洋特征的能力。

Therefore, it is imperative that we understand the fundamental dynamics for maintaining oceanic frontal systems and improve climate models’ ability to simulate and predict these important oceanic features.

在经典海洋环流理论中，黑潮及其延伸体与其他中纬度西边界流系统一样，被解释为海洋环流圈的一部分，这些环流圈由作用于海面的大尺度风应力维持。

In classic ocean circulation theory, the Kuroshio Current and its extension, like other mid-latitude western boundary current regimes, are explained as a part of ocean circulation gyres maintained by the large-scale wind stress acting on the ocean’s surface.

在行星波的作用下，风输入的能量向西输送并沿西边界积聚，形成强大的西边界流——黑潮；黑潮从日本转向东流，沿北太平洋副热带环流圈与副极地环流圈的边界形成强劲的黑潮延伸体急流（KEJ）。

Through the action of planetary waves, the energy input by the winds is transported westward and accumulated along the western boundary, giving rise to the intense western boundary current—the Kuroshio, which swings to the east from Japan to form the energetic Kuroshio Extension Jet (KEJ) along the boundary between the North Pacific subtropical and subpolar gyres.

KEJ十分强劲，以至于出现流体动力不稳定，因而强烈的海洋涡旋可通过从平均洋流中提取能量而在其附近形成，并反过来对平均急流产生反馈[15–17]。

The KEJ is so energetic that it is hydrodynamically unstable, allowing vigorous ocean eddies to form in its vicinity by extracting energy from the mean current, which in turn can feed back onto the mean jet[15–17].

这一经典描述并未考虑黑潮延伸体区域OME-A相互作用的影响。

In this classic description, the effect of OME-A interaction in the Kuroshio Extension region is not taken into consideration.

然而，近期的高分辨率卫星观测和海气耦合模式模拟表明，中尺度海洋锋面和涡旋会向上覆大气释放大量位能。

However, recent high-resolution satellite observations and coupled atmosphere–ocean model simulations indicate a large potential energy release from mesoscale oceanic fronts and eddies to the overlying atmosphere.

图1展示了黑潮延伸体区域中尺度海表温度（SST）与表面湍流热通量（THF）之间的关系，该关系由高分辨率卫星观测SST和再分析表面THF得到。

Figure 1 shows the relationship between mesoscale sea surface temperatures (SSTs) and surface turbulent heat fluxes (THFs) over the Kuroshio Extension region, derived from high-resolution satellite observed SST and reanalysis surface THFs.

多种高分辨率再分析产品呈现出高度一致的SST–THF关系，其统计显著性为P值小于0.01：SST每升高1 °C，THF约增加40–56 W m⁻²（指向大气）。

Various high-resolution reanalysis products reveal a remarkably consistent SST–THF relationship, statistically significant with P value less than 0.01, which gives an approximately 40–56 W m−2 THF increase (into the atmosphere) per 1 °C SST increase.

美国国家大气研究中心开发并开展的解析涡旋的社区地球系统模式（CESM）模拟很好地再现了这一关系（见方法）。

This relationship is well reproduced by an eddy-resolving Community Earth System Model (CESM) simulation developed and conducted by the National Center for Atmospheric Research (see Methods).

得克萨斯农工大学开发并开展的一组更高分辨率耦合区域气候模式（CRCM）集合模拟也很好地再现了这一关系（见方法），但THF对SST变化的敏感度更高，达到56 W m⁻² °C⁻¹。

It is also well reproduced by an ensemble of higher-resolution Coupled Regional Climate Model (CRCM) simulations developed and performed at Texas A&M University (see Methods), except that the sensitivity of THF to SST change is at a higher rate of 56 W m−2 °C−1.

这种强OME-A反馈是否会影响KEJ及其伴随的海洋锋面，是本研究关注的重点。

Whether this strong OME-A feedback will have an impact on the KEJ and the associated oceanic front is the focus of this study.

为研究这一问题，我们分别使用全球和区域气候模式（CESM和CRCM）开展了一组成对模拟。

To examine this question, we conduct a set of twin simulations using both global and regional climate models (CESM and CRCM).

这组成对模拟包括一个标准的高分辨率模式模拟（控制模拟，简称CTRL），以及一个除抑制中尺度SST变率对大气的影响之外、其他设置均相同的模拟（下文称为中尺度涡旋滤除模拟，简称MEFS）。

The twin simulations consist of a standard high-resolution model simulation (the control simulation or CTRL) and an otherwise identical simulation except that the influence of mesoscale SST variability on the atmosphere is suppressed (hereafter referred to as the Mesoscale Eddy Filtered Simulation or MEFS).

MEFS在每个耦合时间步，对传递给大气模式的模拟SST施加空间低通滤波，从而抑制中尺度SST的影响；这样一来，尽管海洋模式仍然模拟海洋涡旋，大气却无法“感知”它们（见方法）。

The suppression of mesoscale SST influence in MEFS is achieved by spatially low-pass-filtering the simulated SST that is given to the atmospheric model at each coupling step, so that the atmosphere does not ‘feel’ ocean eddies even though they are simulated by the ocean model (see Methods).

因此，MEFS中的OME-A反馈大幅减弱，而比较CTRL与MEFS可以评估OME-A反馈对KEJ的影响。

As such, OME-A feedback is greatly reduced in MEFS and a comparison between CTRL and MEFS allows us to assess the impact of OME-A feedback on the KEJ.

这些实验的概念设计与此前一项研究涡旋对湾流影响的工作[18]相似，但本文采用完全耦合的模式框架，而不是单独海洋模式，以便更明确、更准确地模拟OME-A反馈。

The conceptual design of the experiments is similar to a previous study of the effects of the eddies on the Gulf Stream[18], except that here a fully coupled—instead of an ocean-only—modelling framework is employed to allow more explicit and accurate simulations of the OME-A feedback.

图2a、b展示了北太平洋上层250 m海洋的垂直平均的平均动能，结果来自为期11年的全球CESM CTRL和MEFS模拟。

Figure 2a and b displays the vertically averaged mean kinetic energy of the upper 250 m of the ocean from the 11-year global CESM CTRL and MEFS runs over the North Pacific.

显然，抑制OME-A反馈会使KEJ更加蜿蜒、强度更弱（方法和扩展数据图1）。

It is evident that the suppression of OME-A feedback leads to a more meandering and weaker KEJ (Methods and Extended Data Fig. 1).

CTRL与MEFS的平均动能之差清楚表明，在整个北太平洋中，KEJ受OME-A反馈变化的影响最大，而且MEFS中更加蜿蜒的KEJ似乎向北移动（图2c）。

The difference between the mean kinetic energy in CTRL and MEFS clearly shows that over the entire North Pacific Ocean it is the KEJ that is most affected by the change in OME-A feedback and the more meandering KEJ in MEFS appears to shift northward (Fig. 2c).

更加蜿蜒且北移的KEJ与以下观测结果一致：当KEJ处于不稳定（更加蜿蜒）的状态时，其轴线往往向北移动[19]。

The more meandering and northward-shifted KEJ is consistent with the observations that the KEJ axis tends to move northward when the KEJ resides in an unstable (more meandering) regime[19].

此外，KEJ的变化发生在极短的时间内，这一点清楚地体现在CESM CTRL和MEFS模拟第一年平均的纬向平均KEJ之间的鲜明对比中：MEFS中的KEJ比CTRL中明显更弱、更宽（图2d–f），与长期平均的KEJ变化相似（扩展数据图1）。

Furthermore, the KEJ change occurs within a remarkably short time period, which is clearly illustrated by the sharp contrast between the zonal mean KEJ averaged over the first year of the CESM CTRL and MEFS runs: the KEJ in MEFS is considerably weaker and broader than that in CTRL (Fig. 2d–f), similar to the long-term averaged KEJ change (Extended Data Fig. 1).

区域CRCM集合模拟进一步证实了KEJ对OME-A反馈变化的快速响应：抑制OME-A反馈后，其中的KEJ也呈现十分相似的减弱（图2g–i），表明这一发现具有稳健性。

The rapid KEJ response to OME-A feedback changes is corroborated by the ensemble of regional CRCM runs that show a very similar weakening in the KEJ after OME-A feedback is suppressed (Fig. 2g–i), indicating the robustness of the finding.

由于在中纬度地区，海洋通过行星波对大尺度风强迫作出调整的时间尺度为数年至数十年[19]，因此这种快速的KEJ响应不太可能由大尺度大气环流变化引起（方法和扩展数据图2）。

Because oceanic adjustment timescales to large-scale wind forcing through planetary waves are on the order of several years to decades at mid-latitudes[19], it is unlikely that this rapid KEJ response is caused by large-scale atmospheric circulation changes (Methods and Extended Data Fig. 2).

进一步考察模式对OME-A反馈的响应发现，当MEFS中的OME-A反馈受到抑制时，KEJ的减弱伴随着中尺度涡旋能量的显著增加。

A further probe into the model response to OME-A feedback reveals that the weakened KEJ is accompanied with a substantial increase in mesoscale eddy energy when OME-A feedback is suppressed in MEFS.

图3展示了全球CESM和区域CRCM实验中，黑潮延伸体区域抑制反馈模拟与完整反馈模拟的涡动动能（EKE）波数谱之比（MEFS/CTRL），以及涡量平方（涡度方差的一半）的波数谱之比。

Figure 3 shows the ratio (MEFS/CTRL) between wavenumber spectra of eddy kinetic energy (EKE) derived from the suppressed-feedback and full-feedback simulations in the Kuroshio Extension region, as well as the ratio between wavenumber spectra of enstrophy (half of the vorticity variance), for both global CESM and regional CRCM experiments.

两套模式实验均显示，相较于CTRL，MEFS在波长小于约500 km时的EKE和涡量平方都增加，但增幅最大的是波长小于100 km的部分（图3）。

Both model experiments show an increase in EKE and enstrophy for wavelengths less than about 500 km when comparing MEFS to CTRL, but the largest increase occurs at wavelengths less than 100 km (Fig. 3).

具体而言，全球CESM模拟在这一中尺度涡旋范围内的EKE增加近30%，涡量平方增加50%；区域CRCM成对集合模拟中的增幅略小，这可能是由于积分时段较短。

In particular, the global CESM simulations exhibit a nearly 30% EKE increase and a 50% enstrophy increase at this mesoscale eddy range, while the increases in the regional CRCM twin ensembles are slightly smaller, probably owing to the short integration period.

中尺度涡旋活动增强意味着涡旋能量平衡发生变化；因此，我们进一步考察，能否从中尺度涡旋能量学变化的角度解释KEJ对OME-A反馈变化的响应。

The energized mesoscale eddy activity implies a change in eddy energy balance; we therefore asked whether the KEJ response to OME-A feedback change can be explained in terms of the change in mesoscale eddy energetics.

为阐明这一问题，我们利用区域CRCM成对集合模拟开展了海洋涡动位能（EPE）分析（详情见方法和扩展数据图3）。

To shed light on this question, we perform an ocean eddy potential energy (EPE) analysis using the twin ensembles of regional CRCM simulations (see Methods and Extended Data Fig. 3 for details).

EPE的变化率由能量生成与损耗过程的共同作用决定（见方法中的方程（1））。

The rate of EPE change is determined by the interplay between energy production and destruction processes (see equation (1) in Methods).

能量生成主要通过将储存在平均流中的平均有效位能（MAPE）转换为EPE来实现（下文称为MAPE向EPE转换）。

The production is primarily achieved through converting the mean available potential energy (MAPE) stored in the mean flow to EPE (hereafter referred to as MAPE-to-EPE conversion).

能量损耗包括两个过程：一个过程将EPE转换为EKE，为涡旋环流供能（下文称为EPE向EKE转换）；另一个过程则通过海洋混合或向大气输出涡旋能量来耗散EPE（下文称为EPE耗散）[18]。

The destruction consists of two processes: one converts EPE to EKE to energize eddy circulation (hereafter referred to as EPE-to-EKE conversion) and the other acts to dissipate EPE through either oceanic mixing or exporting eddy energy to the atmosphere (hereafter referred to as EPE dissipation)[18].

因此，受到OME-A反馈强烈影响的是EPE耗散。

Thus, it is the EPE dissipation that is strongly affected by OME-A feedback.

为量化OME-A反馈能够在多大程度上影响EPE耗散，我们分析并比较了区域CRCM成对集合模拟中海洋上层250 m垂直积分的EPE收支（扩展数据图3中的内嵌表格）。

To quantify the extent to which OME-A feedback can affect the EPE dissipation, we analyse and compare the vertically integrated EPE budget over the upper 250 m of the ocean in the twin ensembles of regional CRCM simulations (inset tables in Extended Data Fig. 3).

在完整OME-A反馈下，CRCM CTRL表明，从MAPE中提取的EPE有近74%因EPE耗散而损失，不足22%转换为EKE（其余4%对应积分期间EPE的变化）。

With full OME-A feedback, CRCM CTRL reveals that nearly 74% of the EPE extracted from MAPE is lost owing to EPE dissipation and less than 22% is converted to EKE (the remaining 4% is accounted for by the EPE change during the integration period).

这一结果清楚表明，存在OME-A反馈时，黑潮生成的EPE中近四分之三被耗散，只有一小部分（不足四分之一）用于驱动涡旋环流。

This result clearly indicates that in the presence of OME-A feedback, nearly three-quarters of the Kuroshio EPE production is dissipated and only a small portion (less than a quarter) is used to drive eddy circulation.

相比之下，当OME-A反馈受到抑制时，CRCM MEFS显示，EPE耗散和EPE向EKE转换在EPE收支中的作用发生逆转：转换为EKE的EPE（约47%）多于被耗散的EPE（约40%）。

In sharp contrast, when OME-A feedback is suppressed, CRCM MEFS shows that the roles of EPE dissipation and of EPE-to-EKE conversion in the EPE budget are reversed: more EPE is being converted to EKE (about 47%) than being dissipated (about 40%).

EPE向EKE转换的增加，与图3所示MEFS中增强的中尺度涡旋活动一致。

The increase in EPE-to-EKE conversion is consistent with the enhanced mesoscale eddy activity in MEFS as shown in Fig. 3.

因此，显而易见，OME-A反馈对于耗散海洋EPE和维持EPE收支至关重要。

Thus, it is very clear that OME-A feedback is essential for dissipating ocean EPE and maintaining the EPE budget.

区域CRCM成对集合模拟之间的EPE耗散变化，为解释有、无OME-A反馈时模拟KEJ为何表现不同提供了关键。

The change in EPE dissipation between the twin ensembles of regional CRCM simulations provides the key as to why the simulated KEJ behaves differently with and without OME-A feedback.

在完整反馈下，黑潮系统经历强耗散。

With the full feedback, the Kuroshio system experiences strong dissipation.

为生成并维持活跃的涡旋场，KEJ必须足够强劲，以促成强烈的MAPE向EPE转换，从而克服OME-A反馈引起的强EPE耗散。

To generate and maintain an active eddy field, the KEJ must be sufficiently energetic to allow strong conversion from MAPE to EPE in order to overcome the vigorous EPE dissipation owing to OME-A feedback.

当OME-A反馈受到抑制时，EPE耗散大幅减少，使黑潮系统处于耗散较弱的状态。

With the suppressed OME-A feedback, EPE dissipation is greatly reduced, leaving the Kuroshio system in a less dissipative regime.

在这种状态下，转换为EKE的EPE多于向大气耗散的EPE，因而涡旋场更强（图3），而KEJ更加蜿蜒且更弱（图2和扩展数据图4）。

In such a regime, more EPE is being converted to EKE than is being dissipated to the atmosphere, leading to a more energetic eddy field (Fig. 3) and a more meandering and weaker KEJ (Fig. 2 and Extended Data Fig. 4).

图4以示意图概括了所提出的机制，详细的EPE收支分析为该机制提供了支持（方法和扩展数据图3）。

A schematic summary of the proposed mechanism is shown in Fig. 4, which is supported by a detailed EPE budget analysis (Methods and Extended Data Fig. 3).

这些高分辨率海气耦合模式实验结果，为理解控制黑潮延伸体锋面和急流的动力学提供了新视角，表明经典海洋环流理论需要将局地OME-A反馈纳入考虑。

These results from the high-resolution coupled atmosphere–ocean model experiments offer a new perspective on the dynamics controlling the Kuroshio Extension Front and Jet, revealing that local OME-A feedback needs to be taken into consideration by classic ocean circulation theory.

对控制黑潮变率的复杂动力学的这一认识，对于改进气候模式对黑潮延伸体锋面、涡旋及其气候影响的模拟具有重要意义。

This insight into the complex dynamics governing Kuroshio variability has important implications for the improvement of climate models in simulating the Kuroshio Extension Front and eddies as well as their impact on climate.

近期研究表明，沿黑潮延伸体锋面的强SST梯度以及涡旋引起的中尺度SST变率，能够影响太平洋风暴轴[5,6,13]；这增加了如下可能性：气候模式若能更好地模拟黑潮涡旋及其与大气的反馈，就可能改善热带外天气形势的短期预测，以及风暴轴对未来气候变化响应的长期预估。

Recent studies have shown that the sharp SST gradient and the eddy-induced mesoscale SST variability along the Kuroshio Extension Front can have an impact on the Pacific storm track[5,6,13], increasing the likelihood that an improved simulation of Kuroshio eddies and their feedback with the atmosphere in climate models may lead to improvements in short-term prediction of extratropical weather patterns and long-term projection of the storm track response to future climate change.

尽管本研究聚焦于KEJ，其发现也适用于其他海洋锋面，例如湾流锋面（方法和扩展数据图5），并且很可能也适用于存在强OME-A反馈的南大洋锋面[1,20–22]。

Although the focus of this study is on the KEJ, the findings of this study are applicable to other oceanic fronts, such as the Gulf Stream front (Methods and Extended Data Fig. 5) and probably also Southern Ocean fronts, where strong OME-A feedback exists[1,20–22].

这些海洋锋区不仅是热带外海洋对大气的影响可能特别强烈的海洋热点区域[23]，也是当前气候模式很难再现观测结果的区域[24–29]。

Not only are these oceanic frontal zones hotspots of the oceans where extratropical ocean influence on the atmosphere may be particularly strong[23], they are also the regions where present climate models have great difficulties in reproducing observations[24–29].

本文结果或可为理解并减小这些关键区域的模式偏差，以及改进气候模拟和预估提供启示[30]。

The results presented here may shed light on how to understand and reduce model biases in these critical regions and improve climate simulations and projection[30].

方法

Methods

模式配置与试验设计。

Model configuration and experiment design.

本研究的高分辨率、可解析涡旋的全球气候模拟由美国国家大气研究中心使用社区地球系统模式（CESM）开展。

The high-resolution eddy-resolving global climate simulations in this study are conducted by the National Center for Atmospheric Research using the Community Earth System Model (CESM).

模式配置包括采用谱元动力框架的社区大气模式第5版（CAM5）、并行海洋模式第2版（POP2），以及海冰和陆面模式2。

The model configuration includes the Community Atmosphere Model version 5 (CAM5) with a spectral element dynamical core, the Parallel Ocean Program version 2 (POP2), as well as sea ice and land models2.

CAM5和POP2的水平分辨率分别约为0.25°和0.1°。

CAM5 and POP2 have horizontal resolutions of about 0.25° and about 0.1°, respectively.

海洋模式向大气模式提供海表温度（SST）和表层流速，并每6 h接收大气模式利用Large和Yeager31表面层方案计算并更新的通量（参考文献2、32）。

The ocean model provides SST and surface current velocity to the atmosphere model and receives updated fluxes from the atmosphere model calculated using the Large and Yeager31 surface layer scheme every 6 h (refs 2 and 32).

CESM的CTRL试验积分了100年，并进行了14年的预积分。

CESM CTRL was integrated for 100 years with 14 years of spin-up.

配对的MEFS试验从第59年3月1日开始，其设置与CTRL相同，唯一的区别是在每个耦合步长中，将POP2模拟的SST传递给CAM5之前先施加空间低通滤波，以去除中尺度SST变率。

Starting on 1 March of year 59, the twin MEFS was conducted with the same settings as CTRL except that a low-pass spatial filter was applied to the POP2 simulated SST before being passed to CAM5 at each coupling step to remove mesoscale SST variability.

反过来，海洋受到大气表面通量的强迫，而这些通量不含中尺度SST的信号。

The ocean in turn was forced by the atmospheric surface fluxes, which contained no signature of the mesoscale SST.

所用的低通滤波器为1,000 km × 1,000 km的矩形滑动平均滤波器。

The low-pass filter used was a 1,000 km × 1,000 km rectangular boxcar filter.

MEFS积分了11年。

MEFS was integrated for 11 years.

本研究展示的配对模拟比较基于CESM CTRL和MEFS从第59年到第70年的11年模拟。

The twin simulation comparison shown in this study is based on the 11-year CESM CTRL and MEFS simulation from year 59 to year 70.

区域耦合气候模式（CRCM）由得克萨斯农工大学开发，用于开展高分辨率模拟。

The Coupled Regional Climate Model (CRCM) was developed at Texas A&M University to perform simulations at high resolution.

该模式采用定制的耦合软件框架，可在区域海洋与大气模式分量之间的界面上频繁交换质量、动量和能量。

The model consists of a custom coupling software framework that allows frequent mass, momentum and energy exchanges at the interface between its ocean and atmosphere regional model components.

CRCM可在运行时高效地对界面场进行重映射，对海洋表面物理量进行空间滤波，并提供一组不同的表面通量计算方案。

CRCM can efficiently perform remapping of interface fields as well as spatial filtering of ocean surface quantities at runtime, and provides a set of different surface flux formulations.

其大气分量采用美国国家大气研究中心开发的天气研究与预报模式33（WRF），海洋分量采用罗格斯大学和加利福尼亚大学洛杉矶分校开发的区域海洋模拟系统（ROMS）。

It includes the Weather Research and Forecasting33 (WRF) Model developed by the National Center for Atmospheric Research as the atmospheric component and the Regional Ocean Modelling Systems (ROMS) developed by Rutgers University and UCLA as the oceanic component.

CRCM配置了ROMS与WRF共用的Arakawa-C网格，水平分辨率为9 km，范围从3.6° N到66° N、99° E到270° E，覆盖整个北太平洋。

The CRCM is configured with a common Arakawa-C grid shared by ROMS and WRF at 9-km horizontal resolution extending from 3.6° N to 66° N and from 99° E to 270° E to cover the entire North Pacific Ocean.

WRF在垂直方向分为30层，其详细配置遵循近期的一项研究6。

WRF is divided into 30 vertical levels and its detailed configuration follows a recent study6.

ROMS采用地形跟随垂直坐标，共设50层；垂直混合采用K剖面参数化湍流混合闭合方案34，动量混合采用类Smagorinsky的双调和水平混合方案35。

ROMS has 50 levels in a vertical terrain-following coordinate and includes a K-profile parameterization turbulent mixing closure scheme34 for vertical mixing and a bi-harmonic horizontal Smagorinsky-like mixing for the momentum35.

CRCM模拟的耦合策略与CESM相似，区别在于采用WRF的通量计算方案，而且WRF与ROMS每小时耦合一次。

In CRCM simulations, the coupling strategy was similar to that of CESM except that WRF’s flux formulations were used and WRF and ROMS were coupled hourly.

CRCM CTRL由5个积分时长为6个月的模拟构成集合，各成员分别于2003、2004、2005、2006和2007年的10月1日初始化。

The CRCM CTRL comprised an ensemble of five 6-month integrations, initialized on 1 October 2003, 2004, 2005, 2006 and 2007, respectively.

ROMS的每组初始条件均来自一次6年的预积分，该预积分使用协调海洋—海冰参考试验第2版（CORE-II）31作为表面强迫，并以简单海洋资料同化（SODA）36的5日平均输出作为侧边界条件。

Each of these initial conditions for ROMS was derived from a 6-year spin-up run, carried out using version 2 of the Coordinated Ocean-ice Reference Experiments (CORE-II)31 as surface forcing and 5-day averaged Simple Ocean Data Assimilation (SODA)36 output as lateral boundary conditions.

WRF的初始条件来自美国国家环境预报中心—能源部AMIP-II再分析资料（NCEP-II）37。

WRF initial conditions were derived from the NCEP-Department of Energy AMIP-II Reanalysis (NCEP-II)37.

CRCM CTRL集合的侧边界条件中，WRF采用每6 h的NCEP-II再分析资料，ROMS采用SODA的5日平均输出。

The lateral boundary conditions for the CRCM CTRL ensemble were derived from 6-hourly NCEP-II reanalysis for WRF and 5-day average SODA output for ROMS.

MEFS集合的设置与CTRL一致，唯一的区别是在每个耦合步长中，将ROMS模拟的SST提供给WRF之前，先用内嵌的低通Loess滤波器进行处理，其截止波长为经度15° × 纬度5°38。

The set-up of the MEFS ensemble was identical to CTRL except that the ROMS-simulated SST was processed by an embedded low-pass Loess filter with a 15° (longitude) × 5° (latitude) cut-off wavelength38 before being provided to WRF at each coupling step.

CESM MEFS中减弱的KEJ。

Weakened KEJ in CESM MEFS.

对黑潮延伸体区域纬向平均的冬季（10月至翌年3月，ONDJFM）平均U进行合成分析，可以更清楚地展示全球CESM模拟中OME-A反馈被抑制时KEJ的减弱。

The weakened KEJ in global CESM simulations when OME-A feedback is suppressed can be shown more clearly by a composite analysis of zonally averaged winter-season (ONDJFM) mean U over the Kuroshio Extension region.

合成以KEJ轴线为参照，该轴线定义为每年冬季U最大值所在的纬度。

The composite was taken according to the KEJ axis defined by the latitude of maximum U in each winter.

扩展数据图1展示了CTRL、MEFS及MEFS减CTRL的纬向平均U的11个冬季合成结果。

Extended Data Fig. 1 shows the 11-winter-season composite of zonal-mean U from CTRL, MEFS and MEFS minus CTRL.

扩展数据图1a–c所示的长期平均KEJ变化，与图2d–f所示的第一年KEJ变化，以及图2g–i所示区域CRCM模拟的集合平均KEJ变化都极为相似。

The long-term averaged KEJ change shown in Extended Data Fig. 1a–c is remarkably similar to the first-year KEJ change shown in Fig. 2d–f and to the ensemble mean KEJ change simulated by regional CRCM shown in Fig. 2g–i.

大尺度大气环流的影响。

Influence of the large-scale atmospheric circulation.

为进一步证实KEJ的快速响应不太可能由大尺度大气环流变化引起，扩展数据图2a展示了全球CESM CTRL模拟11年平均的年平均大尺度风应力旋度；计算所用风应力先经过经度10° × 纬度10°的滑动平均滤波器进行空间低通滤波。

To further confirm that the rapid KEJ response is unlikely to be caused by large-scale atmospheric circulation changes, Extended Data Fig. 2a shows the annual mean large-scale wind stress curl averaged over the 11-year global CESM CTRL simulation, which is based on spatially low-pass filtered wind stresses using a 10° (longitude) × 10° (latitude) boxcar filter.

黑潮是北太平洋副热带环流的一部分，由热带东风信风与中纬度西风所产生的大尺度负风应力旋度驱动。

As a part of the subtropical gyre in the North Pacific, the Kuroshio is driven by the large-scale negative wind stress curl generated by the tropical easterly trade winds and mid-latitude westerlies.

在抑制OME-A反馈的全球CESM MEFS中，这一大尺度风应力旋度并未出现明显变化（扩展数据图2b）。

In the global CESM MEFS when OME-A feedback is suppressed, this large-scale wind stress curl does not exhibit much change (Extended Data Fig. 2b).

区域CRCM的MEFS与CTRL中也得到了相似的结果。

Similar results are found in the regional CRCM MEFS and CTRL.

这些发现进一步表明，正文所示的KEJ显著减弱不能归因于大尺度大气环流的变化，而更可能受到局地OME-A反馈变化的影响。

These findings further suggest that the substantial weakening of KEJ shown in the main text cannot be attributed to changes in large-scale atmospheric circulations and is more probably influenced by the change in the local OME-A feedback.

EPE的能量收支。

Energy budget for EPE.

本研究使用短期（6个月冬季）区域CRCM积分的配对集合，而非较长期的全球CESM模拟开展能量分析，主要出于研究实施方面的原因：现有全球模拟目前无法提供详细涡旋能量分析所需的高空间和时间分辨率海洋变量输出。

In this study, we use the twin ensembles of short-term (6-month winter season) regional CRCM integrations, instead of the longer-term global CESM simulations, for the energetic analysis, mainly because of a programmatic reason: output of high-resolution (both in space and time) oceanic variables required for a detailed eddy energetic analysis is currently unavailable for the existing global simulations.

此外，使用短期区域CRCM模拟也有若干科学依据。

Additionally, there are a number of scientific justifications for using the short-term regional CRCM simulations.

首先，在较长期模拟中，行星波调整可以影响KEJ对大尺度大气强迫变化的响应，因而难以评估局地OME-A反馈在黑潮涡旋能量过程中的作用（不过，上文已证明，在本研究中这并非影响因素）。

First, the longer-term simulations allow planetary wave adjustment to affect the KEJ response to changes in large-scale atmospheric forcing, making it difficult to evaluate the role of local OME-A feedback in Kuroshio eddy energetics (although in our case we have demonstrated above that this is not a factor).

其次，CESM MEFS采用的全球滤波可能影响热带海洋环流，进而远程影响黑潮涡旋的能量过程，同样会使OME-A反馈对黑潮涡旋能量过程影响的解释变得复杂。

Second, the global filtering adopted in the CESM MEFS can potentially affect tropical ocean circulations, which in turn can remotely influence Kuroshio eddy energetics, again complicating the interpretation of the OME-A feedback effect on Kuroshio eddy energetics.

区域模拟方法可以解决这些问题。

These problems are remedied by the regional modelling approach.

然而，该方法的积分时段较短，无法考察平衡态下的涡旋能量过程。

However, the short integration period in this approach prevents an examination of equilibrium-state eddy energetics.

尽管如此，分析瞬变态下的涡旋能量过程，仍有助于理解OME-A反馈在维持海洋涡旋中的作用。

Nevertheless, an analysis of transient-state eddy energetics can still be enlightening in understanding the role of OME-A feedback in maintaining oceanic eddies.

在分析涡旋能量收支时，EPE定义为145° E–160° E之间的纬向扰动流所对应的位能。

In analysing the eddy energy budget, EPE is defined as the potential energy associated with zonally perturbed flows between 145° E–160° E.

敏感性试验表明，采用不同区域（例如150° E–165° E）不会对EPE收支分析产生实质性影响。

Sensitivity tests suggest that using different regions (for example, 150° E–165° E) does not substantially affect the EPE budget analysis.

EPE的控制方程39为：

The governing equation for EPE39 is:

式（1）：EPE收支方程。

Equation (1): EPE budget.

其中，⟨…⟩表示水平区域平均，b为浮力，uh = (u, v)为水平速度，w为垂直速度，∇h = (∂/∂x, ∂/∂y)，ρ0 = 1,025 kg m−3为海水密度；Nr为与预先定义的背景位密度剖面ρr对应的浮力频率，该背景剖面取为分析区域（145° E–160° E，30° N–42° N）内时间平均位密度（以海表为参考面）的区域平均。

where ⟨…⟩ denotes the horizontal area average, b is the buoyancy, uh = (u, v) is the horizontal velocity, w is the vertical velocity, ∇h = (∂/∂x, ∂/∂y), ρ0 = 1,025 kg m−3 is the water density, and Nr is the buoyancy frequency associated with a predefined background potential density profile ρr that is chosen to be the area average of the time-mean potential density (referenced to the sea surface) in the analysis domain (145° E–160° E, 30° N–42° N).

带撇号和上横线的变量分别表示纬向扰动值和纬向平均值。

Variables denoted by primes and bars correspond to the zonal perturbed and mean values, respectively.

方程（1）左侧的项是EPE的时间变化率，由方程（1）右侧的生成项和损耗项决定。

The term on the left-hand side of equation (1) is the time rate of change of EPE, which is determined by the production and destruction terms on the right-hand side of equation (1).

EPE生成来自两个过程：EPE的平流（方程（1）右侧第一项），以及涡动浮力通量作用于平均经向浮力梯度（方程（1）右侧第二项）；两者的综合作用主要是将MAPE转化为EPE，因此称为斜压转换（即正文中的MAPE向EPE转换）。

EPE production comes from the two processes: advection of EPE (the first term on the right-hand side of equation (1)) and eddy buoyancy fluxes acting on the mean meridional buoyancy gradient (the second term on the right-hand side of equation (1)), the combined effect of which is primarily to convert MAPE to EPE and which is therefore referred to as baroclinic conversion (that is, MAPE-to-EPE conversion in the main text).

EPE的损耗由方程（1）右侧第三项和第四项描述：一项为垂直涡动浮力通量，它将EPE转化为EKE，为涡旋环流供能（即EPE向EKE转换）；另一项为EPE耗散（EPD），通过海洋混合或将涡旋能量输出至大气实现，如正文所述。

Destruction of EPE is described by the third and fourth terms on the right-hand side of equation (1): the vertical eddy buoyancy flux that converts EPE to EKE to energize eddy circulation (that is, EPE-to-EKE conversion) and EPE dissipation (EPD) through either oceanic mixing or exporting eddy energy to the atmosphere, as depicted in the main text.

这些项使用CRCM模拟的日平均诊断输出计算，方程严格平衡。

These terms are computed using daily mean diagnostic output from the CRCM simulations and the equation is exactly balanced.

扩展数据图3汇总了黑潮延伸体区域配对区域模拟集合的EPE分析结果。

The result of EPE analyses from the twin ensembles of regional simulations in the Kuroshio Extension region is summarized in Extended Data Fig. 3.

在包含完整OME-A反馈的CTRL集合中，可以清楚地看到，上层50 m内的EPE收支主要由斜压转换与EPE耗散相互平衡，二者均在近表层达到峰值，并随深度增加呈指数减小。

In the CTRL ensemble with full OME-A feedback, it is evident that within the upper 50 m the main balance in the EPE budget is between the baroclinic conversion and EPE dissipation, both of which peak near the surface and decrease exponentially with depth.

近表层的EPE向EKE转换很小，可以忽略，但在50 m以下成为主导因素。

The EPE-to-EKE conversion is negligibly smaller near the surface, but becomes a dominant factor below 50 m.

由于积分时间较短，倾向项虽不为零，但总体上对黑潮延伸体区域EPE收支的贡献较小。

The tendency term, although non-zero due to the short integration, in general makes a minor contribution to the EPE budget in the Kuroshio Extension region.

从海表到250 m进行垂直积分后（扩展数据图3中的插表），CRCM CTRL集合得到斜压转换为32.2 mW m−2、EPE耗散为23.7 mW m−2、EPE向EKE转换为7.1 mW m−2、时间变化率为1.4 mW m−2；这证实，在存在OME-A反馈时，黑潮的大部分EPE被耗散，而不是转化为EKE，如图4所示。

Upon a vertical integration from the surface to 250m (inset tables in Extended Data Fig. 3), the CRCM CTRL ensemble reveals a 32.2 mW m−2 baroclinic conversion, a 23.7 mW m−2 EPE dissipation, a 7.1 mW m−2 EPE-to-EKE conversion, and a 1.4 mW m−2 time rate of change, confirming that in the presence of OME-A feedback, most of the Kuroshio EPE, rather than being converted to EKE, is dissipated as illustrated in Fig. 4.

鉴于图1所示中尺度THF与SST之间的密切关系，预计OME-A反馈会强烈影响EPE耗散，因此在抑制OME-A反馈的CRCM MEFS集合中，EPE收支发生巨大变化并不意外。

Since OME-A feedback is expected to strongly affect EPE dissipation because of the close mesoscale THF–SST relationship shown in Fig. 1, it is no surprise that the EPE budget is greatly altered in the CRCM MEFS ensemble where OME-A feedback is suppressed.

扩展数据图3清楚地表明，与CTRL相比，MEFS中的EPE耗散降至约三分之一，近表层尤为明显，这证实了OME-A反馈将EPE输送至大气、使其从海洋中移除的作用。

From Extended Data Fig. 3, it is abundantly clear that the EPE dissipation is reduced by approximately threefold, particularly near the surface, in MEFS compared to CTRL, confirming the role of OME-A feedback in removing EPE to the atmosphere.

为维持EPE收支平衡，斜压转换（生成项）也大幅减少，但降幅小于EPE耗散的降幅。

To keep the EPE budget balanced, the baroclinic conversion (production term) is also greatly reduced, albeit not as much as the EPE dissipation reduction.

有趣的是，MEFS中的EPE向EKE转换反而增强，表明在缺少OME-A反馈时，更多EPE转化为EKE。

Interestingly, EPE-to-EKE conversion is even enhanced in MEFS, indicating that more EPE is converted to EKE in the absence of OME-A feedback.

用垂直积分（从海表至250 m深度）的EPE收支，可以更方便地概括这些变化（扩展数据图3中的插表）。

These changes are more conveniently summarized in terms of a vertically integrated (from surface to 250 m depth) EPE budget (inset tables in Extended Data Fig. 3).

与CTRL相比，MEFS中最重要的变化发生在EPE耗散：其减小约69%，从23.7 mW m−2降至7.4 mW m−2。

Compared to CTRL, the most important change in MEFS occurs in EPE dissipation, which is reduced by approximately 69% to 7.4 mW m−2 (from 23.7 mW m−2).

EPE向EKE转换增加约20%，从7.1 mW m−2增至8.5 mW m−2；将MEFS中的EPE耗散与EPE向EKE转换合计，EPE总损耗减小约48%，从30.8 mW m−2降至15.9 mW m−2。

EPE-to-EKE conversion increases by approximately 20% to 8.5 mW m−2 (from 7.1 mW m−2); combining the EPE dissipation and the EPE-to-EKE conversion in MEFS gives rise to a total reduction of EPE destruction by approximately 48% to 15.9 mW m−2 (from 30.8 mW m−2).

斜压转换减小约43%，从32.2 mW m−2降至18.3 mW m−2。

Baroclinic conversion reduces by approximately 43% to 18.3 mW m−2 (from 32.2 mW m−2).

最后，EPE变化率虽然从1.4 mW m−2增至2.4 mW m−2，但与其他各项相比仍然较小。

Finally, the rate of EPE change, though increased from 1.4 mW m−2 to 2.4 mW m−2, is still minor compared to other terms.

因此，在缺少OME-A反馈时，尽管EPE生成减少，实际上却有更多EPE转化为EKE、较少EPE被耗散，这支持了图4所示的机制假说。

Therefore, in the absence of OME-A feedback, even though EPE production is reduced, there is actually more EPE being converted to EKE and less EPE being dissipated, supporting the proposed mechanism, illustrated in Fig. 4.

EPE耗散的大幅减少还会导致积分期间EPE增加，从而提高EPE变化率，不过它对EPE总收支的贡献仍然较小。

The major reduction in EPE dissipation also causes an increase in EPE during the integration period, resulting in an enhanced rate of EPE change, although its contribution to the total EPE budget remains small.

由于斜压转换通过顺梯度的涡动浮力通量作用于平均浮力梯度来生成EPE40,41，因此，在缺少OME-A反馈时，EPE生成的大幅减少意味着涡动浮力通量或平均浮力梯度二者之一减小。

Since EPE production by baroclinic conversion is through down-gradient eddy buoyancy fluxes acting on the mean buoyancy gradient40,41, its major reduction in the absence of OME-A feedback implies that there is a decrease in either the eddy buoyancy fluxes or mean buoyancy gradient.

前一种可能性不大，因为与CTRL相比，MEFS中的中尺度涡旋活动增强，即EPE和EKE均增加。

The former is an unlikely candidate because mesoscale eddy activity is energized, that is, both EPE and EKE increase, in MEFS compared to CTRL.

这意味着，MEFS中抑制OME-A反馈更可能导致平均浮力梯度减弱。

It means that a weakened mean buoyancy gradient is a more likely consequence of OME-A feedback suppression in MEFS.

进一步分析表明，盐度对平均浮力梯度变化的贡献居次，因此斜压转换的减少主要归因于位温梯度减弱。

Further analyses indicate that salinity makes a secondary contribution to the change of mean buoyancy gradient, and thus the decrease in baroclinic conversion is mainly due to the weakened potential temperature gradient.

扩展数据图4比较了全球和区域模拟中沿黑潮延伸体的经向位温梯度合成结果。

Extended Data Fig. 4 shows a comparison of the meridional potential temperature gradient composite along the Kuroshio Extension in global and regional simulations.

与纬向平均U的合成分析相同，该合成以每个冬季的KEJ轴线为参照。

The composite was referenced to the KEJ axis in each winter, as in the composite analysis of zonal-mean U.

正如预期，受地转平衡约束，最强温度梯度与KEJ位于同一位置。

As expected, the strongest temperature gradient collocates with the KEJ owing to geostrophy.

当OME-A反馈被抑制时，与CTRL相比，MEFS中的温度锋减弱约15%，对应于图2中更宽、更弱的KEJ。

When OME-A feedback is suppressed, the temperature front is weakened by about 15% in MEFS compared to CTRL, corresponding to the broader and weaker KEJ shown in Fig. 2.

温度锋与KEJ减弱、斜压转换减少，共同支持了以下论点：当OME-A反馈被抑制时，由于EPE耗散大幅减少、EPE向EKE转换增强，只需从MAPE中提取较少的EPE，就能维持能量更充沛的涡旋场。

The weakened temperature front and KEJ along with the reduced baroclinic conversion support the argument that less EPE needs to be extracted from MAPE to sustain the more energetic eddy field when OME-A feedback is suppressed, owing to the much reduced EPE dissipation and enhanced EPE-to-EKE conversion.

湾流平均流与涡旋活动的变化。

Change of mean flow and eddy activity in the Gulf Stream.

为检验湾流延伸区是否也发生类似变化，我们还分析了全球CESM CTRL和MEFS模拟中该区域的平均流与涡旋活动变化。

To examine whether similar changes occur in the Gulf Stream extension region, we also analysed change of mean flow and eddy activity in the Gulf Stream extension region in the global CESM CTRL and MEFS simulations.

与北太平洋KEJ的响应相似，我们发现，在抑制OME-A反馈的全球MEFS中，湾流延伸区海流显著减弱（约25%；扩展数据图5a–c）；同时，MEFS中中尺度范围（小于约500 km）内的EKE和涡量平方增加（约20%–40%；扩展数据图5d、e）。

Similar to the KEJ response in the North Pacific, we found a substantial weakening (about 25%) of the Gulf Stream Extension current in global MEFS when OME-A feedback is suppressed (Extended Data Fig. 5a–c) and an increase of EKE and enstrophy (approximately 20%–40%) at mesoscale range (below about 500 km) in MEFS (Extended Data Fig. 5d, e).

黑潮延伸体区域和湾流延伸区的平均流与涡旋活动对OME-A反馈抑制表现出一致响应，进一步支持了我们的假说：OME-A反馈可能对调节西边界流状态至关重要。

The consistent mean flow and eddy activity response to suppression of OME-A feedback in the Kuroshio Extension region and Gulf Stream Extension further supports our hypothesis that OME-A feedback may be essential in regulating western boundary current regimes.

Data.

采用0.25°逐日卫星测高海表高度资料AVISO42（http://www.aviso.altimetry.fr/en/data/products/sea-surface-height-products.html），结合涡旋追踪算法43，识别黑潮延伸体区域的涡旋。
