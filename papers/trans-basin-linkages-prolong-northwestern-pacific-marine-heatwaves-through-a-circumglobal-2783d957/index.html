Abstract

西北太平洋（NWP）的海洋热浪正变得日益频繁且更为持续，但其能够维持多季节持续时间的机制仍知之甚少。

Marine heatwaves in the Northwestern Pacific (NWP) have become increasingly frequent and persistent, yet the mechanisms enabling their multiseasonal duration remain poorly understood.

通过观测分析与气候模式试验，我们表明西北太平洋海洋热浪主要由一个准定常 5 波环球波动（CGW）型驱动，该波动型独立于厄尔尼诺–南方涛动而运作。

Through observational analyses and climate model experiments, we demonstrate that NWP marine heatwaves are primarily driven by a quasistationary wavenumber 5 circumglobal wave (CGW) pattern that operates independently of El Niño–Southern Oscillation.

CGW 在夏季调制海表热通量，触发一个自我强化的反馈环路：西北太平洋增暖增强 CGW 型，从而在更长的暖季中放大并延长热浪。

The CGW modulates surface heat fluxes during summer, triggering a self-reinforcing feedback loop where NWP warming intensifies the CGW pattern, amplifying and prolonging heatwaves across extended warm seasons.

此外，CGW 驱动的北大西洋北部夏季增暖通过海洋热惯性持续至冬季，激发沿大圆路径传播、并回传至西北太平洋的波动型，从而在冷季维持热浪。

Additionally, CGW-driven summer warming in the northern North Atlantic persists into winter through oceanic thermal inertia, exciting a great-circle wave pattern that propagates back to the NWP, sustaining heatwaves through cold seasons.

大气波动与跨海盆相互作用的这一交织，使得多年际海洋热浪事件成为可能。

This interplay between atmospheric waves and trans-basin interactions enables multiyear marine heatwave events.

对观测资料与气候模式模拟的分析显示，近几十年来 CGW 的影响在增强，表明在持续的全球变暖背景下，西北太平洋海洋热浪将更为频繁、更为持久。

Analysis of observational data and climate model simulations reveals a strengthening CGW influence in recent decades, indicating more frequent and prolonged NWP marine heatwaves under ongoing global warming.

引言

引言

INTRODUCTION

在西北太平洋（NWP），海洋热浪（MHWs）近几十年来在频率和强度上均呈现显著增加（1–3）。

In the Northwestern Pacific (NWP), marine heatwaves (MHWs) have exhibited a marked increase in both frequency and intensity over recent decades (1–3).

其中许多事件表现出异常的持续性，跨越多个季节，甚至延续数年（1–6）。

Many of these events exhibit exceptional persistence, spanning multiple seasons or even extending across several years (1–6).

此类持久海洋热浪产生深远影响：扰乱海洋生态系统、降低渔业产量，并调制区域气候，从而对生物多样性和依赖海洋资源的沿海经济构成重大威胁（7–11）。

Such prolonged MHWs exert profound impacts, disrupting marine ecosystems, reducing fisheries productivity, and modulating regional climate, thereby posing substantial threats to biodiversity and coastal economies reliant on marine resources (7–11).

因此，阐明驱动持久西北太平洋海洋热浪的物理机制，对于提高预测能力、指导气候适应策略并加强海洋资源管理至关重要（2, 12, 13）。

Elucidating the physical mechanisms driving prolonged NWP MHWs is thus critical for improving predictive capability, informing climate adaptation strategies, and enhancing marine resource management (2, 12, 13).

现有关于海洋热浪形成的框架主要关注局地大气强迫（2, 13–16）、海洋过程（17–20），以及来自主要气候变率模态的远程影响（3, 5, 21–24）。

Existing frameworks for MHW formation focus on local atmospheric forcing (2, 13–16), oceanic processes (17–20), and remote influences from major climate variability modes (3, 5, 21–24).

与这些框架相一致，已有研究将西北太平洋海洋热浪归因于持续的高压系统，它们减少云量、增强太阳辐射吸收，并抑制潜热损失。

Consistent with these, studies attribute NWP MHWs to persistent high-pressure systems that reduce cloud cover, enhance solar radiation absorption, and suppress latent heat loss.

高压条件下增强的大气稳定度抑制了海表风，从而减小潜热通量（25–27）。

Enhanced atmospheric stability under high-pressure conditions suppresses surface winds, which decreases latent heat flux (25–27).

另一些研究强调海洋环流变化的作用，特别是通过西边界流的异常输送（25, 28, 29）。

Other research highlights the role of ocean circulation changes, particularly anomalous transport through western boundary currents (25, 28, 29).

若干研究还将厄尔尼诺–南方涛动（ENSO）识别为关键驱动因子：拉尼娜条件有利于形成促进西北太平洋增暖的反气旋性大气型（24, 30）。

Several studies also identify the El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO) as a key driver, with La Niña conditions fostering anticyclonic atmospheric patterns that promote NWP warming (24, 30).

然而，如前所述，若干西北太平洋海洋热浪（6）尽管中纬度大气具有很强的季节性（31）、且中纬度海气耦合较弱（32）——这两者通常会打断持续的海洋增暖——仍表现出显著的持续性。

Yet, as noted earlier, several NWP MHWs (6) demonstrate remarkable persistence despite the strong seasonality of the extratropical atmosphere (31) and weak ocean-atmosphere coupling in mid-latitudes (32), both of which generally act to disrupt sustained ocean warming.

值得注意的是，诸如 2018–2023 年海洋热浪等事件在厄尔尼诺和拉尼娜位相中均得以持续，表明在局地过程和热带强迫（它们是传统西北太平洋海洋热浪动力学的核心）之外，还有其他机制也在起作用（6, 27, 33）。

Notably, events like the 2018–2023 MHW have persisted across both El Niño and La Niña phases, suggesting that mechanisms beyond local processes and tropical forcing, central to conventional NWP MHW dynamics, also contribute (6, 27, 33).

近期研究表明，驱动东北太平洋海洋热浪（尤其是阿拉斯加湾附近）的异常高压系统嵌于行星尺度波列之中，通过跨海盆相互作用促进海洋热浪发展（23, 34）。

Recent studies show that anomalous high-pressure systems driving Northeastern Pacific MHWs, particularly around the Gulf of Alaska, are embedded within planetary-scale wave trains, facilitating MHW development through trans-basin interactions (23, 34).

这些发现表明，类似的大气波动动力学与跨海盆遥相关可能支撑着西北太平洋海洋热浪的持久持续时间，从而为在传统框架之外理解其持续性提供了一个视角。

These findings suggest that similar atmospheric wave dynamics and trans-basin teleconnections may underpin the prolonged duration of NWP MHWs, offering a perspective to understand their persistence beyond traditional frameworks.

在此基础上，我们提出：环球波动（CGW）型——亦称环球遥相关型（35, 36）——驱动了西北太平洋海洋热浪的延长持续时间。

Building on this, we propose that the circumglobal wave (CGW) pattern, also known as the circumglobal teleconnection pattern (35, 36), drives the extended duration of NWP MHWs.

这一显著的夏季环流特征横跨北半球中纬度带（36）。

This prominent summer circulation feature spans the Northern Hemisphere mid-latitude band (36).

尽管已有研究将 CGW 与陆地极端天气相联系——例如欧洲、亚洲和北美的热浪（37–41）——其在西北太平洋海洋热浪动力学中的作用仍未得到充分探讨。

While prior studies have linked the CGW to terrestrial extreme weather—such as heatwaves in Europe, Asia, and North America (37–41)—its role in NWP MHW dynamics remains underexplored.

鉴于 CGW 活动在北半球夏季达到峰值，并与文献记载的西北太平洋海洋热浪强度和频率最大值相一致（25, 42–46），它为在局地强迫之外启动并维持这些事件提供了一个有说服力的机制。

Given that CGW activity peaks during boreal summer, aligning with the documented maximum in NWP MHW intensity and frequency (25, 42–46), it provides a compelling mechanism for initiating and sustaining these events beyond local forcing alone.

通过观测分析、模式试验以及耦合模式比较计划第六阶段（CMIP6）历史模拟，我们表明 CGW 型不仅在夏季启动西北太平洋海洋热浪，还通过跨海盆相互作用维持它们。

Through observational analyses, model experiments, and Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 (CMIP6) historical simulations, we demonstrate that the CGW pattern not only initiates NWP MHWs in summer but also sustains them through trans-basin interactions.

CGW 的半球尺度结构在夏季同时驱动北大西洋的海表温度距平（SSTAs）以及西北太平洋的海洋热浪。

The CGW’s hemispheric structure simultaneously drives sea surface temperature anomalies (SSTAs) in the North Atlantic during summer alongside MHWs in the NWP.

那些大西洋海表温度距平持续至冬季，激发沿大圆路径传播、并回传至西北太平洋的波动型，从而使海洋热浪从暖季（夏季和秋季）延伸到冷季（冬季和春季）。

Those Atlantic SSTAs persist into winter, exciting a great-circle wave pattern that propagates back to the NWP, extending MHWs from warm seasons (summer and fall) through cold seasons (winter and spring).

这两种机制共同使得西北太平洋海洋热浪能够跨季节持续；当它们在连续年份中重复出现时，则驱动多年际事件。

Together, these two mechanisms enable NWP MHW persistence across seasons and, when repeated across consecutive years, drive multiyear events.

此外，我们发现 CGW 对西北太平洋海洋热浪的影响在近期气候变化下有所增强。

Moreover, we find that the CGW’s influence on NWP MHWs has intensified under recent climate change.

关键的是，这些中高纬波动动力学在很大程度上独立于 ENSO 而运作，这凸显了有必要扩展传统海洋热浪框架，将半球尺度大气变率纳入对西北太平洋未来极端事件的理解之中。

Crucially, these extratropical wave dynamics operate largely independently of ENSO, underscoring the need to expand conventional MHW frameworks to include hemispheric-scale atmospheric variability in understanding future extremes in the NWP.

The spatial pattern of NWP MHWs is revealed by regressing monthly SSTAs onto the area-averaged SSTAs over the NWP region (the NWP index; see Methods).

该回归凸显出以西北太平洋为中心的显著增暖信号，最强距平位于日本以东（图 1A）。

This regression highlights a distinct warming signal centered in the NWP, with the most intense anomalies situated east of Japan (Fig. 1A).

增暖向东南伸入中北太平洋，形成细长型，表明存在大尺度大气和海洋影响。

The warming extends southeastward into the central North Pacific, forming an elongated pattern indicative of large-scale atmospheric and oceanic influences.

对 1870–2023 年 NWP 指数的分析显示出很强的季节位相锁定：在整个时段的所有 30 年片段中，方差均一致地在北半球晚夏［7–8–9 月（JAS）］达到峰值（图 1B）。

Analysis of the NWP index from 1870 to 2023 shows strong seasonal phase locking, with variance peaking consistently in late boreal summer [July-August-September (JAS)] across all 30-year segments throughout the period (Fig. 1B).

这一夏季峰值与先前发现相一致，即西北太平洋海洋热浪在该季节最为频繁、最为强烈（42, 43, 47）。

This summer peak corresponds with prior findings that NWP MHWs are most frequent and intense during this season (42, 43, 47).

夏季海洋对大气强迫更为敏感：此时混合层最浅，热通量被集中到最小热容量中，从而放大海表温度（SST）响应。

Enhanced ocean susceptibility to atmospheric forcing during summer, when the shallowest mixed layer concentrates heat flux into minimal heat capacity, amplifies the sea surface temperature (SST) response.

再结合将大气强迫识别为西北太平洋海洋热浪主要驱动因子的证据（6, 25–27），这一显著季节性表明，夏季特有的大气环流型在其形成中起主导作用。

Coupled with evidence identifying atmospheric forcing as the primary driver of NWP MHWs (6, 25–27), this pronounced seasonality suggests that summer-specific atmospheric circulation patterns play a dominant role in their formation.

我们通过将夏季［6–7–8 月（JJA）］200 hPa 位势高度（Z200）回归到晚夏（JAS）NWP 指数，并施加 1 个月的 SST 滞后以考虑海洋响应延迟，识别了夏季西北太平洋海洋热浪的主要大气驱动因子。

We identified the primary atmospheric driver of summer NWP MHWs by regressing summer [June-July-August (JJA)] 200-hPa geopotential height (Z200) onto the late-summer (JAS) NWP index, applying a 1-month SST lag to account for oceanic response delays.

该回归揭示出一个显著的 5 波 CGW 型，其特征是一个异常高压中心（中心 C）正好位于西北太平洋海洋热浪区域上空（图 1C）。

This regression reveals a pronounced wavenumber 5 CGW pattern, marked by an anomalous High center (center C) directly over the NWP MHW region (Fig. 1C).

中心 B 与 C 在急流出口区彼此靠近。

Centers B and C are closely spaced in the jet-exit region.

为确认它们代表动力学上可区分的分量、而非单一连续脊，我们进行了局地波动活动（LWA）分析（48）。

To confirm that they represent dynamically distinct components, rather than a single continuous ridge, we performed local wave activity (LWA) analysis (48).

LWA 场在 B 和 C 处显示出由一个相对极小值隔开的清晰局地极大值（图 S1），确认它们作为分开的高振幅波包起作用。

The LWA field shows distinct local maxima at both B and C separated by a relative minimum (fig. S1), confirming that they function as separate high-amplitude wave packets.

在去除 ENSO 影响后的 Z200 距平（记为 Z200*；见方法）上重复该分析，再现了相同的 CGW 特征（图 1D），确认这些大气动力学在很大程度上独立于 ENSO 而运作。

Repeating the analysis on Z200 anomalies with ENSO influences removed (denoted as Z200*; see Methods) reproduces the same CGW signature (Fig. 1D), confirming that these atmospheric dynamics operate largely independently of ENSO.

除结果的最后一部分——我们在那里明确比较 ENSO 与非 ENSO 过程对西北太平洋海洋热浪形成的贡献——外，我们在全文中均采用这一去 ENSO 框架。

We apply this ENSO-removal framework throughout, except in the final part of Results, where we explicitly compare ENSO’s contribution to NWP MHW formation with non-ENSO processes.

对回归得到的 CGW 型的谱分析（图 S2）进一步强调其主导的 5 波结构，与图 1D 中的五个高压中心相一致。

Spectral analysis of the regressed CGW pattern (fig. S2) further underscores its dominant wavenumber 5 structure, consistent with the five high-pressure centers in Fig. 1D.

我们根据 Z200 回归中识别出的五个异常高压中心定义 CGW 指数，以量化 CGW 型的强度（见方法）。

We quantify the CGW pattern’s strength by defining a CGW index from the five anomalous high-pressure centers identified in the Z200 regression (see Methods).

本研究中的所有指数——无论是 Z200、SST 还是 CGW——均基于原始距平。

All indices in this study—whether Z200, SST, or CGW—are based on original anomalies.

将夏季 Z200* 距平回归到 CGW 指数，得到的型与使用 NWP 指数得到的型高度相关［相关系数 (r) = 0.915，P < 0.001，20°N 至 80°N］（参见图 S3 与图 1D）。

Regressing summer Z200* anomalies onto the CGW index yields a pattern highly correlated [correlation coefficient (r) = 0.915, P < 0.001, 20°N to 80°N] with that obtained using the NWP index (cf fig. S3 and Fig. 1D).

这确认 CGW 指数能有效度量夏季驱动西北太平洋海洋热浪的大气因子强度。

This confirms the CGW index as an effective measure of the strength of the summer atmospheric driver of NWP MHWs.

夏季 CGW 指数与晚夏 NWP 指数强烈相关（r = 0.740，P < 0.001），凸显 CGW 在调制西北太平洋海洋热浪中的首要作用。

The summer CGW and late-summer NWP indices correlate strongly (r = 0.740, P < 0.001), underscoring the CGW’s primary role in modulating NWP MHWs.

对流层上层 CGW 型呈现相当正压结构，向下投影至地面，在中心 C 处表现为海平面气压（SLP）正异常、反气旋性风距平和正的海表热通量（SHF）距平（图 S4A 和 S5）。

The upper-tropospheric CGW pattern exhibits equivalent barotropic structure, projecting downward to the surface with sea level pressure (SLP) highs, anticyclonic wind anomalies, and positive surface heat flux (SHF) anomalies at center C (figs. S4A and S5).

这种垂直耦合通过减少云量、反气旋下沉以及抑制蒸发冷却来驱动海洋增暖——这些机制与先前西北太平洋海洋热浪研究相一致（6）。

This vertical coupling drives ocean warming through reduced cloud cover, anticyclonic subsidence, and suppressed evaporative cooling—mechanisms consistent with previous NWP MHW studies (6).

夏季海洋混合层热收支分析（见方法）量化了这一关系：CGW 驱动的 SHF 距平占混合层温度倾向总量的 77.4%（图 S5）。

Summer ocean mixed-layer heat budget analysis (see Methods) quantifies this relationship: CGW-driven SHF anomalies account for 77.4% of the total mixed-layer temperature tendency (fig. S5).

为阐明维持夏季 CGW 型 5 波结构及其准定常性质的机制，我们分析了北半球夏季大气的波导属性（见方法）。

To elucidate the mechanisms sustaining the CGW pattern’s wavenumber 5 structure and quasistationary nature in summer, we analyzed the waveguide properties of the boreal summer atmosphere (see Methods).

分析表明，定常波数（Ks）超过 5 的区域与北半球急流的夏季气候态位置密切对齐，形成一个连接 CGW 全部五个异常高压中心（A 至 E；图 2A）的连续波导。

Our analysis shows that regions where the stationary wavenumber (Ks) exceeds 5 align closely with the summer climatological position of the Northern Hemisphere jet stream, forming a continuous waveguide linking all five anomalous High centers of the CGW (A to E; Fig. 2A).

罗斯贝波相速度接近于零（图 2B），从而在急流减速或加速处锚定扰动。

Rossby wave phase speed approaches zero (Fig. 2B), anchoring disturbances where the jet stream decelerates or accelerates.

此外，位涡梯度的局地减小降低了 Ks，从而形成有利于 5 波扰动准共振放大的条件（39, 49, 50）。

Moreover, localized reductions in the potential vorticity gradient lower Ks, creating conditions conducive to quasiresonant amplification of wavenumber 5 disturbances (39, 49, 50).

这种准共振状态增强了 CGW 的相干性与持续性（51），从而在整个北半球夏季维持半球波动型，并有效驱动西北太平洋海洋热浪。

This quasiresonant state enhances the CGW’s coherence and persistence (51), thereby maintaining the hemispheric wave pattern through the boreal summer and effectively driving NWP MHWs.

我们通过对 Z200* 距平相对夏季 CGW 指数做滞后回归，评估了五个 CGW 高压中心的时间演变与持续性。

We evaluated the temporal evolution and persistence of the five CGW high-pressure centers by performing lagged regressions of Z200* anomalies onto the summer CGW index.

所得 Hovmöller 图（30°N 至 60°N；图 2C）表明 CGW 型为准定常：五个中心在整个夏季几乎固定在各自经度上，传播很小。

The resulting Hovmöller diagram (30°N to 60°N; Fig. 2C) demonstrates the CGW pattern’s quasistationary behavior, with all five centers remaining nearly fixed at their respective longitudes throughout summer, showing minimal propagation.

CGW 指数以及中心 A 至 E 处 Z200* 距平的滞后自相关（图 2D）确认了它们的时间稳定性，自相关值在整个季节保持一致偏高。

Lagged autocorrelation of the CGW index and Z200* anomalies at centers A to E (Fig. 2D) confirms their temporal stability, with autocorrelation values remaining uniformly high over the season.

This positive feedback loop enhances both the persistence and magnitude of NWP MHWs throughout the extended warm season.

北大西洋北部 AGCM 试验（图 3H）确认了这一机制：冬季北大西洋北部增暖产生 CGW-C 高压及相联系的波列。

The NNA AGCM experiment (Fig. 3H) confirms this mechanism: Winter NNA warming generates the CGW-C High and associated wave train.

观测分析进一步支持这一点：将冬季 Z200* 距平回归到 NNA 指数（图 3G）揭示出跨海盆波列，在中心 C 上空有尤为显著的高压距平。该型表明，一条大圆波列型自北大西洋北部伸过欧亚大陆北部，随后向南弯曲进入北太平洋，建立起三个清晰的高压中心：一个位于北大西洋北部上空，另一个位于欧亚大陆北部上空，第三个位于西北太平洋区域上空。

Observational analysis further supports this: Regressing winter Z200* anomalies onto the NNA index (Fig. 3G) reveals a trans-basin wave train with a particularly prominent high-pressure anomaly over center C. The pattern indicates that a great-circle wave train pattern extends from the NNA across northern Eurasia before curving southward into the North Pacific, establishing three distinct High centers: one over the NNA, another over northern Eurasia, and a third over the NWP region.

这三个中心与冬季西北太平洋增暖相联系的主导 Z200* 距平型密切吻合（图 1G），确认北大西洋北部驱动的波列为延长冷季（从冬季至次年春季）的西北太平洋海洋热浪创造了有利的大气条件。

These three centers closely match the dominant Z200* anomaly pattern associated with winter NWP warming (Fig. 1G), confirming that the NNA-driven wave train creates atmospheric conditions conducive to NWP MHWs through the extended cold season (from winter to the following spring).

Strengthening CGW-MHW linkages under climate change over recent decades

我们通过考察 43 个 CMIP6 模式 1850–2014 年的模拟（表 S1）以及二十世纪再分析（20thC-Reav3），评估 CGW 强迫、西北太平洋海洋热浪及其跨海盆相互作用在近几十年是否有所加强。

We evaluated whether the CGW forcing, NWP MHWs, and their trans-basin interactions have strengthened over recent decades by examining 1850–2014 simulations from 43 CMIP6 models (table S1) alongside the Twentieth Century Reanalysis (20thC-Reav3).

CMIP6 多模式平均（MMM）与 20thC-Reanv3 在夏季 Z200* 对其 NWP 指数的回归中均呈现出 5 波 CGW 型（图 S14），与作为观测参照的美国国家环境预报中心（NCEP）/美国国家大气研究中心（NCAR）再分析 1（NCEP-R1）中识别出的型密切吻合（参见图 1D）。

Both the CMIP6 multimodel mean (MMM) and the 20thC-Reanv3 exhibit a wavenumber 5 CGW pattern in summer Z200* regressions onto their NWP indices (fig. S14), closely matching the pattern identified in the National Centers for Environmental Prediction (NCEP)/National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR) Reanalysis 1 (used as the observational reference; NCEP-R1) (cf Fig. 1D).

这种一致性确认 CMIP6 模式有效捕捉了观测到的、由 CGW 驱动的西北太平洋海洋热浪动力学。

This consistency confirms that CMIP6 models effectively capture the observed CGW-driven dynamics of NWP MHWs.

对 1850 至 2014 年观测与模拟的夏季 CGW 指数的分析揭示出，所有数据集均存在显著的增加趋势（图 4A）。

The analysis of the observed and modeled summer CGW index from 1850 to 2014 reveals a pronounced increasing trend across all datasets (Fig. 4A).

CMIP6-MMM、20thC-Reav3 和 NCEP-R1 均显示出 CGW 活动的显著增强，尤其是自 20 世纪后期以来。

The CMIP6-MMM, 20thC-Reav3, and NCEP-R1 all demonstrate a significant intensification of CGW activity, particularly from the late 20th century onward.

NWP 指数中也观测到类似但较弱的增加趋势（图 4B）。

A similar but weaker increasing trend is also observed in the NWP index (Fig. 4B).

我们进一步对这些序列去趋势，并计算去趋势后 NWP 指数与 CGW 指数之间的 30 年滑动相关（图 4C），发现随时间有统计显著的关系加强。

We further detrended these series and performed a running 30-year correlation between the detrended NWP and CGW indices (Fig. 4C), finding a statistically significant strengthening relationship over time.

自 1970 年以来，该相关在 CMIP6-MMM 中以每世纪 0.34 的速率增加（P < 0.001），在 NCEP-R1 中以每世纪 0.12 的速率增加（P = 0.090）。

Since 1970, the correlation has increased at a rate of 0.34 per century in CMIP6-MMM (P < 0.001) and 0.12 per century in NCEP-R1 (P = 0.090).

这些增加记录了西北太平洋区域逐渐增强的海气联系。

These increases document a progressively stronger atmosphere-ocean connection in the NWP region.

这种增强的耦合表明，本研究识别的自我强化反馈机制已变得更为高效，可能导致该区域夏季海洋热浪更为持续、更为强烈。

This enhanced coupling suggests that the self-reinforcing feedback mechanism identified in our study has become more efficient, potentially leading to more persistent and intense summer MHWs in this region.

夏季 NNA 与 CGW 的关系表现出甚至更为显著的加强（图 4D）。

The summer NNA and CGW relationship exhibits an even more pronounced strengthening (Fig. 4D).

自 1970 年以来，30 年滑动相关在 CMIP6-MMM 中以每世纪 0.59 上升（P < 0.001），在 20thC-Reav3 中以每世纪 1.04 上升（P < 0.001），而 NCEP-R1 显示每世纪 0.45 的增加（P < 0.001）。

Since 1970, the 30-year running correlations rise at 0.59 per century (P < 0.001) in CMIP6-MMM and 1.04 per century (P < 0.001) in 20thC-Reav3, while NCEP-R1 shows a 0.45 per century increase (P < 0.001).

夏季 CGW 对北大西洋北部海表温度距平影响的这一显著增强表明，近几十年来北大西洋北部区域对 CGW 强迫更为敏感，从而促进在延长冷季中维持西北太平洋海洋热浪的跨海盆反馈。

This marked enhancement of the CGW’s influence on NNA SSTAs during summer indicates that the NNA region has become more responsive to CGW forcing in recent decades, facilitating the trans-basin feedback that sustains NWP MHWs during the extended cold season.

自 1970 年以来，所有数据集中 NWP 指数与 NNA 指数的冬季相关也有所加强（图 4E），尽管数值仍低于 95% 显著性阈值（临界 r = 0.367）。

Winter correlations between the NWP and NNA indices have also strengthened since 1970 across all datasets (Fig. 4E), although values remain below the 95% significance threshold (critical r = 0.367).

这种增强的大西洋反馈可能与大西洋多年代际振荡（AMO）有关，后者自 1960 年代后期以来由冷位相峰值转为暖位相，可能增强了北大西洋北部触发跨海盆波动型的能力（53）。

This enhanced Atlantic feedback may be associated with the Atlantic multidecadal oscillation (AMO), which shifted from a cold phase peak to a warm phase since the late 1960s, potentially enhancing the NNA’s capacity to trigger a trans-basin wave pattern (53).

这表明，将北大西洋北部条件与冬季西北太平洋增暖联系起来的跨海盆遥相关，在近几十年很可能已变得更为显著。

This suggests that the trans-basin teleconnection linking NNA conditions to winter NWP warming has likely become more pronounced in recent decades.

为检验对不同去趋势方法的稳健性，除我们主要的线性方法外，我们还应用了二次去趋势和 EEMD 去趋势（图 S15 和表 S2）。

To test robustness to different detrending methods, we applied quadratic and EEMD detrending in addition to our primary linear approach (fig. S15 and table S2).

CGW-NNA 关系在所有方法和数据集中表现出最稳健的加强，正趋势为每世纪 0.30 至 1.04（全部 P < 0.001）。

The CGW-NNA relationship shows the most robust strengthening across all methods and datasets, with positive trends of 0.30 to 1.04 per century (all P < 0.001).

冬季 NWP-NNA 关系在大多数情形下也显著加强（九种方法–数据集组合中的七种），趋势为每世纪 0.12 至 0.79。

The winter NWP-NNA relationship also significantly strengthens in most cases (in seven of the nine method-dataset combinations), with trends of 0.12 to 0.79 per century.

CGW-NWP 关系表现出更大的敏感性：CMIP6-MMM 显示一致的正趋势（每世纪 0.12 至 0.34），而观测在非线性方法下显示不显著的趋势。

The CGW-NWP relationship exhibits greater sensitivity: CMIP6-MMM shows consistent positive trends (0.12 to 0.34 per century), while observations show nonsignificant trends with nonlinear methods.

这些结果表明，跨海盆相互作用（CGW-NNA 和 NWP-NNA）稳健加强，而 CGW-NWP 的加强在 CMIP6 模式中以及采用线性去趋势时最为明显，表明受到包括总体增暖趋势和多年代际变率在内的更广泛气候变化信号的影响。

These results demonstrate that trans-basin interactions (CGW-NNA and NWP-NNA) strengthen robustly, while CGW-NWP strengthening is most evident in CMIP6 models and with linear detrending, suggesting influence from broader climate change signals including the overall warming trend and multidecadal variability.

Evaluating ENSO’s role in CGW and NWP MHW mechanisms

基于表明 ENSO 可能调制或影响 CGW 型的研究（54）——主要是通过其对印度夏季风的影响（36, 55, 56）——我们通过将夏季海表温度距平回归到 NWP 指数，评估热带太平洋在塑造西北太平洋海洋热浪中的潜在作用（图 5A）。

Building on studies suggesting that ENSO may modulate or influence the CGW pattern (54), primarily through its impact on the Indian summer monsoon (36, 55, 56), we assessed the tropical Pacific’s potential role in shaping NWP MHWs by regressing summer SSTAs onto the NWP index (Fig. 5A).

该分析显示出标志性的西北太平洋增暖，并伴有热带太平洋的拉尼娜型冷却。

This analysis shows the hallmark NWP warming, accompanied by La Niña–like cooling in the tropical Pacific.

相反，回归到冷舌指数（CTI；见方法）的夏季海表温度距平也表现出西北太平洋增暖，但具有向副热带中太平洋的清晰东南向伸展，而非西北太平洋海洋热浪的完整纬向足迹（图 5B）。

Conversely, summer SSTAs regressed onto the cold tongue index (CTI; see Methods) also exhibit NWP warming but with a distinct southeastward extension into the subtropical central Pacific, rather than the full zonal footprint of NWP MHWs (Fig. 5B).

这一差异表明，与拉尼娜相联系或由其触发的西北太平洋 SST 增暖型并不能完全再现观测到的西北太平洋海洋热浪型。

This discrepancy suggests that the NWP SST warming pattern associated with or triggered by La Niña does not fully reproduce the observed NWP MHW pattern.

此外，将 Z200 距平回归到 CTI 显示出与 CGW 中心的部分对齐（图 S16），但所得型并不由 5 波结构主导（图 S17），表明 ENSO 并非 CGW 型或西北太平洋海洋热浪的主导驱动因子。

Furthermore, regressing Z200 anomalies onto the CTI shows partial alignment with CGW centers (fig. S16), yet the resulting pattern is not dominated by a wavenumber 5 structure (fig. S17), suggesting that ENSO is not the dominant driver of either the CGW pattern or NWP MHWs.

因此，ENSO 强迫对夏季 CGW 和西北太平洋海洋热浪的精确贡献值得进一步研究。

Thus, the precise contribution of ENSO forcing to the summer CGW and NWP MHWs merits further investigation.

在观测记录（1948–2024）中，晚夏 NWP 指数与夏季 CGW 指数表现出强正相关（r = 0.740，P < 0.001；图 5C，左下），凸显 CGW 在驱动西北太平洋海洋热浪中的核心作用。

In the observational record (1948–2024), late-summer NWP and summer CGW indices exhibit a strong positive correlation (r = 0.740, P < 0.001; Fig. 5C, bottom left), highlighting the CGW’s central role in driving NWP MHWs.

这一关系在厄尔尼诺（CTI > +0.5 SD；暖色）和拉尼娜（CTI < −0.5 SD；冷色）条件下均持续存在，表明 CGW 强迫独立于 ENSO 位相而运作。

This relationship persists through both El Niño (CTI > +0.5 SD; warm-colored) and La Niña (CTI < −0.5 SD; cool-colored) conditions, indicating that CGW forcing operates independently of ENSO phase.

为分离 ENSO 与 CGW 的贡献，我们在偏斜高斯分布假定下，使用 1000 次抽样的 bootstrap 检验，比较了拉尼娜（CTI < 0）和 CGW 为正（CGW > 0）条件下晚夏 NWP 指数的 PDF（图 5C，上部）。

To disentangle ENSO and CGW contributions, we compared the PDFs of the late-summer NWP index under La Niña (CTI < 0) and positive CGW (CGW > 0) conditions using 1000-sample bootstrap tests with a skewed Gaussian distribution assumption (Fig. 5C, top).

Kolmogorov-Smirnov（KS）检验表明两条 PDF 之间存在统计显著差异（P < 0.001），从而强化了 CGW 与 ENSO 代表西北太平洋海洋热浪的不同强迫机制。

A Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) test indicates a statistically significant difference between the two PDFs (P < 0.001), reinforcing that CGW and ENSO represent separate forcing mechanisms for NWP MHWs.

此外，CGW 为正条件下 NWP 指数的众数（M = 0.364 SD）几乎是拉尼娜条件下（M = 0.040 SD）的九倍，表明 CGW 强迫比单独的拉尼娜激发更强的西北太平洋增暖。

Furthermore, the modal NWP index under positive CGW (M = 0.364 SD) is nearly nine times greater than under La Niña (M = 0.040 SD), demonstrating that CGW forcing elicits stronger NWP warming than La Niña alone.

类似的 PDF 分析比较了负 CTI 与正 NWP 条件下的夏季 CGW 指数值（图 5C，右下），进一步表明局地西北太平洋 SST 增暖比 ENSO 强迫更有效地放大夏季 CGW 型。

A similar PDF analysis comparing summer CGW index values under negative CTI and positive NWP conditions (Fig. 5C, bottom right) further demonstrates that local NWP SST warming more effectively amplifies the summer CGW pattern than ENSO forcing.

KS 检验确认这些分布之间存在显著差异（P < 0.001）：正 NWP 条件下 CGW 指数 PDF 的众数值（M = 0.444 SD）约为负 CTI 条件下（M = 0.040 SD）的 11 倍，凸显局地西北太平洋增暖比拉尼娜强迫更有效地激发 CGW 型。

A KS test confirms a significant difference between these distributions (P < 0.001), with the CGW index’s PDF under positive NWP conditions showing a modal value (M = 0.444 SD) that is ~11 times greater than under negative CTI conditions (M = 0.040 SD), highlighting that local NWP warming is more efficient at exciting the CGW pattern than La Niña forcing.

我们通过计算 CTI、NWP 和 CGW 指数之间的标准相关与偏相关，评估 ENSO 对夏季 CGW-NWP 关系的影响（图 5D）。

We evaluated the influence of ENSO on the summer CGW-NWP relationship by computing both standard and partial correlations among the CTI, NWP, and CGW indices (Fig. 5D).

标准 CGW-NWP 相关为 0.740，解释约 55% 的 NWP 方差；去除 ENSO 影响后，偏 CGW-NWP 相关仍保持偏高（r = 0.708），表明这一联系在很大程度上独立于 ENSO。

The standard CGW-NWP correlation is 0.740, explaining ~55% of NWP variance; after removing ENSO effects, the partial CGW-NWP correlation remains high (r = 0.708), demonstrating that this linkage is largely independent of ENSO.

相比之下，CTI-NWP 标准相关为 −0.344（约 10% 的方差），一旦计入 CGW 影响，便降至 −0.168（约 3% 的方差，P = 0.181），从而变得不显著。

In contrast, the CTI-NWP standard correlation is −0.344 (~10% of variance), and it falls to −0.168 (~3% of variance, P = 0.181) once CGW influences are accounted for, rendering it nonsignificant.

类似地，当 NWP 指数保持不变时，CTI-CGW 相关从 −0.321 降至 −0.104（P = 0.406）。

Similarly, the CTI-CGW correlation drops from −0.321 to −0.104 (P = 0.406) when the NWP index is held constant.

这些结果表明，ENSO 对西北太平洋海洋热浪和 CGW 变率即使有贡献也仅是边际的，而 CGW 本身作为西北太平洋海洋热浪的主要驱动因子脱颖而出，在很大程度上独立于 ENSO 强迫。

These results indicate that ENSO contributes only marginally, if at all, to NWP MHWs and CGW variability, whereas the CGW itself stands out as the primary driver of NWP MHWs, largely independent of ENSO forcing.

DISCUSSION

本研究建立了一个理解西北太平洋海洋热浪形成与持续性的框架，强调中纬度大气波动动力学与跨海盆相互作用的关键作用。

This study establishes a framework for understanding the formation and persistence of NWP MHWs, emphasizing the pivotal role of extratropical atmospheric wave dynamics and trans-basin interactions.

在夏季，一个准定常 5 波 CGW 型驱动西北太平洋增暖，并被一个自我强化的反馈环路所放大：CGW 触发西北太平洋增暖，随后该增暖又增强 CGW（图 6A）。

During summer, a quasistationary wavenumber 5 CGW pattern drives NWP warming, which is amplified by a self-reinforcing feedback loop: The CGW triggers NWP warming, which subsequently strengthens the CGW (Fig. 6A).

这一局地海–气耦合在整个延长的暖半年中维持海洋热浪。

This local ocean-atmosphere coupling sustains MHWs throughout the extended warm half-year.

此外，跨海盆反馈将北大西洋与西北太平洋联系起来：夏季由 CGW 诱发的北大西洋北部（NNA）增暖因海洋热惯性而持续至冬季。

Additionally, trans-basin feedback connects the North Atlantic to the NWP: summer CGW-induced warming in the NNA persists into winter due to oceanic thermal inertia.

在夏季 CGW 消散之后，这一持续至冬季的 NNA 增暖在欧亚上空激发大尺度波列（图 6B），从而在整个延长的冷半年中维持西北太平洋海洋热浪。

Following the dissipation of the summer CGW, this lingering winter NNA warming generates a large-scale wave train across Eurasia (Fig. 6B), sustaining NWP MHWs through the extended cold half-year.

这两个季节性机制共同解释了西北太平洋海洋热浪如何在整个年循环中持续，并在连续年份中反复出现时驱动多年际事件。

Collectively, these two seasonal mechanisms explain how NWP MHWs persist across a full annual cycle and, when repeated across consecutive years, drive multiyear events.

长达一个世纪的再分析资料集（20thC-Reav3）、CMIP6 历史模拟以及近期观测资料（NCEP-R1）共同揭示，自 1970 年以来 CGW–西北太平洋相互作用与跨海盆反馈在增强，这很可能由全球变暖、大西洋多十年际振荡（AMO）的位相转变，或二者共同驱动。

Century-long reanalysis datasets (20thC-Reav3), CMIP6 historical simulations, and recent observational data (NCEP-R1) collectively reveal a strengthening of the CGW-NWP interaction and trans-basin feedback since 1970, likely driven by global warming, the AMO’s phase shift, or both.

这些增强表明，在持续的全球变暖背景下，西北太平洋海洋热浪有可能变得更为严重、更为持久、更为频繁。

These enhancements suggest a potential for increasingly severe, prolonged, and frequent NWP MHWs under continued global warming.

尽管我们的 AGCM 试验表明西北太平洋海表温度距平可以激发 CGW，我们承认其他大尺度因子也可能对驱动 CGW 型有贡献。

While our AGCM experiments demonstrate that NWP SST anomalies can excite the CGW, we acknowledge that other large-scale factors may also contribute to driving the CGW pattern.

由北极放大（57, 58）、气溶胶强迫（59, 60）、急流移动（61）以及其他过程引起的大气环流变化，可能影响 CGW 活动及其与西北太平洋海洋热浪的关系。

Changes in atmospheric circulation due to Arctic amplification (57, 58), aerosol forcing (59, 60), jet stream shifts (61), and other processes could influence CGW activity and its relationship with NWP MHWs.

全面考察这些因子在 CGW–西北太平洋海洋热浪关系中的作用，需要超出本研究范围的大量额外分析。

A comprehensive investigation of the roles of these factors in the CGW-NWP MHW relationship would require extensive additional analyses beyond the scope of the present study.

我们在此建立的物理框架——表明西北太平洋的海–气耦合可通过自我强化反馈激发并维持 CGW 型——为未来研究这些额外因子如何调制或增强这一自我强化反馈机制提供了基础。

The physical framework that we establish here, demonstrating that ocean-atmosphere coupling in the NWP can excite and sustain the CGW pattern through self-reinforcing feedback, provides a foundation for future investigations into how these additional factors may modulate or enhance this self-reinforcing feedback mechanism.

尽管我们的框架解释了使西北太平洋海洋热浪能够跨季节、并可能多年持续的机制，但当更强的大气或海洋内部动力学打断反馈环路时，海洋热浪即告终止。

While our framework explains the mechanisms enabling multiseasonal and potential multiyear persistence of NWP MHWs, MHW termination occurs when the feedback loops are interrupted by stronger internal atmospheric or oceanic dynamics.

在某些年份或季节，大气内部变率可能压过海–气耦合机制。

During certain years or seasons, internal atmospheric variability may overwhelm the air-sea coupling mechanisms.

例如，当大气内部动力学有利于西北太平洋区域出现异常低压条件、而非通常由暖海表温度距平诱发的高压异常时，夏季自我强化反馈便无法建立。

For example, when the atmosphere’s internal dynamics favor anomalous low-pressure conditions over the NWP region rather than the high-pressure anomaly that would normally be induced by warm SSTAs, the summer self-reinforcing feedback cannot be established.

类似地，在冬季，若大气内部变率产生的环流型阻止了对 NNA 增暖所预期的高压响应形成，则跨海盆遥相关便无法维持西北太平洋海洋热浪。

Similarly, during winter, if atmospheric internal variability produces circulation patterns that prevent the formation of the expected high-pressure response to NNA warming, then the trans-basin teleconnection fails to sustain the NWP MHW.

在此类情形下，尽管西北太平洋与 NNA 区域均存在暖海表温度距平，正反馈机制仍被打断，海洋热浪事件随之终止。

In such cases, despite the presence of warm SST anomalies in both the NWP and NNA regions, the positive feedback mechanisms are interrupted and the MHW event terminates.

尽管本研究着重刻画 CGW–西北太平洋反馈与跨海盆反馈而非其根本成因，若干因子可能对这些演变中的动力学有贡献：混合层深度减小从而增强海表温度–大气耦合（19, 20, 62, 63），急流位置的纬度移动从而重塑 Rossby 波波导（57, 61, 64），北极放大从而减弱经向温度梯度并调制大气波动动力学（51, 57, 58, 65），以及西北太平洋区域附近北太平洋阻塞活动的变化（50），等等。

Although our study emphasizes delineating the CGW-NWP and trans-basin feedbacks rather than their root causes, several factors may contribute to these evolving dynamics: decreasing mixed-layer depths that enhance SST-atmosphere coupling (19, 20, 62, 63), latitudinal shifts in jet stream location that reshape Rossby wave waveguides (57, 61, 64), Arctic amplification that weakens meridional temperature gradients and modifies atmospheric wave dynamics (51, 57, 58, 65), and changes in North Pacific blocking activities near the NWP region (50), among others.

需要进一步研究中纬度大气波动动力学的演变特征、相关的大气–海洋与跨海盆相互作用，以及它们在驱动影响海洋生态系统、渔业和沿海社区的海洋极端事件中的作用。

Further research is needed to explore the evolving nature of extratropical atmospheric wave dynamics, associated atmosphere-ocean and trans-basin interactions, and their roles in driving ocean extreme events that affect marine ecosystems, fisheries, and coastal communities.

为量化波动振幅并识别不同的波包，我们按照（48）计算局地波动活动（LWA）。

To quantify wave amplitude and identify distinct wave packets, we calculated LWA following (48).

LWA measures the meridional displacement of the jet stream from its zonal-mean position, providing an objective metric for identifying high-pressure/blocking regions.

其中 A 为局地波动活动，λ 为经度，φ 为纬度，a 为地球半径，Q 为给定气压层（此处为 200 hPa）的准地转位涡（QGPV），Q¯ 为 QGPV 的纬向平均，φs 为南侧参考纬度。

where A is the LWA, λ is longitude, φ is latitude, a is Earth’s radius, Q is quasigeostrophic potential vorticity (QGPV) at a given pressure level (200 hPa here), Q¯ is the zonal mean of QGPV, and φs is the southern reference latitude.

准地转位涡计算为 Q = f + (1/(a cos φ)) ∂v/∂λ − (1/(a cos φ)) ∂(u cos φ)/∂φ

The QGPV is calculated as Q = f + (1/(a cos φ)) ∂v/∂λ − (1/(a cos φ)) ∂(u cos φ)/∂φ

其中 f 为科里奥利参数，u 和 v 分别为纬向风和经向风。

where f is the Coriolis parameter, and u and v are zonal and meridional winds, respectively.

混合层温度倾向方程（72）可表示为 ∂Tm/∂t = [Qnet − Qsw(h)]/(ρ cp h) − (um ∂Tm/∂x + vm ∂Tm/∂y) − we (Tm − Th)/h + DIFF

其中 Tm 为混合层内垂直平均的位温，t 为时间，Qnet 为净海表热通量，Qsw(h) 为穿透混合层底的短波辐射，ρ = 1026 kg m−3 为海水密度，cp = 4200 J kg−1K−1 为海水比热容，h 为混合层深度，(um,vm) 为混合层内垂直平均的水平流速，we 为混合层底的夹卷速度，Th 为混合层底部的位温。

where Tm is the potential temperature vertically averaged over the mixed layer, t is time, Qnet is the net SHF, Qsw(h) is the shortwave radiation that penetrates through the base of the mixed layer, ρ=1026 kg m−3 is seawater density, cp=4200 J kg−1K−1 is the specific heat capacity of seawater, h is the mixed-layer depth, (um,vm) is the horizontal current velocity vertically averaged over the mixed layer, we is entrainment velocity at the base of the mixed layer, and Th is potential temperature at the bottom of the mixed layer.

DIFF 项表示扩散。

The term DIFF represents diffusion.

在我们的分析中，垂直平流与 DIFF 一并作为残差处理。

In our analysis, vertical advection and DIFF are treated together as residuals.

深度 h 处的穿透短波辐射按指数衰减函数参数化（73） Qsw(h) ≈ Qsw(0) [α e^{−h/k1} + (1 − α) e^{−h/k2}]

The penetrative shortwave radiation at depth h is parameterized following an exponential decay function (73) Qsw(h) ≈ Qsw(0) [α e^{−h/k1} + (1 − α) e^{−h/k2}]

其中 Qsw(0) 为海表短波辐射，α = 0.58 为较长穿透谱段的能量份额，k1=0.35 m 和 k2=23 m 分别为长波段与短波段的衰减系数。

where Qsw(0) is the surface shortwave radiation, α = 0.58 is the fraction of energy in the longer-penetrating spectral band, k1=0.35 m and k2=23 m are the attenuation coefficients for the long and shortwave bands, respectively.

波导分析：定常波数与 Rossby 波相速度

Waveguide analysis: Stationary wavenumber and Rossby wave phase speed

定常波数（Ks）与 Rossby 波相速度（cp）是描述行星波传播与行为的大气动力学基本参数。

The stationary wavenumber (Ks) and the Rossby wave phase speed (cp) are fundamental parameters in atmospheric dynamics that describe the propagation and behavior of planetary waves.

定常波数 Ks 定义为（74） Ks² = β∗ / U

The stationary wavenumber Ks is defined as (74) Ks² = β∗ / U

其中 β∗ 为有效经向绝对涡度梯度，由下式给出 β∗ = β − ∂²U/∂y²

where β∗ is the effective meridional absolute vorticity gradient, given by β∗ = β − ∂²U/∂y²

其中 β = df/dy = 2Ω cos ϕ / a 为行星涡度梯度，Ω 为地球自转角速度，ϕ 为纬度，a 为地球半径，U 为纬向风速。

where β = df/dy = 2Ω cos ϕ / a is the planetary vorticity gradient, Ω is Earth’s rotation rate, ϕ is latitude, a is Earth’s radius, and U is the zonal wind speed.

方程 3 同时考虑了行星涡度效应和背景风曲率的影响，从而在确定定常 Rossby 波的空间尺度时更为精确。

Equation 3 accounts for both planetary vorticity effects and the influence of the background wind curvature, making it more precise in determining the spatial scale of stationary Rossby waves.

Ks 表示 Rossby 波传播的阈值：纬向波数 k < Ks 的波动可自由传播，而 k > Ks 的波动衰减；当 k ≈ Ks 时，波动沿波导传播，放大或衰减最小（35, 75）。

The Ks represents the threshold for Rossby wave propagation: waves with zonal wavenumber k < Ks propagate freely, whereas waves with k > Ks decay; when k ≈ Ks, waves propagate along the waveguide with minimal amplification or attenuation (35, 75).

Rossby 波相速度（cp）由下式给出 cp = U − β/(k² + l²)

The Rossby wave phase speed (cp) is given by cp = U − β/(k² + l²)

其中 k 和 l 分别为纬向和经向波数。

where k and l are the zonal and meridional wavenumbers, respectively.

在我们的分析中，通过设定 l = 0 聚焦于纯纬向传播。

In our analysis, we focus on purely zonal propagation by setting l = 0.

where X represents either SST, Z200, or V200, and γ is the regression coefficient map of variable X regressed onto the CTI index.

该方法有效去除与 ENSO 相关变率的线性分量，同时保留独立信号，包括与 CGW 型相关的信号。

This approach effectively removes the linear component of ENSO-related variability while preserving independent signals, including those associated with the CGW pattern.

其中 α 表示 NWP 指数的滞后 1 个月自相关，ρ(0) 表示 NWP–CGW 相关的峰值，k 为峰值之后的月数。

随机能量平衡模式

To validate the relationship between MLD and SST persistence, we applied a stochastic energy balance model framework.

海洋混合层温度演变可近似为一阶自回归过程，由随机大气强迫与热阻尼驱动 dT/dt = −λT + F(t)

其中 T 为海表温度距平，λ 为阻尼系数，F(t) 表示随机大气强迫。

where T is the SST anomaly, λ is the damping coefficient, and F(t) represents stochastic atmospheric forcing.

阻尼时间尺度为 τ=C/λ，其中 C=ρcp H 为混合层热容量，ρ 为海水密度，cp 为比热容，H 为混合层深度。

对于月时间步长（Δt = 1 month），海表温度距平的滞后 1 自相关 α 通过下式与阻尼时间尺度相联系 α = e^{−Δt · λ /(ρ cp H)}

For monthly time steps (Δt = 1 month), the lag-1 autocorrelation α of SST anomalies is related to the damping timescale through α = e^{−Δt · λ /(ρ cp H)}

对两边取自然对数 ln(α) = −Δt · λ /(ρ cp H)

Taking the natural logarithm of both sides ln(α) = −Δt · λ /(ρ cp H)

这表明 ln(α) 与 H 成反比。因此，对于混合层深度不同的两个区域 ln(α1)/ln(α2) = H2/H1

This shows that ln(α) is inversely proportional to H. Therefore, for two regions with different MLD ln(α1)/ln(α2) = H2/H1

我们使用气候态混合层深度值计算理论自相关比值，并与观测的滞后 1 自相关进行比较，以验证混合层深度差异可以解释海表温度持续性的差别。

we calculated the theoretical autocorrelation ratio using climatological MLD values and compared it with the observed lag-1 autocorrelations to validate that MLD differences explain the differential SST persistence.

我们使用 CAM6 的 F1850 组件集（76, 77）开展强迫 AGCM 试验，水平分辨率配置为约 2.5° 经度 × 1.9° 纬度，垂直 30 层，自地表延伸至 3.65 hPa。

We conducted forced AGCM experiments using the F1850 compset of CAM6 (76, 77), configured with a horizontal resolution of ~2.5° longitude × 1.9° latitude and 30 vertical layers extending from the surface to 3.65 hPa.

共进行了三组试验：一组控制（CTRL）试验，以及两组敏感性试验（NWP 和 NNA），每组包含 10 个集合成员。

A total of three experiments were performed: a control (CTRL) run, and two sensitivity runs (NWP and NNA), each consisting of 10 ensemble members.

在 CTRL 试验中，规定月变化的气候态海表温度与海冰资料（78）驱动 CAM6。

In the CTRL run, monthly-varying climatological SST and sea ice data (78) were prescribed to drive CAM6.

NWP 与 NNA 试验以理想化高斯型海表温度型作为强迫（图 S18）。

The NWP and NNA runs were forced with idealized Gaussian-shaped SST patterns (fig. S18).

NWP AGCM 试验强迫中心具有 2°C 距平，东西（W-E）方向 e 折半径为 15°，南北（S-N）方向为 10°。

The NWP AGCM Run forcing center had a 2°C anomaly with an e-folding radius of 15° in the west-east (W-E) direction and 10° in the south-north (S-N) direction.

NNA AGCM 试验强迫中心具有 1°C 距平，W-E 方向 e 折半径为 15°，S-N 方向为 15°。

The NNA AGCM Run forcing center had a 1°C anomaly with an e-folding radius of 15° in the W-E direction and 15° in the S-N direction.

每组试验积分 2 年。

Each experiment was integrated for 2 years.

通过分析敏感性试验与 CTRL 试验之差，确定大气对规定海表温度距平强迫的响应。

The differences between the sensitivity runs and the CTRL run were analyzed to determine the atmospheric response to the prescribed SSTA forcing.
