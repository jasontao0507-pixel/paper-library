摘要

Abstract

我们的星球正在迅速变暖，同时海洋热浪(MHW)的频率和强度也随之增加。

Our planet is warming rapidly, accompanied by an increase in the frequency and intensity of marine heatwaves (MHWs).

MHW除了对海洋生态系统的影响外，还可以调节区域气候系统，包括亚洲季风。

Beyond their impacts on marine ecosystems, MHWs can also modulate regional climate systems, including the Asian monsoon.

本文利用 1982—2024 年去趋势海表温度异常，研究北印度洋海洋热浪的变率、驱动因素及其季风影响。

Here, we investigate the variability, drivers, and monsoon impacts of MHWs in the North Indian Ocean using detrended sea surface temperature anomalies over the period 1982–2024.

对海洋热浪强度进行经验正交函数（EOF）分析，识别出两个主要变率模态。

An Empirical Orthogonal Function (EOF) analysis of MHW intensity identifies two leading modes of variability.

第一模态（PC1）解释 22% 的方差，表现为全盆地海洋热浪，其中阿拉伯海强度尤其突出；该模态与季风风减弱、蒸发和云量减少以及短波辐射增强有关，进而导致上层海洋增暖。

The first mode (PC1), explaining 22% of the variance, is characterized by basin-wide MHWs with enhanced intensity in the Arabian Sea and is associated with weakened monsoon winds, reduced evaporation and cloud cover, and enhanced shortwave radiation, leading to upper-ocean warming.

第二模态（PC2）解释 8% 的方差，呈偶极结构：正位相时孟加拉湾海洋热浪增强而阿拉伯海活动受抑，负位相时则相反。

The second mode (PC2), accounting for 8% of the variance, exhibits a dipole structure, with intensified MHWs in the Bay of Bengal and suppressed activity in the Arabian Sea during its positive phase, and the opposite pattern during its negative phase.

大尺度气候模态调节这些空间型。

Large-scale climate modes modulate these patterns.

与 PC1 相似的全盆地海洋热浪通常与厄尔尼诺成熟期有关，并与季风季节内振荡（MISO）由活跃期向中断期的转换同时发生。

Basin-wide MHWs resembling PC1 are usually associated with the mature phase of El Niño, coinciding with the transition from active to break phases of the Monsoon Intraseasonal Oscillation (MISO).

在相似的 MISO 条件下，拉尼娜更有利于出现 PC2+ 型增暖。

Under similar MISO conditions, La Niña tends to favor PC2+-type warming.

这些模态伴随不同的降水响应：PC1 和 PC2+ 与印度南部偏湿、北部偏干有关，而 PC2− 对应更大范围的干旱。

These modes are accompanied by distinct rainfall responses: PC1 and PC2+ are linked to wetter conditions over southern India and drier conditions in the north, whereas PC2− corresponds to more widespread dryness.

海洋热浪的终止与季风风重新增强有关；后者既抑制海洋继续增暖，也通过加强蒸发和水汽输送而增加降水。

The termination of MHWs is associated with the re-intensification of monsoon winds, which both suppresses further ocean warming and enhances rainfall through increased evaporation and moisture transport.

综合而言，结果指向海洋热浪、季风季节内变率与 ENSO 之间的潜在相互作用，并表明某些气候条件可能促使 MISO 相关海洋增暖转化为海洋热浪，从而影响变暖气候下季风的可预报性。

Together, these results point to a potential interaction between MHWs, monsoon intraseasonal variability, and ENSO and suggest that certain climate conditions may favor the transition of MISO-related ocean warming into marine heatwaves, with implications for monsoon predictability in a warming climate.

1 导言

1 Introduction

地球正以前所未有的速度变暖，2024 年成为有记录以来陆地和海洋温度最高的一年（Rohde, 2025; World Meteorological Organization, 2025）。

Our planet is warming at an unprecedented rate, and 2024 was recorded as the hottest year on record for both land and ocean temperatures (Rohde, 2025; World Meteorological Organization, 2025).

海洋温度上升因其对气候系统的深远影响以及对极端天气事件的强化作用而日益受到科学界关注（Johnson et al., 2023）。

The rise in ocean temperature has gained increasing scientific attention due to its far-reaching implications for the climate system and the intensification of extreme weather events (Johnson et al., 2023).

在世界各大洋中，印度洋增暖最快，因此是理解变暖世界中海气相互作用的关键区域（Roxy et al., 2014; Venegas et al., 2023）。

Among the world's oceans, the Indian Ocean is warming the fastest, making it a critical region for understanding ocean-atmosphere interactions in a warming world (Roxy et al., 2014; Venegas et al., 2023).

印度洋是南亚季风的主要水汽源；南亚季风是规模最大的季风系统，数百万人依赖它维持农业、供水安全和生计（Dey and Döös, 2021）。

The Indian Ocean is the principal source of moisture for the South Asian Monsoon, which is the largest monsoon system, on which millions of people depend for agriculture, water security, and livelihoods (Dey and Döös, 2021).

因此，印度洋热力结构的任何变化都有可能对季风变异性和强度(Roxy et al.， 2015； Yadav and Roxy， 2019； Skliris et al.， 2022； Joseph et al.， 2024)产生重大影响。

Any change in the Indian Ocean's thermal structure, therefore, has the potential to substantially influence monsoon variability and intensity (Roxy et al., 2015; Yadav and Roxy, 2019; Skliris et al., 2022; Joseph et al., 2024).

既往研究主要关注印度洋平均态增暖，尤其是包括阿拉伯海在内的西部海盆（Oppo and Rosenthal, 2010; Swapna et al., 2013; Roxy et al., 2014; D'Mello and Kumar, 2018; Pratik et al., 2018; Zhang Y. et al., 2018; Mathew et al., 2018; McMonigal et al., 2022; Albert et al., 2023; Nisha et al., 2023; Shee et al., 2023; Sajidh et al., 2026; Joseph et al., 2026）；相比之下，对持续数日至数周的海表温度异常偏高期——即海洋热浪（MHW）——关注较少。

While previous studies have focused on the mean-state warming of the Indian Ocean, particularly the western basin including the Arabian Sea (Oppo and Rosenthal, 2010; Swapna et al., 2013; Roxy et al., 2014; D'Mello and Kumar, 2018; Pratik et al., 2018; Zhang Y. et al., 2018; Mathew et al., 2018; McMonigal et al., 2022; Albert et al., 2023; Nisha et al., 2023; Shee et al., 2023; Sajidh et al., 2026; Joseph et al., 2026), relatively limited attention has been paid to understanding periods of anomalously high Sea Surface Temperature (SST) lasting days to weeks, known as marine heatwaves (MHWs).

近期研究表明，在人为增暖和内部气候变率驱动下，全球海洋多个区域的海洋热浪正变得更频繁、强度更高且持续更久（Oliver et al., 2018; Saranya et al., 2022; Chatterjee et al., 2022; Chakraborty et al., 2023; Cheng et al., 2023; Koul et al., 2023; Capotondi et al., 2024; Gupta et al., 2024）。

Recent studies suggest that MHWs are becoming more frequent, more intense, and longer in duration across various regions of the global ocean, driven by anthropogenic warming and internal climate variability (Oliver et al., 2018; Saranya et al., 2022; Chatterjee et al., 2022; Chakraborty et al., 2023; Cheng et al., 2023; Koul et al., 2023; Capotondi et al., 2024; Gupta et al., 2024).

Saranya et al.（2022）表明，西印度洋和孟加拉湾北部海洋热浪频率正在增加，且常与背景海洋增暖及厄尔尼诺事件有关。

Saranya et al. (2022) demonstrated that the frequency of MHWs in the western Indian Ocean and northern Bay of Bengal is increasing, often in association with background ocean warming and El Niño events.

这些海洋热浪又会影响印度夏季风降水格局：其作用取决于海洋热浪所在位置，可抑制印度中部降水并增强南部降水。

These MHWs, in turn, influence Indian summer monsoon rainfall patterns by suppressing rainfall over central India and enhancing it over the south, depending on the location of the MHW.

类似地，Chatterjee et al.（2022）发现，阿拉伯海北部海洋热浪事件呈增加趋势，并与平均 SST 上升和厄尔尼诺强迫有关。

Similarly, Chatterjee et al. (2022) found an increasing trend in MHW events over the northern Arabian Sea linked to increasing mean SST and El Niño forcing.

Chakraborty et al.（2023）指出，阿拉伯海西部的海洋热浪强度高于东部，且这些事件显著影响生物地球化学循环。

Chakraborty et al. (2023) showed that MHW intensity is higher in the western Arabian Sea than in the east and that these events significantly influence biogeochemical cycles.

Liang et al.（2024）发现，印度洋偶极子（IOD）与孟加拉湾海洋热浪日数高度相关。

Liang et al. (2024) found high correlations between the Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD) and MHW days in the Bay of Bengal.

Kumar et al.（2023）指出，孟加拉湾海洋热浪由净热通量变化和反气旋涡旋驱动。

Kumar et al. (2023) documented that the MHWs in the Bay of Bengal are driven by changes in the net heat flux and anticyclonic eddies.

此外，Gupta et al.（2023）表明，孟加拉湾表层海洋热浪在厄尔尼诺和正 IOD 年较为频繁，而次表层海洋热浪通常由拉尼娜和负 IOD 引起的温跃层变化所驱动。

Furthermore, Gupta et al. (2023) showed that the surface MHWs in the Bay of Bengal are frequent during El Niño and positive IOD years, whereas subsurface MHWs are usually driven by the thermocline changes driven by La Niña and negative IOD.

这些研究集体证实，北印度洋中的MHW正在增强，尽管在区域重点和衡量标准上有所不同。

Collectively, these studies affirm that MHWs in the North Indian Ocean are intensifying, although they differ in regional focus and metrics.

重要的是，所有这些研究都使用固定基线季节气候态定义海洋热浪，这会将长期海洋增暖与短期事件混杂在一起。

Importantly, all these studies use a fixed baseline seasonal climatology to define MHWs, which can mix long-term ocean warming with short-term events.

这使得MHW在近几十年显得更加频繁，甚至可能导致海洋进入“永久MHW状态”的情景，特别是在像印度洋这样的迅速升温的区域。

This makes MHWs appear more frequent in recent decades and could even lead to scenarios where oceans enter a “permanent MHW state,” particularly in rapidly warming regions like the Indian Ocean.

的确，近期预测表明，按固定基线定义，到 2050 年印度洋可能会持续超过海洋热浪阈值（Roxy et al., 2024）。

Indeed, recent projections suggest the Indian Ocean could permanently exceed MHW thresholds by 2050 under fixed-baseline definitions (Roxy et al., 2024).

为解决这一问题，一些研究建议用去趋势 SST 异常或移动基线定义海洋热浪，从而将真正的海洋热浪事件与长期增暖区分开来（Jacox, 2019; Rosselló et al., 2023; Amaya et al., 2023; Capotondi et al., 2024）。

To address this, several studies have proposed defining MHWs using detrended SST anomalies or a moving baseline, which helps distinguish true marine heatwave events from long-term warming (Jacox, 2019; Rosselló et al., 2023; Amaya et al., 2023; Capotondi et al., 2024).

近期研究建议，仅将“海洋热浪”用于通过移动基线方法识别的事件；用“长期温度趋势”描述海温缓慢变化，并用“总热暴露”描述长期增暖与海洋热浪的共同作用（Amaya et al., 2023）。

A recent study suggests reserving the term “Marine Heatwaves” for events identified using a moving baseline approach, while using “long-term temperature trend” for slow changes in the ocean temperature and using “total heat exposure” to describe the combination of long-term warming and marine heatwaves (Amaya et al., 2023).

这避免了缓慢增加的背景SST被错误分类为MHW。

This avoids the misclassification of slowly increasing background SST as MHWs.

尽管改进方法已经出现，但在北印度洋海洋热浪定义中采用去趋势或移动基线方法的研究仍然有限。

Despite the emergence of improved methodologies, the use of detrended or moving-baseline approaches in defining MHWs in the North Indian Ocean remains limited.

此外，尚无综合研究考察这一气候敏感海盆中此类事件的变率、驱动因素和季风后果。

Moreover, no comprehensive study has yet examined the variability, drivers, and monsoonal consequences of these events in this climate-sensitive basin.

这项研究填补了这一关键空白。

This study fills that critical gap.

本研究采用去趋势 SST 异常框架识别北印度洋海洋热浪，从而将短期海洋热浪动力过程与长期增暖信号分离。

In this present study, we apply a detrended SST anomaly framework to identify MHWs in the North Indian Ocean, allowing us to disentangle short-term marine heatwave dynamics from the long-term warming signal.

在这一改进识别方法的基础上，我们详细研究海洋热浪变率的主导模态，诊断包括海洋和大气强迫机制在内的物理驱动因素，并考察其对南亚季风降水的可能影响。

Building on this improved detection, we conduct a detailed investigation into the dominant modes of MHW variability, diagnose their physical drivers, including oceanic and atmospheric forcing mechanisms, and investigate their possible impacts on the monsoon rainfall over South Asia.

通过联系海洋高温极端与季风变率，本研究为理解上层海洋热异常如何影响区域气候提供了新认识，并对南亚区域的预测和气候风险评估具有启示。

By bridging marine heat extremes and monsoonal variability, our study offers a new understanding of how upper-ocean thermal anomalies influence regional climate, with implications for prediction and climate risk assessment across South Asia.

2 Data and methods

2.1 所用数据

2.1 Data used

为识别北印度洋海洋热浪并研究其驱动因素，我们使用 ERA5 再分析资料中的逐日 SST 及大气变量，包括 10 米风、海平面气压（MSLP）、海表潜热通量、地表净短波辐射、总云量、总降水和整层积分水汽通量（Hersbach et al., 2020）。

To identify MHWs and investigate their drivers in the North Indian Ocean, we use daily SST and atmospheric variables including 10-meter winds, mean sea level pressure (MSLP), surface latent heat flux, net shortwave radiation at the surface, total cloud cover, total precipitation, and vertically integrated water vapor flux from the ERA5 reanalysis dataset (Hersbach et al., 2020).

ERA5 SST 在 1979 年至 2007 年 8 月期间基于 HadISST2，自 2007 年 9 月起基于 OSTIA，并受益于严格的数据同化（Titchner and Rayner, 2014; Good et al., 2020; Hersbach et al., 2020）。

The ERA5 SST is based on HadISST2 from 1979 to August 2007 and on OSTIA from September 2007 onward, benefiting from rigorous data assimilation (Titchner and Rayner, 2014; Good et al., 2020; Hersbach et al., 2020).

我们使用ERA5 SST识别MHW事件并分析其驱动力，以确保与大气场的一致性，这些场均来源于ERA5。

We use ERA5 SST to identify MHW events and analyze their drivers to ensure consistency with the atmospheric fields, all of which are derived from ERA5.

此外，我们使用 NOAA OISST 计算海洋热浪特征（如频率、持续时间和强度；见图 1），以利用这一资料被广泛采用的观测记录（Reynolds et al., 2002）。

In addition, we compute the MHW characteristics (e.g., frequency, duration and intensity; see Figure 1) using NOAA OISST to take advantage of its widely used observational record (Reynolds et al., 2002).

我们的分析涵盖1982-2024年期间，在此期间，ERA5和OISST提供了一致和可靠的数据。

Our analysis covers the period 1982–2024, during which both ERA5 and OISST provide consistent and reliable data.

Notably, similar results are found when using OISST, indicating that our conclusions are not sensitive to the choice of SST dataset (see Supplementary Figures S14–S16).

2.2 海洋热浪分类

2.2 Marine heat waves classification

海洋热浪通常定义为逐日 SST 异常连续至少 5 天超过随季节变化的第 90 百分位阈值的时段（Hobday et al., 2016）。

The MHWs are typically defined as periods when daily SST anomalies exceed the seasonally varying 90th percentile threshold for five or more consecutive days (Hobday et al., 2016).

这一阈值通常使用固定基线 气候态计算。

This threshold is usually computed using a fixed baseline climatology.

然而，最近的研究突出表明了这种方法的一个主要局限性。

However, recent studies have highlighted a key limitation of this approach.

随着全球SST的上升，相对于固定基线定义的MHW的频率可以人为地增加，导致"不寻常"成为"新常态"的(Jacox， 2019； Rosselló et al.， 2023； Amaya et al.， 2023； Capotondi et al.， 2024)。

As global SSTs rise, the frequency of MHWs defined relative to a fixed baseline can increase artificially, causing the “unusual” to become the “new normal” (Jacox, 2019; Rosselló et al., 2023; Amaya et al., 2023; Capotondi et al., 2024).

为了解决这一问题，有人提议，MHW的定义应取决于所评估的预期影响。

To address this, it has been proposed that the definition of MHWs should depend on the intended impact being assessed.

例如，在研究海洋生物的适应性等生物影响时，固定基线可能是适当的，因为它捕捉到相对于历史常态的绝对热应力。

For instance, when studying biological impacts such as the adaptability of marine organisms, a fixed baseline may be appropriate, as it captures absolute thermal stress relative to a historical normal.

相反，在评估气候影响、尤其是与大气反馈和降水有关的影响时，应采用移动基线或去趋势 SST 异常，以便更好地将阶段性极端事件与长期增暖分离。

In contrast, for assessing climate impacts, particularly those related to atmospheric feedbacks and precipitation, a moving baseline or detrended SST anomalies should be used to better isolate episodic extreme events from long-term warming.

最近的一些研究进一步建议保留“海洋热浪”一词，用于使用移动的气候态 (Amaya et al.， 2023)定义的事件。

Some recent studies have further recommended reserving the term “marine heatwave” for events defined using a moving climatology (Amaya et al., 2023).

在这项研究中，我们使用去趋势后的 SST异常来定义MHW。

In this study, we define MHWs using detrended SST anomalies.

当去趋势 SST 异常连续至少 5 天超过随季节变化的第 90 百分位时，该事件被识别为海洋热浪。

An event is identified as an MHW when the detrended SST anomaly exceeds the seasonally varying 90th percentile for at least five consecutive days.

百分位阈值和异常值是用1982年至2024年的整个时期计算的。

The percentile threshold and the anomalies are calculated using the full 1982–2024 period.

首先采用以当日为中心的 11 天移动窗口构建季节气候态，以捕捉逐日变率。

A seasonal climatology is first constructed using an 11-day centered moving window to capture day-to-day variability.

随后用 31 天二项式滤波器对该气候态进行平滑，以保证时间连续性并降低高频噪声（Hobday et al., 2016）。

Then this climatology is smoothed using a 31-day binomial filter to ensure temporal coherence and reduce high-frequency noise (Hobday et al., 2016).

The mean and linear trends of key MHW metrics, including the frequency, duration, and mean intensity, computed using the detrended SST anomalies, are shown in Figure 1.

MHW事件的频率在孟加拉湾比阿拉伯海更高，而阿拉伯海中的MHW则倾向于持续时间更长，显示较高的平均强度。

The frequency of MHW events is higher in the Bay of Bengal compared to the Arabian Sea, whereas the MHWs in the Arabian Sea tend to last longer and show higher mean intensity.

与采用固定基线并报告显著增加趋势的研究不同（Saranya et al., 2022; Chatterjee et al., 2022; Gao et al., 2022; Kumar et al., 2023），基于去趋势 SST 时，我们并未在这些指标中观察到大范围统计显著趋势，但仍出现了一些区域性特征。

Although we do not observe widespread statistically significant trends in these metrics based on detrended SSTs, unlike studies using a fixed baseline that report significant increases (Saranya et al., 2022; Chatterjee et al., 2022; Gao et al., 2022; Kumar et al., 2023), but some regional patterns emerge.

特别是，MHW持续时间显示阿拉伯海的某些部分有显著增长的趋势，而孟加拉湾的强度则显著下降。

In particular, MHW duration shows a significant increasing trend across some portion of the Arabian Sea, while the Bay of Bengal exhibits a significant decrease in MHW intensity.

为理解并量化去除 SST 趋势对识别海洋热浪的重要性，下面以 OISST 为例说明。

To understand and quantify the importance of detrending SSTs in the identification of MHWs, we present the following example using OISST.

我们关注阿拉伯海和孟加拉湾的海盆尺度海洋热浪事件，其定义为覆盖至少 25% 的海盆且连续持续至少 15 天的事件。

We focus on basin-scale MHW events, defined as those covering at least 25% of the basin and lasting for a minimum of 15 consecutive days, in both the Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal.

采用去趋势 SST 异常时，我们在阿拉伯海识别出 20 个事件、在孟加拉湾识别出 17 个事件；采用原始（未去趋势）SST 时则分别为 17 个和 24 个。

Using detrended SST anomalies, we identified 20 events in the Arabian Sea and 17 in the Bay of Bengal, compared to 17 and 24 events, respectively, using raw (non-detrended) SSTs.

值得注意的是，2010 年以前，采用去趋势异常在阿拉伯海和孟加拉湾分别识别出 12 个和 9 个海洋热浪事件，而采用未去趋势 SST 时仅分别识别出 1 个和 2 个。

Notably, before 2010, 12 MHW events were detected in the Arabian Sea and 9 in the Bay of Bengal using detrended anomalies, whereas only 1 and 2 events, respectively, were identified using non-detrended SSTs.

相反，2010 年以后，采用去趋势资料在两个海盆均识别出 8 个事件，而采用原始 SST 时在阿拉伯海和孟加拉湾分别识别出 16 个和 22 个事件。

In contrast, after 2010, 8 events were identified in each basin using detrended data, while 16 events in the Arabian Sea and 22 in the Bay of Bengal were found using raw SSTs.

这清楚地表明，如果不消除长期变暖趋势，可能导致高估最近的MHW频率。

This clearly illustrates that failing to remove long-term warming trends can lead to an overestimation of recent MHW frequency.

例如，仅 2023 年一年，采用原始 SST 时阿拉伯海和孟加拉湾分别识别出 202 个和 93 个海洋热浪日，而采用去趋势异常时分别仅为 55 天和 0 天。

For instance, in 2023 alone, 202 MHW days were identified in the Arabian Sea and 93 in the Bay of Bengal using raw SSTs, compared to only 55 and 0 days, respectively, using detrended anomalies.

这些结果强调，必须去除趋势，才能刻画阶段性高温极端，而不是长期背景增暖。

These results underscore the necessity of detrending to accurately characterize episodic heat extremes rather than long-term background warming.

2.3 气候指数

2.3 Climate indices

为评估 ENSO 和 IOD 等大尺度自然气候变率模态的影响，我们采用 Niño-3.4 指数（代表 ENSO）和偶极模态指数（DMI，代表 IOD）。

To assess the influence of large-scale modes of natural climate variability, such as the ENSO and the IOD, we utilize the Niño-3.4 index (for ENSO) and the Dipole Mode Index (DMI, for the IOD).

Niño-3.4 指数定义为 Niño-3.4 区域（5°N—5°S，170°—120°W）相对于 1981—2010 年气候态的区域平均 SST 异常。

The Niño-3.4 index is defined as the area-averaged SST anomalies over the Niño-3.4 region (5°N–5°S, 170°–120°W), relative to a 1981–2010 climatology.

DMI 表示赤道印度洋西部（50°—70°E，10°S—10°N）与赤道印度洋东南部（90°—110°E，10°S—0°）之间的异常 SST 梯度。

The DMI represents the anomalous SST gradient between the western equatorial Indian Ocean (50°–70°E, 10°S–10°N) and the southeastern equatorial Indian Ocean (90°–110°E, 10°S–0°).

这两个指数均来自NOAA物理科学实验室(NOAA PSL)，该实验室使用HadISST1.1数据集(Rayner et al.， 2003)计算。

Both indices are obtained from the NOAA Physical Sciences Laboratory (NOAA PSL), where they are computed using the HadISST1.1 dataset (Rayner et al., 2003).

Unlike the previous two modes, this phase is associated with suppressed monsoon activity across much of the Indian Subcontinent, likely due to weakened low-level monsoon circulation and the absence of moisture‘convergence.

这一配置再次对抗典型季风气压梯度，使跨赤道风减弱；这一次主要发生在西部海盆（图 6f）。

This configuration again opposes the typical monsoon pressure gradient, weakening the cross-equatorial winds, this time primarily over the western basin (Figure 6f).

Therefore, the influence of MISO on MHW variability is examined in a subsequent section.

5 季风季节内振荡与海洋热浪

5 Monsoon intraseasonal oscillation and marine heatwaves

印度夏季风在 5—10 月表现出显著季节内变率，通常称为季风季节内振荡（MISO）（Goswami and Mohan, 2001; Rajeevan et al., 2010）。

The Indian summer monsoon exhibits pronounced intraseasonal variability from May to October, commonly referred to as the Monsoon Intraseasonal Oscillation (MISO) (Goswami and Mohan, 2001; Rajeevan et al., 2010).

MISO 的特征是活跃（湿）位相与中断（干）位相交替出现，每个位相通常持续数天。

MISO is characterized by alternating active (wet) and break (dry) phases, each typically persisting for several days.

多项研究表明，MISO-相关降水异常与SST可变性之间有双向相互作用，MISO相关大气强迫作用影响SST，SST异常作用反过来调节MISO降水(Kemball-Cook and Wang， 2001； Vecchi and Harrison， 2002； Fu et al.， 2003， 2007； Roxy and Tanimoto， 2007； Krishnamurthy and Kirtman， 2009； Zhang L. et al.， 2018)。

Numerous studies have demonstrated a two-way interaction between MISO-related precipitation anomalies and SST variability, whereby atmospheric forcing associated with MISO influences SST, and SST anomalies, in turn, modulate MISO rainfall (Kemball-Cook and Wang, 2001; Vecchi and Harrison, 2002; Fu et al., 2003, 2007; Roxy and Tanimoto, 2007; Krishnamurthy and Kirtman, 2009; Zhang L. et al., 2018).

例如，Vecchi and Harrison（2002）指出，孟加拉湾北部 SST 降低约超前季风中断位相 1 周；Roxy and Tanimoto（2007）则表明，阿拉伯海正 SST 异常超前于 MISO 活跃位相的降水增强。

For instance, Vecchi and Harrison (2002) showed that SST cooling in the northern Bay of Bengal precedes monsoon break phases by approximately 1 week, while Roxy and Tanimoto (2007) demonstrated that positive SST anomalies over the Arabian Sea lead enhanced precipitation during the active phase of MISO.

这些调查结果表明，与MISO-有关的海气相互作用也可能在MHW的开发和调制中发挥重要作用，本节将对此进行审查。

These findings suggest that MISO-related air-sea interactions may also play an important role in the development and modulation of MHWs, an aspect that is examined in this section.

MISO 位相采用扩展经验正交函数分析得到（Suhas et al., 2012）。

MISO phases are derived using Extended Empirical Orthogonal Function analysis (Suhas et al., 2012).

使用 ERA5 降水资料计算的 8 个 MISO 位相中降水、MSLP、近地面风和潜热通量合成异常（见补充图 S5—S8），与 Suhas et al.（2012）基于 IMD 资料得到的结果一致。

Composite anomalies of precipitation, MSLP, near-surface winds, and latent heat flux during the eight MISO phases, computed using the ERA5 rainfall data (see Supplementary Figures S5–S8), align with those shown by Suhas et al. (2012) using IMD data.

例如，MISO 第 1 位相期间，增强对流中心位于赤道附近，随后在后续位相中向北迁移。

For example, during MISO Phase 1, enhanced convection is centered near the equator, which then migrates northward over subsequent phases.

类似地，最初 4 个位相期间印度次大陆上空存在异常高压中心，随后位相中转变为低压。

Similarly, an anomalous high-pressure center is seen over the Indian subcontinent during the initial four phases, transitioning to low pressure in the later phases.

有趣的是，其中一些 MISO 合成型与不同海洋热浪 EOF 模态下海洋热浪发生前的条件相似。

Interestingly, some of these MISO composite patterns resemble the pre-MHW conditions seen in different EOF modes of MHWs.

例如，PC1 和 PC2+ 的降水异常型（见图 5）与 MISO 第 4、5 位相相似：增强对流集中在印度南部和孟加拉湾，而印度中部和孟加拉湾北部降水受抑。

Taken together with the onset-phase analysis, this indicates that MHWs are most often initiated during MISO Phases 3–4, when surface winds are weak and evaporative cooling is reduced (Supplementary Figures S7, S8), creating favorable conditions for ocean surface warming.

尽管风速和潜热损失已经恢复，偏高的海洋热浪频率仍持续至第 5 位相，这凸显了上层海洋储热和记忆的作用，使较早发生的海洋热浪可持续到海表强迫最弱的位相之后。

The persistence of elevated MHW frequency into Phase 5, despite a recovery of winds and latent heat loss, highlights the role of upper-ocean heat storage and memory, allowing MHWs initiated earlier to persist beyond the phase of weakest surface forcing.

总体而言，这些结果支持 MISO 相关海表条件对北印度洋海洋热浪变率的调节作用，这与既往关于季节内变率影响 SST 的研究一致（Kemball-Cook and Wang, 2001; Vecchi and Harrison, 2002; Fu et al., 2003, 2007; Roxy and Tanimoto, 2007; Zhang L. et al., 2018）。

Overall, these results support a modulation of North Indian Ocean MHW variability by MISO-related surface conditions, consistent with previous studies showing an influence of intraseasonal variability on SST (Kemball-Cook and Wang, 2001; Vecchi and Harrison, 2002; Fu et al., 2003, 2007; Roxy and Tanimoto, 2007; Zhang L. et al., 2018).

Conversely, during La Niña years, the background state differs (Supplementary Figure S11), and the associated circulation anomalies can favor reduced winds and weaker evaporative cooling over the Bay of Bengal, thereby increasing the likelihood of localized MHW development (Krishnamurthy and Kirtman, 2009).

综合而言，这些结果表明季节内（MISO）和年际（ENSO）变率可相互作用，共同塑造北印度洋海洋热浪发生的空间分布。

Together, these results suggest that intraseasonal (MISO) and interannual (ENSO) variability can interact to shape the spatial distribution of North Indian Ocean MHW occurrence.

The composite of key atmospheric variables averaged over 5 days following the MHW end dates (Figure 10), reveals contrasting patterns compared to those observed prior to the MHW events, with enhanced rainfall over the Indian Subcontinent.

对 PC1 模态而言，北印度洋，尤其是阿拉伯海上空，存在明显的气压负异常（图 10a）。

For the PC1 mode, negative pressure anomalies are evident over the North Indian Ocean, particularly over the Arabian Sea (Figure 10a).

MHW期间的高SST可以缓慢地在海表上方加热空气，增加空气温度。

High SSTs during MHWs can slowly heat the air immediately above the ocean surface, increasing air temperature.

这些较暖空气密度降低并上升，导致高空辐散。

This warmer air becomes less dense and rises, leading to divergence at higher altitudes.

根据质量连续性，这种上升运动会导致地面气压降低。

By mass continuity, this upward motion induces a decrease in surface pressure.

在解释数日后海洋热浪对大气变量的影响时，必须考虑暖空气上升与高层辐散之间的时间滞后。

The time lag between warm air rising and upper-level divergence is important to account for when explaining the impact of MHWs on atmospheric variables a few days later.

对三个不同模态的海洋热浪日进行 SST 与 MSLP 回归分析，结果显示北印度洋大部为负值，表明 SST 与 MSLP 呈反向关系（补充图 S12）。

The regression analysis between SST and MSLP during the MHW days for the three different modes shows negative values across most of the North Indian Ocean, indicating that SST and MSLP are inversely related (Supplementary Figure S12).

异常气压的这种降低有助于增强气候态季风气压梯度——其特征是印度次大陆上空低压、南印度洋上空高压——进而增强季风环流（图 10d）。

This reduction in anomalous pressure helps strengthen the climatological monsoon pressure gradient, characterized by low pressure over the Indian subcontinent and high pressure over the southern Indian Ocean, which, in turn, enhances the monsoon circulation (Figure 10d).

增强的风促进海洋蒸发增加，表现为潜热通量负异常（图 10g），说明海洋将在海洋热浪期间储存的更多热量传递给大气。

These strengthened winds promote increased evaporation over the ocean, as reflected in the negative latent heat flux anomalies (Figure 10g), indicating more heat being transferred from the ocean (which was stored during the MHW event) to the atmosphere.

这种增强的蒸发也支持更大的云层形成，这反过来又限制了进入海表的太阳辐射量(图 10)。

This enhanced evaporation also supports greater cloud formation, which in turn limits the amount of incoming solar radiation reaching the ocean surface (Figure 10).

因此，海洋一方面通过潜热通量散热，另一方面因辐射输入减少而失热，从而有助于终止海洋热浪事件。

As a result, the ocean loses heat both through latent heat flux and reduced radiative input, helping to terminate the MHW event.

这些MHW后大气的调整伴随着印度西部和东北部，孟加拉国，缅甸，孟加拉湾地区(图 10m)上空的降水增强。

These post-MHW atmospheric adjustments are accompanied by enhanced precipitation over western and northeastern India, Bangladesh, Myanmar, and the Bay of Bengal region (Figure 10m).

阿拉伯海蒸发增强可能增加可用水汽，随后水汽被输送至印度西部和中部；异常整层积分水汽输送（IVT）矢量指示了这一过程。

The increased evaporation over the Arabian Sea possibly contributes to greater moisture availability, which is subsequently transported toward western and central India, as indicated by the anomalous vertically integrated water vapor transport (IVT) vectors.

类似地，孟加拉湾蒸发增强可能加强印度东北部、缅甸、孟加拉国及孟加拉湾的水汽辐合，这与指向上述区域的 IVT 增强一致。

Similarly, elevated evaporation over the Bay of Bengal possibly enhances moisture convergence over northeastern India, Myanmar, Bangladesh, and the Bay of Bengal, consistent with strengthened IVT directed toward these regions.

这种空间型提示一种潜在反馈机制：海洋热浪诱发的海气相互作用可在全盆地海洋热浪事件衰减后放大季风降水，这与既往关于 MISO—SST 季风降水相互作用的研究一致（Roxy and Tanimoto, 2007; Zhang L. et al., 2018）。

This spatial pattern suggests a potential feedback mechanism, wherein MHW-induced ocean-atmosphere interactions can amplify monsoon rainfall following the decay of a basin-wide MHW event, consistent with the previous studies on MISO-SST monsoon rainfall interactions (Roxy and Tanimoto, 2007; Zhang L. et al., 2018).

7 总结和讨论

7 Summary and discussion

MHW一般定义为每天SST异常连续5天或更多天超过使用固定气候态计算出的季节性变化的第90百分位阈值。

MHWs are typically defined as periods when daily SST anomalies exceed the seasonally varying 90th percentile threshold, calculated using a fixed climatology, for five or more consecutive days.

然而，近期研究指出，随着全球 SST 上升，固定基线会人为增大观测到的海洋热浪频率增幅，使“异常”实质上成为“新常态”。为解决这一问题，我们用去趋势 SST 异常定义海洋热浪，从而将内部变率与长期增暖分离，并研究北印度洋海洋热浪的变率、驱动因素和季风影响。

However, recent studies have indicated that using a fixed baseline can artificially raise the observed increase in MHW frequency as global SSTs rise, effectively making the “unusual” the “new normal.” To address this issue, we defined MHWs using detrended SST anomalies, thereby isolating internal variability from long-term warming, and investigated the variability, drivers, and monsoon impacts of MHWs in the North Indian Ocean.

为客观分离海洋热浪变率的主导空间结构，我们对海洋热浪强度进行 EOF 分析。

To objectively isolate the dominant spatial structures of MHW variability, we apply an EOF analysis to MHW intensity.

该方法直接从资料中识别空间相干的大尺度海洋热浪型，无需采用基于空间覆盖范围的任意阈值（例如要求海洋热浪占据海盆的固定比例）。

This approach identifies coherent, large-scale MHW patterns directly from the data, avoiding the need for arbitrary thresholds based on spatial coverage (e.g., requiring MHWs to occupy a fixed fraction of the basin).

海洋热浪强度的 EOF 分析揭示出两个主导变率模态。

The EOF analysis of MHW intensity revealed two dominant modes of variability.

第一模态解释 22% 的总方差，表现为全盆地海洋热浪，其中阿拉伯海信号尤其强。

The first mode, explaining 22% of the total variance, shows basin-wide MHWs with particularly strong signals in the Arabian Sea.

这种模式与北印度洋上空的异常高压和南半球的相应低压相连。

This mode is linked to an anomalous high pressure over the North Indian Ocean and a corresponding low pressure in the Southern Hemisphere.

这种配置削弱气候态气压梯度，从而削弱季风风场，进而抑制蒸发(从而减少蒸发冷却)和云层形成。

This configuration weakens the climatological pressure gradient and hence the monsoon winds, which in turn suppresses evaporation (and hence reducing evaporative cooling) and cloud formation.

因此，短波辐射可更有效地穿透并加热上层海洋，从而促进海洋热浪形成（图 6）。

As a result, shortwave radiation penetrates more effectively, warming the upper ocean and promoting MHWs (Figure 6).

该模态主要与厄尔尼诺成熟期有关，这与既往研究一致：厄尔尼诺通过印度洋全盆模态影响印度洋 SST，从而为海盆尺度海洋热浪的发展提供预先条件（Xie et al., 2009）。

This mode is predominantly associated with the mature phase of El Niño, consistent with previous studies showing that El Niño influences Indian Ocean SSTs through the Indian Ocean Basin Mode, thereby preconditioning the basin for large-scale MHW development (Xie et al., 2009).

第二模态解释 8% 的总方差，呈偶极型：正位相时孟加拉湾出现海洋热浪而阿拉伯海活动受抑，负位相时则相反。

The second mode, accounting for 8% of the total variance, shows a dipole-like pattern where its positive phase is characterized by MHWs in the Bay of Bengal and suppressed activity in the Arabian Sea, while the negative phase exhibits the opposite pattern.

正位相由与 EOF 第一模态类似的机制驱动，但其影响在孟加拉湾上空更为局地化。

The positive phase is driven by mechanisms similar to EOF mode 1, though the effects are more localized over the Bay of Bengal.

负位相时，海洋热浪集中在西印度洋，尤其是索马里沿岸附近。

In the negative phase, MHWs are concentrated over the western Indian Ocean, particularly near the Somali coast.

在这种情况下，季风风减弱也局限于该区域，可能减弱上升流及相关冷却，从而使 SST 升高。

In this case, the weakening of monsoon winds is also confined to the region, potentially reducing upwelling and the associated cooling, thereby allowing SSTs to rise.

有趣的是，与其它模式相比，短波辐射在这种模式中似乎发挥的主导作用较少。

Interestingly, shortwave radiation appears to play a less dominant role in this mode compared to the others.

PC2+在拉尼娜年间更为常见，而PC1在厄尔尼诺年间更为常见。

The PC2+ is found to be more common during the La Niña years, whereas PC1 is found to be more common during El Niño years.

相比 PC1 和 PC2+，PC2− 事件与 ENSO 的联系较弱，仅少数 PC2− 事件发生在厄尔尼诺早期，而 PC1 主要与厄尔尼诺成熟期有关。

In contrast, PC2- events show only a weak association with ENSO compared to PC1 and PC2+, with only a few occurring during the early phase of El Niño, whereas PC1 is primarily associated with the mature phase of El Niño.

重要的是，PC1 虽反映 ENSO 成熟期相关的盆地尺度预先条件，其演变也受季节内季风变率调节；PC2+ 则更强地刻画了演变中的 ENSO 状态与 MISO 动力相互作用所产生的海洋热浪变率。

Importantly, while PC1 reflects basin-scale preconditioning associated with the mature phase of ENSO, its evolution is also modulated by intraseasonal monsoon variability, whereas PC2+ more strongly captures MHW variability arising from the interaction between evolving ENSO states and MISO dynamics.

尤其是，PC2+ 往往发生在拉尼娜发展期，并与 MISO 第 4—5 位相一致。

In particular, PC2+ tends to occur during the developing phase of La Niña and also aligns with MISO phases 4–5.

海洋热浪发生前后的 MISO 位相演变进一步表明，PC1 和 PC2+ 海洋热浪倾向于在 MISO 第 3—4 位相发生，此时海表风较弱、蒸发冷却减弱。

Analysis of MISO phase evolution around MHW onset further shows that PC1 and PC2+ MHWs are preferentially initiated during MISO Phases 3–4, when surface winds are weak and evaporative cooling is reduced.

第 4—5 位相海洋热浪频率持续偏高，反映了上层海洋储热驱动的持续性，即使此时海表强迫已开始恢复。

Elevated MHW frequency during Phases 4–5 reflects persistence driven by upper-ocean heat storage, even as surface forcing begins to recover.

这表明，PC1 和 PC2+ 所代表的海洋热浪并非仅响应大尺度背景强迫，而是受到 ENSO 演变与季节内季风变率共同影响的调节。

This suggests that MHWs represented by PC1 and PC2+ are not solely a response to large-scale background forcing, but are modulated by the combined influence of ENSO evolution and intraseasonal monsoon variability.

这些不同 EOF 模态也以不同方式调节季风降水。

These different EOF modes also modulate monsoon rainfall in distinct ways.

PC1 和 PC2 正位相期间，印度南部往往偏湿而北部相对偏干，这种降水分布通常与 MISO 第 3—4 位相有关。

During the positive phases of PC1 and PC2, southern India tends to experience wetter conditions, while northern India becomes relatively drier, a rainfall distribution commonly associated with MISO phases 3–4.

相比之下，PC2-与印度大半个次大陆的广泛干燥有关。

In contrast, PC2- is linked to widespread dryness across much of the Indian subcontinent.

我们还调查了MHW之后的降水反应，发现PC1事件的终止通常会给印度西北和东北部以及孟加拉国和缅甸带来更多的降水。

We also investigated the post-MHW rainfall response and found that the termination of PC1 events typically brings enhanced rainfall to northwest and northeast India, as well as to Bangladesh and Myanmar.

相比之下，PC2+事件结束后，印度中部的降水增加，而PC2-终止导致印度南部和中部的降水增加。

In contrast, the end of PC2+ events is followed by increased rainfall over central India, while PC2- termination leads to enhanced rainfall over both south and central India.

这些滞后影响可能由中断位相后季风风场恢复所驱动；恢复的风场促进海洋释放海洋热浪事件期间储存的热量和水汽。

These delayed impacts are likely driven by the revival of monsoon winds following a break phase, which promotes the release of oceanic heat and moisture stored during the MHW event.

增强的风使海洋蒸发加强；潜热通量负异常印证了这一点，并表明热量从海洋传向大气。

These strengthened winds enhance evaporation over the ocean, as evidenced by negative latent heat flux anomalies, indicating heat transfer from ocean to atmosphere.

相关水汽通量有利于形成更多云，从而进一步减少入射太阳辐射、稳定海表，并强化向较湿大气条件的转变。

The associated moisture flux contributes to increased cloud formation, which can further reduce incoming solar radiation, stabilizing the ocean surface and reinforcing the transition toward wetter atmospheric conditions.

值得注意的是，约三分之一的 PC1 和 PC2+ 海洋热浪事件终止于 MISO 第 6 位相；这一动力活跃位相以季风环流增强为特征。

Notably, we find that approximately one-third of PC1 and PC2+ MHW events terminate during MISO Phase 6, a dynamically active phase characterized by strengthening monsoon circulation.

以该位相海洋热浪终止为条件的合成显示，相对于无海洋热浪活动的 MISO 周期，MISO 第 6、7 位相存在系统性降水异常；这表明海洋热浪衰减除受 MISO 变率塑造外，也可主动调节季节内季风降水。

Composites conditioned on MHW termination during this phase reveal systematic precipitation anomalies during MISO Phases 6 and 7 relative to MISO cycles without any MHW activity, indicating that MHW decay can actively modulate intraseasonal monsoon rainfall in addition to being shaped by MISO variability.

这一结果为海洋热浪与 MISO 的双向耦合提供证据：季节内季风动力调节海洋热浪生命周期，而海洋热浪衰减又反馈于季风降水。

This result provides evidence for a two-way coupling between MHWs and the MISO, in which intraseasonal monsoon dynamics regulate the lifecycle of MHWs, while MHW decay feeds back onto monsoon rainfall.

虽然这些结果表明海洋热浪衰减可主动调节季节内季风降水，但对其内在物理机制的详细研究超出本文范围，仍是未来工作的重要方向。

Although these results indicate that MHW decay can actively modulate intraseasonal monsoon rainfall, a detailed investigation of the underlying physical mechanisms is beyond the scope of this study and remains an important direction for future work.

我们的发现与 Roxy et al.（2017）强调的机制一致；该研究表明，阿拉伯海和孟加拉湾 SST 增暖在为印度中部极端降水事件提供水汽方面起关键作用。

Our findings are consistent with the mechanisms highlighted by Roxy et al. (2017), who showed that SST warming in the Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal plays a critical role in fueling extreme rainfall events over central India.

该研究将此类事件总水汽的近 36% 追溯至阿拉伯海，超过孟加拉湾（26%）与中印度洋（9%）之和。

They traced nearly 36% of the total moisture during such events to the Arabian Sea, more than from the Bay of Bengal (26%) and central Indian Ocean (9%) combined.

这突出表明了SST异常现象的重要性，例如MHW期间的异常现象，作为关键的水分来源。

This underscores the importance of SST anomalies, such as those during MHWs, as a key moisture source.

此外，近几十年来，观测到阿拉伯海水分贡献在统计学上显著增加，这表明MHW等海洋热事件在气候变化下加剧季风极端现象中的作用越来越大。

Additionally, a statistically significant increase in Arabian Sea moisture contribution has been observed over recent decades, suggesting a growing role for oceanic heat events like MHWs in intensifying monsoon extremes under climate change.

总体而言，本研究强调了MHW的空间结构和时间在塑造季风变异性方面的关键作用。

Overall, this study underscores the critical role of both the spatial structure and timing of MHWs in shaping monsoon variability.

其结果对了解和预测气候变暖情况下的区域降水极端现象具有重大影响。

The results have significant implications for understanding and predicting regional rainfall extremes in a warming climate.

既往研究表明，西印度洋增暖往往通过削弱陆海气压梯度及相关季风风场而减少印度次大陆降水（Roxy et al., 2015）。

Previous studies have shown that warming of the western Indian Ocean tends to reduce rainfall over the Indian subcontinent by weakening the land-sea pressure gradient and associated monsoon winds (Roxy et al., 2015).

与此相反，我们的结果表明，印度洋升温，尤其是通过海洋热浪，可以通过增加蒸发增加南亚部分地区的降水量。

In contrast, our results suggest that Indian Ocean warming, particularly through marine heatwaves, can enhance precipitation over parts of South Asia by increasing evaporation.

此外，我们的调查结果表明，MHW、ENSO和MISO之间可能发生相互作用，为今后的研究提出了重要方向。

Moreover, our findings suggest a possible interaction between MHWs, ENSO, and the MISO, raising important directions for future research.

更广泛地说，结果指向大尺度气候模态与季节内变率之间的层级耦合：ENSO 设定印度洋背景热力状态，而 MISO 调节海洋热浪的发生时机、区域表现和终止过程。

More broadly, our results point to a hierarchical coupling between large-scale climate modes and intraseasonal variability, in which ENSO sets the background thermodynamic state of the Indian Ocean, while the MISO modulates the timing, regional expression, and termination of marine heatwaves.

多项研究表明，海气相互作用可影响 MISO 降水（Roxy and Tanimoto, 2007; Ajayamohan et al., 2008; Wang et al., 2009; Lin et al., 2010; Sabeerali et al., 2014; Zhang L. et al., 2018）。

Several studies have shown that the air-sea interaction can influence the MISO precipitation (Roxy and Tanimoto, 2007; Ajayamohan et al., 2008; Wang et al., 2009; Lin et al., 2010; Sabeerali et al., 2014; Zhang L. et al., 2018).

因此，关键问题包括:MHW通过调节SST和大气对流回馈MISO吗?

Therefore, key questions include: Do MHWs feed back onto MISO by modulating SST and atmospheric convection?

海洋热浪能否改变 MISO 各位相的传播速度或强度？

Can MHWs alter the propagation speed or intensity of MISO phases?

海洋热浪是否会改变不同 MISO 位相对应的标准降水型？

Do MHWs shift the canonical rainfall patterns associated with different MISO phases?

它们能否延迟或加速 MISO 位相转换？

Could they delay or accelerate MISO phase transitions?

ENSO 和 IOD 等更大尺度模态如何调节 MISO—海洋热浪关系？

How do larger-scale modes such as ENSO and the IOD modulate the MISO-MHW relationship?

虽然这些问题超出本研究范围，但它们为深化对海气耦合的认识、提高变暖气候下的季风预报技巧开辟了新方向。

While these questions lie beyond the scope of the present study, they open up new directions for advancing our understanding of ocean-atmosphere coupling and improving monsoon forecasting skill in a changing climate.

特别是，由于我们使用去趋势后的 SST异常来定义MHW，未来的研究可以调查在使用包含长期变暖趋势的原始SST时，类似的MHW—MISO相互作用是否持续存在。

In particular, since we define MHWs using detrended SST anomalies, future research could investigate whether similar MHW-MISO interactions persist when using raw SSTs that include long-term warming trends.

综上，基于去趋势 SST 异常识别的北印度洋海洋热浪，是由 ENSO 驱动的盆地尺度预先条件和季节内季风变率共同塑造的动态演变事件，而非长期增暖的简单表现。

Taken together, our results suggest that marine heatwaves in the North Indian Ocean, as identified from detrended SST anomalies, represent dynamically evolving events shaped by ENSO-driven basin-scale preconditioning and intraseasonal monsoon variability, rather than simple manifestations of long-term warming.
