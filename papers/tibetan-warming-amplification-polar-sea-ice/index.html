摘要

Abstract

预估显示极地增暖速度快于全球平均，这一现象被称为极地放大，已得到广泛研究。

The polar regions are projected to warm faster than the global average, known as polar amplification, which have been extensively investigated.

预估还显示高海拔地区同样存在增暖放大，尤其是在青藏高原。

Warming amplification is also projected at high altitudes, particularly over the Tibetan Plateau.

然而，人们对青藏高原增暖放大对更广泛气候系统（包括极地海冰）的影响知之甚少。

However, little is known about the influence of Tibetan Plateau amplification on the wider climate systems, including polar sea-ice.

本文利用数值模拟表明，在高排放情景的预估中，青藏高原增暖放大分别贡献了约 20–30% 的北极海冰损失和约 10–15% 的南极海冰损失。

Here, using numerical simulations, we show Tibetan Plateau amplification accounts for ~20-30% and ~10-15% of the Arctic and Antarctic sea-ice loss, respectively, projected under a high-emission scenario.

值得注意的是，青藏高原增暖放大对预估海冰损失的相对贡献随全球增暖水平的提高而增大。

Notably, the relative contribution of Tibetan Plateau amplification to projected sea-ice loss increases with the level of global warming.

对北极而言，青藏高原增暖放大主要通过增强向北的大气热量输送来驱动海冰损失。

For the Arctic, Tibetan Plateau amplification primarily drives sea-ice loss by enhancing northward atmospheric heat transport.

对南极而言，本文提出了一条两阶段的海气耦合路径：增强的向南海洋热量输送使南半球中低纬海表温度升高，进而改变大尺度大气环流，有利于南极增暖和海冰损失。

For the Antarctic, a two-stage ocean-atmosphere-coupled pathway is proposed: the enhanced southward oceanic heat transport warms the southern low-to-mid-latitude sea-surface temperatures, which subsequently modify the large-scale atmospheric circulation, favorable for Antarctic warming and sea-ice loss.

引言

Introduction

工业革命以来，地球经历了快速而持续的增暖趋势，目前预估将达到——并很快超过——《巴黎协定》设定的 1.5 °C 阈值[1,2,3]。

Since the Industrial Revolution, Earth has experienced a rapid and sustained warming trend, and is now projected to reach—and soon exceed—the 1.5 °C threshold set by the Paris Agreement[1,2,3].

预估的增暖在空间上并不均匀，在两极和高海拔地区（尤其是青藏高原，TP）最为显著，这一现象通常被称为增暖放大[4,5,6]。

The projected warming is not spatially uniform, most pronounced at the poles and in high-altitude regions, particularly the Tibetan Plateau (TP), a phenomenon commonly referred to as warming amplification[4,5,6].

过去几十年间，预估的北极和南极放大（尤其是北极）的成因与后果已得到广泛探讨[7,8]，而青藏高原增暖放大（TPA）潜在的全球影响却远未受到同等关注。

While the causes and consequences of the projected Arctic and Antarctic amplifications—especially in the Arctic—have been extensively explored over the past few decades[7,8], the potential global influence of the Tibetan Plateau amplification (TPA) has received far less attention.

青藏高原通常被称为地球“第三极”，通过动力和热力两方面过程在塑造全球天气与气候中扮演关键角色。

Commonly known as the Earth’s Third Pole, the TP plays a pivotal role in shaping global weather and climate through both mechanical and thermal processes.

在动力方面，青藏高原主要在冬季影响气候：它将中纬度西风急流沿其南北两侧分为两支，从而对下游天气和气候系统产生强烈影响[9,10,11]。

Mechanically, the TP influences climate primarily in winter by splitting the mid-latitude westerly jet into two branches along its northern and southern flanks, thereby exerting a strong impact on downstream weather and climate systems[9,10,11].

其地形特征还调制着全球海洋环流，包括全球经向翻转环流以及厄尔尼诺–南方涛动的变率[12,13,14]。

Its orographic features also modulate global ocean circulation, including the global meridional overturning circulation and the variability of the El Niño–Southern Oscillation[12,13,14].

在热力方面，青藏高原通过其感热驱动的“气泵”效应对气候——特别是亚洲夏季风——施加强烈影响[15,16,17]。

Thermally, the TP exerts a strong influence on climate—particularly the Asian summer monsoon—via its sensible-heat-driven air-pump effect[15,16,17].

近几十年来，青藏高原的增暖速率快于全球平均，尽管仍弱于北极观测到的增暖[5,18]。

In recent decades, the TP has experienced a faster warming rate than the global average, although still weaker than that observed over the Arctic[5,18].

近期研究揭示了 TPA 对东亚降水型[19]以及西北太平洋热带气旋活动[20]的影响。

Recent studies have revealed the influence of the TPA on the East Asian precipitation patterns[19] and tropical cyclone activity in the western North Pacific[20].

重要的是，TPA 的气候影响并不局限于下游的亚洲–太平洋地区，甚至延伸到两极。

Importantly, the climatic influence of the TPA is not confined to the downstream Asian-Pacific region and even extends to the two poles.

尤其是，最新文献揭示了 TPA 与北极放大之间的大气共振[21,22]。

Particularly, the latest literature unveiled the atmospheric resonance between the TPA and Arctic amplification[21,22].

尽管如此，不同排放情景下 TPA 对极地海冰退缩的贡献程度在很大程度上仍未被探索。

Nonetheless, to what extent the TPA under different emission scenarios contributes to polar sea-ice retreat remains largely unexplored.

此外，与北极海冰相比，TPA 对南极海冰的作用方式尚不如前者清楚。

Moreover, compared with its Arctic counterpart, how the TPA influences the Antarctic sea ice is less clear.

回答这些问题对于加深我们对观测和气候模式预估中海冰快速减少的理解至关重要[23,24]。

Answering these questions is critical to improve our understanding of the rapid sea-ice decline both in observations and climate model projections[23,24].

本研究利用一套气候模式模拟，分离并量化了 TPA 对北极和南极海冰退缩的贡献，并揭示了其背后的动力机制。

Using a suite of climate model simulations, this study isolates and quantifies the contribution of the TPA to the sea-ice retreat over the Arctic and Antarctic and unravels the underlying dynamical mechanisms.

我们的发现为理解全球增暖背景下青藏高原与另外两极之间的气候联系提供了有价值的见解，并有助于更好地认识气候引起的极地环境变化所带来的潜在风险[25,26,27]。

Our findings offer valuable insights into the climatic linkages between the TP and the other two poles under global warming, and contribute to a better understanding of the potential risks associated with climate-induced changes in polar environments[25,26,27].

结果

Results

TPA 对北极和南极海冰退缩的贡献有多大？

How much the TPA contributes to Arctic and Antarctic sea-ice retreat?

我们利用通用地球系统模式（CESM；见“方法”）进行了一对包含全部强迫的全耦合现代与未来瞬变模拟，以及一对时间片模拟，后者分别将青藏高原上空的大气（图 1 中绿线勾勒的区域）松弛逼近到其现代和未来状态。

A pair of fully-coupled present-day and future transient simulations, including all forcings, and a pair of time-slice simulations with the atmosphere over the TP (outlined by green line in Fig. 1) nudged towards its present-day and future states, respectively, are performed using the Community Earth System Model (CESM; see “Methods”).

基于这些模拟，我们比较了典型浓度路径 8.5（RCP8.5）下气候对全球增暖的响应与仅对 TPA 的响应（图 1）。

Based on these simulations, the climate responses to global warming under Representative Concentration Pathways 8.5 (RCP8.5) and in response to TPA alone are compared with each other (Fig. 1).

尽管幅度弱得多，TPA 同样引起全球尺度的增暖，在北半球尤为明显，其 SST 响应型与全球增暖的总响应相似（图 1a、b、d、e）。

Despite much weaker in magnitude, the TPA also induces a global-scale warming, particularly in the Northern Hemisphere, with the SST responses displaying a similar pattern as in the total response to global warming (Fig. 1a, b, d, e).

本研究采用四个海冰指标，即海冰密集度（SIC）、海冰厚度（SIT）、海冰范围（SIE）和海冰体积（SIV），其中前两者刻画海冰的空间分布，后两者估算两极海冰的总量（见方法）。

In this study, four sea-ice metrics are applied in this study, i.e., sea-ice concentration (SIC), sea-ice thickness (SIT), sea-ice extent (SIE), and sea-ice volume (SIV), with the first two depicting the spatial distribution of sea-ice, while the last two estimating the total amount of sea-ice over the two poles (Methods).

TPA 引起的两极非对称增暖导致泛北极地区和南极半岛周边的 SIC 减少（图 1c、f）。

The TPA-induced asymmetric warming over the two poles causes SIC reduction over the pan-Arctic and around the Antarctic Peninsula (Fig. 1c, f).

图 2 更细致地展示了 TPA 引起的海冰损失。

Figure 2 gives a closer look at the TPA-induced sea-ice loss.

我们另外进行了一对部分耦合模拟，用单层平板海洋模式（SOM；见“方法”）替代完整的海洋模式。

Another pair of partially-coupled simulations was performed, with the full ocean model replaced by a single-layer slab ocean model (SOM; see “Methods”).

由于该模式中海洋被视为静止，仅考虑海气界面上的能量交换，这使我们能够检验海洋动力过程在对 TPA 响应中的重要性。

Since in this model the ocean is treated as motionless, only energy exchange upon the ocean-atmosphere interface is considered, which allows us to examine the importance of ocean dynamics in the response to TPA.

注意，为使图形清晰，此处采用不同的约定来标注统计显著的结果（黑色打点），而在图 1 中交叉阴影线用于标注不显著的结果。

Note here that a different convention is used to denote statistically significant results (black stippling), while in Fig. 1, cross-hatching is used to denote non-significant results, for clarity of the figure.

此标注方案适用于其余各图。

This marking scheme is applied to the remaining figures.

TPA 引起的北极海冰密集度（SIC）减少在巴伦支–喀拉海、东西伯利亚海和楚科奇海最为显著（图 2a），与全球增暖相关的型态相似——尽管幅度较弱（补充图 1a）。

The TPA-induced reduction in Arctic sea-ice concentration (SIC) is most pronounced in the Barents-Kara Seas, East Siberian Sea and Chukchi Sea (Fig. 2a), resembling—although with a weaker amplitude—that associated with global warming (Supplementary Fig. 1a).

SOM 模拟中北极 SIC 下降的幅度与全耦合模拟相当（图 2b）。

The magnitude of Arctic SIC decline in the SOM simulations is comparable to that in the fully-coupled simulations (Fig. 2b).

在 RCP8.5 下，TPA 贡献了约 26.7% 的北极海冰范围（SIE）总损失；排除海洋动力过程后，该比例降至约 19.9%（图 3a）。

The TPA accounts for approximately 26.7% of the total Arctic sea-ice extent (SIE) loss under RCP8.5, which decreases to about 19.9% when ocean dynamics is excluded (Fig. 3a).

这提示，海洋环流对 TPA 的调整在 TPA 引起的北极 SIC 减少中作用有限。

This suggests that ocean circulation adjustment in response to the TPA plays a limited role in the TPA-induced Arctic SIC reduction.

TPA 引起的北极海冰厚度（SIT）减少的空间分布（图 2e、f）也与全球增暖所致的空间分布相似（补充图 1c）。

The spatial distribution of Arctic sea-ice thickness (SIT) reduction induced by the TPA (Fig. 2e, f) also mirrors that from global warming (Supplementary Fig. 1c).

然而，SOM 模拟中 SIT 的减少明显小于全耦合模拟。

However, the SIT reduction in the SOM simulations is obviously smaller than in the fully-coupled ones.

TPA 对北极海冰体积（SIV）损失的贡献从全耦合模拟的 32.5% 降至 SOM 模拟的 15.9%（图 3c），这可能反映出与 SIE 相比，SIV 对海洋动力变化更为敏感[28,29]。

The contribution of the TPA to Arctic sea-ice volume (SIV) loss drops from 32.5% in the fully-coupled simulations to 15.9% in the SOM simulations (Fig. 3c), likely reflecting the higher sensitivity of SIV, compared with SIE, to changes in ocean dynamics[28,29].

全球增暖引起的南极 SIC 减少幅度总体上弱于北极（补充图 1b）。

The magnitude of Antarctic SIC reduction due to global warming is generally weaker than that in the Arctic (Supplementary Fig. 1b).

全耦合模拟中 TPA 引起的南极 SIC 下降在别林斯高晋海和威德尔海达到最大（图 2c）。

Antarctic SIC decline induced by the TPA in the fully-coupled simulations maximizes over the Bellingshausen Sea and Weddell Sea (Fig. 2c).

相比之下，SOM 模拟中 SIC 下降仅出现在威德尔海，且幅度小于全耦合试验（图 2d）。

In contrast, in the SOM simulations SIC decline is only found over the Weddell Sea, and with a lesser magnitude as in the fully-coupled runs (Fig. 2d).

TPA 对南极 SIE 减少的相对贡献从全耦合模拟的 15.1% 大幅降至 SOM 模拟的约 3.3%（图 3b）。

The relative contribution of the TPA to Antarctic SIE reduction drops substantially from 15.1% in the fully-coupled simulations to approximately 3.3% in the SOM simulations (Fig. 3b).

这提示，海洋环流对 TPA 的调整在 TPA 引起的南极海冰损失中起主导作用。

This suggests that ocean circulation adjustment in response to the TPA plays a dominant role in the TPA-induced Antarctic sea-ice loss.

南极 SIT 的减少也呈现类似的型态（图 2g、h，图 3d）。

A similar pattern is observed for Antarctic SIT reduction (Figs. 2g, h, 3d).

此外，值得注意的是，TPA 对 SIE 和 SIV 损失的相对贡献均随全球增暖水平的提高而增大（图 3 中红线）。

Furthermore, it is noteworthy that the relative contribution of the TPA to both SIE and SIV loss increases with the level of global warming (red lines in Fig. 3).

具体而言，TPA 对北极 SIE 和 SIV 损失的相对贡献从 RCP2.6 和 RCP4.5 情景下的约 20% 增至 RCP8.5 情景下的约 30%，对南极则从约 10% 增至约 15%。

Specifically, the relative contribution of TPA to Arctic SIE and SIV loss increases from ~20% under RCP2.6 and RCP4.5 scenarios to ~30% under RCP8.5 scenarios, and from ~10% to ~15% for its Antarctic counterpart.

这可能部分归因于更暖气候中更强的 TPA 信号：TPA 指数——定义为青藏高原地表增暖（在图 1 绿线勾勒区域内作面积加权）与全球平均地表增暖之比——从 RCP2.6 情景下的 1.11，增至 RCP4.5 情景下的 1.33 和 RCP8.5 情景下的 1.38（补充图 2）。

This could be partially attributed to a stronger TPA signal in a warmer climate: the TPA index—defined as the ratio of the TP surface warming (area-weighted over the region outlined by the green line in Fig. 1) to global-mean surface warming—increases from 1.11 to 1.33 and 1.38 under RCP2.6, RCP4.5, and RCP8.5 scenarios, respectively (Supplementary Fig. 2).

TPA 如何引起北极和南极海冰退缩？

How the TPA induces Arctic and Antarctic sea-ice retreat?

向极热量输送的变化在极地增暖和海冰退缩中起着关键作用[30,31]。

Changes in poleward heat transport play a crucial role in polar warming and sea-ice retreat[30,31].

为评估 TPA 对经向热量输送的影响，我们首先注意到海冰损失本身可以反馈作用于热量输送型态[32,33]。

To assess the influence of the TPA on meridional heat transport, we first note that sea-ice loss itself can feedback on heat transport patterns[32,33].

为消除这一混淆效应，我们进行了另一对青藏高原松弛逼近的全耦合 CESM 模拟，其中海冰也被松弛逼近到其历史气候态（青藏高原+海冰松弛逼近的全耦合试验；见“方法”）。

To remove this confounding effect, we conducted another pair of TP-nudged fully-coupled CESM simulations, in which sea-ice was also nudged towards its historical climatology (TP+ sea-ice-nudged fully-coupled runs; see “Methods”).

此外，还进行了一对青藏高原松弛逼近的仅大气时间片模拟，其 SST/SIC 条件固定在 2000 年水平，用以检验在无地表边界变化的情况下 TPA 对大气热量输送（AHT）的影响。

Moreover, a pair of TP-nudged atmosphere-only time-slice simulations, with SST/SIC conditions fixed at the year 2000 level, is performed to check the TPA’s influence on atmospheric heat transport (AHT) in the absence of surface boundary changes.

TPA 增强了进入北极的总向极热量输送（THT），对南极的增强程度较小（图 4a）。

The TPA enhances the total poleward heat transport (THT) into the Arctic and, to a lesser extent, into the Antarctic (Fig. 4a).

尽管 TPA 也增加了 60°N 附近的向极海洋热量输送（OHT），但其幅度远小于 AHT，也弱于仅大气模拟中的 AHT 响应。

Although the TPA also increases poleward oceanic heat transport (OHT) near 60°N, its magnitude is considerably smaller than that of AHT, and it is also weaker than the AHT response in the atmosphere-only simulations.

这支持了前文的结论，即海洋动力过程在 TPA 引起的北极海冰退缩中作用甚微。

This supports the earlier conclusion that ocean dynamics plays a minimal role in Arctic sea-ice retreat induced by the TPA.

有趣的是，虽然在南半球中低纬 TPA 引起的向南 OHT 增强超过了 AHT，但在 60°S 附近却被 AHT 反超。

Interestingly, while the TPA-induced enhancement of southward OHT exceeds that of AHT in the southern low-to-mid latitudes, it is surpassed by AHT near 60°S.

这提示，尽管海洋环流对 TPA 的调整很重要，但它们可能并不直接驱动南极海冰损失。

This suggests that, although ocean circulation adjustments to the TPA are important, they may not directly drive Antarctic sea-ice loss.

这一假设将在下一节进一步探讨。

This hypothesis will be further explored in the following section.

为进一步阐明经向热量输送响应背后的机制，我们将 AHT 响应进一步分解为四个分量：平均干静力能（MDSE）、平均潜热能（MLE）、涡动干静力能（EDSE）和涡动潜热能（ELE）（图 4b、d）。

To further elucidate the mechanisms behind the meridional heat transport response, the AHT responses are further decomposed into four components: mean dry static energy (MDSE), mean latent energy (MLE), eddy dry static energy (EDSE), and eddy latent energy (ELE) (Fig. 4b, d).

EDSE 主导了进入北极的向北 AHT，而代表水汽输送的 MLE 和 ELE 也有适度贡献。

The EDSE dominates the northward AHT into the Arctic, while the MLE and ELE—representing moisture transport—also modestly contribute.

相比之下，MDSE 相对于其他分量表现为负贡献。

In contrast, the MDSE exhibits a negative contribution relative to the other components.

这提示，TPA 主要通过加强向极传播的涡旋来增强干静力能和水汽的向北输送，最终促成北极增暖和海冰损失。

This suggests that the TPA enhances the northward transport of both dry static energy and water vapor primarily through strengthening poleward-propagating eddies, ultimately contributing to Arctic warming and sea-ice loss.

对于 OHT，响应按大洋盆地分解——即大西洋、太平洋和印度洋（图 4c）。

For OHT, the responses are decomposed by ocean basins—namely, the Atlantic, Pacific, and Indian Oceans (Fig. 4c).

进入北极的 OHT 增强主要归因于大西洋。

The enhanced OHT into the Arctic is primarily attributed to the Atlantic Ocean.

相比之下，太平洋主导了南半球中低纬向南 OHT 的增加，而印度洋的贡献极小。

In contrast, the Pacific Ocean dominates the increase in southward OHT across the southern low-to-mid latitudes, while the Indian Ocean contributes minimally.

接下来，我们探究 TPA 引起北极和南极海冰退缩的机制。

Next, we investigate the mechanisms through which the TPA induces sea-ice retreat in the Arctic and Antarctic.

鉴于极地气候对青藏高原增暖的响应存在已知的季节性[34]，我们首先考察 TPA 引起的两极地表增暖的季节循环（补充图 3）。

Given the known seasonality in the polar climate responses to the TP warming[34], we first examine the seasonal cycles of the TPA-induced surface warming over both poles (Supplementary Fig. 3).

TPA 引起的北极地表增暖在北半球秋季（9–10–11 月）而非北半球冬季（12–1–2 月）达到最大（补充图 3a）。

The TPA-induced Arctic surface warming maximizes in boreal autumn (September-October-November), rather than in boreal winter (December-January-February) (Supplementary Fig. 3a).

这可能部分归因于冬季北半球平流层极涡（SPV）的加强，后者与向上传播的行星波减少有关（补充图 4a、b）。

This might be partly attributed to a strengthening of the northern stratospheric polar vortex (SPV) during winter, associated with a reduction in upward-propagating planetary waves (Supplementary Fig. 4a, b).

SPV 异常向下延伸引起整个对流层北半球西风急流加速（补充图 4c、d），从而抵消极地增暖[35]。

Downward extension of the SPV anomaly induces an acceleration of the northern westerly jet throughout the whole troposphere (Supplementary Fig. 4c, d), which counteracts polar warming[35].

相比之下，在海冰自由演变和海冰松弛逼近的全耦合模拟中，TPA 引起的南极地表增暖均在南半球冬季（6–7–8 月）达到最大。

In contrast, the TPA-induced Antarctic surface warming maximizes during austral winter (June-July-August) in both the sea-ice-free-running and sea-ice-nudged fully-coupled simulations.

然而，仅大气模拟在南极几乎没有增暖信号（补充图 3b），这与前文关于海洋动力过程在 TPA 引起的南极海冰损失中起主导作用的发现一致。

However, the atmosphere-only simulations exhibit little to no warming signal over the Antarctic (Supplementary Fig. 3b), consistent with the earlier finding that ocean dynamics play a dominant role in Antarctic sea-ice loss induced by the TPA.

海冰响应的季节分解（补充表 1）总体上与地表增暖信号一致。

Seasonal breakdown of sea-ice responses (Supplementary Tab. 1) is overall consistent with the surface warming signal.

尽管如此，为使两极的量化分析可比且直观，上述定量结果基于年化指标，而下文的机制诊断将分别聚焦于北极的北半球秋季和南极的南半球冬季。

Nonetheless, to make the quantification analysis for the two poles comparable and intuitive, the quantitative results above are based on annualized metrics, while the following mechanism diagnosis will focus on boreal autumn for the Arctic and austral winter for the Antarctic, respectively.

强 TPA 增强了北半球中高纬的经向温度梯度，促使西风急流向极偏移（图 5a、d）。

Strong TPA enhances the meridional temperature gradient across the northern mid-to-high latitudes, prompting a poleward shift of the westerly jet stream (Fig. 5a, d).

根据线性波动理论，向极传播的涡旋受其临界线——即纬向平均风等于涡旋相速的区域——的约束[36,37]。

According to linear wave theory, poleward-propagating eddies are constrained by their critical lines—regions where the zonal-mean wind equals the eddy phase speed[36,37].

随着西风急流北移，以风暴路径为特征的相关涡旋活动也向更高纬度移动（图 5b、e）。

As the westerly jet shifts northward, the associated eddy activity, characterized by storm tracks, is also displaced towards higher latitudes (Fig. 5b, e).

这支持了前文的发现，即 EDSE 输送在进入北极的向北 AHT 中起主导作用（图 4b、d）。

This supports the earlier finding that the EDSE transport plays a dominant role in the northward AHT into the Arctic (Fig. 4b, d).

加强的向极传播涡旋增强了干静力能和水汽向北极的输送，从而促进北极地表增暖（图 5c、f）并促成海冰损失。

The intensified poleward-propagating eddies enhance the transport of both dry static energy and water vapor into the Arctic, thereby prompting the Arctic surface warming (Fig. 5c, f) and contributing to sea-ice loss.

仅大气模拟得到的北极增暖型与青藏高原+海冰松弛逼近的全耦合模拟一致，尽管幅度和空间范围较小。

The Arctic warming pattern derived from the atmosphere-only simulations is consistent with that from the TP+sea-ice-nudged fully-coupled simulations, although the magnitude and spatial extent are smaller.

基于 CESM 和一个理想化模式的模拟均表明，TPA 激发出一列向东北传播的 Rossby 波列，在西北太平洋和白令海上空分别形成反气旋和气旋中心（补充图 5）。

Simulations based on both CESM and an idealized model show that the TPA stimulates a northeastward propagating Rossby wave train, with an anti-cyclonic and cyclonic center over the Northwest Pacific and Bering Sea, respectively (Supplementary Fig. 5).

这一环流型促进了温度和水汽从日本海经白令海峡向东北平流进入北极（补充图 6）。

This circulation pattern promotes the northeastward temperature and moisture advection from Sea of Japan, through Bering Strait, into the Arctic (Supplementary Fig. 6).

此外，TPA 引起的极地以外 SST 异常（图 6a）也可能对北极增暖有所贡献[38,39]。

Besides, the extra-polar SST anomalies induced by the TPA (Fig. 6a) may also contribute to Arctic warming[38,39].

TPA 对全球 SST 型的影响及其对北极地表温度的后续效应在补充讨论中进一步论述。

The influence of the TPA on the global SST pattern and the subsequent effect on Arctic surface temperature is further discussed in Supplementary Discussions.

最后，我们考察 TPA 促成南极海冰损失的机制。

Finally, we examined the mechanisms through which the TPA contributes to Antarctic sea-ice loss.

根据图 4，我们提出一个两阶段机制假设：首先，TPA 通过增强向南的海洋热量输送使南半球中低纬 SST 升温；随后，南半球中低纬 SST 增暖驱动大尺度大气环流异常，最终引起南极增暖和海冰损失。

According to Fig. 4, a two-stage mechanism is hypothesized: first, the TPA warms the southern low-to-mid-latitude SSTs through enhancing southward oceanic heat transport; then, the southern low-to-mid-latitude SST warming drives large-scale atmospheric circulation anomalies, which ultimately induce Antarctic warming and sea-ice loss.

我们首先讨论 TPA 对南半球中低纬 SST 的增暖作用（图 6a）。

We begin by addressing the warming of southern low-to-mid-latitude SST by the TPA (Fig. 6a).

TPA 在副热带–热带上层海洋（1000 m 以浅）触发逆时针流函数异常，表明副热带环流圈（STC）北支减速、南支加速（补充图 7a）。

The TPA triggers counterclockwise streamfunction anomalies in the upper subtropical-tropical ocean (above 1000 m), indicating a deceleration of the northern and an acceleration of the southern branches of the subtropical cell (STC) (Supplementary Fig. 7a).

进一步分解表明，这一响应主要由太平洋的变化驱动（补充图 7c）。

Further decomposition reveals that this response is primarily driven by changes in the Pacific Ocean (Supplementary Fig. 7c).

太平洋 STC 南支的加速促进了热带暖水的向南输送，这与图 4c 中太平洋在向南 OHT 中的主导作用一致。

The accelerated southern branch of the Pacific STC promotes the southward transport of warm tropical waters, aligning with the dominant role of the Pacific Ocean in the southward OHT seen in Fig. 4c.

此外，大西洋经向翻转环流对 TPA 呈现偶极型响应：在 1000 m 以深减弱，而在该深度以浅加强（补充图 7b）。

Additionally, the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation exhibits a dipole response to the TPA: it weakens below 1000 m but strengthens above this depth (Supplementary Fig. 7b).

TPA 还在热带太平洋引起西风异常，进而在南半球副热带太平洋产生气旋式环流异常（补充图 8a、b）。

The TPA also induces a westerly anomaly over the tropical Pacific, which generates a cyclonic circulation anomaly over the southern subtropical Pacific (Supplementary Fig. 8a, b).

该气旋式异常导致局地正的地表风旋度异常（∇ × [τ/(ρf)]，其中 τ、ρ 和 f 分别表示风应力矢量、海水密度和科里奥利参数 f = 2Ω sin φ，Ω 和 φ 分别表示地球自转角速度和纬度；补充图 8c）。

This cyclonic anomaly leads to the local positive surface wind curl anomalies (∇ × [τ/(ρf)], where τ, ρ and f denote wind stress vector, sea water density and the Coriolis parameter f = 2Ω sin φ, with Ω and φ denoting the angular velocity of Earth’s rotation and latitude, respectively; Supplementary Fig. 8c).

根据地表风应力旋度与埃克曼输送散度之间的关系（∇ · UE = k · ∇ × [τ/(ρf)]，其中 UE 和 k 分别表示埃克曼输送和垂直方向单位矢量）[40]，正的地表风旋度对应局地埃克曼上升流，即水柱的拉伸。

According to the relationship between surface wind stress curl and Ekman transport divergence (∇ · UE = k · ∇ × [τ/(ρf)], where UE and k denote Ekman transport and unit vector in vertical direction)[40], positive surface wind curl corresponds to local Ekman upwelling, that is, stretching of water column.

根据位涡守恒定理（f/D = 常数，假设相对涡度可忽略，其中 D 表示深度）[40]，在南半球，水柱的拉伸可导致向南的 Sverdrup 输送。

Based on the theorem of potential vorticity conservation (f/D = constant, assuming negligible relative vorticity, where D denotes depth)[40], in the Southern Hemisphere, the stretching of the water column could lead to the southward Sverdrup transport.

这与补充图 7c 中发现的太平洋 STC 南支加强相一致，后者促进热带暖水向南输送到南半球中低纬，可能对南极增暖和海冰损失有所贡献。

This is consistent with the strengthening southern branch of the Pacific STC found in Supplementary Fig. 7c, which facilitates the southward transport of warm tropical waters into the southern low-to-mid latitudes, potentially contributing to Antarctic warming and sea-ice loss.

接下来，我们考虑南半球中低纬 SST 异常如何导致南极增暖。

Next, we consider how the southern low-to-mid-latitude SST anomalies lead to Antarctic warming.

海洋热收支分析（见补充方法）表明，在 TPA 引起的增暖最大的南极半岛周边（图 6a–c），SST 增暖的主要贡献者是由地表净辐射驱动的热力过程（补充图 9b），而非水平平流等海洋动力过程（补充图 9a）。

Ocean heat budget analysis (see Supplementary Methods) shows that the dominant contributor to SST warming around the Antarctic Peninsula, where the TPA-induced warming maximizes (Fig. 6a–c), is the thermodynamic process (Supplementary Fig. 9b) driven by surface net radiation, rather than ocean dynamical processes such as horizontal advection (Supplementary Fig. 9a).

更细致的分解表明，地表向上湍流热通量（即潜热和感热损失）的减少是局地 SST 增暖的主要驱动因素（补充图 9c–e）。

A more detailed decomposition shows that the reduction in surface upward turbulent heat fluxes (i.e., latent and sensible heat loss) is the primary driver of local SST warming (Supplementary Fig. 9c–e).

这意味着 TPA 引起的 SST 异常促进了向南的大气暖平流，后者通过抑制向上湍流热通量使地表增暖[41,42]。

This implies that the TPA-induced SST anomalies promote southward atmospheric warm advection, which in turn warms the surface by suppressing upward turbulent heat fluxes[41,42].

我们另外进行了两组仅大气模拟，分别由 TPA 引起的全球（图 6a）和南半球中低纬（补充图 10a）SST 异常驱动。

Two additional atmosphere-only simulations driven by the TPA-induced global (Fig. 6a) and southern low-to-mid-latitude SST anomalies (Supplementary Fig. 10a), respectively, were conducted.

由全球 SST 异常强迫的仅大气模拟很好地再现了南极半岛周边的增暖（图 6d），而这主要归因于南半球中低纬的 SST 异常（图 6e）。

The atmosphere-only simulations forced by global SST anomalies well reproduce the warming around the Antarctic Peninsula (Fig. 6d), which is primarily attributable to the southern low-to-mid-latitude SST anomalies (Fig. 6e).

所有这些模拟都捕捉到相似的大气环流型，即威德尔海上空的高压中心和别林斯高晋海上空的低压中心（图 6b–e）。

All these simulations capture a similar atmospheric circulation pattern, namely, a high-pressure center over the Weddell Sea and a low-pressure center over the Bellingshausen Sea (Fig. 6b–e).

由此在两个中心之间产生的向南暖平流促进了局地地表增暖和海冰损失。

The resulting southward warm air advection between these centers promotes local surface warming and sea-ice loss.

50°S–20°N 的 SST 强迫被进一步分解为 6 个子区域（补充图 10d–i），并据此进行了 6 个仅大气试验（补充表 S2）。

The SST forcing over 50°S-20°N is further decomposed into 6 subdomains (Supplementary Fig. 10d–i), driven by which 6 atmosphere-only experiments are performed (Supplementary Tab. S2).

与已有文献[34,43,44]一致，热带印度洋（类似印度洋偶极子负位相的型态；补充图 10e）和热带大西洋（补充图 10d）的 SST 异常引起的环流和地表增暖型，与 50°S–20°N SST 强迫驱动的结果相似（补充图 11a、b）。

Consistent with previous literature[34,43,44], the SST anomalies over the tropical Indian (negative Indian Ocean Dipole-like pattern; Supplementary Fig. 10e) and Atlantic Ocean (Supplementary Fig. 10d) induce a similar circulation and surface warming pattern to those driven by the SST forcing over 50°S-20°N (Supplementary Fig. 11a, b).

然而，类厄尔尼诺的 SST 型态（补充图 10f）导致一个高压中心控制南极西部，不利于暖空气向南平流至南极半岛（补充图 11c）。

However, the El Niño-like SST pattern (Supplementary Fig. 10f) leads to a high-pressure center dominating the western part of the Antarctic, unfavorable for the southward warm air advection towards the Antarctic Peninsula (Supplementary Fig. 11c).

此外，南大西洋（补充图 10g）和南太平洋（补充图 10i）的 SST 异常也可能对南极增暖有所贡献（补充图 11d、f），而南印度洋的 SST 异常（补充图 10h）贡献极小（补充图 11e）。

In addition, the SST anomalies over the South Atlantic (Supplementary Fig. 10g) and South Pacific (Supplementary Fig. 10i) could also contribute to the Antarctic warming (Supplementary Fig. 11d, f), while the SST anomaly over South Indian Ocean (Supplementary Fig. 10h) makes a minimal contribution (Supplementary Fig. 11e).

讨论

Discussion

本研究定量评估了 TPA 对北极和南极海冰退缩的贡献。

This study quantitatively assesses the contribution of the TPA to Arctic and Antarctic sea-ice retreat.

我们的结果表明，TPA 强烈加速极地海冰退缩，其速率取决于全球增暖的水平。

Our results indicate that TPA strongly accelerates polar sea-ice retreat at a rate dependent upon the level of global warming.

具体而言，TPA 对北极海冰损失的相对贡献从低排放情景下的约 20% 增至高排放情景下的约 30%，对南极则从约 10% 增至约 15%。

Specifically, the relative contribution of TPA to Arctic sea-ice loss increases from ~20% under low-emission scenarios to ~30% under high-emission scenarios, and from ~10% to ~15% for its Antarctic counterpart.

在北半球，TPA 增强了北半球中高纬的经向温度梯度，从而加强向极传播的涡旋。

In the Northern Hemisphere, the TPA enhances the meridional temperature gradient over the northern mid-to-high latitudes, strengthening the poleward-propagating eddies.

这些涡旋将更多热量和水汽输送进北极，促进地表增暖和海冰减少。

These eddies transport additional heat and moisture into the Arctic, promoting surface warming and sea-ice decline.

在南半球，TPA 主要通过一条两阶段的海气耦合路径引起南极海冰损失：首先，TPA 增强向南的 OHT 并使南半球中低纬 SST 增暖；随后，南半球中低纬 SST 异常引起大尺度大气环流异常，最终导致南极增暖和海冰损失。

In the Southern Hemisphere, the TPA induces Antarctic sea-ice loss mainly through a two-stage ocean-atmosphere coupled pathway: first, the TPA enhances southward OHT and warms the southern low-to-mid-latitude SSTs; then, the southern low-to-mid-latitude SST anomalies induce large-scale atmospheric circulation anomalies, which ultimately lead to Antarctic warming and sea-ice loss.

图 7 简要展示了上述机制。

Figure 7 briefly illustrates the above-mentioned mechanisms.

鉴于其严重的气候和社会影响，观测和预估的极地海冰退缩的归因在近几十年已成为热点课题。

Given its severe climate and social impacts, attribution of the observed and projected polar sea-ice retreat has become a hot topic in recent decades.

已有文献揭示，除局地反馈（反照率反馈、温度直减率反馈）[45,46]外，极地以外的强迫也对极地海冰退缩有所贡献。

Previous literature revealed that, except for local feedbacks (albedo feedback, lapse-rate feedback)[45,46], extra-polar forcings also contribute to polar sea-ice retreat.

特别是，研究发现热带 SST 变化是极地海冰变化的关键驱动因素[47,48,49,50]。

In particular, tropical SST changes are found to be key drivers of polar sea-ice changes[47,48,49,50].

本研究提出的部分机制建立在这些发现的基础上，我们进一步将这一因果链延伸至 TPA。

Part of the mechanisms proposed in this study are built upon their findings, and we further extend the causal linkage to include the TPA.

最重要的是，我们量化了 TPA 对极地海冰退缩的相对贡献，结果表明其不可忽略。

Most importantly, we quantified the relative contribution of the TPA to the polar sea-ice retreat, which turns out to be non-negligible.

此外，我们的发现有助于加深对增暖世界中正在显现的“三极耦合”潜在成因的理解[51]。

Moreover, our findings could help deepen our understanding of the potential causes of the emerging three-pole coupling in a warming world[51].

另外，TPA 是否也像本研究所示那样，对近几十年北极海冰的急剧退缩以及南极海冰从稳定转为快速减少的令人警觉的状态转变有所贡献，值得未来更深入的研究。

In addition, whether the TPA also contributes to the drastic Arctic sea-ice retreat and the alarming regime shift from stable to rapidly declining Antarctic sea-ice in recent decades, similar to this study, or not, deserves a deeper investigation in the future.

通过使用大气和海冰松弛逼近技术，本研究主要关注 TPA 对极地海冰的单向影响。

By using the atmosphere and sea-ice nudging techniques, this study mainly focuses on the one-way impact of the TPA on polar sea-ice.

然而，近来越来越多的文献表明高原与极地之间存在双向遥相关[51,52,53,54]。

However, recently, a growing body of literature indicates a two-way tele-connection between the plateau and polar regions[51,52,53,54].

我们推测这种双向遥相关可能进一步加剧高原增暖和极地海冰损失，这值得未来进一步研究。

We hypothesize that this two-way tele-connection could further boost the plateau warming and polar sea-ice loss, which warrants further investigation in the future.

本研究的一个关键潜在局限在于此处使用的松弛逼近方法，对其结果应持一定程度的谨慎。

A key potential caveat of this study concerns the nudging methods used here, which should be interpreted with a degree of caution.

有观点认为，松弛逼近会向模式引入人为热量，这可能导致源于这种人为加热而非青藏高原增暖或海冰损失的虚假信号[55,56]。

It has been argued that nudging introduces artificial heat into the model, which might lead to spurious signals, stemming from this artificial heating, rather than the TP warming or sea-ice loss[55,56].

然而，这种虚假效应难以量化和/或减小，需要新的模拟方法。

However, this spurious effect is challenging to quantify and/or reduce, requiring new modeling approaches.

方法

Methods

模式描述

Model description

本研究的模拟使用 CESM（1.2.0 版）进行[57]。

The simulations in this study were conducted using the CESM (version 1.2.0)[57].

CESM 由若干分量模式组成：大气分量为通用气候模式 5（CAM5），海冰分量为通用海冰模式 4（CICE4），海洋分量为并行海洋程序 2（POP2）。

CESM consists of several component models: the Community Climate Model 5 (CAM5) for atmosphere, the Community Ice CodE 4 (CICE4) for sea-ice, and the Parallel Ocean Program 2 (POP2) for ocean.

耦合了大气、海洋和海冰分量的模拟称为全耦合试验。

Simulations that couple the atmosphere, ocean, and sea-ice components are referred to as fully-coupled runs.

除全耦合模拟外，还进行了另外两类试验。

In addition to the fully-coupled simulations, two additional types of experiments are performed.

第一类是一组部分耦合模拟，其中完整的海洋模式（POP2）被单层 SOM[58] 替代，同时保留大气和海冰分量。

The first is a set of partially-coupled simulations, in which the full ocean model (POP2) is replaced by a single-layer SOM[58], while retaining the atmosphere and sea-ice components.

在 SOM 中，海洋被视为静止但在整个深度内充分混合，通过给定混合层深度和海洋热量输送辐合来实现。

In the SOM ocean is treated as motionless but well-mixed throughout its depth by prescribing the mixed-layer depth and ocean heat transport convergence.

因此，该模式仅考虑海气界面上的能量交换，不会发生海洋环流变化。

Therefore, in this model, only energy exchange upon the ocean-atmosphere interface is considered, and no ocean circulation changes will occur.

第二类是一组仅大气模拟，使用 CAM5 并给定 SST 和 SIC 边界条件。

The second is a set of atmosphere-only simulations, using CAM5 with prescribed SST and SIC boundary conditions.

本研究采用的水平分辨率为 1.9° × 2.5°（纬度 × 经度）。

The horizontal resolution used in this study is 1.9° × 2.5° (latitude × longitude).

模拟方案

Simulation scheme

如表 1 所示，我们使用 CESM 共进行了 14 组试验，以分离并量化 TPA 对极地海冰损失的相对贡献。

A total of 14 experiments were conducted using CESM to isolate and quantify the relative contribution of the TPA to polar sea-ice loss, as is shown in Table 1.

试验设计遵循三步框架：

The experimental design follows a three-step framework:

（1）全耦合瞬变模拟：进行了四组全耦合瞬变模拟，分别由 CMIP5 历史时期（1850–2005 年）和三个未来情景（2006–2100 年，即 RCP2.6、RCP4.5 和 RCP8.5）的气候强迫（温室气体浓度、气溶胶排放等）驱动；

(1) Fully-coupled Transient Simulations: four fully-coupled transient simulations were performed, driven by the climate forcings (greenhouse gas concentration, aerosol emissions, and so on) of CMIP5 historical (1850-2005) and three future scenarios (2006-2100), namely, RCP2.6, RCP4.5, and RCP8.5, respectively;

（2）分支瞬变模拟：从这些瞬变模拟出发，在两个时间点分支出 5 个成员的 20 年瞬变试验集合：(i) 1986 年，代表现代气候（1986–2005 年，记为 HIST）；(ii) 2080 年，代表 RCP2.6、RCP4.5 和 RCP8.5 下的未来条件（2080–2099 年）。

(2) Branched Transient Simulations: from these transient simulations, five-member ensembles of 20-year transient experiments were branched at two time points: (i) 1986, to represent the present-day climate (1986–2005, denoted as HIST), and (ii) 2080, to represent future conditions under RCP2.6, RCP4.5, and RCP8.5 (2080–2099).

每个集合成员的初始大气温度场施加微小扰动（约 1 × 10−14 K），以考虑内部变率；

Each ensemble member was initialized with slight perturbations in the atmospheric temperature field (~1 × 10−¹⁴ K) to account for internal variability;

（3）青藏高原松弛逼近时间片模拟：另外进行了十组时间片试验，利用大气松弛逼近技术在青藏高原上空施加现代和预估的未来大气场。

(3) TP-nudging Time-slice Simulations: ten additional time-slice experiments were conducted that impose present-day and projected future atmospheric fields over the TP using the atmospheric nudging technique.

这些模拟使我们能够在不同气候情景下受控地评估青藏高原对海冰演变的影响。

These simulations allow for controlled assessment of the TP’s influence on sea-ice evolution under different climate scenarios.

注意，(2) 中提到的 20 年瞬变模拟是从 (1) 中的长时间全耦合模拟重启的。

Note that the 20-year transient simulations mentioned in (2) are restarted from the long-time fully-coupled simulations in (1).

也就是说，理论上这些模拟同样考虑了长时间的海洋调整，与 200 年的青藏高原松弛逼近全耦合试验类似，因而二者具有可比性。

That is, theoretically, long-time ocean adjustment is also considered in these simulations, similar to the 200-year TP-nudged fully-coupled experiments, which makes them comparable.

对于松弛逼近的时间片模拟，以 HIST 和 RCP2.6/4.5/8.5 模拟每 3 小时输出的气温、水平风和地面气压场作为参考场。

For the nudged time-slice simulations, 3-h output of air temperature, horizontal wind, and surface pressure from the HIST and RCP2.6/4.5/8.5 simulations are used as reference fields.

这些场用于将青藏高原上空的大气场松弛逼近到各自的气候态：HIST 为 1986–2005 年，未来 RCP 情景为 2080–2099 年。

These fields were used to nudge the atmospheric fields over the TP towards their respective climatological states: 1986–2005 for HIST and 2080–2099 for the future RCP scenarios.

松弛逼近每 3 个模式小时施加一次。

Nudging is applied every 3 model hours.

松弛逼近的垂直范围根据青藏高原区域平均的历史与未来状态年平均气温差的垂直廓线确定（补充图 12a）。

The vertical extent of nudging was determined based on the vertical profile of annual-mean air temperature differences between the historical and future states, averaged over the TP (Supplementary Fig. 12a).

水平松弛逼近区域见补充图 12b。

The horizontal nudging domain is shown in Supplementary Fig. 12b.

使用松弛系数指定松弛逼近的强度，0 表示自由演变，1 表示完全松弛逼近。

A nudging coefficient was used to specify the strength of nudging, with a value of 0 representing free-running conditions and 1 indicating full nudging.

在青藏高原上空，100 hPa 以下松弛系数设为 1，并线性减小至 50 hPa 处的 0，形成一个垂直缓冲区。

Over the TP, the nudging coefficient was set to 1 below 100 hPa and linearly reduced to 0 at 50 hPa, creating a vertical buffer zone.

由于青藏高原地形复杂，未设置水平缓冲区。

Due to the complex topography of the TP, no horizontal buffer zone was applied.

与早先在青藏高原施加人为地表加热的研究[19,20,21]不同，我们的方法引入了青藏高原增暖的完整垂直范围。

In contrast to earlier studies that imposed artificial surface heating over the TP[19,20,21], our approach induced the full vertical extent of the TP warming.

这一选择反映了青藏高原增暖在垂直方向上范围广的特性，其可能产生更广泛、更显著的气候影响，类似于北极放大下观测到的情形[59,60,61]。

This choice reflects the vertically extensive nature of the TP warming, which could exert broader and more pronounced climatic impacts, similar to those observed under Arctic amplification[59,60,61].

松弛逼近模拟得到的垂直温度廓线与参考廓线高度吻合，证实了松弛逼近方案的有效性（补充图 12a）。

The vertical temperature profiles derived from the nudged simulations closely match the reference profiles, confirming the effectiveness of the nudging scheme (Supplementary Fig. 12a).

松弛逼近方法的更多细节见 Peings 等[62]和 Xu 等[63,64]。

For further details on the nudging methodology, see Peings et al.[62] and Xu et al.[63,64].

由于海冰损失本身可强烈影响全球气候[33,65,66]，我们另外进行了一对全耦合模拟（TP_HIST_fixaa 和 TP_RCP85_fixaa），将北极和南极海冰松弛逼近到历史状态。

Because sea-ice loss itself can strongly influence global climate[33,65,66], an additional pair of fully-coupled simulations (TP_HIST_fixaa and TP_RCP85_fixaa) was conducted with Arctic and Antarctic sea-ice nudged toward the historical state.

这些模拟采用了最先进的混合海冰松弛逼近技术[67]，将针对 SIC 的直接海冰松弛逼近[66]与针对 SIT 的“幽灵通量”松弛逼近[68]相结合。

These simulations employed a state-of-the-art hybrid sea-ice nudging technique[67], combining direct sea-ice nudging for SIC[66] and ghost-flux nudging for SIT[68].

根据 Audette 等[67]，两极最佳的松弛时间尺度组合为 SIC 1 天、SIT 5 天。

According to Audette et al.[67], the best combination of nudging timescale for both poles is 1 day for SIC and 5 days for SIT.

为保证质量守恒，模式中补充了额外的淡水通量以及与海冰融化相当的水质量。

To ensure mass conservation, the model was supplemented with additional freshwater fluxes and equivalent water mass from sea-ice melt.

对所有这些模拟，均取最后 100 年用于分析。

For all these simulations, the last 100 years were used for analysis.

全耦合、SOM 和仅大气模拟分别舍弃了 100 年、50 年和 10 年的起转（spin-up）时段。

Spin-up periods of 100, 50, and 10 years were discarded for the fully-coupled, SOM, and atmosphere-only simulations, respectively.

尽管有人认为海洋达到准平衡态需要数百年甚至数千年的起转[69,70,71]，也有一些研究采用了 100 年甚至更短的起转时间[33,72,73]。

Although some might suggest that hundreds, even thousands, years of spin-up is essential for the ocean to reach a quasi-equilibrium state[69,70,71], some others applied a 100-year, or even shorter, spin-up time[33,72,73].

本研究中，我们发现极地海冰响应的演变在最后 100 年模拟中已达到准平衡态（补充图 13），证明此处采用的 100 年起转是合理的。

In this study, we found that polar sea-ice response evolution has reached a quasi-equilibrium state in the last 100-year simulations (Supplementary Fig. 13), justifying the 100-year spin-up applied here.

其余所有强迫均固定在 2000 年水平。

All remaining forcings were fixed at year-2000 levels.

注意，SOM 和仅大气模拟仅针对历史和 RCP8.5 情景进行。

Note that SOM and atmosphere-only simulations were conducted only for the historical and RCP8.5 scenarios.

此外，还进行了 11 组仅大气模拟，以评估 TPA 引起的 SST 异常的气候效应。

In addition, 11 atmosphere-only simulations were conducted to assess the climatic effects of SST anomalies induced by the TPA.

这些模拟由青藏高原+海冰松弛逼近的全耦合试验（TP_HIST_fixaa 和 TP_RCP85_fixaa）得到的 SST 异常强迫，而其他所有外强迫均固定在 2000 年水平。

Their simulations were forced by SST anomalies derived from the TP+sea-ice-nudged fully-coupled runs (TP_HIST_fixaa and TP_RCP85_fixaa), while all other external forcings were fixed at year-2000 levels.

每组模拟运行 33 年，前 3 年作为起转舍弃以保证模式稳定。

Each simulation was run for 33 years, with the first 3 years discarded as spin-up to ensure model stability.

仅最后 30 年用于分析。

Only the final 30 years were used for analysis.

其中两组模拟分别由 TP_HIST_fixaa 和 TP_RCP85_fixaa 的全球 SST 场（图 6a）驱动，记为 TP_HIST_global 和 TP_RCP85_global。

Two of these simulations were driven by the global SST fields from TP_HIST_fixaa and TP_RCP85_fixaa (Fig. 6a), respectively, and are referred to as TP_HIST_global and TP_RCP85_global.

为分离区域贡献，另外进行了八组模拟，使用 TP_HIST_fixaa 的 SST，但将特定海区的 SST 替换为 TP_RCP85_fixaa 的 SST。

To isolate regional contributions, eight additional simulations were performed using SSTs from TP_HIST_fixaa, but with SSTs over specific ocean regions replaced by those from TP_RCP85_fixaa.

此外，还进行了一组模拟（TP_RCP85_50S-20N），使用 TP_RCP85_fixaa 在 50°S 至 20°N 整个区域的 SST 异常，其他区域保持 TP_HIST_fixaa 的 SST。

Additionally, one simulation (TP_RCP85_50S-20N) was performed using SST anomalies over the entire region spanning 50°S to 20°N from TP_RCP85_fixaa, while maintaining TP_HIST_fixaa SSTs elsewhere.

经向热量输送

Meridional heat transport

本研究中经向 AHT 和 OHT 的计算方法遵循 Yang 等[74]详细描述的步骤，此处简要概述。

The calculation methods for meridional AHT and OHT used in this study follow the procedures described in detail by Yang et al.[74] and are briefly summarized here.

总 AHT 按下式计算：

The total AHT is computed using the following equation:

AHT=∫02πdλ∫ptpsav(cpθ+lvq)cosφ/gdp(1)

AHT=∫02πdλ∫ptpsav(cpθ+lvq)cosφ/gdp(1)

其中 λ、p、ps、pt、a、v、cp、θ、lv、q、φ 和 g 分别表示经度、气压、垂直积分的下界和上界、地球半径、经向风、干空气比热容、干位温、汽化潜热、比湿、纬度和重力加速度。

where λ, p, ps, pt, a, v, cp, θ, lv, q, φ and g denote longitude, air pressure, lower and upper bounds of vertical integration, Earth’s radius, meridional wind, specific heat capacity of dry air, dry potential temperature, latent heat of vaporization, specific humidity, latitude, and gravitational acceleration.

总 AHT 可按下列各式进一步分解为四部分，即 MDSE、MLE、EDSE 和 ELE：

The total AHT can be further decomposed into four parts, that is, MDSE, MLE, EDSE, and ELE, according to the following equations:

AHTMDSE=∫02πdλ∫ptpsacpv¯θ¯cosφ/gdp(2)

AHTMDSE=∫02πdλ∫ptpsacpv¯θ¯cosφ/gdp(2)

AHTMLE=∫02πdλ∫ptpsalvv¯q¯cosφ/gdp(3)

AHTMLE=∫02πdλ∫ptpsalvv¯q¯cosφ/gdp(3)

AHTEDSE=∫02πdλ∫ptpsacpvθcosφ/gdp−AHTMDSE(4)

AHTEDSE=∫02πdλ∫ptpsacpvθcosφ/gdp−AHTMDSE(4)

AHTELE=∫02πdλ∫ptpsalvvqcosφ/gdp−AHTMLE(5)

AHTELE=∫02πdλ∫ptpsalvvqcosφ/gdp−AHTMLE(5)

其中上划线表示月平均。

where overbar denotes monthly mean.

此外，需要指出的是，上述计算基于 p 坐标，由 CAM5 原始的 σ-p 混合坐标插值得到。

In addition, it should be noted that the above calculations are based on the p-coordinate, interpolated from the original CAM5 σ-p hybrid coordinate.

这可能导致质量不守恒：

This could lead to the non-conservation of mass:

∫02πdλ∫ptpsavcosφ/gdp≠0(6)

∫02πdλ∫ptpsavcosφ/gdp≠0(6)

因此，经向风应按下式修正：

Therefore, meridional wind should be updated according to the following equation:

v*=v−∫02πdλ∫ptpsavcosφ/gdp/∫02πdλ∫ptpsacosφ/gdp(7)

v*=v−∫02πdλ∫ptpsavcosφ/gdp/∫02πdλ∫ptpsacosφ/gdp(7)

OHT 按下式计算：

OHT is calculated based on the following equation:

OHT(φ,θt,θb)=ρcp∫θt(φ)θb(φ)(Ψ(φ,θ)−Ψ(φ,θb))dθ(8)

OHT(φ,θt,θb)=ρcp∫θt(φ)θb(φ)(Ψ(φ,θ)−Ψ(φ,θb))dθ(8)

其中 φ、θ、θt、θb、ρ、cp 和 Ψ 分别表示纬度、海洋位温、纬度 φ 处闭合环流所达到的最大和最小位温、海水密度、海水比热容和经向流函数。

where φ, θ, θt, θb, ρ, cp, and Ψ denote latitude, ocean potential temperature, maximum and minimum potential temperature reached by a closed circulation at a latitude φ, sea water density, specific heat capacity of sea water, and meridional stream function.

海冰与风暴路径诊断

Sea ice and storm track diagnostics

北极和南极海冰范围（SIE）分别通过对 30°N 以北和 30°S 以南区域内 SIC 超过 15% 的格点面积求和得到。

Arctic and Antarctic sea-ice extent (SIE) is calculated by summing the grid cell areas where SIC exceeds 15%, within the regions north of 30°N and south of 30°S, respectively.

海冰体积（SIV）为各格点 SIT 乘以 SIC 之和。

Sea-ice volume (SIV) is computed as the sum of SIT multiplied by SIC on each grid.

从欧拉视角，风暴路径活动量化为经 2.5–6 天带通滤波后逐日位势高度的方差[75]。

Storm track activity, from an Eulerian perspective, is quantified as the variance of daily geopotential height after applying a 2.5–6 days bandpass filter[75].

该指标捕捉天气尺度变率，常用于诊断中纬度风暴路径强度。

This metric captures synoptic-scale variability and is commonly used to diagnose mid-latitude storm track intensity.

统计显著性检验

Statistical significance test

本研究采用自助法重采样检验评估两个样本之间差异的统计显著性。

A bootstrap resampling test is employed in this study to assess the statistical significance of the differences between two samples.

设 A₁ 和 A₂ 为两个样本，其大小分别为 P₁ 和 P₂。

Let A₁ and A₂ be the two samples with respective sizes P₁ and P₂.

从每个样本中随机抽取大小为 p₁ 和 p₂ 的子集（其中 p₁ ≤ P₁，p₂ ≤ P₂），重复 1000 次。

From each sample, subsets of size p₁ and p₂ (where p₁ ≤ P₁ and p₂ ≤ P₂) are randomly drawn 1000 times.

每次迭代计算重采样子集之间的差异，并用这些差异的分布估计 95% 置信区间。

For each iteration, the difference between the resampled subsets is calculated, and the distribution of these differences is used to estimate the 95% confidence interval.

取重采样分布的第 2.5 和第 97.5 百分位数分别作为下界和上界。

The 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of the resampled distribution are taken as the lower and upper bounds, respectively.

若零落在该置信区间之外，则认为两样本之间的差异在 95% 置信水平上统计显著。

If zero lies outside this confidence interval, the difference between the two samples is considered statistically significant at the 95% confidence level.

否则，差异被视为不显著。

Otherwise, the difference is deemed not significant.

该方法的更多细节参见 Gilleland[76]。

For additional details on the method, refer to Gilleland[76].

本研究中重采样子集的大小为 10。

In this study, the resampling subset size is 10.

Student t 检验给出非常相似的检验结果（未展示）。

Student t-test yields very similar testing results (not shown).

TPA 对全球 SST 的影响

Influence of the TPA on global SST

TPA 引起的全球 SST 异常如图 6a 所示。

The global SST anomalies induced by the TPA are shown in Fig. 6a.

此处解释 TPA 如何引起这些 SST 异常。

Here, how the TPA induces such SST anomalies is explained.

强烈的青藏高原地表增暖产生局地低层气旋式异常（补充图 8a 和 b），并在对流层上层转变为反气旋式异常，类似于已有充分记载的青藏高原热力抽吸效应[1,2,3]。

The strong TP surface warming generates a local low-level cyclonic anomaly (Supplementary Fig. 8a and b), which transitions into an anti-cyclonic anomaly in the upper troposphere, resembling the well-documented thermal pumping effect of the TP[1,2,3].

青藏高原上空相应的地表辐合增强了热带印度洋的南风，增加潜热损失，形成负位相印度洋偶极子（IOD）型（图 6a）[4]。

The associated surface convergence over the TP enhances southerly winds over the tropical Indian Ocean, increasing latent heat loss and giving rise to a negative phase Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD)-like pattern (Fig. 6a)[4].

类似地，TPA 引起的热带太平洋西风异常使西太平洋暖池的暖水向东移动，有利于厄尔尼诺型 SST 型的发展[5]。

Similarly, the TPA-induced westerly anomalies over the tropical Pacific Ocean shift warm waters from the western Pacific warm pool eastward, favoring the development of an El Niño-like SST pattern[5].

此外，CESM 和线性斜压模式（LBM；见补充方法）均表明，TPA 激发出一列向东传播的波列，分别在北太平洋和副热带太平洋产生反气旋式和气旋式异常（补充图 5）。

Furthermore, both CESM and a linear baroclinic model (LBM; see Supplementary Methods) reveals that the TPA excites an eastward propagating wave train that generates anti-cyclonic and cyclonic anomalies over the North and subtropical Pacific, respectively (Supplementary Fig. 5).

北太平洋上空的反气旋式异常呈正压结构（补充图 8a–b），引起埃克曼下沉（补充图 8c）及相应的 SST 增暖，类似于太平洋年代际振荡的负位相（PDO；图 6a）[6]。

The anti-cyclonic anomaly over the North Pacific, exhibiting a barotropic structure (Supplementary Fig. 8a-b), induces Ekman downwelling (Supplementary Fig. 8c) and associated SST warming, resembling a negative phase of the Pacific Decadal Oscillation (PDO; Fig. 6a)[6].

TPA 引起的极地以外区域 SST 异常对极地增暖的影响

Influence of the TPA-induced extra-polar regional SST anomalies on polar warming

我们另外进行了 8 组仅大气模拟（补充表 2），由 TPA 引起的极地以外区域 SST 异常（补充图 10b–i）驱动，以分离不同大洋盆地区域 SST 对极地增暖的贡献。

We performed 8 additional atmosphere-only simulations (Supplementary Tab. 2) driven by the TPA-induced extra-polar regional SST anomalies (Supplementary Fig. 10b-i), to isolate the contributions of regional SSTs from different ocean basins to polar warming.

对北极而言，负 PDO 型和厄尔尼诺型 SST 型均在斯堪的纳维亚半岛上空引起高压异常（补充图 14）。

For the Arctic, both the -PDO-like and El Niño-like SST patterns induce a high-pressure anomaly over the Scandinavia Peninsula (Supplementary Fig. 14).

该高压中心西侧由此产生的南风异常增强了北大西洋上空暖空气的向极输送，促成北极地表增暖。

The resulting southerly wind anomalies on the western flank of this high-pressure center enhance poleward transport of warm air over the North Atlantic, contributing to Arctic surface warming.

对南极而言，负 IOD 型以及南大西洋和南太平洋的 SST 异常是所观察到的威德尔海上空高压中心和别林斯高晋海上空低压中心及随后南极增暖的成因（补充图 11）。

For the Antarctic, the -IOD-like pattern and the SST anomalies over the South Atlantic and South Pacific are responsible for the observed high-pressure center over the Weddell Sea and low-pressure center over the Bellingshausen Sea, and the subsequent Antarctic warming (Supplementary Fig. 11).

EP 通量计算

EP flux calculation

Eliassen-Palm 通量（EP 通量）按下列各式计算[7]：

Eliassen-Palm flux (EP flux) is calculated according to the following equations[7]:

Fϕ=ρ0acosϕ(uz¯v′θ′¯/θz¯−v′u′¯)(1)

Fϕ=ρ0acosϕ(uz¯v′θ′¯/θz¯−v′u′¯)(1)

Fz=ρ0acosϕ{[f−(acosϕ)−1(u¯cosϕ)ϕv′θ′¯/θz¯−w′u′¯]}(2)

Fz=ρ0acosϕ{[f−(acosϕ)−1(u¯cosϕ)ϕv′θ′¯/θz¯−w′u′¯]}(2)

DF=∇·F→ρ0acosϕ=(acosϕ)−1∂∂ϕ(Fϕcosϕ)+∂Fz∂zρ0acosϕ(3)

DF=∇·F→ρ0acosϕ=(acosϕ)−1∂∂ϕ(Fϕcosϕ)+∂Fz∂zρ0acosϕ(3)

其中 Fϕ、Fz 和 DF 分别表示 EP 通量的经向和垂直分量及其散度。

where Fϕ, Fz and DF denote the meridional and vertical components of EP flux and its divergence.

u、v、w、ϕ、θ、a、f、z 和 ρ0 分别表示风速的纬向、经向和垂直分量、纬度、位温、地球半径、科里奥利参数、对数气压和空气密度。

u, v, w, ϕ, θ, a, f, z and ρ0 denote zonal, meridional and vertical components of wind velocity, latitude, potential temperature, Earth’s radius, Coriolis parameter, log pressure and air density.

上划线和撇号分别表示纬向平均和对纬向平均的偏差。

Overbars and primes denote zonal-mean and departure from zonal mean, respectively.

线性斜压模式

Linear baroclinic model

线性化斜压模式（LBM）[8]是基于线性化原始方程的理想化模式，用于研究大气对青藏高原非绝热加热的响应。

The Linearized Baroclinic Model (LBM)[8], an idealized model based on the linearized primitive equations, is employed to investigate the atmospheric response to diabatic heating over the TP.

该模式配置为 T42 水平分辨率（约 2.8° × 2.8°，即 64 × 128 个格点）和 20 个垂直层。

The model is configured with a T42 horizontal resolution (approximately 2.8° × 2.8°, or 64 × 128 grid points) and 20 vertical levels.

在青藏高原区域施加给定的非绝热加热源，以模拟与 TPA 相关的热力强迫。

A prescribed diabatic heating source is imposed over the TP region to simulate the thermal forcing associated with the TPA.

模式积分 20 天，取最后 5 天的响应作为准定常解。

The model was integrated for 20 days, with the response during the final 5 days treated as the quasi-steady solution.

海洋近表层热收支分析

Ocean near-surface heat budget analysis

海洋近表层热收支按下式诊断[6]：

Ocean near-surface heat budget is diagnosed according to the following equation[6]:

dθdt=−V→·∇θ+QnetρcpH+residual(4)

dθdt=−V→·∇θ+QnetρcpH+residual(4)

其中 θ、V、∇、Qnet、ρ、cp 和 H 分别表示海洋位温、水平海流速度、水平梯度算子、向下净地表热通量（包括短波和长波通量以及感热和潜热通量）、海水密度、比热和深度。

where θ, V, ∇, Qnet, ρ, cp and H denote ocean potential temperature, horizontal ocean current velocity, horizontal gradient operator, downward net surface heat flux (including short and long wave fluxes, and sensible and latent heat fluxes), density, specific heat and depth of sea water.
