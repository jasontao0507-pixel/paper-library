摘要

Abstract

南海北部陆架区（NSCS）的海洋热浪（MHW）发生愈加频繁，对海洋生态系统和人类生计构成严重威胁。

Marine heatwaves (MHWs) have become increasingly frequent in the northern shelf of the South China Sea (NSCS), posing serious threats to marine ecosystems and human livelihoods.

然而，现有研究大多关注季节尺度和年际尺度的驱动因素，季节内变率的作用仍缺乏充分研究，这限制了我们预测海洋热浪的能力。

However, most existing studies focus on seasonal and interannual drivers, while the role of intraseasonal variability remains underexplored, limiting our ability to predict MHWs.

本研究考察马登—朱利安振荡（MJO）对NSCS春季海洋热浪的调制，旨在阐明季节内时间尺度上的大气和海洋机制。

This study examines the modulation of spring MHWs by the Madden–Julian Oscillation (MJO) in the NSCS, aiming to clarify the underlying atmospheric and oceanic mechanisms at intraseasonal timescales.

统计分析表明，MJO第2和第3位相通过增强向下短波辐射、减少潜热通量，使海洋热浪发生概率提高超过100%；其背景是当地对流受到抑制、海面风减弱。

Statistical analysis reveals that MJO phases 2 and 3 significantly increase the probability of MHW occurrence by more than 100% through enhanced downward shortwave radiation and reduced latent heat flux, driven by suppressed local convection and weakened surface winds.

在识别出的28次大范围海洋热浪事件中，12次与活跃的MJO第2和第3位相有关；期间季节内海表温度异常的振幅最高达到0.55°C。

Among 28 large‐scale MHW events identified, 12 were associated with active MJO phases 2 and 3, during which the amplitude of intraseasonal sea surface temperature anomalies reached up to 0.55°C.

此外，MJO诱发的季节内增暖叠加低频暖背景，共同触发了NSCS有记录以来持续时间最长、累积强度最高的MHW-21事件：该事件持续43天，累积强度达到71.8°C。

Furthermore, MJO‐induced intraseasonal warming, in conjunction with a low‐frequency warm background, jointly triggered the longest duration and highest cumulative intense MHW‐21 on record in the NSCS, which lasted for 43 days and reached a cumulative intensity of 71.8°C.

研究结果表明，季节内振荡对海洋热浪发展期间的混合层增暖有显著贡献，凸显了从多时间尺度视角研究海洋热浪驱动机制的必要性。

Our results demonstrate the substantial contribution of intraseasonal oscillations to mixed‐layer warming during MHW development, emphasizing the necessity of investigating the driving mechanisms of MHWs from a multi‐timescale perspective.

Marine heatwaves (MHWs) are increasingly recognized as major threats to marine ecosystems and local economies, especially in regions that are both economically valuable and ecologically sensitive.

南海北部陆架区（NSCS）对粤港澳大湾区有重要影响；该地区是中国经济最重要、人口最密集的区域之一。

The northern shelf of the South China Sea (NSCS) crucially impacts the Guangdong‐Hong Kong‐Macao Greater Bay Area, which is one of China's most economically important and densely populated regions.

本研究利用卫星观测和再分析资料，考察NSCS海洋热浪的驱动机制。

This study utilizes satellite observations and reanalysis data to investigate the driving mechanisms of MHWs in the NSCS.

结果表明，作为热带大气环流主导模态的马登—朱利安振荡（MJO），在第2和第3位相期间对这些事件具有重要调制作用。

By providing new insights into the mechanism of MHWs in the NSCS, this study highlights the importance of considering intraseasonal oscillations in MHW prediction and impact assessment.

1. 引言

1. Introduction

海洋热浪（MHW）是海洋中发生的离散、持续性极端水体增暖事件（Hobday et al., 2016）。

Marine heatwaves (MHWs) are discrete and persistent extreme water warming events that occur in the ocean (Hobday et al., 2016).

这些事件的水平范围可达数千千米，垂直方向可从海表延伸至数百米深处，持续时间则可从数天到数年（Oliver et al., 2021）。

These events can span thousands of kilometers horizontally, extend from the surface to hundreds of meters vertically, and last from a few days to several years (Oliver et al., 2021).

在全球持续变暖的背景下，世界各地海洋热浪的频率、持续时间和空间范围均显著增加（Oliver et al., 2018）。

The frequency, duration and spatial extent of MHWs around the world have increased significantly in the context of continued global warming (Oliver et al., 2018).

当前发生的极端海洋热浪正在改变全球生态系统，并带来一系列重要的社会经济后果，包括珊瑚白化、生物多样性丧失、渔业资源减少、水产养殖受损以及物种行为改变（Frölicher & Laufkötter, 2018; Smale et al., 2019; Smith et al., 2021）。

The current occurrence of extreme MHWs is transforming global ecosystems and leading to a range of significant societal and economic consequences, including coral bleaching, biodiversity loss, reduced fishery resources, deteriorating aquaculture, and shifts in species behavior (Frölicher & Laufkötter, 2018; Smale et al., 2019; Smith et al., 2021).

过去十年，全球海洋的多个区域都观测到越来越多的海洋热浪（Frölicher et al., 2018）。

Over the past decade, a growing number of MHWs have been observed in various regions of the global ocean (Frölicher et al., 2018).

既有研究表明，海洋热浪的驱动机制复杂且多样。

Previous studies have demonstrated that the driving mechanisms of MHWs are complex and varied.

局地过程和大尺度气候模态都可以在不同空间和时间尺度上影响海洋热浪的发生（Holbrook et al., 2019）。

Both local processes and large‐scale climate modes can affect the occurrence of MHWs in different spatial and temporal scales (Holbrook et al., 2019).

在局地尺度，大气强迫和海洋动力过程被认为是海洋热浪的主要驱动因素（Vogt et al., 2022）。

Locally, atmospheric forcing and oceanic dynamics have been identified as the main drivers of MHWs (Vogt et al., 2022).

Li et al. (2022) 发现，2016年黄海破纪录的海洋热浪由西太平洋副热带高压西伸所伴随的太阳辐射增强和风速减弱造成。

Li et al. (2022) found that the 2016 record‐breaking MHW in the Yellow Sea was caused by enhanced solar radiation and weakened wind speeds associated with the westward extension of the western Pacific subtropical high (WPSH).

Oliver et al. (2017) 指出，澳大利亚东边界流向南加强是2015/16年塔斯曼海极端海洋热浪的主要驱动因素。

Oliver et al. (2017) revealed that the southward intensification of the Australian Eastern Boundary Current was the primary driver of the extreme MHW event in the Tasman Sea during 2015/16.

Bian et al. (2024) 强调，海洋热浪的主导机制高度依赖其所在位置和空间尺度；随着空间尺度增大，主要驱动因素由局地海洋平流转向大气强迫。

Bian et al. (2024) emphasized that the dominant mechanisms behind MHWs are highly dependent on their location and spatial scale, with the primary driver of MHWs shifting from local oceanic advection to atmospheric forcing as the spatial scale increases.

大尺度气候模态通过遥相关调节大气状况与海洋环流，同样显著影响海洋热浪的发生（Holbrook et al., 2019）。

The adjustment of large‐scale climate modes to atmospheric conditions and ocean circulation through teleconnection also significantly influences the occurrence of MHWs (Holbrook et al., 2019).

例如，近几十年来，太平洋年代际振荡（PDO）调制了东北太平洋的海洋热浪。

For example, the Pacific Decadal Oscillation (PDO) has modulated MHWs in the Northeast Pacific in recent decades.

在PDO正位相期间，异常东南风减弱沿岸冷水上升流，并增加向下的海表净热通量，使海洋热浪持续更久、发生更频繁（Ren et al., 2023）。

During positive PDO phases, anomalous southeasterly surface winds reduce coastal cold upwelling and increase the net downward surface heat flux, resulting in longer and more frequent MHWs (Ren et al., 2023).

Saranya et al. (2022) 表明，印度洋增暖与厄尔尼诺变率共同作用，形成了该区域一些强度最大、持续时间最长的海洋热浪。

Saranya et al. (2022) demonstrated that warming in the Indian Ocean, combined with El Niño variability, has produced some of the most intense and prolonged MHWs in the region.

马登—朱利安振荡（MJO）可以驱动热带印度洋东南部强烈的季节内温度变率，并通过影响潜热通量（LHF）和短波辐射（SWR）调制海洋热浪（Cui et al., 2024）。

The Madden‐Julian Oscillation (MJO) can drive strong intraseasonal temperature variability in the southeastern tropical Indian Ocean and modulate MHWs by influencing latent heat flux (LHF) and shortwave radiation (SWR) (Cui et al., 2024).

Gregory et al. (2024) 揭示了不同气候模态对海洋热浪影响之间的协同作用，表明MJO可以在次季节时间尺度上调制厄尔尼诺—南方涛动（ENSO）对海洋热浪的影响。

Gregory et al. (2024) revealed synergies between the impacts of different climate modes on MHWs, showing that the MJO can modulate the impacts of the El Niño‐Southern Oscillation (ENSO) on MHWs at sub‐seasonal timescales.

这些结果说明，海洋热浪的驱动因素随区域和时间尺度而变化，因而需要针对具体区域开展分析。

These results indicate that the drivers of MHWs vary by regions and timescales, underscoring the importance of region‐specific analyses.

南海（SCS）是世界最大的边缘海之一，与中国及多个东南亚国家相邻。

The South China Sea (SCS) is one of the largest marginal seas in the world, bordered by China and several Southeast Asian countries.

渔业、水产养殖和旅游业是该区域的重要经济支柱（Wang et al., 2023）。

Fisheries, aquaculture, and tourism are important economic pillars in the region (Wang et al., 2023).

然而，频繁的海洋热浪日益威胁当地生态系统和经济，造成大范围珊瑚白化、有害藻华暴发以及沿海基础设施严重受损（Yao et al., 2020; Zuo et al., 2015）。

However, ecosystems and economies are increasingly threatened by frequent MHW events, which have resulted in widespread coral bleaching, harmful algal bloom outbreaks and severe damage to coastal infrastructure (Yao et al., 2020; Zuo et al., 2015).

极端天气事件增多，又进一步加剧南海面临的环境和经济压力（Tan et al., 2022）。

The increase in extreme weather events is further exacerbating the environmental and economic pressures faced by the SCS (Tan et al., 2022).

目前已有许多关于南海海洋热浪的研究（Han et al., 2023; Sun et al., 2024; Yao & Wang, 2021）。

Currently, numerous studies have been conducted on MHWs in the SCS (Han et al., 2023; Sun et al., 2024; Yao & Wang, 2021).

在全球变暖趋势下，南海经常发生海洋热浪，尤其是南海北部陆架区（NSCS）；那里的事件正在变得更强、更频繁（Li et al., 2023; Tan et al., 2022）。

Under the trend of global warming, MHWs frequently occur in the SCS, particularly in the northern shelf of the SCS (NSCS), where MHWs are becoming stronger and more frequent (Li et al., 2023; Tan et al., 2022).

沿海地区有许多人口密集的城市，也拥有丰富多样的生态系统类型。

Many densely populated cities are located in coastal regions, which are characterized by a rich diversity of ecosystem types.

因此，海洋热浪显著影响这些地区，威胁生态健康和人类生计（Starko et al., 2024）。

As a result, MHWs significantly impact these areas, threatening both ecological health and human livelihoods (Starko et al., 2024).

NSCS生产力较高，主要受季风气候驱动。

The NSCS exhibits high productivity, primarily driven by the monsoon climate.

沿岸羽流急流将富含营养盐的水输送至该区域，使叶绿素浓度升高，并使该区域出现有色溶解性有机物（Dong et al., 2004; Ye et al., 2020; Zhao et al., 2018）。

Nutrient‐rich waters are transported into the region by coastal plume jets, leading to elevated chlorophyll concentrations and the presence of colored dissolved organic matter (Dong et al., 2004; Ye et al., 2020; Zhao et al., 2018).

NSCS的海洋热浪能够显著影响叶绿素浓度，进而直接影响海洋生态系统（He et al., 2023）。

MHWs in the NSCS can have a significant impact on chlorophyll concentrations and thus directly affect marine ecosystems (He et al., 2023).

此外，沿海地区的海洋热浪还会改变沿海城市的热环境，直接关系到中国南方居民的健康（Hu, 2021）。

Additionally, MHWs in coastal regions can impact the thermal environment of coastal cities, with direct implications for human health in southern China (Hu, 2021).

因此，研究NSCS的海洋热浪十分重要。

Therefore, the study of MHWs in the NSCS is of great importance.

从年际尺度看，研究显示东太平洋型厄尔尼诺事件倾向于增强NSCS海洋热浪的特征，而中太平洋型（CP）厄尔尼诺事件通常会削弱这些特征（N. Zhang et al., 2025）。

From an interannual perspective, studies have shown that Eastern Pacific El Niño events tend to intensify MHW characteristics, whereas Central Pacific (CP) El Niño events generally weaken these characteristics in the NSCS (N. Zhang et al., 2025).

Q. Wang et al. (2022) 利用OFES模式和锚系观测资料揭示了NSCS海洋热浪的分布特征与驱动机制。

Q. Wang et al. (2022) revealed the distribution characteristics and driving mechanisms in the NSCS using OFES model and mooring data.

他们发现，海气通量在推动海洋热浪发展中起关键作用，而海流通常在海洋热浪衰退阶段使海洋降温。

They found that the air–sea fluxes played a crucial role in driving the development of MHWs, while ocean currents generally contributed to cooling the ocean during the MHW decline phase.

Wang et al. (2023) 进一步研究了NSCS海洋热浪特征及其驱动机制的季节变化，揭示了大陆架边缘的跨陆坡水体交换在调节海洋热浪发生中的作用。

Wang et al. (2023) further investigated the seasonal variations in the characteristics and driving mechanisms of MHWs in the NSCS, revealing the role of cross‐slope water exchange at the edge of the continental shelf in regulating the occurrence of MHWs.

已有研究表明，季风变化会导致南海北部海表温度（SST）出现显著季节内变率（T.‐C. Chen et al., 2000; Mao & Wang, 2018）。

The influence of monsoon changes has been demonstrated to result in significant intraseasonal variability of sea surface temperature (SST) in the northern SCS (T.‐C. Chen et al., 2000; Mao & Wang, 2018).

在南海夏季风爆发前后，由于季节内海表热通量异常，南海北部的SST可在2–3天内变化2–3°C（Wu, 2010）。

SST changes in northern SCS can reach 2–3°C within 2–3 days around the SCS summer monsoon onset due to intraseasonal surface heat flux anomalies (Wu, 2010).

已有研究探讨了季节内振荡（ISO）通过影响短波辐射（SWR）和潜热通量（LHF），在不同海域促进海洋热浪发生的作用（Cui et al., 2024; Dasgupta et al., 2024; Mandal et al., 2024）。

Studies have explored the role of intraseasonal oscillations (ISOs) in promoting MHWs in different ocean areas by influencing SWR and LHF (Cui et al., 2024; Dasgupta et al., 2024; Mandal et al., 2024).

海洋热浪通常持续数天至数周，与ISO的时间尺度相近；大多数海洋热浪事件在时间上也与较强的季节内变率重合。

The duration of MHWs typically ranges from several days to several weeks, with similar time scales to ISOs, and most MHW events coincide intime with stronger intraseasonal variability.

ISO驱动的海气相互作用在季节内时间尺度上调节SST方面发挥重要作用。

Ocean–atmosphere interactions driven by ISOs play a significant role in regulating SST at intraseasonal timescales.

因此，从季节内视角研究海洋热浪的驱动因素，对提高预测和预警能力至关重要（Gregory et al., 2024; Jacox et al., 2022）。

Investigating MHW drivers from the intraseasonal perspective is therefore essential for improving prediction and early warning capabilities (Gregory et al., 2024; Jacox et al., 2022).

然而，NSCS海洋热浪研究主要关注年际和季节尺度特征，对ISO作用的关注相对有限。

However, research on MHWs in the NSCS has primarily focuses on their interannual and seasonal characteristics, with relatively limited attention to the role of ISOs.

因此，本研究考察1982—2022年观测到的海洋热浪，探究ISO对NSCS这些事件的影响。

Therefore, this study investigates MHWs observed from 1982 to 2022 and explores the impact of the ISOs on these events in the NSCS.

2. 数据与方法

2. Data and Methods

2.1. 数据

2.1. Data

逐日SST取自美国国家海洋和大气管理局（NOAA）的最优插值海表温度（OISST）2.1版数据集（Huang et al., 2021）；其时段为1982年1月至2022年12月，空间分辨率为0.25° × 0.25°。

Daily SST is derived from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) Optimal Interpolation Sea Surface Temperature (OISST) data set version 2.1 (Huang et al., 2021), which spans from January 1982 to December 2022 and has a spatial resolution of 0.25° × 0.25°.

该数据集空间分辨率高、连续性好，因此广泛用于区域和全球尺度的海洋热浪识别（Oliver et al., 2021）。

The high spatial resolution and good continuity of this data set have enabled its extensive use in identifying MHWs at regional and global scales (Oliver et al., 2021).

该数据集的时间分辨率为1小时（Hersbach et al., 2020）。

2.2. 海洋热浪的定义

2.2. Definition of MHW

本研究依照Hobday et al. (2016) 提出的定义识别海洋热浪。

In this study, MHWs are identified following the definition proposed by Hobday et al. (2016).

当逐日SST超过阈值（第90百分位）且连续维持5天时，定义为一次海洋热浪事件；如果两次事件之间的间隔少于2天，则将其视为一次连续事件。

An MHW event is defined as occurring when the daily SST exceeded the threshold (90th percentile) and persisted for 5 consecutive days, and two events are considered as a single consecutive event if the interval between them is less than 2 days.

需要注意，海洋热浪的识别取决于气候态基准的选择；由于长期SST趋势的存在，固定基准与移动基准会影响识别结果。

It is important to note that the identification of MHWs depends on the choice of a climatological baseline, and the use of a fixed versus a shifting baseline can affect detection due to long‐term SST trends.

为与既有研究保持一致，我们采用覆盖1982—2022年的固定气候态基准。

To ensure consistency with previous studies, we adopted a fixed climatological baseline spanning from 1982 to 2022.

为计算某一日历日的SST第90百分位阈值及气候态，我们首先对气候态时段（1982—2022年）每一年的该日及其前后共11天窗口内的SST取平均。

To calculate the 90th percentile SST threshold and climatology for a given calendar day, we first average the SST over an 11‐day window centered on that day for each year within the climatological period (1982–2022).

由此得到该日逐年的SST时间序列。

This generates a year‐by‐year time series of SST for that day.

随后取这些值的平均数作为气候态均值，并将这些值按降序排列后所得分布的第90百分位作为阈值。

The climatological mean is then computed as the average of these values, and the 90th percentile, calculated from the distribution of these values in descending order, is taken as the threshold.

用来表征海洋热浪的指标包括频次（给定时段内的海洋热浪事件数）、持续时间（开始日至结束日之间的天数）、平均强度（事件期间SSTA的平均值）、最大强度（事件的最大强度）和累积强度（事件强度的累积值）。

Various indices are calculated to characterize MHWs, including frequency (the number of MHW events occurring within a given time period), duration (the number of days between the start and end dates of MHW), mean intensity (the average SSTA during the MHW event), maximum intensity (the maximum intensity of MHW event) and cumulative intensity (the accumulated intensity of MHW event).

2.3. MJO的识别

2.3. Identification of MJO

MJO事件通过澳大利亚气象局的逐日实时多变量MJO指数（RMM）确定，资料覆盖1982—2022年（Wheeler & Hendon, 2004）。

The MJO events are determined through the daily Real‐Time Multivariate MJO Index (RMM) from the Australian Bureau of Meteorology, covering the period from 1982 to 2022 (Wheeler & Hendon, 2004).

RMM指数（RMM1和RMM2）由NCEP资料中的15°S—15°N平均OLR、200 hPa纬向风和850 hPa纬向风异常的两个主成分构成。

The RMM indices (RMM1 and RMM2) are comprised of the two principal components of the 15°S to 15°N average OLR, zonal wind at 200 hPa and zonal wind anomalies at 850 hPa, derived from the NCEP data.

RMM指数分为1—8共八个位相，对应环流和对流异常向东传播的过程。

The RMM index consists of eight phases, numbered 1–8, which correspond to the eastward propagation of circulation and convection anomalies.

第8和第1位相表示MJO对流位于印度洋西部和中太平洋（CP），第2和第3位相则表示印度洋上空的对流增强。

Phases 8 and 1 indicate MJO convection over the western Indian Ocean and CP, while phases 2 and 3 represent enhanced convection over the Indian Ocean.

第4和第5位相表示MJO对流位于海洋大陆，第6和第7位相表示西太平洋上空对流增强。

Phases 4 and 5 indicate MJO convection over the Maritime Continent, and phases 6 and 7 represent enhanced convection over the western Pacific.

当振幅［(PC1² + PC2²)¹ᐟ²］大于1时，我们认为MJO处于活跃状态。

We consider the MJO to be active when the amplitude [(PC12 + PC22)1/2] is >1.

In this equation, the left‐hand side represents the temperature trend term, the first term on the right‐hand side is the sea surface heat flux forcing term, the second term represents the horizontal advection term and the third is the residual term.

这里，T为混合层平均温度，h为混合层深度，u⃗ = (u, v) 为纬向和经向速度。

Qpen represents the penetrating SWR through the base of the mixed layer which is a function of h and SWR (Shi et al., 2022).

ρ = 1026 kg/m³和Cp = 3990 J/(kg·°C) 分别为海水密度和海水比热容。

ρ = 1026 kg/m3 and Cp = 3990 J/(kg·°C) are the density of seawater and the specific heat capacity of seawater, respectively.

为得到式（1）各项的异常，从每一项中减去相应的气候态值。

The spatial patterns of relevant atmospheric variables indicate that during phases 2 and 3, a reduction in TCC and surface wind speed enhances SWR and reduces evaporative cooling, thereby promoting SST warming and facilitating the occurrence of MHWs.

这解释了受 MJO 调节的大气环流如何促使局地海洋升温，进而有利于海洋热浪发生。

This explains how atmospheric circulation modulated by the MJO contributes to local ocean warming, thereby favoring the occurrence of MHWs.

3.3. Case Study: Extreme MHW Event Occurred in May 2021

我们已经表明，MJO 第 2、3 位相能够通过影响季节内海表温度变率，调节南海北部陆架海洋热浪的发生。

We have demonstrated that MJO phases 2 and 3 can modulate the occurrence of MHWs in the NSCS by influencing intraseasonal SST variability.

相比之下，季节内海表温度变率通过增强表面热通量，更直接地促进增暖趋势，从而成为决定海洋热浪强度和持续时间的主要驱动因素。

In contrast, intraseasonal SST variability contributed more directly to the warming trend through enhanced surface heat fluxes, thus acting as a primary driver of the MHW's intensity and duration.

这两个分量共同调节了 MHW-21 的发生与演变。

Together, these two components jointly modulated the occurrence and evolution of MHW‐21.

4. 讨论与结论

4. Discussion and Conclusion

近几十年来，南海北部陆架海洋热浪事件愈发频繁，严重威胁当地生态系统和人类生计（Z. Chen et al., 2025; Yao et al., 2020）。

The NSCS has experienced an increasing frequency of MHW events in recent decades, posing serious threats to local ecosystems and human livelihoods (Z. Chen et al., 2025; Yao et al., 2020).

然而，现有关于这些事件驱动机制的研究主要聚焦年际和季节尺度，对季节内变率的作用仍认识不足（Q. Wang et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2023）。

However, current research on the driving mechanisms of these events has primarily focused on interannual and seasonal timescales, while the role of intraseasonal variability remains poorly understood (Q. Wang et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2023).

季节内振荡对海洋热浪的开始和演变具有关键的调节作用，其影响不应被忽视。

ISOs exert a critical modulating influence on the onset and evolution of MHWs, and their influence should not be overlooked.

这一认识缺口给海洋热浪的预测和预警带来重大挑战。

This knowledge gap presents significant challenges for the prediction and early warning of MHWs.

本研究从季节内尺度出发，考察 MJO 对南海北部陆架春季海洋热浪的调节作用，从而有助于更全面地理解这些极端海洋事件的驱动机制。

In this study, we investigate the modulation of spring MHWs by the MJO over the NSCS from an intraseasonal perspective, thus contributing to a more comprehensive understanding of the driving mechanisms underlying these extreme oceanic events.

MJO 的不同位相对大气环流产生不同影响，进而对南海北部陆架海洋热浪的发生造成不同作用。

Different MJO phases exert distinct influences on atmospheric circulation, thereby leading to varied impacts on MHW occurrence in the NSCS.

统计结果显示，MJO 第 2、3 位相与海洋热浪发生概率显著升高相关，其中第 2 位相可使平均发生概率提高多达 60%。

Statistical results show that MJO phases 2 and 3 are associated with a significantly increased probability of MHW occurrence, with phase 2 increasing the average probability by up to 60%.

在这些位相，印度洋上空对流增强，抑制了南海北部陆架上空的局地对流，导致向下短波辐射增强、海面风减弱；两者均促进混合层增暖，有利于海洋热浪发展。

During these phases, enhanced convection over the Indian Ocean suppresses local convection over the NSCS, resulting in increased downward SWR and weakened surface winds, both of which promote mixed‐layer warming and favor the development of MHWs.

对南海北部陆架 28 次大范围海洋热浪事件的进一步分析发现，受 MJO 第 2、3 位相影响的事件都伴随季节内暖事件，其持续时间显著更长、强度显著更高，均超过未与 MJO 第 2、3 位相相关的事件。

Further analysis of 28 large‐scale MHW events in the NSCS reveals that those influenced by MJO phases 2 and 3 are consistently accompanied by intraseasonal warm events and exhibit significantly longer durations and higher intensities than those not associated with MJO phases 2 and 3.

超前—滞后分析表明，MJO 通过影响对流活动和局地风速，进而改变短波辐射与潜热通量，驱动季节内海表温度变率，从而调节海洋热浪事件的发生。

Lead–lag analysis indicates that the MJO drives intraseasonal SST variability by influencing convective activity and local wind speed, which lead to SWR and LHF, thereby modulating the occurrence of MHW events.

对极端 MHW-21 事件的个例研究表明，20–90 天季节内振荡和低频海表温度异常在其发展中发挥了不同但互补的作用。

A case study of the extreme MHW‐21 event reveals that the 20–90‐day ISO and low‐frequency SSTA played distinct but complementary roles in its development.

由 MJO 引起的季节内海表温度变率对这次极端事件的演变具有显著的调节作用。

The intraseasonal SST variability induced by the MJO exerted a significant modulatory influence on the evolution of this extreme event.

具体而言，在 MJO 第 2、3 位相，印度洋上空活跃的对流抑制了南海北部陆架上空的对流，使向下短波辐射增强，并在混合层中产生明显的增暖趋势。

Specifically, active convection over the Indian Ocean during MJO phases 2 and 3 suppressed convection over the NSCS, leading to enhanced downward SWR and a pronounced warming tendency in the mixed layer.

值得注意的是，在 MHW-21 的发展阶段，仅 20–90 天短波辐射异常就贡献了混合层总增暖率的 103.9%（总增暖率为 0.051°C/天，其中 0.053°C/天来自该异常），凸显季节内过程在触发这次事件中的主导作用。

Notably, during the onset phase of MHW‐21, the 20–90‐day SWR anomaly alone contributed 103.9% of the total mixed‐layer warming tendency (0.053°C/day out of 0.051°C/day), underscoring the dominant role of intraseasonal processes in triggering the event.

此外，Y. Chen et al.（2023）指出，2021 年 5 月下旬，MJO 在海洋大陆上空消散。

In addition, Y. Chen et al. (2023) reported that the MJO dissipated over the Maritime Continent in late May 2021.

此后不久，热带气旋“Choi‐wan”在南海生成，进一步抑制北部地区的对流。

Shortly afterward, Tropical Cyclone “Choi‐wan” developed over the SCS, further suppressing convection in the northern region.

随着该热带气旋于 6 月初北移，它触发了南海夏季风的爆发。

As the TC moved northward in early June, it triggered the onset of the SCS summer monsoon.

由此造成的潜热通量减小及相关的上升流使混合层冷却，进而促使海洋热浪结束。

The resulting decrease in LHF and the associated upwelling acted to cool the mixed layer, thereby contributing to the termination of the MHW.

与此同时，与季节循环相关的低频海表温度异常建立了有利的热背景，支持 MHW-21 事件的发生。

Meanwhile, the low‐frequency SSTA linked to seasonal cycle established a favorable thermal background that supported the onset of the MHW‐21 event.

ENSO 等大尺度气候模态的位相转换可以显著影响南海低频海表温度变率，进而调节海洋热浪的发生（Liu et al., 2022）。

Phase shifts of large‐scale climate modes such as ENSO can significantly influence low‐frequency SST variability in the SCS, thereby modulating the occurrence of MHWs (Liu et al., 2022).

我们分析了不同 ENSO 位相下，MJO 对南海北部陆架海洋热浪发生概率的影响。

We analyzed the impact of the MJO on the probability of MHW occurrence over the NSCS under different ENSO phases.

结果表明，在拉尼娜年份，当 MJO 处于第 2 至第 5 位相时，海洋热浪的发生概率显著增加（补充材料 S1 中的图 S5）。

The results show that during La Niña years, the probability of MHW occurrence significantly increases when the MJO is in phases 2 to 5 (Figure S5 in Supporting Information S1).

因此，有必要考虑不同时间尺度气候模态的共同作用，以研究海洋热浪的驱动机制。

Therefore, it is necessary to investigate the driving mechanisms of MHWs by considering the combined effects of climate modes operating at different timescales.

本研究阐明了季节内振荡，尤其是 MJO，在南海北部陆架春季海洋热浪的发生和发展中的关键作用。

This study has elucidated the key role of ISOs, particularly the MJO, in the occurrence and development of spring MHWs in the NSCS.

值得注意的是，近期研究表明，在全球变暖背景下，MJO 的可预测性正在提高，其位相和振幅的可靠预报提前期延长（Du et al., 2024）。

Notably, recent studies have shown that the predictability of the MJO is increasing under global warming, with extended lead times for reliable forecasts of MJO phases and amplitude (Du et al., 2024).

这种可预测性的提高有望显著促进我们理解和预报南海北部陆架的海洋热浪。

This enhanced predictability holds significant potential for advancing our understanding and forecasting of MHWs in the NSCS.

准确预测 MJO 可为海洋热浪事件提供更早的预警，为该地区的海洋生态系统管理和减灾工作提供重要支持；当地社区与经济活动都与海洋健康密切相关。

Accurate MJO predictions could enable earlier warnings for MHW events, providing essential support for marine ecosystem management and disaster mitigation efforts in this region, where communities and economies are closely linked to marine health.

由于海洋热浪的形成机制复杂，未来需要研究不同尺度扰动之间的相互作用及其对海洋热浪的影响，尤其应关注年际背景变率（如 ENSO）和天气尺度事件（如热带气旋）对海洋热浪强度与频率的影响（Han et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2022）。

Since the formation mechanisms of MHWs are complex, future research is needed to investigate the interactions between different scale disturbances and their impact on MHWs, particularly focusing on the influence of interannual background variability (e.g., ENSO) and synoptic‐scale events (e.g., tropical cyclones) on the intensity and frequency of MHWs (Han et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2022).

此外，进一步研究不同气候模态与季节内振荡之间的相互作用，对于理解它们对海洋热浪的共同影响至关重要（Dutheil et al., 2024）。

Additionally, further exploration of the interactions between different climate modes and ISOs is crucial to understand their combined impact on MHWs (Dutheil et al., 2024).

还必须认识到年际背景变率对极端海表温度事件发生的贡献。

It is also essential to recognize the contribution of interannual background variability to the occurrence of extreme SST events.

通过采用综合的多尺度视角，并研究不同扰动之间复杂的相互作用，我们可以更好地理解南海北部陆架海洋热浪的形成与演变。

By adopting a comprehensive multi‐scale perspective and investigating the complex interactions between different disturbances, we can better understand the formation and evolution of MHWs in the NSCS.

本研究主要强调，MJO 在第 2、3 位相通过调节海气热通量促进海洋热浪发生的作用。

This study primarily highlights the role of the MJO in promoting MHWs during phases 2 and 3 through its modulation of air– sea heat fluxes.

然而，不同 MJO 位相下海洋动力过程的影响仍需进一步研究。

However, the influence of oceanic dynamical processes under different MJO phases requires further investigation.

未来研究应使用更高分辨率的海洋模式，开展详细的混合层热收支分析，从而更定量地评估大气和海洋对海洋热浪发展的贡献。

Future research should utilize higher‐resolution ocean models to conduct detailed mixed‐layer heat budget analyses, enabling a more quantitative assessment of atmospheric and oceanic contributions to MHW development.

此外，海气背景条件的季节差异可能导致 MJO 对海洋热浪的影响随季节而异。

In addition, seasonal differences in the ocean–atmosphere background conditions may lead to varying MJO impacts on MHWs.

例如，冬季南海北部陆架受冬季风主导，冬季风改变区域环流和海气相互作用，进而影响混合层热收支，使 MJO 活动对海洋热浪的作用不同于春季（Wu & Chen, 2015）。

For example, during winter, when the winter monsoon dominates in the NSCS, it modifies regional circulation and air–sea interactions, thereby affecting the mixed‐layer heat budget and resulting in different effects of MJO activity on MHWs compared to spring (Wu & Chen, 2015).

理解这些季节性调节作用，对于改进不同时期海洋热浪的预测至关重要。

Understanding these seasonal modulations is essential for improving the prediction of MHWs across different time periods.
