摘要

Abstract

北大西洋环流和温度分布格局深刻影响着全球及区域气候，涵盖从天气尺度[1]到季节尺度[2,3,4]、年代际尺度[5]、多年代际尺度[6,7]及更长尺度[8,9]的所有时间尺度。

North Atlantic Ocean circulation and temperature patterns profoundly influence global and regional climate across all timescales, from synoptic[1] to seasonal[2,3,4], decadal[5], multidecadal[6,7] and beyond[8,9].

2023年北半球夏季发生了一场极端、接近海盆尺度的海洋热浪，并于7月达到峰值。

During 2023, an extreme and near-basin-scale marine heatwave developed during Northern Hemisphere summer, peaking in July.

增暖几乎遍及北大西洋的所有区域，包括副极地海洋；该区域过去50–100年的降温趋势被认为与经向翻转环流减弱有关[10,11]。

The warming spread across virtually all regions of the North Atlantic, including the subpolar ocean, where a cooling trend over the past 50–100 years has been linked to a slowdown in the meridional overturning circulation[10,11].

然而，导致这次异常强烈海洋表层增暖的机制仍不清楚。

Yet the mechanisms that led to this exceptional surface ocean warming remain unclear.

本文结合受观测约束的大气再分析资料、海洋观测与模式模拟，表明这次极端海洋增暖事件是由作用于极浅表层混合层的海气热通量造成的，而非异常海洋热输送所致。

Here we use observationally constrained atmospheric reanalyses alongside ocean observations and model simulations to show that air–sea heat fluxes acting on an extremely shallow surface mixed layer, rather than anomalous ocean heat transport, were responsible for this extreme ocean warming event.

结果显示，主导驱动因素是异常弱风使混合层强烈抬升（变浅），从而导致北大西洋浅薄表层的温度迅速升高。

The dominant driver is shown to be anomalously weak winds leading to strongly shoaling (shallowing) mixed layers, resulting in a rapid temperature increase in a shallow surface layer of the North Atlantic.

此外，太阳辐射异常在一些大致对应本区域主要航道的位置上，对区域尺度增暖有所贡献，提示硫酸盐排放减少也可能在局地产生了作用。

Furthermore, solar radiation anomalies made regional-scale warming contributions in locations that approximately correspond to some of the region’s main shipping lanes, suggesting that reduced sulfate emissions could also have played a localized role.

鉴于近几十年已观测到混合层变浅趋势，且预估表明这一趋势将延续至未来，北大西洋海洋热浪的严重程度将进一步加剧。

With a trend towards shallower mixed layers observed over recent decades, and projections that this will continue into the future, the severity of North Atlantic marine heatwaves is set to worsen.

正文

Main

海洋热浪的特征是某一区域海洋温度异常偏暖并持续一段时间[12,13]，有可能严重损害海洋生态系统[14]及依赖这些生态系统的人类系统[15]。

Marine heatwaves are characterized by sustained periods of abnormally warm ocean temperatures in a given region[12,13], with the potential for severe damage to marine ecosystems[14] and reliant human systems[15].

海洋热浪还会影响大气状况，包括改变天气形势以及影响邻近区域的陆地热浪[16,17]。

Marine heatwaves can also influence atmospheric conditions, including altering weather patterns and affecting land-based heatwaves in adjacent regions[16,17].

2023年夏季北大西洋海洋热浪与欧洲大范围极端热浪相伴发生，尤其是在7月；它的强度如此之大，以至于对当年创纪录的全球平均温度作出了显著贡献（文献[18]）。

The 2023 summertime marine heatwave in the North Atlantic was associated with extreme heatwaves over large areas of Europe, particularly during July, and was so large in magnitude that it was a substantial contributor to record global mean temperatures that developed that year (ref. [18]).

6–9月欧洲部分地区还发生了强风暴和致洪暴雨，可能因上游异常偏暖的北大西洋上空蒸发增强和大气水汽含量升高而加剧[19]。

There were also severe storms and flooding rains across parts of Europe during June–September, probably exacerbated by enhanced evaporation and higher atmospheric moisture content upstream over the hotter-than-average North Atlantic[19].

2023年全年降水异常显示，欧洲及英国许多地区的总降雨量相对于1991–2020年处于最高的10–20%范围内（文献[18]）。

Year-long precipitation anomalies for 2023 reveal many regions across Europe and the United Kingdom with total rainfall in the top 10–20% compared with 1991–2020 (ref. [18]).

鉴于北大西洋海洋热浪可能对生态系统造成重大损害，并使欧洲部分地区及其他区域出现严重热浪和致洪暴雨，理解2023年这一非同寻常事件的驱动因素十分重要。

Given the potential for North Atlantic marine heatwaves to cause substantial ecosystem damage and bring about severe heatwaves and flooding rains over parts of Europe and other regions, it is important to understand the drivers of the extraordinary 2023 event.

Further warming of the oceans adjacent to Greenland and in Hudson Bay occurred during August.

An examination of the atmospheric circulation and surface wind speed anomalies (Fig. 2) reveals a coincidence between regions of anomalously weak winds and the regions of anomalous North Atlantic surface warming during June and July described above.

The anomalously weak winds during June 2023 were largely associated with a weakening of the climatological high-pressure system over the North Atlantic (Extended Data Fig. 1).

This weakening of the prevailing anticyclonic circulation is consistent with previously identified teleconnections from a developing El Niño–Southern Oscillation and warmer waters in the tropical eastern Pacific, which act to reduce the northeast trade winds in the Atlantic[20,21].

Further climate or synoptic-scale anomalies probably also played a role, given that the exceptional wind reduction peaked only in June (Extended Data Fig. 2a).

During July 2023, weakened winds then became more pronounced in the northwest Atlantic (Fig. 2c and Extended Data Fig. 2b), coinciding with that region’s surge in SST anomalies (compare Fig. 1e with Fig. 1d), whereas to the east, the weakened wind field contracted.

During August, virtually all of the regions that remained anomalously warm coincided with weaker than average winds (Fig. 2d).

海面风速减弱可通过多种方式影响海气热通量。

Reduced winds over the ocean affect air–sea heat fluxes in several ways.

首先，海洋潜热和感热损失通常都会减少，因为这些热交换与表面风速的平方成比例[22]。

First, both latent and sensible ocean heat loss typically decrease, as these exchanges of heat scale with the square of surface wind speed[22].

海洋蒸发减少又有助于减少低层和中层云量；2023年6月和7月低风速区域确实出现了这种情况（扩展数据图3）。

Reduced ocean evaporation can, in turn, contribute to reduced low-level and mid-level cloudiness, which was indeed the case over regions of low wind speed during June and July 2023 (Extended Data Fig. 3).

查看扩展数据图3 ↗

此外，海面风速减弱可能因海盐飞沫气溶胶减少以及破碎波产生的白沫减少而降低表面反照率[23,24]，使更多入射太阳辐射进入海洋。

Furthermore, weakened winds over the ocean may lead to reduced surface albedo as a result of reduced sea-spray aerosols and reduced white-capping from breaking waves[23,24], which allows more incoming solar radiation to enter the ocean.

因此，低风速与较晴朗天空相结合，有利于增加进入海洋的净表面热通量。

In combination with clearer skies, low wind speeds are thus conducive to an increase in the net surface heat flux entering the ocean.

一旦海洋表层增暖，通常会降低大气边界层稳定度并增强低层干空气混合，从而降低低层相对湿度，减少低层云。

Once the surface layer of the ocean has warmed, this tends to reduce atmospheric boundary layer stability and increase mixing of dry air at low levels, reducing the low-level relative humidity and leading to fewer low-level clouds.

在夏季，这会形成放大反馈，使海洋表层进一步增暖。

In summer, this leads to an amplifying feedback with further surface ocean warming.

The observed net surface heat flux anomalies during summer 2023 as well as the anomalies in the two dominant components—incoming shortwave radiation and outgoing latent heat fluxes—are shown in Extended Data Fig. 4.

This breakdown into constituent terms reveals that the regions of anomalous net ocean heat gain are primarily associated with above-average incoming solar radiation (Extended Data Fig. 4, middle column), with an extra contribution from more localized regions of lower-than-average latent heat loss (Extended Data Fig. 4, right column).

向外长波和感热通量引起的变化要小得多，大部分区域的异常通常小于约10 W m−2（图未示）。

Variations resulting from outgoing longwave and sensible heat fluxes are much smaller, with anomalies generally less than about 10 W m−2 for most regions (figure not shown).

总体而言，6月东部北大西洋以及7月西部北大西洋增暖最强的区域，与海洋表面异常吸热区域相对应（扩展数据图4）。

Overall, the regions of greatest warming during June in the eastern North Atlantic and July in the western North Atlantic correspond to regions of anomalous surface ocean heat gain (Extended Data Fig. 4).

相比之下，7月东部北大西洋大部分海域出现海洋异常失热（扩展数据图4g）；这与西欧邻近大气增暖相关，并对该区域7月的高温和热浪有所贡献[19]。

By contrast, there is anomalous ocean heat loss across much of the eastern North Atlantic during July (Extended Data Fig. 4g), which has been linked to a warming of the atmosphere adjacent to western Europe that contributed to the hot temperatures and heatwaves seen across the region during July[19].

这些增强的蒸发通量也可能促成了欧洲7月的异常降雨[19]。

These enhanced evaporative fluxes might also have contributed to the anomalous rainfall that occurred over Europe during July[19].

除上述海气热交换过程外，减弱的东北信风还延伸至撒哈拉沙漠上空，尤其是在2023年6月（图2b）。

As well as the air–sea heat exchange processes described above, the weakened northeasterly trade winds extended over the Sahara Desert, particularly during June 2023 (Fig. 2b).

此前研究已将东北信风减弱与向西进入大西洋的沙尘输送减少联系起来[25]。

Weakened northeast trades have previously been linked to a reduction in dust transport westward into the Atlantic Ocean[25].

撒哈拉沙尘输送的这种减少与暖SST异常有关，因为沙尘会在入射太阳辐射到达海面之前将其散射[26]。

Such a reduction in Saharan dust transport has been associated with warm SST anomalies because dust acts to scatter incoming solar radiation before it reaches the ocean surface[26].

另一个被提出用于解释2023年入射太阳辐射增加的因素，是国际海事组织2020年协议实施后主要航道沿线的硫酸盐气溶胶排放减少[27,28]。

Another factor proposed to account for the increase in incoming solar radiation in 2023 is reduced sulfate aerosol emissions along the main shipping lanes following the International Maritime Organization 2020 agreement[27,28].

然而，近期研究指出，考虑到地球能量不平衡存在较大年际变率，以及这些变化实施以来的观测记录较短，这一信号在海盆尺度上可能难以检测[29]。

However, recent work has suggested that this signal may be difficult to detect on a basin scale given the large interannual variability in Earth’s energy imbalance and the short observational record since these changes came into effect[29].

尽管这一问题仍在持续研究，本文后面将表明，对于整个北大西洋表层创纪录的增暖而言，太阳辐射异常的相对贡献小于混合层变浅的影响。

Although this topic remains an area of continued research, we will later show that, in terms of the record-breaking basin-wide warming of the surface North Atlantic, the relative contribution of solar radiation anomalies was less than the impact of shoaling mixed layers.

夏季海洋表层形成明显层结，温暖表水覆盖在较冷深水之上。

During summer, the surface layer of the ocean becomes well stratified, with warm surface waters overlying colder deep waters.

因此，这一季节的混合层深度（MLD）主要取决于表面风强度；风会在由其驱动的湍流混合层内混合和翻动表层海水。

Mixed layer depth (MLD) during this season is thus largely set by the strength of surface winds, which act to mix and overturn surface waters within the wind-driven turbulent mixed layer.

因此，2023年夏季风速创纪录偏低的区域大体与异常浅混合层相吻合，并不令人意外（图2和3；其他海洋再分析产品的比较见扩展数据图10）。

It is little surprise then that the regions of record-low wind speeds during summer 2023 largely coincided with unusually shallow mixed layers (Figs. 2 and 3; other ocean reanalysis products are compared in Extended Data Fig. 10).

6月在东部、随后7月在西部，海面创纪录的弱风使这些区域的MLD达到有记录以来最浅水平。

During June in the east and then subsequently July in the west, record weak winds over the surface of the ocean led to the shallowest ever recorded MLDs in those regions.

再加上比平均状态更晴朗的天空、较低云反照率[27]以及跨海气界面的净吸热，产生了一种放大反馈，使北大西洋大部分海域形成极浅、极暖的混合层。

Coupled with clearer-than-average skies, lower cloud albedo[27] and net heat gain across the air–sea interface, this led to an amplifying feedback that produced very shallow and very warm mixed layers across much of the North Atlantic Ocean.

尽管表层增暖先后分别在6月的东部北大西洋以及7月的西北部北大西洋发展，两次突发表层增暖似乎受相同的总体机制控制，即由异常弱风、晴朗天空和浅混合层驱动的表层加热。

Although the progression of surface warming played out separately over the eastern North Atlantic during June and then in the northwestern North Atlantic during July, the same overall mechanism seems to have controlled both bursts of surface warming; namely, surface heating driven by anomalously weak winds, clear skies and shallow mixed layers.

大西洋主要航道沿线硫酸盐气溶胶排放减少[27,28,31,32]也被提出是2023年创纪录增暖的驱动因素。

Reduced sulfate aerosol emissions along the main Atlantic shipping lanes[27,28,31,32] has also been proposed as a driver of the record-breaking 2023 warming.

通过分别假定（1）气候态海气热通量和（2）气候态MLD，重新计算北大西洋温度收支异常（图4a和5a），我们证明，在海盆尺度上，异常弱风造成的浅混合层是北大西洋全海盆创纪录增暖的主导驱动因素。

By recalculating the North Atlantic temperature budget anomalies assuming (1) climatological air–sea heat fluxes and (2) climatological MLDs (Figs. 4a and 5a), we have demonstrated that, at a basin scale, shallow mixed layers resulting from anomalous weak winds were the dominant driver of the record basin-wide North Atlantic warming.

我们还发现证据表明，航运排放减少可能发挥了局地作用：异常晴朗的天空沿着一条与该区域主要航道相吻合的纬度带增强了增暖异常（扩展数据图9）。

We also find evidence that reduced shipping emissions could have played a localized role, with anomalous clear skies contributing enhanced warming anomalies along a band of latitudes coinciding with the region’s main shipping lanes (Extended Data Fig. 9).

Given that ERA5 does not include interannual variability in shipping emissions, we further tested these results by recalculating the MLT budget with an additional +1 W m−2 anomaly in incoming solar radiation applied everywhere over the North Atlantic (Methods).

这一短波辐射异常约为文献[31]所给出的航运排放减少对北大西洋影响的海盆平均估计值的两倍，而该文献数值已处于现有估计的高端。

This shortwave radiation anomaly is approximately double the basin-average estimate for the impact of reduced shipping emissions over the North Atlantic in ref. [31], which is at the high end of available estimates.

在展示的所有模式和观测分析中，增加1 W m−2太阳辐射后结果仍然稳健，所示结果仅有极小变化（例如见扩展数据图11）。

In all analyses shown, both model and observations, our results remain robust to this addition of 1 W m−2 solar radiation, with minimal changes to the results shown (see, for example, Extended Data Fig. 11).

An analysis of the past few decades of ocean and atmosphere measurements reveals no notable trend in surface wind speed over the North Atlantic Ocean, but there is a marked trend towards shallower MLDs across all seasons (Fig. 3e and, for summer, Extended Data Figs. 7a, 8a), as well as a notable trend towards increased incoming solar radiation in summer (Extended Data Fig. 7c).

In the absence of surface wind trends, the mixed layer shoaling trend must be driven by a combination of stronger summer heating and surface freshwater input, both of which act to enhance the stratification of the upper ocean.

This analysis reveals that the shoaling North Atlantic mixed layer trend over the past four decades is primarily driven by surface ocean warming, with a smaller contribution from surface freshening in the tropics and subpolar latitudes.

多年代际海洋表层增暖主要来自长期人为增暖，而入射太阳辐射的趋势可能提供额外贡献（根据ERA5数据估计为每十年+0.58 W m−2；扩展数据图7c）；正如既有研究所指出，这与航运排放减少和太阳活动周期变化相一致[35]。

The multidecadal surface ocean warming is primarily because of long-term anthropogenic warming, with a possible further contribution from a trend in incoming solar radiation (estimated to be +0.58 W m−2 per decade from ERA5 data; Extended Data Fig. 7c), which is consistent with reduced shipping emissions and variations in the solar cycle, as noted in previous work[35].

混合层变浅趋势为大西洋出现更高概率的严重夏季海洋热浪提供了前期条件，这类热浪与2023年观测到的类型相同。

A trend towards shallower mixed layers preconditions the Atlantic for an increase in the likelihood of severe summertime marine heatwaves of the type observed in 2023.

北太平洋也已发现类似的严重海洋热浪前期条件[36,37]。

Similar preconditioning of severe marine heatwaves has also been noted for the North Pacific Ocean[36,37].

总结与结论

Summary and conclusions

总之，2023年夏季北大西洋表层混合层的异常增暖，在观测记录中无论空间尺度还是强度均前所未有，形成了接近全海盆范围的异常，并在海盆大部分地区一直持续至今。

To summarize, the anomalous warming of the surface mixed layer in the North Atlantic Ocean during summer 2023 was unprecedented in both spatial scale and magnitude over the observational record, forming a near-basin-wide anomaly that has persisted over much of the basin until today.

我们识别出，这次北大西洋极端海洋热浪的主导驱动因素是低风速和异常浅混合层，同时异常晴朗的天空和增强的海洋表面热通量提供了区域性贡献。

We identify the dominant drivers of this extreme North Atlantic marine heatwave to be low wind speeds and anomalously shallow mixed layers, alongside a regional contribution from anomalous clear skies and increased surface ocean heat flux.

此外，本研究强调混合层变浅对极端夏季海洋热浪发展的重要性，因为在气候态增暖季节，即使没有进入海洋的异常辐射或湍流热通量，浅混合层也能导致快速表层增暖。

Moreover, our study highlights the importance of a shoaling mixed layer in the development of extreme summertime marine heatwaves, because during seasons of climatological warming, a shallow mixed layer can lead to rapid surface warming without the need for anomalous radiation or turbulent heat fluxes into the ocean.

2023年观测到的创纪录浅混合层，似乎还受到北大西洋已观测长期混合层变浅趋势的增强，而该趋势预计将延续至未来。

The record shallow mixed layers seen in 2023 seem to have been bolstered by a long-term mixed layer shoaling trend observed in the North Atlantic, one that is projected to continue into the future.

因此，预计未来几年及更长时期内发生类似事件的可能性将增加。

The likelihood of similar events occurring is thus expected to increase in the coming years and beyond.

鉴于盛行大气环流将海洋气团输送至欧洲及其他区域，同时夏季加热区邻近格陵兰及大西洋经向翻转环流源区水体，这可能对未来区域乃至全球尺度气候产生重大影响。

With the prevailing atmospheric circulation moving oceanic air masses towards Europe and other regions, alongside the proximity of the summertime heating to Greenland and the source waters for the Atlantic meridional overturning circulation, this has potential major implications for future climate at regional to global scales.

Methods

观测与再分析数据

Observational and reanalysis data

本文分析的大气场和海气通量场取自欧洲中期天气预报中心（ECMWF）第五代再分析资料（ERA5）[38]，使用1981–2023年由逐小时数据得到的日平均场和月平均场，水平网格分辨率为1/4°。

The atmospheric and air–sea flux fields analysed in this study are taken from the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) Reanalysis Version 5 (ERA5)[38], using daily mean fields derived from hourly data and monthly averaged fields from 1981 to 2023 over a 1/4° horizontal resolution grid.

所分析ERA5变量包括表面风速（m s−1）及表面风矢量、表面海气热通量（W m−2）、平均海平面气压（hPa）和云覆盖率。

Variables analysed from ERA5 include surface wind speed (m s−1) and surface wind vectors, surface air–sea heat fluxes (W m−2), mean sea-level pressure (hPa) and cloud cover fraction.

SST（°C）也取自ERA5。

SST (°C) is also taken from ERA5.

净海气热通量等于入射短波辐射减去向外潜热、感热和长波辐射热通量之和。

The net air–sea heat flux is the sum of incoming shortwave radiation less outgoing latent, sensible and longwave radiative heat fluxes.

表面净短波（太阳）辐射对应于到达地球表面的太阳辐射量（含直接及散射辐射），减去由地球表面反射的量（由反照率控制）。

The surface net shortwave (solar) radiation corresponds to the amount of solar radiation that reaches the surface of the Earth (both direct and diffuse) minus the amount reflected by the Earth’s surface (which is governed by the albedo).

ERA5再分析在估计表面净短波（太阳）辐射时，仅计入气候态气溶胶效应和大型火山喷发导致的气溶胶变化。

In the ERA5 reanalysis, only climatological aerosol effects and aerosol changes due to large volcanic eruptions are incorporated into the estimate of the net surface shortwave (solar) radiation.

具体来说，云及大气颗粒物（气溶胶）会将部分入射太阳辐射反射回太空，但除火山喷发外，并未计入气溶胶效应的年际变化。

In particular, the incoming solar radiation is partly reflected back to space because of both clouds as well as particles in the atmosphere (aerosols), but without interannual variability in aerosol effects aside from volcanic eruptions.

其余辐射到达地表，其中一部分因表面反照率效应而被反射。

The remainder is incident on the Earth’s surface, for which some of it is reflected owing to surface albedo effects.

ERA5同化多种卫星观测的云液态水，详见ERA5在线文档，并将云覆盖率和云反照率表述为再分析的地表及单层参数。

ERA5 assimilates cloud liquid water from a variety of satellites, as detailed in the ERA5 online documentation, formulating cloud fraction and cloud albedo as surface and single-level parameters of the reanalysis.

为检验结果对航运排放减少所产生气溶胶效应的敏感性，我们进一步分析观测和模式MLT收支，分别在2023年所有月份的太阳辐射项上增加1 W m−2，以及仅在太阳辐射效应最强的5–8月夏季月份增加该值。

To test the sensitivity of our results to aerosol effects from reduced shipping emissions, we further analysed the observed and modelled MLT budgets by adding 1 W m−2 to the solar radiation terms for all months in 2023, and also to just the summer months May–August when solar radiation effects are at their maximum.

增加的+1 W m−2热通量约为文献[31]对北大西洋航运排放减少影响所估计海盆平均值0.56 W m−2的两倍，而后者已处于现有估计的高端。

The added heat flux of +1 W m−2 is approximately double the basin-average estimate for the impact of reduced shipping emissions over the North Atlantic of 0.56 W m−2 in ref. [31], which is at the high end of available estimates.

在所有情况下，增加1 W m−2太阳辐射后结果仍然稳健，所示结果的变化可忽略不计（扩展数据图11）。

In all cases our results remain robust to this addition of 1 W m−2 solar radiation, with negligible changes to the results shown (Extended Data Fig. 11).

查看扩展数据图11 ↗

次表层海洋温度和MLD取自一个基于观测的数据集，即大气物理研究所（IAP）海洋水文数据[39,40,41]第3版，覆盖1981–2023年，水平分辨率为1°（下称“IAP数据”）。

Subsurface ocean temperature and MLDs are obtained from an observation-based dataset; the Institute of Atmospheric Physics (IAP) hydrographic data[39,40,41] version 3 available from 1981 to 2023 with 1° horizontal resolution (hereafter referred to as ‘IAP data’).

主要关注的纬度带覆盖赤道至60° N的北大西洋，基本避开数据覆盖较差的区域，如高纬度地区，尤其是冰覆盖区。

The main latitude band of interest spans the North Atlantic from the equator to 60° N, which mostly avoids regions of low data coverage, such as the high-latitude regions, particularly ice-covered areas.

不过，Argo浮标覆盖稀疏仍是边缘海面临的问题，尽管IAP网格化气候资料融合了Argo及其他历史海洋水文观测，包括投弃式测温仪（XBT）数据。

Sparse coverage by Argo floats is, however, a problem in marginal seas, although the gridded IAP climatology merges both Argo and other historical hydrographic observations, including XBTs.

尽管如此，海洋内部温度与MLD的可靠重建仅能达到月平均分辨率。

Nonetheless, reliable reconstruction of interior ocean temperature and MLD is only available at monthly mean resolution.

我们应用TEOS-10软件[43]中的Gibbs海水（GSW）海洋学工具箱[42]，将IAP原位温度转换为保守温度[44]。

The Gibbs SeaWater (GSW) Oceanographic Toolbox[42] from the TEOS-10 software[43] is applied to convert the IAP in situ temperature to conservative temperature[44].

通过使用垂向稳定化软件[45]，使水柱在月时间尺度上达到静力稳定。

Static stability of the water column on timescales of months is achieved by applying vertical stabilization software[45].

随后利用GSW海洋学工具箱中的函数，计算以海表为参考的位温θ及位密度ρ。

The surface-referenced potential temperature, θ, and potential density, ρ, are then calculated using functions from the GSW Oceanographic Toolbox.

遵循既有研究[46]，海洋MLD定义为水柱月平均位密度首次比10 m深度的密度大0.125 kg m−3时的深度。

Ocean MLD is calculated as the depth at which the monthly averaged potential density in the water column first exceeds the density at 10-m depth by 0.125 kg m−3, following previous work[46].

我们采用0.03 kg m−3密度阈值重复了MLD异常及相关MLT收支分析，本文结果对这一选择具有稳健性。

The MLD anomalies and the related MLT budget analyses were repeated using a density threshold of 0.03 kg m−3 and the results presented here are robust to this choice.

还将参考深度由10 m改为5 m，重新评估MLD异常，得到了稳健的空间格局。

MLD anomalies were also re-evaluated using a reference depth of 5 m instead of 10 m and robust patterns were obtained.

我们还分别计算了T和S对表面密度趋势的贡献（扩展数据图8）。

The separate contributions of T and S to the surface density trends were also calculated (Extended Data Fig. 8).

查看扩展数据图8 ↗

为此，将月气候态盐度场（1981–2010年平均）与随时间变化的温度场结合，推导温度贡献。

This was done by combining the monthly climatological salinity field (averaged during 1981–2010) with the time-varying temperature fields to derive the temperature contribution.

在估计盐度对密度趋势的影响时，则反过来使用气候平均温度场以及随时间变化的盐度场。

When estimating the salinity effects on density trends, the opposite was done, namely, using the climatological mean temperature fields alongside the time-varying salinity fields.

通过检查IAPv4、ORAS5和EN4.2.2数据集集合平均（EN4-ESM）中相应的MLD异常，我们评估了由IAPv3数据估计的2023年MLD异常的稳健性。

The robustness of the estimated 2023 anomalies in MLD derived from IAPv3 data was evaluated by examining the corresponding MLD anomalies in the IAPv4, ORAS5 and ensemble mean of EN4.2.2 datasets (EN4-ESM).

四个数据集在2023年均得到总体一致的创纪录浅MLD异常格局（扩展数据图10）。

Overall consistent patterns of record shallow MLD anomalies were obtained during 2023 in all four datasets (Extended Data Fig. 10).

查看扩展数据图10 ↗

然而，不同产品估计的MLD及上层海洋温度梯度长期多年代际趋势存在差异（扩展数据图10），与此前对北太平洋的发现一致[36]。

However, there are variations in the long-term multidecadal trends in MLD and upper ocean temperature gradients estimated by the different products (Extended Data Fig. 10), consistent with previous findings for the North Pacific[36].

查看扩展数据图10 ↗

长期趋势差异部分源自Argo时期之前的数据稀疏，但近期记录中也存在差异。

The differences in long-term trends are in part the result of data sparsity before the Argo period, although variations can also be seen in the more recent record.

还应注意，EN再分析在估计长期趋势方面可靠性较低[47]，因为没有任何观测时，其缺测值会松弛至气候态。

Note also that the EN reanalysis is less reliable for long-term trend estimates[47], as missing values relax to climatology in the absence of any observations.

尽管如此，在所考察的所有海洋再分析中，2023年相对于以往年份都出现了混合层突然异常变浅。

Nonetheless, in all ocean reanalyses considered, 2023 sees a sudden anomalous shoaling of the mixed layer compared with previous years.

海洋模式模拟

Ocean model simulations

为评估2023年北大西洋最强增暖的驱动机制，我们积分了两组分别由不同观测大气再分析场（ERA5和JRA55-do）强迫的海洋模式模拟。

Two ocean model simulations forced by different observation-based atmospheric reanalysis fields, namely, ERA5 and JRA55-do, were integrated to evaluate the driving mechanisms of the North Atlantic peak warming during 2023.

两组模式模拟均捕捉到2023年北大西洋表层增暖开始阶段的时间演变，其中ERA5强迫模拟尤为突出（图1a）。

The two model simulations both capture the temporal evolution of the onset of surface layer warming over the North Atlantic during 2023, particularly the ERA5-forced simulation (Fig. 1a).

模式配置采用澳大利亚社区气候与地球系统模拟器海洋模式第2版（ACCESS-OM2），名义水平分辨率为0.25°，垂向50层（表层网格厚度2.3 m）。

The model configurations are taken from the Australian Community Climate and Earth System Simulator Ocean Model Version 2 (ACCESS-OM2) with the configurations run at a nominal 0.25° horizontal resolution with 50 vertical levels (surface grid cell thickness of 2.3 m).

ACCESS-OM2是由给定大气驱动的全球海洋–海冰模式；此前文献已描述本文使用的版本[48]，后续工作说明了相关改进[49]。

ACCESS-OM2 is a global ocean–sea-ice model driven by a prescribed atmosphere; the version used here has been described previously[48], with improvements described by follow-up work[49].

海洋模式分量为模块化海洋模式（MOM）5.1版（文献[50]），与洛斯阿拉莫斯海冰模式5.1.2版（文献[51]）耦合。

The ocean model component is the Modular Ocean Model (MOM) version 5.1 (ref. [50]), coupled to the Los Alamos sea ice model version 5.1.2 (ref. [51]).

边界层垂向混合采用K剖面参数化（KPP[52]）。

Vertical mixing in the boundary layer is parameterized using the K profile parameterization (KPP[52]).

更多模式细节可见已发表文献[48,49]。

Further model details can be found in previously published work[48,49].

两组海洋模式模拟的强迫设置如下。

The forcing of the two ocean model simulations is as follows.

本文主要分析的试验称为ERA5模拟，由ERA5大气再分析场强迫[38]。

In the main run analysed here, referred to as the ERA5 simulation, the model is forced by ERA5 atmospheric reanalysis fields[38].

第二组模拟称为JRA55试验，使用面向海洋–海冰模式强迫的日本55年再分析（JRA55-do）数据集。

In the second simulation, referred to as the JRA55 run, the Japanese 55-year Reanalysis (JRA55-do) dataset for forcing ocean–sea-ice models is used.

在JRA55试验中，先由1958–2018年JRA55-do v1.4场强迫运行六个循环以完成预积分（遵循OMIP-5预积分方案[53]），随后将第六个循环延长：2019年使用JRA55-do v1.5.0，之后使用延迟模式JRA55-do v1.5.0.1至2023年底（文献[54]）。

In the JRA55 run, after spinning up over six cycles forced by the 1958–2018 JRA55-do v1.4 fields (following the OMIP-5 spinup protocol[53]), the sixth cycle is extended using JRA55-do v1.5.0 for 2019 and the delayed-mode JRA55-do v1.5.0.1 to the end of 2023 (ref. [54]).

ERA5试验于1957年底初始化，随后利用ERA5大气强迫运行1958–2023年，径流取自JRA55-do（与JRA55试验相同）。

The ERA5 run is initialized at the end of 1957 and then run over 1958–2023 using ERA5 atmospheric forcing, with runoff taken from JRA55-do (as for the JRA55 run).

两组模式试验均使用《世界海洋图集2013》v2版的温度和盐度场初始化[55,56]。

Both model runs were initialized using World Ocean Atlas 2013 v2 temperature and salinity fields[55,56].

本文分析的模式数据包括ERA5和JRA55模拟的SST与表面热通量；对于ERA5试验，还包括重建表层MLT收支（详见下文）所需的所有变量，既涵盖2023年，也涵盖自1981年起模拟的所有年份，以构建1981–2010年基准气候平均MLT收支，并据此评估2023年异常场。

The model data analysed here include SST and surface heat fluxes from both the ERA5 and JRA55 simulations and, for the ERA5 run, all variables required to reconstruct a surface MLT budget (detailed below) both during 2023 and also during all years of the model simulation from 1981 onwards, to construct the baseline 1981–2010 climatological mean MLT budget from which we evaluate the 2023 anomaly fields.

两组模式试验的北大西洋SST演变总体与观测一致（图1a），2023年北大西洋混合层变浅格局亦如此（扩展数据图6）。

The North Atlantic SST evolution of the two model runs is, overall, consistent with observations (Fig. 1a), as is the pattern of MLD shoaling across the North Atlantic during 2023 (Extended Data Fig. 6).

2023年海气表面热通量异常的幅度也与观测一致，5–8月净表面热通量异常对MLT收支贡献的分解表明了这一点（比较图4a和5a）。

The magnitude of air–sea surface heat flux anomalies during 2023 is also consistent with observations, as revealed in the breakdown of the anomalous net surface heat flux contributions to the MLT budget during May–August (compare Figs. 4a and 5a).

两组模式试验与观测中，净表面热通量驱动的MLT增暖空间分布也较为一致。

The geographic pattern of net surface-heat-flux-driven MLT warming also compares well between both model runs and observations.

Surface layer temperature budget

上层海洋MLT收支可写为

A budget for the upper ocean MLT can be written as

∂θh∂t=Qnet−Qhρ0cph+平流+卷入+混合

∂θh∂t=Qnet−Qhρ0cph+advection+entrainment+mixing

其中，θh为MLT，t为时间，Qnet为净表面海气热通量（W m−2），Qh为短波辐射穿过混合层底部所对应的热通量（同为W m−2），ρ0为海水密度（取1,027 kg m−3），cp = 3,992 J K−1 kg−1为海水比热容，h为MLD（m）；advection表示三维海洋平流引起的净加热，entrainment表示与混合层变浅或加深相关的热通量，mixing项包括垂向混合、涡致混合等参数化次网格过程导致的净热通量。

in which θh is the MLT, t is time, Qnet is the net surface air–sea heat flux (W m−2), Qh is the heat flux corresponding to shortwave radiation penetration across the base of the mixed layer (also in W m−2), ρ0 is seawater density (taken to be 1,027 kg m−3), cp = 3,992 J K−1 kg−1 is the heat capacity of seawater, h is the MLD (m), advection represents net heating owing to three-dimensional ocean advection, entrainment represents the heat flux associated with mixed layer shoaling or deepening and the mixing term includes the net heat flux owing to parameterized subgrid-scale processes such as vertical mixing and eddy-induced mixing.

卷入项可写为：

The entrainment term can be written as:

−1h∂h∂t(θh−θent),

−1h∂h∂t(θh−θent),

表示与混合层变浅或加深相关的卷入，其中θent为脱卷入/卷入水体的温度，θh为MLT。

representing entrainment associated with mixed layer shoaling or deepening, with θent the temperature of detrained/entrained water and θh the MLT.

模式中穿过混合层底部的短波辐射（Qh）由MOM5在积分时计算，并作为热收支诊断量输出；其中，短波辐射随深度分配至海洋内部[57]，并受季节气候态叶绿素分布调制[58]。

The shortwave penetration across the base of the mixed layer (Qh) in the model is computed online and output as a heat budget diagnostic by MOM5, in which shortwave radiation is distributed into the ocean interior as a function of depth[57], modulated by the seasonal climatological chlorophyll distribution[58].

基于观测估计的穿过混合层底部的短波辐射取为Qh = QSW · F(z)，其中QSW为ERA5再分析给出的跨海气界面短波辐射，F(z)为指数衰减函数[59]，

The penetration of shortwave radiation across the base of the mixed layer for the observationally based estimate is taken as Qh = QSW. F(z), in which QSW is the shortwave radiation across the air–sea interface from the ERA5 reanalysis and F(z) is the exponential decay function[59],

F(z)=Re−zh1+(1−R)e−zh2

F(z)=Re−zh1+(1−R)e−zh2

其中z为深度（向下为正），R = 0.58，h1 = 0.35 m，h2 = 23 m。

where z is depth (positive downwards), R = 0.58, h1 = 0.35 m and h2 = 23 m.

就表面热通量异常对MLT倾向项贡献的幅度而言，两组海洋模式模拟总体得到稳健结果（图未示），包括其短波、长波、感热和潜热通量分项。

The two ocean model simulations give, overall, robust results in terms of the magnitude of the anomalous surface heat flux contributions to the MLT tendency terms (figure not shown), including the breakdown into shortwave, longwave, sensible and latent heat flux terms.

更详细的MLT收支分析侧重于ERA5试验，该试验在积分时保存了量化MLT平衡月变化所需的所有项，包括三维海洋平流和混合，详见下文。

The focus of our more detailed MLT budget analysis is the ERA5 run, which was integrated with all required terms saved to quantify monthly variations in the MLT balance, including three-dimensional ocean advection and mixing, as described below.

ERA5模式温度收支还可以与利用观测约束ERA5再分析以及IAP表层海洋温度和混合层场估计得到的收支进行比较。

The temperature budget of the ERA5 model can also be compared with estimates using the observationally based ERA5 reanalysis combined with IAP estimates of surface ocean temperature and mixed layer fields.

然而，现有观测数据不足以约束其他表层MLT收支项，例如海洋平流、混合及垂向卷入。

However, insufficient observational data are available to constrain the other surface MLT budget terms, such as ocean advection and mixing, as well as vertical entrainment.

尽管如此，我们仍可比较模式与观测再分析场中的表面海气热通量贡献。

We can nonetheless compare the surface air–sea heat flux contributions across the model and observed reanalysis fields.

模式MLT收支中的所有项，包括海洋平流、混合和卷入的全部分量，均可通过将完全闭合的逐网格模式热收支[50,60,61]在表层混合层内积分（此处采用与上述观测MLD相同的定义），再除以表层MLD来诊断。

All terms in the model MLT budget, including all components of ocean advection, mixing and entrainment, can be diagnosed by integrating the fully closed cell-by-cell model heat budget[50,60,61] over the surface mixed layer (defined here using the same definition as the observed MLD, described above) and dividing by the surface MLD.

这里使用月平均热收支诊断量和月平均MLD进行积分。

Here this integration is performed using monthly averaged heat budget diagnostics and the monthly averaged MLD.

卷入项是三维模式热收支中唯一未显式包含的项，以残差方式计算：即MLT倾向（通过每月月初和月末的温度与MLD瞬时场差值计算）与总混合层热含量倾向（即所有逐点热收支过程之和除以ρ0cph）之差。

The entrainment term, the only term not explicitly included in the three-dimensional model heat budget, is computed by residual as the difference between the tendency of the MLT (computed by taking the difference of snapshots of the temperature and the MLD at the beginning and end of each month) and the total mixed layer heat content tendency (that is, the sum of all point-by-point heat budget processes, divided by ρ0cph).

这一计算可得到闭合收支，但忽略了MLD月内变化与逐网格模式热收支项之间的相关。

Such a calculation produces a closed budget but neglects correlations between submonthly variations in the MLD and cell-by-cell model heat budget terms.

不过，针对2023年比较这些月平均收支项与利用日平均值获得的对应项（日平均仅2023年可用）后发现，差异很小（量级低于5%；图未示）。

However, a comparison performed in 2023 between these monthly averaged budget terms and similar terms obtained using daily averages (only available for 2023) showed only small differences (order less than 5%; figure not shown).

观测MLT收支分析（图5）通过比较估计的混合层储热速率与仅由表面热通量驱动的加热和冷却（扣除穿过混合层底部的短波辐射）得到；残差隐含包含全部海洋平流、卷入和混合项，以及空间和时间数据覆盖不完整、MLD与温度估计不确定性、ERA5净海气热通量不确定性（同时涉及2023年及1981–2010年基准期）所产生的任何误差。

The MLT budget analysis for observations (Fig. 5) is derived by comparing the estimated mixed layer heat storage rate to the heating and cooling driven by surface heat fluxes alone (less shortwave penetration at the base of the mixed layer), with the residual implicitly including all ocean advection, entrainment and mixing terms, as well as any errors owing to incomplete data coverage in space and time, uncertainty in estimating MLDs and temperatures and uncertainty in the ERA5 net air–sea heat fluxes (both in 2023 and in the baseline period 1981–2010).

与模式模拟一样，观测收支也忽略了表面热通量与MLD变化在月内的相关，尽管对日和月时间尺度模式收支的分析表明这一项较小。

As for the model simulations, the observational budget also neglects submonthly correlations between the surface heat flux and MLD variations, although analysis of the model budget at daily and monthly timescales suggests that this term is small.

MLT倾向∂θh/∂t根据逐月观测，采用月平均MLT的二阶中心差分计算。

The MLT tendency, ∂θh/∂t, is calculated from monthly observations as a centred second-order difference of monthly mean MLT.

由于观测仅提供月MLD值，这使MLT增暖信号相对于具有日分辨率的模式MLT收支受到明显平滑。

With only monthly MLD values available from observations, this results in substantial smoothing of the MLT warming signal compared with the daily resolved model MLT budget.

观测计算中的残差项格局显示出一些特征，包括海洋环流作用引起的未分辨温度变化，既包括湾流等处的水平变化，也包括混合层底部卷入和垂向混合导致的垂向变化。

The pattern of residual terms seen in the observed calculation reveals features that include unresolved temperature changes due to ocean circulation effects both laterally, such as in the Gulf Stream, and also vertically, because of entrainment and vertical mixing at the base of the mixed layer.

相比之下，模式MLT收支显式分辨了海洋环流和混合的贡献，因此能够展示所有项。

By contrast, the MLT budget of the model explicitly resolves the contributions from ocean circulation and mixing, and so all terms can be shown.

我们进一步重新计算2023年的MLT收支短波辐射异常，分别考察2023年MLD异常、表面入射短波辐射异常以及穿过混合层底部辐射异常对MLT的影响。

The shortwave radiation anomalies in the MLT budget are further diagnosed by recalculating these anomalies during 2023 but separately considering the effects on MLT of 2023 anomalies in MLD, surface incoming shortwave radiation and radiation through the base of the mixed layer.

这一计算仅是重新计算2023年MLT收支：将其他变量全部固定在气候平均值，再分别引入2023年的MLD异常（MLD′）、表面短波热通量异常（QSW′）以及跨混合层底部辐射通量引起的异常（QSW,H′）。

This calculation simply recomputes the 2023 MLT budget holding all other variables at their climatological mean, then separately including the 2023 anomalies in MLD (MLD′), surface shortwave heat flux (QSW′) and anomalies owing to the radiative flux across the base of the mixed layer (QSW,H′).

ERA5强迫模式试验和观测所得数值分别展示在图4a、5a及扩展数据图9中。

The resulting values are shown from the ERA5-forced model run and observations in Figs. 4a and 5a and Extended Data Fig. 9, respectively.

查看扩展数据图9 ↗

这一分解表示，在MLD′、QSW′和QSW,H′这三个因素中仅有一个按2023年数值变化的假设下，MLT收支异常中短波辐射分量的符号和幅度。

This decomposition indicates the sign and magnitude of the shortwave radiation component of the MLT budget anomalies assuming that only one of these three factors (MLD′, QSW′ and QSW,H′) varied with its 2023 values.

例如，MLD′数值表示在入射短波辐射及穿过混合层底部的辐射均遵循气候平均状态的假设下，2023年短波辐射项的大小。

For example, the MLD′ values indicate the size of the 2023 shortwave radiation term assuming that the incoming shortwave radiation, and the radiation through the base of the mixed layer, followed the climatological mean.

相反，QSW′项令MLD和QSW,H遵循气候平均状态，而QSW按其2023年数值变化。

The QSW′ term conversely takes MLD and QSW,H to follow the climatological mean, with QSW varying with its 2023 values.

最后，QSW,H′假定QSW和MLD遵循气候平均状态，仅穿过混合层底部的辐射通量按2023年数值演变。

Last, QSW,H′ assumes that QSW and MLD follow the climatological mean, with only the radiative flux through the base of the mixed layer evolving with its 2023 values.

这一方法能够简便地评估哪些因素对2023年北大西洋夏季增暖的形成最重要，尽管根据定义，这些贡献相加并不等于1。

This approach is a simple way to evaluate what factors were the most important in generating the summertime warming in the North Atlantic during 2023, although the contributions are not additive to unity by definition.
