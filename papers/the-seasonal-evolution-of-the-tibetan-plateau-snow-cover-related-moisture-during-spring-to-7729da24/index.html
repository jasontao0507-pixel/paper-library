Using the objectively analyzed FGGE II-b upper-air data, the large-scale circulation, heat sources and moisture sinks over the Tibetan Plateau and surrounding areas are examined for a 9-month period from December 1978 to August 1979. In addition to the FGGE data, special soundings obtained during the Chinese Qinghai-Xizang (Tibet) Plateau Meteorological Experiment (QXPMEX) from May to August 1979 are also used in the objective analyses.

Chinese / 中文

利用客观分析的 FGGE II-b 高空资料，考察了1978年12月至1979年8月这9个月期间青藏高原及其周边地区的大尺度环流、热源和水汽汇。除 FGGE 资料外，客观分析还使用了中国青海—西藏（青藏）高原气象试验（QXPMEX）于1979年5月至8月获得的特殊探空资料。

front-p01-s009 · PDF p.1
Source / 源文

The evolution of the large-scale flow patterns, temperature, outgoing longwave radiation (OLR) and vertical circulation is described in order to identify the distinct seasonal changes from winter to summer that lead to the onset of the Asian summer monsoon. The Tibetan Plateau maintains a large-scale thermally driven vertical circulation which is originally separated from the planetary-scale monsoon system. The rising motion exists only on the western Plateau in winter and then spreads to the whole Plateau as the season progresses. The monsoon onset over Asia is an interaction process between the Plateau-induced circulation and the circulation associated with the principal rainbelt migrating northward.

Chinese / 中文

为识别导致亚洲夏季风爆发的、从冬季到夏季的显著季节变化，描述了大尺度流场、温度、向外长波辐射（OLR）和垂直环流的演变。青藏高原维持着一个由热力驱动的大尺度垂直环流，该环流起初与行星尺度季风系统相分离。冬季上升运动仅存在于高原西部，随后随着季节推进扩展到整个高原。亚洲季风的爆发是高原诱导环流与随主要雨带向北迁移的环流之间的相互作用过程。

Source / 源文

During winter the Plateau is a heat sink, but it is surrounded by regions of more intense cooling. In spring the Plateau becomes a heat source, but the cooling in the surrounding areas continues. The sensible heat flux from the surface provides the major source of heating on the Plateau. However, additional contribution from condensation heating is observed in the western Plateau during all seasons and, more significantly, in the eastern Plateau during summer. The sensible heating of the elevated Plateau surface and the radiative cooling in the environment maintain the horizontal temperature contrast that drives the thermally direct vertical circulation.

Chinese / 中文

冬季高原是冷源，但其周围被更强的冷却区包围。春季高原转变为热源，而周边地区的冷却仍在持续。来自地表的感热通量是高原加热的主要来源。不过，在所有季节的高原西部都能观测到凝结加热的额外贡献；更显著的是，夏季高原东部也有这种贡献。高海拔高原地表的感热加热与环境中的辐射冷却维持了水平温度反差，从而驱动热力直接环流。

The detailed examination of the warming process of the upper troposphere during two transition periods, i.e., the onset of the Southeast Asian monsoon in May and that of the Indian monsoon in June, reveals that the temperature increase over the eastern Plateau during the first onset was mainly the result of diabatic heating, whereas that over the Iran-Afghanistan-western Plateau region leading to the second onset was caused by intense subsidence.

对两个过渡期上对流层增温过程的详细考察——即5月东南亚季风爆发和6月印度季风爆发——表明，第一次爆发期间高原东部的增温主要是非绝热加热的结果；而导致第二次爆发的伊朗—阿富汗—高原西部区域的增温，则由强下沉运动造成。

front-p01-s012 · PDF p.1
Source / 源文

There are large diurnal variations in the boundary layer and vertical circulation over the Plateau. As a result of diurnal heating of the surface, a deep mixed layer of nearly uniform potential temperature exists over the Plateau in the evening (1200 UTC), suggesting the role of thermal convection in the upward transport of heat. However, moisture is not well mixed vertically and there is a large horizontal temperature gradient in the boundary layer. From late spring to summer the boundary layer becomes more stable for dry convection. On the other hand, the vertical distributions of equivalent potential temperature in late spring and afterwards show a conditionally unstable stratification for moist convection with the increase of moisture of surface air.

Chinese / 中文

高原上的边界层和垂直环流存在很大的日变化。由于地表的日变化加热，傍晚（1200 UTC）高原上存在一个位温近乎均匀的深厚混合层，这表明热对流在热量向上输送中发挥作用。然而，水汽在垂直方向上混合并不充分，边界层内还存在很大的水平温度梯度。从晚春到夏季，边界层对干对流变得更加稳定。另一方面，晚春及其后相当位温的垂直分布显示，随着地表空气水汽增加，层结对湿对流呈条件不稳定。

Source / 源文

The importance of the Tibetan Plateau (the Qinghai-Xizang Plateau) as an elevated heat source for the establishment and maintenance of the Asian summer monsoon circulation has been discussed by many authors [see e. g., Yeh (1981, 1982), Gao et al., (1981), Murakami (1987a, b), Sumi and Murakami (1984) for reviews]. Recently, rapid progress has been made in observational studies using the data obtained during the First Global Atmospheric Research Program (GARP) Global Experiment (FGGE) and the Chinese Qinghai-Xizang Plateau Meteorology Experiment (QXPMEX) in 1979 (see, e. g., Johnson et al., 1987; Yeh, 1988; Zhang et al., 1988; Ji et al., 1989). In addition, a series of recent numerical experiments in which large-scale uplifts of the Tibetan Plateau were linked to the climatic changes on the geological time scale elucidated the effects of the Plateau on the planetary-scale circulations (Kutzbach et al., 1989; Manabe and Broccoli, 1990).

Chinese / 中文

许多作者讨论了青藏高原（青海—西藏高原）作为高海拔热源、对亚洲夏季风环流建立和维持的重要性[综述见例如 Yeh（1981，1982）、Gao 等（1981）、Murakami（1987a，b）以及 Sumi 和 Murakami（1984）]。近年来，利用1979年第一次全球大气研究计划（GARP）全球试验（FGGE）和中国青海—西藏高原气象试验（QXPMEX）期间获得的资料，观测研究取得了快速进展（例如见 Johnson 等，1987；Yeh，1988；Zhang 等，1988；Ji 等，1989）。此外，一系列近期数值试验将青藏高原的大尺度隆升与地质时间尺度上的气候变化联系起来，阐明了高原对行星尺度环流的影响（Kutzbach 等，1989；Manabe 和 Broccoli，1990）。

main-p02-s003 · PDF p.2
Source / 源文

Yeh et al. (1957) [also in Staff Members of Academia Sinica (1958)], using the data for 1954-1956, discussed the influence of the Tibetan Plateau on the circulation over East Asia. They noted that, in summer, the wind blows cyclonically around the Plateau at the 3km level, while at 6km and above there is a huge anticyclone with its center over the southern periphery of the Plateau. This indicates that the temperature over the Plateau is higher than the surrounding air. They also estimated the mean long-wave radiative cooling, the short-wave absorption, the condensation heating and the sensible heat flux from the ground over the Tibetan Plateau in summer and in winter. From these they inferred that the Plateau is a heat source in summer and probably a heat sink in winter.

Chinese / 中文

Yeh 等（1957）[亦见 Staff Members of Academia Sinica（1958）]利用1954—1956年的资料，讨论了青藏高原对东亚环流的影响。他们指出，夏季在3 km高度风围绕高原作气旋式环流，而在6 km及以上存在一个巨大的反气旋，其中心位于高原南缘。这表明高原上空的温度高于周围空气。他们还估算了冬、夏季青藏高原上空的平均长波辐射冷却、短波吸收、凝结加热和来自地面的感热通量，并据此推断高原夏季是热源、冬季可能是冷源。

Flohn (1957, 1960), using observations from earlier expeditions, also concluded the existence of a thermal anticyclone above the Plateau. He suggested that the seasonal heating of the elevated surface of the Tibetan Plateau and the consequent reversal of the meridional temperature and pressure gradients south of 35°N trigger the large-scale change of the general circulation over East Asia and the monsoon burst over the Indian sub-continent. Similar suggestions were also made by Koteswaram (1958) and by Murakami (1958).

Chinese / 中文

Flohn（1957，1960）利用早期考察的观测资料，也得出高原上空存在热力反气旋的结论。他提出，青藏高原抬升地表的季节性加热，以及由此导致的35°N以南经向温度梯度和气压梯度反转，会触发东亚大气环流的大尺度变化和印度次大陆的季风爆发。Koteswaram（1958）和 Murakami（1958）也提出了类似看法。

Since the 1950s, a network of surface and upper-air stations on the Tibetan Plateau has been continuously expanded by the People’s Republic of China. Using the Chinese observations, Flohn (1968) examined summer meteorological conditions on the Plateau and its vicinity. He suggested the importance of both the sensible heat flux in the semi-arid western portion of the Plateau and the latent heat release over the Himalayas, Assam, Bengal and adjacent mountains for the occurrence of the warm center of the Tibetan anticyclone in the upper troposphere.

自20世纪50年代以来，中华人民共和国不断扩展青藏高原上的地面和高空站网。Flohn（1968）利用中国观测资料考察了高原及其邻近地区的夏季气象条件。他指出，高原半干旱西部的感热通量，以及喜马拉雅山、阿萨姆、孟加拉及邻近山地的潜热释放，对于上对流层西藏反气旋暖中心的形成都很重要。

The results presented in the previous sections have shown that during spring and summer the Plateau is a heat source. The relationship between the Tibetan heat source and the evolution of the Asian summer monsoon is, however, not immediately obvious. Early studies (e. g., Flohn, 1957, 1960; Staff Members of Academia Sinica, 1958) suggested that the seasonal heating of the elevated surface of the Plateau is responsible for the warm center of the South Asian (Tibetan) anticyclone in summer. Murakami and Ding (1982) compared the large-scale circulation and temperature fields before and after the onset of the 1979 Indian monsoon and noted a large temperature increase over the western end of the Tibetan Plateau. 7. 总结和结论

main-p25-s002 · PDF p.25
Source / 源文

We have described the seasonal changes in the large-scale flow patterns, temperature and OLR fields, vertical circulation, and those of heat sources and moisture sinks over the Tibetan Plateau and surrounding areas during a 9-month period from December 1978 to August 1979, using the objectively analyzed FGGE Level II-b and QXPMEX-79 datasets. The major findings of this work may be summarized as follows:

Chinese / 中文

我们利用客观分析的 FGGE Level II-b 和 QXPMEX-79 数据集，描述了1978年12月至1979年8月9个月期间青藏高原及其周边地区大尺度流型、温度和 OLR 场、垂直环流，以及热源和水汽汇的季节变化。主要发现概括如下：

Source / 源文

1) The thermal effect of the Tibetan Plateau is evident from the positive temperature anomalies over the Plateau. During the three seasons, the air above the Plateau is always warmer than the air over the surrounding areas when compared at the same level and at the same latitude.

Chinese / 中文

1）青藏高原的热力作用从高原上的正温度距平中可以看出。在三个季节，与同一高度、同一纬度比较，高原上空空气始终比周边地区空气暖。

main-p25-s004 · PDF p.25
Source / 源文

2) There is a large-scale vertical circulation thermally induced by the Plateau. This circulation is originally separated from the circulation associated with the principal monsoon system which migrates northward from winter to summer. The rising motion is seen near the surface of the western Plateau in December and spreads over the entire Plateau as the season progresses. The existence of the Plateau-induced vertical circulation is also evident in the distribution of subsidence in the surrounding desert areas, as pointed out by He et al. (1987).

Chinese / 中文

2）存在由高原热力诱导的大尺度垂直环流。该环流起初与从冬季向夏季向北迁移的主要季风系统相关环流相分离。12月高原西部近地表可见上升运动，随着季节推进扩展到整个高原。正如 He 等（1987）指出，周边沙漠地区下沉运动的分布也表明了高原诱导垂直环流的存在。

main-p25-s005 · PDF p.25
Source / 源文

3) An “explosive” expansion of the warm air over the Plateau occurs from late spring to early summer, leading to the reversal of meridional temperature gradient south of the Plateau. This warming causes the South Asian anticyclone to shift northward and the low-level southwesterlies to develop in the Indian Ocean. The area of ascent over the Plateau and that with the migrating rainbelt merge together, extending the monsoon rains to the north.

Chinese / 中文

3）从晚春到初夏，高原上空暖空气“爆发式”扩展，导致高原以南经向温度梯度反转。这一增温使南亚反气旋向北移动，并使印度洋上空低层西南风发展。高原上空上升区与随雨带迁移的上升区合并，使季风降雨向北扩展。

Source / 源文

4) During winter the Plateau is a weak heat sink and it changes into a heat source in spring. However, the Plateau is surrounded by regions of intense cooling, i. e., the desert areas of Saudi Arabia and northern India, the Arabian Sea and the China Plain. The differential heating between the Plateau and its neighborhood maintains the temperature contrast that drives ascent over the Plateau and subsidence in the surrounding areas.

Chinese / 中文

4）冬季高原是弱冷源，春季转变为热源。然而，高原被强冷却区域包围，即沙特阿拉伯和印度北部的沙漠区、阿拉伯海以及中国平原。高原与邻近地区之间的差异加热维持了温度反差，驱动高原上升以及周围地区下沉。

Source / 源文

5) The sensible heat flux from the ground surface is the dominant factor in the heat budget of the Tibetan Plateau. The contribution of condensation heating is found in the western Plateau in all three seasons, and in the eastern Plateau after the onset of summer rains.

Chinese / 中文

5）地表感热通量是青藏高原热量收支中的主导因素。三个季节高原西部都有凝结加热贡献；夏季降雨开始后，高原东部也有凝结加热贡献。

Source / 源文

6) A detailed study of He et al. (1987) showed that the onset of the Asian summer monsoon exhibits two distinct periods of abrupt changes resulting in the successive commencements of summer rains over Southeast Asia and the west coast of India. The major tropospheric warming occurs first in May over the areas extending from the eastern Plateau to the South China Plain, which results in the reversal of the meridional temperature gradient south of the eastern Plateau and the commencement of the low-level southwesterlies over the Bay of Bengal. Then, the onset of the Indian monsoon occurs in June with the increase of temperature over a wide area covering Saudi Arabia, Iran, Afghanistan and the western Plateau.

7) The analyses of the warming processes of the upper troposphere during the two transition periods show that the diabatic heating is the primary cause of the temperature increase over the eastern Tibetan Plateau during the first onset. 7）两个过渡期上对流层增温过程的分析表明，第一次爆发期间青藏高原东部温度升高的主要原因是非绝热加热。然而，第二次爆发期间，高原以西温度升高的关键机制是下沉导致的绝热增温。
